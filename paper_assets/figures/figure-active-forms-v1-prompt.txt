Use case: infographic-diagram.
Create one polished scientific infographic for the active-forms section of a research preprint. Landscape 16:9, ideally 2048 × 1152 pixels, opaque white background with white corners. Match a modern scientific figure: large navy sans-serif typography, pale cyan panels, teal tissue cells and retained patterns, amber writing/feedback arrows, thin gray dividers and generous whitespace. Subtle translucent cell illustrations are welcome; avoid ornate decoration. Prioritize legibility and clear causal routes. This is a conceptual explanation of separate model experiments, not a measured plot or saved simulation output. Do not invent numbers or add text beyond the wording specified below.

TITLE, large:
Active forms

SUBTITLE:
Organization can help maintain the conditions for its own return.

MAIN LAYOUT: three equal, spacious panels across the page. These are separate experiments, NOT stages in one experiment; do not connect the panels with arrows. Each panel has a heading, a clear central diagram, and two or three short text lines. Keep the words large enough for a manuscript figure. Use cells and small spatial-record grids consistently, but make the experimental contrasts obvious.

LEFT PANEL
Heading: HISTORY CHANGES RESTORATION
Show a short left-to-right route inside this panel: supplied exposure to a simple teal spatial pattern, then an acquired reference field, then a partly disturbed tissue returning toward that pattern. Amber writing arrow from exposure/body activity into the reference; teal reading arrow from reference toward the later tissue. Do not imply that the particular exposure arose autonomously.
Labels: Experience / Reference / Later restoration
Main text:
Experience changes what the system later restores.
Control text:
Blocking writing or reading removes the exposure effect.

CENTRE PANEL
Heading: ORGANIZATION HELPS REPAIR
Show a paired comparison. Two small surviving tissue patches contain the SAME numbers of teal and pale cells; one has an organized teal band, the other has those same colored cells spatially scrambled. Labels: Arranged / Scrambled
Both patches interact with IDENTICAL damaged record grids, shown with the same missing/gray squares. Each branch writes an outgoing record; only that record proceeds across a small dashed replacement line into a fresh-body assay. Do not draw a surviving-body-to-fresh-body copying arrow. A tiny record icon on each crossing makes the information boundary unambiguous. The arranged branch can show a modestly clearer outgoing pattern, never perfect restoration or a numerical result.
Text above or beside the paired comparison:
Same damaged record. Same body values.
Main result text:
Surviving arrangement improves the record's later usefulness.
Boundary text:
Only the repaired record enters the fresh-body test.

RIGHT PANEL
Heading: WRITER RELATIONSHIPS MATTER
Show two matched small grids with the same starting record and writer-feedback geometry. In the left grid an amber feedback arrow addresses its corresponding teal site; in the right grid an amber arrow addresses a displaced site. This is a change in WHERE FEEDBACK ACTS, not merely a shifted picture of the final body. Labels: Aligned / Displaced
Text:
Same starting record; changed feedback placement.
Main result text:
The relationship changes reconstruction.
Small condition line:
Shared guide and machinery supplied.

LOWER CONTRAST STRIP, much shallower than the three main panels:
Heading: RECOGNITION DOES NOT GUARANTEE RENEWAL
At left, a small cellular-automaton grid leads to an eye/decoder icon with the text:
CA: an observer decoded history
At right, a separate small attempted writing loop has an interrupted arrow, with the text:
Tested live writers failed renewal screening
Use no percentages, bar charts, equal signs or shared numerical scale. These were different information-processing routes, not a matched contest. Small centered line within this strip:
Different transformations; not a resource-matched comparison.

BOTTOM TAKEAWAY, large navy:
Active contribution is demonstrated; autonomous maintenance remains open.

Small legible footer:
Separate model experiments. Conceptual diagrams, not measured fields.

CONSTRAINTS: Exact spelling, large readable type and ample margins. No numbered progression or connecting arrows between the three panels. No consciousness imagery, faces, mystical forces, metaphysical claims, DNA-as-record icon, invented data, flawless regeneration, or implication that the maintenance machinery evolved or maintains itself. Do not repeat the ruliad/Platonic-space strip from the companion discussion image. The focus is the causal evidence for active embodied organization. Keep the image visually clean and inviting rather than a dense methods poster.

Transmitted E requires all three recipients stable E in both isolated holds.
Stable E requires an E endpoint and at least 95% E samples in the last
quarter. Contact stability is separate. Also report first-newcomer sampled
resistance/recovery on 0,15,...,300, first-challenge endpoint/stability,
complete carrier replacement and the stricter every-renewal-window E rule.

For each history, route, failing-sequence partner and length, report the
original historical and heldout outcomes plus both reciprocal swaps. A
qualified chain requires retention, open transmission, negative RNA/cut
readouts and qualified reference/capacity/wipe evidence. Do not substitute
failure of this composite criterion for demonstrated loss of retention:
report retention and every readout independently.

For the neighbour route, a strong reciprocal switch requires historical
chain success and heldout retention/readout failure, loss of retention and
all readouts when its higher first value enters the historical tail, and a
qualified chain when the historical first value enters its heldout tail.
Report high-first disruption and low-first rescue separately. First-challenge
recovery reversal is another, distinct comparison. Mixed or null effects
remain informative; no threshold or single-value explanation is assumed.
Require all registered neighbour pairs to qualify before a blanket reciprocal
switch claim. Report the robust newcomer route's preservation separately;
its failure does not technically invalidate a valid neighbour comparison.

Scope causal conclusions to these fixed substitutions and backgrounds. This
panel does not estimate an incoming-protein dose threshold, general success
probability, unique molecular memory carrier, written-history advantage,
autonomous reproduction, or biological cancer treatment. It does not test
pause timing/duration or further recipient generations.

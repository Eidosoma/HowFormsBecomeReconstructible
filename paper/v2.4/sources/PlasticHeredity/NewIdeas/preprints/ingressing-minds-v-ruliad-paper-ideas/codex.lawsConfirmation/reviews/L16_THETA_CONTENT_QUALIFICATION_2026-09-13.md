## Plain-language result

The arrangement of theta's values matters, not merely their amount or
strength. Put the same-strength field in the arrangement for pattern A and
fresh bodies develop A; arrange it for B and they develop B. Shuffling the
same values largely removes that target preference. The bodies begin the
main comparison identically and reference feedback is off.

Average amplitude-qualified target agreement is **95.79% for A and92.69%
for B**, compared with41.50%/43.38% under their respective shuffled fields.
The primary is a different quantity: the improvement in own-minus-other
pattern preference, **+47.29 [44.73,50.31] percentage points**. All eight
randomised blocks and all32 lives are positive, in each role separately too.
This is not a near-threshold result and no80% success hurdle was used.

However, the field was built directly from the answer and remained present
throughout development. The experiment shows that theta **can specify** these
patterns; it does not show that a native random field had learned them, that
the specification was autonomously written, or that descendants renew it.

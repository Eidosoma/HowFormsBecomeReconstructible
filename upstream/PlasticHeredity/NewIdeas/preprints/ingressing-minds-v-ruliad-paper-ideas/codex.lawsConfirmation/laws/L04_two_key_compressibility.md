## Source-audit finding

L4 is not confirmed as written. The bounded ECA observer effect is strong:
all four named class-4 rules reproduce with the particle map at widths 63–65
and noise 0.01, while their raw anchor rates are zero. At width 128 none of
the four remain in band, and several class-3 rules also pass, so `redeems
class 4` is neither scale-universal nor class-specific.

`Compressible background required` is not established. Only two or three
low-coverage rules occur per selected panel, the dictionary is rule-specific,
and coverage-versus-capability correlations are negative. E28's prospective
compressibility gate also fails.

All E28 prospective gates fail. Its brief bulk-census signal is post hoc, and
the retained generation-2/4/6 values do not replay even though a generation-10
full-precision control does. No conserved/nonconserved ablation exists; the
observer is a binarized block census, and total voltage is reset across half
the daughter at fission.

E63's within-minus-same point estimates are positive on three substrates, but
the registered clone validation fails all three. Its positive round-4 rule was
applied after the same cached aggregates existed, and the purported cluster
bootstrap discards resampled-lineage multiplicities.

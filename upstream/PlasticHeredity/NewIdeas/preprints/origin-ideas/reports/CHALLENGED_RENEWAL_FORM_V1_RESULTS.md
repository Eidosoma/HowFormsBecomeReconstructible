# Challenged-Renewal Statistical-Form V1 Results

Date: 2026-08-23  
Outcome: prospective controlled one-boundary **nonpass**

## Main result

The selective-retention mechanism did not restore parent-specific statistical
form after break and renewal. Under the frozen mechanics exclusions, active
median restoration was -0.0395 for contact and -0.0364 for proximity. Every one
of the six resolved active parent means was negative under both observers.
Active-minus-passive restoration gain was -0.0020 (p=0.906) for contact and
+0.00067 (p=0.906) for proximity. Thus the controlled result is effectively
zero and in neither case significant.

This is a direct test of a GARD-like idea: renewed daughters contained many new
grains, so exact ingredients were not required. The requested endpoint was the
return of a similar type-aware statistical pattern. That endpoint did not
return.

## Parent retrieval

Mechanically resolved active successors retrieved their own parent only 2/16
times under contact and 3/16 under proximity, with coverage of only one and two
of eight parents. Contact performance is at the 2/16 chance expectation;
proximity gave grouped p=0.0625 but failed the prospective threshold, had a
negative median own-parent margin, and did not beat controls by the required
amount. Active type-blind retrieval was 2/16 under both observers; passive
typed retrieval was 1/16.

The two orientations of a parent were barely more similar to each other than
to descendants of other parents. The within-minus-cross-parent mature
similarity difference was 0.00157 for active contact and 0.00067 for active
proximity. The filter therefore did not preserve a robust parent-specific
cluster in phenotype space.

## Mechanics

All 32 simulations completed N=64 renewal, but the strict resolution gate
passed only 9/16 active, 11/16 passive, and 9/16 paired successors, below the
required 12 in each category. Most exclusions came from debris-heavy splits;
three active/passive attempts also failed exact attachment/challenge delivery.
All seed, parent-checkpoint, source-half, proposal-prefix, and frozen-source
hash audits passed.

The form conclusion is not an artefact of those exclusions. In a declared
post-outcome sensitivity using every completed successor, active restoration
remained negative: mean/median -0.0352/-0.0322 for contact and
-0.0339/-0.0266 for proximity. Only 1/16 active successors had positive
restoration under either observer, compared with 4/16 passive successors.
These values do not replace the frozen analysis.

## Interpretation

The experiments now separate two phenomena cleanly:

- the aggregate can break, renew, and selectively retain same-type incoming
  grains; but
- that local filter produces no detected return of the particular parent's
  larger-scale statistical form.

So this simulator currently shows selective self-organization, not a reproduced
version of PH. More seeds, F12, or recurrence would not repair the missing
one-boundary restoration signal. Any future branch should change a mechanism
or identify a better physically justified carrier, rather than relax this gate.

No claim is made about real dust, planet formation, or astrophysical heredity.

# TT050 — causal re-isolation diagnosis

Reviewer-priority development test, 1 November 2026. This takes priority over
further TT049 repair studies. E119 is preparing the four-rewrite duration test;
TT050 supplies causal controls and reusable trajectory instrumentation. Do not
replace the question of delayed versus persistent harm with another unchanged
one-rewrite confirmation. This study diagnoses the first rewrite; it makes no
duration claim and launches no serial successor.

## Evidence and fixed population

E077 matched terminal injury, birth and noise across treatments AND horizons.
Its low immediate/later beneficiary overlap is not evidence of independent
assessment injuries. E098's independent-writer mixture pruning contrast is
+1.81 percentage points [−0.68, 4.20], unresolved; acquisition and terminal cues
were still matched. E079 resolves magnitude effects at both supplied sign
settings, not sign equivalence. E099 retains a writer-noise benefit under
ordinary selected-edge capture. Neither licenses assuming that fallback signs
are harmful because they are task-irrelevant.

Use the first eight already exposed E077 blocks, 41000–41007, chosen by ID before
new interventions or reads: every native/mixture × sham/pulse source, all four
founders and four recipients, 512 starting records. No selection by an old or
new result, isolation count, match feasibility or success. Verify the saved
source, selected-edge masks and interventions against E077's completion hashes.
These are reused sources with new interventions, not prospectively fresh
lineages. Existing reader outcomes are not pooled with new measurements.

Selection E is exactly E077's incoming-zero, outgoing-nonzero, exact-fallback
set at g1. I is unchanged g1; P sets E to positive zero once. Opposite founders
retain the original paired draws. Native and mixture sources and both histories
remain separate as well as pooled; mixture is the principal descriptive panel.

## Interventions, sealed before receiving outcomes

All constructions use only the current I packet and E, including its signs and
magnitudes. This is an externally supplied mechanism experiment, not autonomous
repair. No future injury mask, body state, task orientation, assessment result
or old outcome enters the construction.

1. Start with P's graph and add selected edges in increasing flattened edge ID
   only when they join different connected components. The resulting set R is
   a deterministic minimum-cardinality extension restoring every connected
   component of I, including all its port connections. C retains I's values on
   R and prunes E minus R. It prevents new isolation before subsequent injury.
2. Construct an equal-size retained set S that leaves cells isolated. Visit in
   cell-ID order the nonport cells newly isolated by P. Forbid their incident
   selected edges whenever at least |R| candidates would remain; keep every
   feasible such prohibition. Match each R edge in ID order to an unused
   candidate minimizing the absolute difference in log absolute weight; break
   ties by edge ID. If no such cell can be held isolated, set S=R, flag the
   comparison infeasible, and retain it as a zero structural contrast. Empty
   selections and all other infeasible/aliased cases remain.
3. L_native retains the actual I values on S. C versus L_native is exact
   count-matched pruning within E; removed mass, signs and graph location can
   differ and are disclosed. L places the R donor values at their assigned S
   positions. C versus L additionally matches the entire restored signed
   weight multiset, absolute mass and edge count. This transplant is explicitly
   different from pure pruning; positions and local normalization can differ.
4. For both C and L, cross native donor signs versus all donor signs reversed
   with native magnitudes versus one-quarter magnitudes. This gives eight
   reconnection arms, plus I, P and L_native: eleven physical starting arms.
   All nonselected bytes remain identical to P. Signs-only comparisons preserve
   support, absolute weights and natural denominators exactly. Magnitude
   changes alter relative influence, including that of unchanged outside edges.

The equal-weight placement comparison cannot identify a unique mediation
fraction for isolation: graph location, port paths and injury vulnerability
also change. The count match alone is insufficient. Concordance across these
controls, realized isolation prevention, immediate benefit and subsequent cost
is the intended causal diagnosis. An ineffective manipulation after injury is
reported, not discarded. An arbitrarily weak nonzero edge is not a neutral
support intervention under natural normalization.

## Same-body capture controls

At the first rewrite, for P and native C separately, retain the same body and
construct the E099-style 2×2 selected-edge capture factorial. H is the ordinary
exact-zero hybrid writer; O uses coactivity on E; S uses O signs/H magnitudes;
M uses H signs/O magnitudes. Outside E retain H byte for byte. H is already the
core outgoing arm; six additional outgoing arms require no further writers.
Declare any selected support disagreement: those comparisons are not pure sign
or magnitude effects. Keep them, including zeros, signed zeros and harms.
This intervenes on capture after a fixed body, not on noise or isolated-cell
contrast. No positive filter effect or particular sign effect is assumed.

## Analysis and interpretive limits

Principal paired estimates: P−I and C−I at h0 and h1; C−P at both horizons;
C−L_native and C−L at h1, with corresponding h0 effects. Report both sign
effects and both magnitude effects, and their placement interactions; report
all same-body capture contrasts. Retain all-case and feasible-pair summaries,
but the latter cannot replace the population result. All denominators and
alias counts remain visible. Compare realized postinjury isolation and port
connectivity between C, L and P without treating these associations as proof
of unique mediation.

Average draws within record, founders/recipients/histories within source kind
and block, then blocks equally. Eight blocks, not thousands of contexts, are
the inference units. Give paired gains and harms, block values and pointwise
95% whole-block percentile intervals (10,000 draws, fixed TT050 analysis seed).
Also give bounded Hoeffding intervals so zero observed variation is not reported
as population certainty. No familywise positive verdict, new threshold search,
significance-driven extension or claim of equivalence from a nonsignificant
contrast. Removing a cost and retaining a benefit must be assessed jointly;
confidence intervals crossing zero do not establish removal. Eight reused
blocks diagnose controls; fresh confirmation remains a separate frozen step.

## Verification, resources and stopping

First run synthetic graph fixtures, independent intervention checks, and an
excluded E077 benchmark-source bridge (900041, R=1, 512 steps). A short complete
pipeline on that source checks all arms, diagnostic exports and analysis.
Freeze inputs before full qualification. Two parallel full-size qualification
workers reuse the same excluded 900041 source, tiled to four recipient slots;
this measures runtime, not independent evidence. Each executes the complete
eleven-arm writer, 28 terminal slots, two draws and independent replay at 2,048
steps. No fresh scientific outcomes are exposed during qualification.

If qualification and CPU/storage admission pass, run the eight fixed development
blocks on two normal-priority workers (CPUs 6/7), one numerical thread each,
within the shared 12-thread limit. Forecast 1.5 × four waves × measured full
qualification wave + 120 seconds. Require forecast <= eight hours; deadline
twice forecast. Qualification deadline one hour. Storage ceiling 2 GiB, 192 MiB
per block, 4 GiB free reserve; stop and preserve evidence on any guard or audit
failure. No automatic retry, block replacement or cohort reduction.

Detach qualification and conditionally admitted development with frozen inputs,
PID/log and durable phase status. After one startup check, leave the job unpolled
until the user requests status. No TT049 successor or other scientific campaign
starts automatically. Frozen files and failed attempts are never overwritten.

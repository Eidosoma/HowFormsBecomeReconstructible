Scope fixed on 10 October 2026 after reading the completed source assay's protocol,
prediction, configuration, completion receipt and frozen manifest, before reading
its performance report or analysis. The preceding TT011 findings and the existence
of the fallback supplement are exposed. This is an audit of development evidence,
not a new experiment or a prospective confirmation.

The source is `p05_approximate_alignment_v0_1/runs/pilot_v1`: 64 exposed recipients,
eight blocks, nine records, 16 fresh draws and one original-injury draw. Audit its
declared method and all outcomes regardless of the primary direction. Preserve
failed source validations and all local failures; never repair frozen source data.

Interpretation must distinguish ordinary sign repair from historical task content.
The recipient-only completion baseline is one algorithm, not every possible
history-free reconstruction. All hybrids receive old-record support and magnitudes;
donor alignment also receives a donor and fixes all task ports. A donor advantage
over that baseline would not establish irreducible historical information, unique
RDT support, new-cohort generalization or autonomous inheritance. Completion
success would test this supplied-resource intervention, not reconstruction from
the damaged record alone. Coordinate compatibility and task content remain
separate explanatory questions.

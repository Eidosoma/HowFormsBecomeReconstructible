## Plain-language result

We took established E-state lineages halfway through their relay, introduced
M-state cells as a temporary opposing cue, then removed those cue cells.
We asked whether the original carriers would keep the new M state and pass
it to six successive groups of fresh E cells. They did not: the carriers
stayed E, and the M cue cells instead became E during open contact.

The important control is that ordinary M donors also failed to change fresh
E recipients under the new transmission setting. Those donors likewise
became E. This means we cannot yet credit the established lineages with
unusual resistance to rewriting. The chosen conditions favor E even without
the established lineage history. A working ordinary-M transmission condition
is needed before revisiting that comparison.

## What happened

| Comparison | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Four main M-cue conditions: two ancestries x two writing modes | 0/4 durable M rewrites; all three original carriers retain E |
| Opposing M cues during open writing contact | All three become E in each main condition |
| Writing-stage RNA block or boundary cut | Original carriers remain E; opposing cues remain M |
| E shams and cue-free aging controls | Original carriers remain E |
| Ordinary-M capacity assay | M donors become E; fresh E recipients remain E |
| Ordinary-E capacity assay | Donors and recipients remain E |
| Six new handovers per rewrite branch | No retained-M recipient rounds in any coarse branch |

All 28 coarse rewrite branches retain three E carriers after writing and
isolation. Relay-stage block controls cannot reveal whether a *successfully
rewritten M lineage* would transmit M: writing never produced that starting
condition. Fresh recipients start E, so remaining E is **not new E acquisition
or new evidence of E inheritance** in this panel.

The fixed always-copy-open-route binary reference matches **161/198** coarse
checkpoints. Its 37 mismatches reflect overprediction of M copying in open
contact. They are not evidence for a new mechanism, and the comparator was
neither fitted to outcomes nor required to agree for an execution pass.

## Relation to previous work and limits

P5 demonstrated externally scheduled E renewal into initially M recipients
under hypol/power 1. P6 instead used depol/power 60 with initially E recipients
for the onward assay. These are different conditions and directions, not a
contradiction. Both ordinary-E and restored-E ancestries gave the same measured
P6 outcomes. No special acquired-history protection has been demonstrated.

P6 used miRNA Kd 1.25e-14, carried over from restoration/E transmission work.
Earlier P1/P3 successful M acquisition used 8.801117367933935e-16, about 14.2
times lower. Group size and topology also differ. This motivates calibration,
not a conclusion that lower transport will necessarily rescue M transmission.

E/M are native regulatory classifications, not complete healthy/cancer
diagnoses. hypol is a model gate configuration, not a calibrated electrical
clamp. The cells do not divide, migrate, reproduce or schedule their own
handovers. These results neither refute PH in general nor prove that memory
cannot be rewritten. They establish the limits of this particular test.

Each block includes four founders, four recipients and two original histories;
paired resampling keeps these together and weights histories equally. The
three primary percentile intervals use confidence 1 − .05/3, with 10,000

form nonzero records and was not clamped empty.

Secondary pointwise 95% estimates support a change beyond threshold crossings:
rule-aligned transfer rises **0.4741 → 0.5922 → 0.6125**, with h3 improvement

Recovery remains incomplete and heterogeneous. The h3 mixture is **27.15
points below native [23.44, 30.96]**, pointwise 95%. There are **367 gains
and 69 losses**, 242 successes at both endpoints and 346 failures at both.
Wrong-sign output cases fall **701 → 443 → 405**; correct-sign but weak cases
are **12 → 61 → 10**. Fully opposite-rule successes rise **4 → 16 → 29**.
Thus improvement is not universal correction. Wrong-sign means can be weak;
these counts do not classify stable wrong identities or individual edge signs.
Native change is **−1.17 points [−4.00, 1.46]**, unresolved; do not claim either
a demonstrated native decline or equivalence.

## What is now established

E067's partial-recovery finding repeats on an independently generated cohort.
The original-rule response can improve through ordinary rewriting after one
initial incompatible-magnitude mixture construction. Subsequent writers use
only the current packet, fresh body and cues: no earlier archive, teacher,
output target or continuing body state. The absolute gain excludes an account
based only on native decline; the matched-copy and erased-start comparisons
exclude attrition alone and the observed erased baseline as sufficient accounts.

E068 generated all acquisition, 32-renewal ancestry, sham/pulse capture and
three native source continuations anew, then ran the unchanged E067 recovery
test. No E065–E067 scientific cases or scores were pooled. The new h3 estimate
59.47% is close to E067's 58.69%, but this is not a formal between-cohort
equivalence test and must not be described as one.

The confirmation retains shared cue birth/noise, exact-zero fallback, float64
and ideal strength normalization. It therefore does not resolve robustness to
changing those facilities, identify a unique causal mediator, demonstrate full
repair or fine founder-specificity, or establish biological rejuvenation.

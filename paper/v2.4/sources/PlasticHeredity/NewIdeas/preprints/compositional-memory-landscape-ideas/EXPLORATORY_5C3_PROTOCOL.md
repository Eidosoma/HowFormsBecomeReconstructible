# Exploratory Experiment #5C.3 Protocol

## Question and claim boundary

#5C.3 freezes the best #5C.1 or #5C.2 intervention for every task and direction
before producing any confirmation future. It asks whether those engineered
landscapes support bidirectional 48-generation recovery on the reused fresh
panel. A positive result establishes target-aware storage capacity only.

## Frozen confirmation

- Use 64 new futures, 48 generations, and checkpoints 1, 6, 12, 24, and 48.
- From the primary legacy common reset compare winner, opposite-history beta,
  original beta, and dose-matched shuffled and random controls.
- From exact target, exact rival, and 25% corrupted target compare winner with
  original beta.
- From the secondary geometric reset compare winner, opposite-history beta,
  and original beta.
- Follow both first daughters for depth three in 32 futures for winner versus
  original from the legacy reset.
- Generate the entire confirmation twice in separate trees and require exact
  array equality.
- Use 4,096 task/matrix-cluster bootstrap repetitions and one joint Holm
  correction over both contracts and every registered contrast.

The report separates A and B trajectories, screen-to-confirmation shrinkage,
terminal control specificity, every registered gate and contrast, and the
daughter-lineage diagnostic. These summaries are descriptive and cannot alter
the frozen gates. Missing registered contrasts, incomplete A/B pairs, or
incomplete daughter arms are technical failures rather than negative evidence.

## Primary gates and classification

The legacy-reset contract requires generation-48 target probability at least
0.25, own-minus-opposite crossover at least 0.10 with positive confidence
bound, significant superiority to all four controls at generations 24 and 48,
corruption and exact-rival recovery, exact-target retention, survival
noninferiority within 0.05, and at least half of A/B pairs positive.

- Both contracts: `FRESH_PANEL_BIDIRECTIONAL_CAPACITY_RESCUED`.
- One contract: `CONTRACT_SPECIFIC_FRESH_CAPACITY_RESCUED`.
- Legacy failure but a coherent geometric-reset result:
  `BALANCED_RESET_DEPENDENT_CAPACITY`.
- Neither: `FRESH_PANEL_CAPACITY_NOT_RESCUED`.

Any technical failure is `TECHNICALLY_UNADJUDICATED`. Even a positive primary
classification requires a later, prospectively frozen untouched-panel
replication before a preprint claim. No follow-up experiment launches
automatically.

## Numerical and control amendment (2026-08-29)

The first production attempt stopped after completing 14 of 18 first-pass
matrix shards. Six high-dose reversed-control directions exposed two separate
issues: three reachable controls required projection scales above the former
implementation ceiling of 64, while three exact reversed directions could not
attain the registered RMS dose even after the clipped exponential path was
fully saturated. The winner, opposite-history, original, shuffled, and random
arms were feasible in every registered direction.

The deterministic dose solver now searches through scale 4096 and reports the
maximum sampled dose when a requested dose is genuinely unreachable. The
reversed arm is removed uniformly from every task rather than retained only
where feasible or silently run at a smaller dose. The four remaining controls
stay dose matched, and every endpoint, winner, seed, task, future count,
horizon, gate threshold, and correction rule is unchanged apart from the gate
now requiring superiority to all four feasible controls.

Registration now also preflights every frozen winner, opposite-history beta,
and shuffled/random control for positivity, row/column budget preservation,
and requested dose before any confirmation future is generated.

No stochastic outcome from the incomplete shards was inspected to choose this
amendment. The failed attempt is preserved separately, and the amended campaign
must receive a fresh registration and run from scratch; no checkpoint may cross
the registration boundary.

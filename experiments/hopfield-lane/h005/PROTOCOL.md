# H005: matched present expression and new descendant futures

Prospective development specification, 11 October 2026. H004's confirmation
is complete and unchanged. H005 uses already exposed H003 development
checkpoints and generates new future branches; it is not a new-base-network
confirmation and does not reuse the H004 cohort for selection.

## Question and comparison boundary

Compare the G16 own-record packets produced by H003's gain-1 and gain-4
histories, paired within the same base network, founder and descendant.
Transfer each packet into the same future apparatus: gain 4 at input sites
0–3, eight sequential sweeps per cue, complete body-only writing, 50% fresh
undirected edge deletion per replacement. The original teacher is absent.
Base weights, cue convention, future births, damage and update tapes are
paired between histories and controls. Only the incoming packet differs.

Current expression is measured by independent, undamaged fresh-body reads of
that packet. These diagnostic bodies never write or enter the descendant
chains. The inherited state at the fork is the packet, not the probe body.
The four task-aligned output-patch means (two patches under two cues), their
minimum and the original strict success condition (> .5 throughout) define
the observer. Equal present expression does not mean equal complete state or
equal latent probability of successful expression.

## Inputs, matching and admission

Use every H003 development block, seeds 13000–13023, all four founders and
four descendant replicas: 384 prespecified candidate packet pairs. The
engineering run uses the separately exposed benchmark block 12900 and is
excluded. Extract G16 packets and their writing bodies from sealed raw data;
check each packet against the complete writer equation. Copy the extracted
inputs and source manifests into H005 before any new simulation.

Read every pair under four new independent present-probe tapes shared between
histories. A pair is eligible only if both records pass all four probes AND
the four output means match exactly between histories on every probe. There
is no caliper, pair reassignment, nearest-neighbor search or matching-rule
revision. Retain the full eligibility matrix, exact margins, raw probe states,
norms and signed cue connections, including all nonmatching pairs.

Use 16 additional independent undamaged probes per pair to validate current
expression. They do not change eligibility. Save all enrollment and validation
results, their hashes, and the declared directional forecast before generating
any descendant futures for any block. No future field enters enrollment.

At least 64 pairs in at least 16 of the 24 base networks must meet the matching
rule. For matched pairs, validation success must average >=.90 in each history.
The 90% whole-block bootstrap intervals for the between-history difference in
success and each of the four mean output signals must lie wholly within
[-.05, .05]. These are explicit, approximate equivalence checks under the
chosen observer, not equality of all future-relevant state. Sparse enrollment
or failed present-expression validation blocks qualification; neither permits
wider matching or a substitute cohort. All candidate futures are still retained.

## New futures and record controls

For every candidate pair, generate eight independent future branches through
16 additional replacements (ending at total generation 32). Branch randomness
uses a new H005 namespace, independent of current probes and all H003 tapes.
At each branch/generation, births, damage and update orders are shared between
the two histories and all arms. Forks and founders are nested within their
base network; they are not independent inferential units. Continue every
branch after failure so recovery is observable.

Retain seven record arms for each history:

1. renewal: the history's own G16 record and complete body-driven rewriting;
2. equalized: give both histories the gain-4-history packet, then identical
   complete rewriting; all subsequent states must agree exactly;
3. foreign: the same-history packet from the opposite-rule founder, using
   donor mapping [2,3,0,1]; complete rewriting continues;
4. protected copy: preserve the source packet without injury or writing;
5. damaged copy: accumulate edge deletion without writing;
6. read off: develop with the gained base matrix alone, then write the body;
7. write off: develop normally, but produce a zero outgoing packet.

Equalization tests that packet identity, not a history label or retained donor
activity, determines the simulated future. It does not localize the responsible
entries within the packet. Protected and damaged copying retain their lower
computation cost. Every arm uses the same final reader. There is no repeated
training, ancestral rescue or access to future outcomes.

Primary endpoint: fresh undamaged reader success after 16 further replacements.
Independently record fresh-reader success after 1, 4 and 8 replacements,
damaged-body success at every replacement, and uninterrupted success through
all 16 body developments plus the final read. Terminal recovery and
uninterrupted persistence are separate outcomes. Intermediate horizons,
unmatched pairs and copying comparisons are descriptive and cannot replace
the primary.

## Frozen development decisions

The directional hypothesis is that the gain-4-history packet supports better
continuation than its matched gain-1-history counterpart under this identical
future apparatus. Average forks and eligible pairs within each nonempty base
network, then weight those networks equally. Always report which blocks are
empty, the eligible count per block and all 384 candidate-pair results.

Qualification requires the enrollment/current-expression checks above, all
integrity checks, and all of the following at the primary horizon:

- own-record gain-4-history minus gain-1-history success >=.10;
- within gain-4 history, own minus foreign, read-off, write-off and damaged-copy
  success each >=.15;
- positive one-sided Bonferroni lower bounds for those five differences,
  using quantile .05/5 from 20,000 joint whole-network bootstrap draws.

Use analysis seed 950005 and namespace `H005 whole-block bootstrap`. The same
block resample applies to all outcomes and present validation. Display other
intervals as marginal two-sided 95% intervals. At least two nonempty blocks
are needed to estimate an interval, but the 16-block coverage gate remains.
No sample enlargement, optional stopping, model selection or favorable
secondary outcome can rescue a failed gate. An opposite-direction result is
reported as such and fails this directional candidate. This is development,
not final confirmation or a calibrated future-probability predictor.

Matching after different past interventions does not identify the total or
direct causal effect of those histories. Paired transplantation into common
futures tests the consequences of different packets within the selected set.
The result is restricted to the declared present observer, matched set,
apparatus, disturbances and finite horizon. Ordinary record strength and cue
connection are retained descriptively; no fitted predictor is claimed. A
later predictor would need to beat present-expression, history and strength
baselines on held-out base networks. No unique theory is established by
finding that a coarse phenotype omits information.

## Execution and retained evidence

The common-future and matching boundary was checked against the neighboring
theory lane's P02 protocol, read only. H005 imports no theory implementation,
fitted predictor, private neural source, private result or trained state.
The Hopfield reader/writer implementation remains the lane's own public model.

Freeze this protocol, code, configuration and extracted source packets before
new probes. Freeze enrollment and the directional forecast before new futures.
Retain current states, every future body, outgoing packet, damage mask, reader,
endpoint, source receipt and block statistic. Packet entries are exactly
{-1/64,0,+1/64}; lossless int8 codes scaled by 64 reduce storage without changing
the dynamics. Verify this coding exactly and compare fixtures against the
existing H003 reader. Audit every boundary, cue clamp, endpoint, control
identity, semantic random stream and terminal replay. Numerical replay must
agree exactly; the existing scalar-energy check retains tolerance 1e-12.

Use one worker with one numerical thread. Benchmark one complete block with
all eight futures and audit before the main launch. Forecasts over 600 seconds
run detached with stdin disconnected, frozen source, log, PID and durable
status under ../RUN_POLICY.md from the lane root. Do not poll detached runs
without a user request. Preserve failures and original outputs. This study
does not edit the forms manuscript or publish a release.

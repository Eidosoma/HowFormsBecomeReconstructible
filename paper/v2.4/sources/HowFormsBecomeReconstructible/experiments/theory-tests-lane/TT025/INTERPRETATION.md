# TT025: actual rewrites reduce informative-record usefulness

The matched receiving diagnosis is complete and verified. Corrected writing
reduces informative-record recognition from **88.770% initially to 73.730%
after eight rewrites**, a **15.039-percentage-point loss**. The four block
effects are −12.109, −19.922, −11.719 and −16.406 points. This is the frozen
primary descriptive contrast. The measured loss concerns reconstruction from
the actual records under matched fresh receiving conditions.

The first complete corrected rewrite already reduces recognition by **3.223
points**, with a negative mean in every block. All eight cycle means are
negative, although individual draws and some block/cycle combinations improve.
The harm is therefore present before a long serial history accumulates. A
complete rewrite here lasts 1,000 updates; this result does not locate harm
within that episode or identify an effective shorter writing window.

The correction retains its substantial advantage over full capture: final
recognition is higher by **26.172 informative points** and **20.410 common
points**, positive in every block. Correction does not remove the informative
loss relative to preservation. Zero initial memory also loses usefulness, so
removing the initial captured-memory content does not eliminate the problem.
Memory subsequently updates and supplies feedback in that historical policy.

Corrected versus zero-memory final recognition is −1.855 informative points
and +0.781 common points, with mixed block effects. The informative ordering
differs from TT024's terminal sample. These small contrasts do not establish
superiority or equivalence. The common corrected aggregate loss is much smaller
than the informative loss, but its 61 harmed draws and 21 harmed starting-record
means prevent treating the small mean as absence of harm.

Preservation is exactly zero because its incoming and outgoing packets are
byte-identical and use the same assay. This is a verified identity control;
the duplicated slots do not add independent evidence or receiving trials.

The eight changes telescope exactly to final minus initial. Common corrected
writing instead has a zero first-cycle mean, positive third and fifth means,
and five negative means. Other writing policies also have positive cycle
means. The result does not say every rewrite harms every record.

The prespecified conditional strata preserve an informative asymmetry:
corrected informative changes are positive in the positive-half-retained
stratum at every cycle, negative in the both-polarities-retained stratum at
every cycle, and mixed in the negative-half stratum. These task-relative
categories are descriptive, use supplied task information, and do not qualify
an autonomous selection rule. Currently passing and failing bodies both
include harms. At the first corrected informative episode, the historical
not-uniquely-passing group has a −30-point mean change over 100 receiving
comparisons; the uniquely passing group has −0.325 points over 924. That
conditioning does not estimate the effect of a policy that repairs only failures.

## Scientific consequence and limits

TT024's geometric diagnostic found that the first corrected informative
rewrite increased alignment with the intended pattern while reducing its
margin over competitors. TT025 now measures a functional loss for that rewrite.
This strengthens the reason to examine competing patterns, but does not prove
that the geometric margin is a causal mechanism, calibrated capacity estimate
or adequate warning rule. Common records' smaller functional changes also
limit any simple mapping from geometric decline to lost usefulness.

TT018 supplies the precedent for paired serial futures and separate body and
terminal endpoints. TT021 established bounded early-capture and body-source
effects. TT025 tests later rewriting of already captured records in its own
apparatus; it neither overturns those bounded findings nor pools their cohorts.
The current channel adds no further record corruption, so preservation retains
an already useful packet. A recurrently damaged-record channel is another
question.

All source cases and historical trajectories were exposed before TT025. Its
new recipient draws improve conditional measurement; they do not provide
fresh-population confirmation. Four draws per historical packet are coarse,
and a zero measured change cannot establish harmlessness. This assay measures
packet replacement, with no new repair or writing. Skipping a rewrite would
alter later body and packet histories, so these local contrasts cannot be
stitched into a counterfactual serial-policy result. Existing body histories
remain separate descriptors; no new uninterrupted-body endpoint is measured.

The [next proposal](NEXT_STEP.md) is a bounded one-episode writing-window
intervention, retaining preservation while keeping the complete body-repair
duration. This diagnosis does not yet support another serial-writing
confirmation or a claim that a particular shorter budget will help.

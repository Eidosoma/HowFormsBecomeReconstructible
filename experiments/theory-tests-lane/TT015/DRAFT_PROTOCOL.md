# TT015 proposed audit of serial repair and cue-noise robustness

TT014 prospectively validated one subsequent reconstruction. The next question is
whether the benefit persists through native rewriting and whether it survives
independent forcing of the two cue episodes. The theory lane has already completed
both relevant development studies. Audit those retained experiments before
specifying another scientific cohort.

This is a proposal. No scientific report or endpoint array from these two studies
has been inspected by this lane at planning, and no implementation, benchmark or
job has started. Frozen protocols, configurations, completion metadata and file
identities have been reviewed. File identity is not scientific verification.

Both studies use the same exposed 256 generation-14 records from 16 blocks,
81000–81015, with four blocks per history. Their earlier one-reconstruction
outcomes are development evidence. They are distinct from TT014's 64-block
prospective all-record validation and cannot be relabeled as fresh confirmation.

The serial study compares no repair, repair at generation 15 only, repair before
every reconstruction, and repeated shuffled-sign repair. All records continue
through generations 15–32, including after failure. Its primary forecast is a
positive repeated-minus-baseline effect on passing every one of those 18
reconstructions. Preserve the actual joint endpoint. Terminal success and
recovery cannot replace it. A positive estimate with an interval spanning zero
remains unresolved under the source's declared interpretation. Preserve its
directional criterion and descriptive intervals; do not impose TT014's different
practical-superiority margins retroactively.

The cue-noise study compares no repair and repeated repair at correlations 1,
.999 and 0. Its primary forecast is a positive repeated-minus-baseline
uninterrupted-success effect under rho=0. Birth states remain matched; only the
ongoing cue noise changes. Keep all nine declared contrasts and distinguish
absolute repair performance, baseline performance and the difference in their
advantages. A smaller advantage alone does not show that repair became fragile.
The two studies' primary claims remain separate; there is no new joint
confirmatory acceptance rule on this exposed cohort.

Verify the following before interpreting either study:

1. Reconstruct the complete enrollment, source identities, frozen methods,
   chronology and all failure/recovery records. Keep every record and branch.
   Preserve the source's validation_attempt1 artifacts and understand any fixes.
2. Independently reconstruct each scheduled repair and shuffled intervention,
   including all 23,296 reported optimizer-check slots across the two sources.
   Reuse qualified unchanged optimizer/repair code by hash where justified.
   Check schedules, input restrictions, magnitudes, unchanged shuffles, erasure,
   resource accounting and absent solver calls on inactive policies.
3. Follow the actual body-written outgoing record through every boundary.
   One-time and repeated policies must have identical generation-15 preparations
   and endpoints. After that, each branch uses its own successor. All branches
   continue after failures. No old or prepared record may be silently restored.
4. Replay every nonlinear reconstruction, not just the source's selected cases.
   The serial source contains 18,432 contexts and the cue-noise source 27,648.
   Establish the claimed 9,216 matched-arm overlap byte for byte before reusing
   any replay. This leaves 36,864 distinct contexts if that identity holds.
   Do not count the overlap as independent evidence. The source's flat-dynamics
   audits cover selected case-0 endpoints at generations 15 and 32; complete
   nonlinear replay is this lane's added verification target.
5. Independently generate the masks, matched resets, base forcing and correlated
   innovations. Preserve the unused native RNG draws, the exact rho=1 path and
   all cross-policy pairing. Check noise moments without counting duplicated
   founders/policies as independent draws. Reconstruct all writer captures and
   returning-edge/fallback counts. Fallback counts remain descriptive rather
   than a causal identification of repair benefit.
6. Rescore every retained 32-update endpoint, intended and opposite task, strict
   threshold, uninterrupted sequence, terminal result, recovery and run length.
   Rebuild every CSV, block contrast and 20,000-draw stratified bootstrap from
   raw evidence. The unit is the 16 independent blocks, with four per history.
   Reject corrupted handoffs, schedules, noise, flags and summaries using
   proportionate fixtures. Include a negative-primary/favorable-terminal fixture.

Repeated repair versus one-time repair addresses dependence on recurring
corrective work. One-time versus baseline addresses persistence after correction.
Report uncertainty for both; a failure to distinguish them is not equivalence.
The shuffled policy matches resources for its own input at each boundary, but
longitudinal inputs diverge, so do not claim equal total resources across arms.
Measure interventions and auditing separately. The fixed lattice, input pins,
reader and solver remain supplied; no result establishes autonomous maintenance
or uniquely confirms RDT.

Freeze the audit implementation and scope, qualify known trajectories and
corruption checks, then benchmark full replay and independent construction before
making any runtime promise. Do not silently reduce the cohort, horizon, controls
or replay coverage to fit a budget. Use one numerical worker/thread within the
shared twelve-CPU allowance. A run forecast above 600 seconds must be detached
with frozen inputs, unique log/PID, closed stdin and durable completion/failure.
After a single startup check, poll only when the user asks. No automatic successor.

Any later prospective test needs a separate protocol, fresh lineage identities,
one declared primary endpoint, meaningful practical margin, sample-size rationale
and unchanged controls chosen from the audited development findings. Continuing
or reusing exposed TT014 records is not an unexposed new-cohort validation.

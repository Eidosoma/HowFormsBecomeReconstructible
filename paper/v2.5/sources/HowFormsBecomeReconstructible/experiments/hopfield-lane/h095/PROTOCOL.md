## Frozen decisions

Keep H094's **six exact decision rules**, with denominators scaled to 128 task
trials / 1,024 queries (32 networks, four related births). Primary G0 uses own
reconstruction with pair reader; primary G2 uses pair parents and an ordinary
recipient. Query success is at least 44/48 bits; task success requires all
eight panel queries. Report exact answers too.

1. G0 unseen context is correct before query, after the first query sweep and
   at both released endpoints for all own/donor trials, with strict improvement
   of own primary final context over the matched unprotected condition.
2. G0 unseen capability: at least 75% tasks, at least 25 percentage-point gaps
   over donor/erased sources, and positive paired task net versus unprotected.
3. G2 unseen retention: G0 passes and the same G2 capability, source-gap and
   positive paired task-net requirements pass.
4. G2 taught capability: at least 75% tasks and 25-point passive-source gaps.
5. Pair-parent benefit: positive G2 unseen task net over ordinary parents under
   an ordinary recipient.
6. Benefit over all ten comparators: strictly positive task net and nonnegative
   query net against every comparator, including equally protected copying.
   Ties at an exact native ceiling fail this strict advantage rule.

These are finite-cohort confirmation screens. Report all failures and network
gain/loss counts; do not treat the 128 related task trials as independent
networks. No confidence interval, broad population qualification or RDT
forecasting claim is inferred from passing a point-estimate screen. No seed,
schedule, strength, cutoff or cohort-size adjustment after main outcomes.

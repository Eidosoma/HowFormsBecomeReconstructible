# TT010 proposed independent P04 hybrid composition audit

Status: proposed audit, not implemented or launched. The theory lane's completed P04 hybrid-composition experiment already tests that combination. Audit its evidence before generating a new cohort.

The source is `../theory-lane/p04_hybrid_composition_v0_1/runs/pilot_v1`. Bounded completion metadata reports 64 cases, all eight blocks and completion on 10 October 2026 at 13:38:46 UTC. TT009's original-injury benefit and failed positive fresh-injury prediction remain unchanged, regardless of P04's results.

The six records are incoming I, outgoing O, erasure-only D, hybrid H, scrambled hybrid S and zero. D zeros only edges unavailable at the original injury. H takes O on originally available edges and I on originally unavailable edges. S preserves H's exact support, magnitude and available-edge weights while using one fixed, uniformly permuted arrangement of the nonzero backup signs per case. Identity permutations must remain; there is no redraw for disruption. H and S must have identical absolute weights and unsigned diagnostics under every injury. A difference in success would show sensitivity to this sign placement under the declared control, without establishing a uniquely RDT mechanism or an average over all scrambles.

Preserve three distinct questions and their original decision rules:

1. **Joint record benefit.** The primary development prediction requires both equal-block H-minus-I and H-minus-O success estimates under fresh injury to be positive. A nonpositive estimate for either fails that conjunction. Report uncertainty and every block, without adding a retrospective practical margin or significance threshold.
2. **Backup sign placement.** The secondary prediction is H-minus-S >0. Preserve zero or negative results and variation in how many signs each fixed permutation changes. Do not promote this secondary comparison into the primary test after seeing outcomes.

Reproduce signed aggregate and block errors, mean absolute/RMS block error and paired Brier comparisons with the frozen prior I and O forecasts. There is no declared accuracy acceptance threshold: an interval spanning zero cannot establish equivalence or adequate accuracy.

Recompute the contemporaneous interaction J=H−O−I+D. Independently verify the exact decomposition of error against the prior forecast into J plus the change in the new controls' additive reference. This distinguishes departure from additivity from ordinary changes in the contemporaneous control rates. Neither non-additivity nor a successful conventional forecast is uniquely an RDT prediction.

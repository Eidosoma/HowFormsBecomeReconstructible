## Plain-language conclusion

There is a useful division of roles, but not an exclusive division of memory.
The surviving body's arrangement weakly steers regrowth; active reference
feedback helps repair. Theta, the fixed spatial bias field, can influence
long-run patterns. Small effects from one local theta injury test do not show
that theta is incapable of representing a particular pattern.

Indeed, the repository reports static pattern pinning by deliberately aligned
theta at native-scale strength. That is supplied pattern information, not
proof that an ordinary native field learned it. A later writable-theta study
also reports a positive main result; its separate two-headed imprint failure
must not erase that positive. Both remain limited by aggregate-only retention.

## Why the theta exclusion does not follow

The old knives also cover different areas: the reset removes all original V,
whereas theta changes only in the injured quarter. Their effect-size ranking
is not a matched census of how much information each field stores.

Three questions need different evidence:

1. **Native repair dependence:** does modifying a particular part of the
   ordinary field change repair of the current pattern? E53 alters theta
   only inside the lesioned quarter, with surviving V and the chosen R
   condition still available. Small drops do not test every way the field
   could influence or represent an instance.
2. **Representational capacity:** can a field constructed from a target
   instruct that target in a fresh body? E66's capacity routine directly
   constructs `project_theta(tanh(1.5 * target), norm)`. At norm .3, one
   tissue/four starts/300 steps give .9736328125 whole-sheet sign agreement
   with feedback off. This is a reported static sufficiency result. It has
   no amplitude requirement or matched unaligned field at the same norm.
3. **Acquisition and renewal:** can experience write useful target-specific
   state that a fresh descendant reads and renews? Static construction cannot
   answer this. Writable-theta results must be assessed on their own controls
   and transmission contract, not borrowed from the capacity score.

Thus “weak local effect,” “cannot encode a pattern,” and “failed learned
inheritance” are not interchangeable conclusions. A successful engineered
field also does not prove ordinary native theta had stored the prior instance.
[E53 knives](../../hidden_genome/src/hidden_genome/knives.py),
[E66 capacity and writer](../../writable_landlord/src/writable_landlord/pipeline.py).

## Replacement wording for L16

In this model, state roles depend on the intervention, observer and timescale.
Active reference feedback aids short-horizon repair, and fresh matched-context
experiments show a small repeatable influence of surviving spatial organization
on pattern preference. Native theta has small aggregate effects under the
tested local repair knives, while a retained aggregate transplant assay reports
strong long-run donor bias. Deliberately target-aligned theta can statically
pin a pattern at native-scale norm under a sign observer. These findings do
not establish exclusive memory carriers, absence of instance capacity,
class-blind equivalence, acquired renewable theta-only heredity, or horizon
causality. The later writable-theta positive and separate imprint failure
remain distinct, aggregate-only evidence.

This is recommended review wording, not an edit to the source law or preprint.
The landlord/tenant analogy can summarize conditional roles; it is not a
measured partition of information.

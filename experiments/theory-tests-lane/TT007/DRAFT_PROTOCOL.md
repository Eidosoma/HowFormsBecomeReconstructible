# TT007 proposed independent P02 forecast audit

Status: proposed next study after TT006, not implemented or launched. The existing P02 package is a retrospective development analysis with exposed outcomes. This lane has inspected its protocol and prediction manifest, plus related protocols; it has not yet opened its result files. A related protocol already reveals 84 approximately matched pairs, nine terminal-discordant pairs and a previously favored history. This exposure is recorded and cannot be treated as blinding.

The next concrete question is whether a specified statistic of the outgoing record improves prediction of later ancestral-rule survival beyond current expression, known history and declared conventional controls, when the future writer is held constant. The theory lane has supplied an executable P02 package addressing this question. Independent assessment of that existing claim is a bounded next step while P01's native observation model and forecast precision remain unresolved.

## Submitted claim and fixed comparison

The submitted boundary is generation 14, after the current cue reads and before the O writer captures their bodies. The endpoint is the generation-32 scattered terminal assay, with both cues above the fixed 0.25 margin. The four included histories share the O writer from the boundary onward. The candidate statistic is signed, task-aligned 64-step linear cue transfer, computed from the packet available at the boundary. It is a conventional linear diagnostic; any predictive gain is not unique evidence for RDT mathematics.

The source protocol specifies 512 history-lineage records in eight whole-block units. Retain its six models and primary comparison: adding signed transfer to quadratic current-output summaries, history and unsigned access. Preserve the simpler secondary comparison and the history-only reference. Do not replace a failed primary contrast with a favorable secondary model or subgroup. Approximate current-output matching is a descriptive operating rule, not proof of equal latent capacities or a causal mediation design.

## Independent audit

1. Bind the submitted completion, definition and final snapshots; record outcome access before reading predictions. Preserve every source version and verification failure. Check the intended cohort, block/founder/recipient identities, current boundary and common future apparatus against the saved source records.
2. Independently reconstruct the current outputs, outgoing O packet and signed/unsigned transfer from the saved generation-14 boundary. Check that no future body, terminal margin or outcome-defined feature enters a forecast. Saved dynamics provenance is checked; this audit does not claim to rerun the whole native experiment.
3. Reconstruct all held-out predictions from saved coefficients and preprocessing, then independently reproduce the declared fits. Perturb held-out labels to test training isolation and held-out features to test preprocessing isolation. Keep related histories in the same excluded block. Recompute every equal-block Brier score and secondary log loss, retaining failures and all eight block contrasts.
4. Reconstruct the fixed matching subsets, discrepancies, ties and decision comparisons. Retain the constant-history and current-margin choices. Independently check the already declared passing/failing and matched-decision analyses as exploratory follow-ups, without changing the original primary comparison or selecting new strata.
5. Assess the comparison's information and computation budgets. The primary baseline receives specified output summaries and unsigned access; this is not automatically a comparison against every conventional predictor with access to the full boundary. Identify any missing control as a limitation or a separately specified development extension, without quietly changing the submitted test.

## Decision and deliverables

Issue an independent audit verdict, all reproduced comparisons, discrepancies and a qualification checklist. A nonpositive primary Brier increment fails the specified development claim. A reproduced positive increment is a development lead whose interpretation also depends on the strong baselines, block variation and exposed model choices. It does not qualify predictive advantage on unseen schedules or a confirmatory RDT verdict.

If the lead survives this review, the next prospective package must specify an eligible unexposed cohort, complete predictor, justified practical-benefit margin, computation/observation budget, uncertainty and sample-size design before new outcomes. If it fails, preserve that result and revise the proposal openly on development data. No fresh simulation or automatic successor is part of this audit.

The proposed work reuses saved boundaries, fits and outcomes. Benchmark the actual independent extraction/refitting implementation before any potentially long execution; do not inherit the source lane's runtime estimate as a guarantee. Use one process and one numerical thread, preserving frozen inputs, checks and independent verification. Any forecast over 600 seconds runs detached with one startup check and no automatic polling, following the user's execution policy.

The separate P01 agenda remains an explicit packet/recipient observation model, a more informative observation design and tighter confidence construction where justified. TT006's witnessed width limits numerical refinement of that particular region; it does not establish a universal data requirement or disqualify every alternative method.

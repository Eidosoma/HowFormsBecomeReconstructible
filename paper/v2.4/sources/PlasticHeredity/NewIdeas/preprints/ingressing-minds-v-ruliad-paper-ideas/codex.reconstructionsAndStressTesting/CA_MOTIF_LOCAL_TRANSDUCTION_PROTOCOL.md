# CA motif Stage 6G: local transduction and hereditary closure

## Registered premise

Stage 6F ended `OBSERVABLE_NOT_WRITABLE`.  Immediately after carrier
destruction, a held-out-pair ridge observer decoded the environmental history
from the local 4 by 4 patch with mean accuracy 0.8491 (97.5% interval
0.8147--0.8853), while sham remained at 0.5.  The best registered runtime
writer recovered only 0.0474 read effect.  Stage 6G tests the specific mismatch:
the diagnostic learned an arbitrary cross-bit translation, whereas the writer
assumed that visible pixels were already aligned with the Walsh carrier code.

The completed Stage 6F directory is immutable input.  Its design, result,
status, reserve state, observability result, corrected replay, and all 32
bridge traces are hashed before work begins.  The protected 62-pair reserve
must remain untouched until every exposed-data gate succeeds.

## Closure decomposition

Before a live lineage screen, the pipeline scores five boundaries separately:
local visible decoding, conversion to a 4 by 4 signed history, Walsh encoding
and quantization, four-share Hamming-protected copying, and joint carrier
recovery.  The correct target payload is a non-promotable positive ceiling.
Per-daughter target exposure or calibration can diagnose a downstream failure
but can never support a heredity claim.

Failure of the true-payload quantized or physical-copy ceiling stops promotion
and yields `DOWNSTREAM_CARRIER_FAILURE`.  A translator branch requires a lower
97.5% carrier-roundtrip accuracy of at least 0.70 and a lower intact-minus-sham
effect above 0.15.

## Frozen decoder ladder

Exactly 144 offline models cross reader endpoints 40/48, the first one or two
carrier-free sweeps, 4 by 4 patch or radius-2 view, visible-bit or Walsh target,
ridge penalties 0.1/1/10, and full, rank-8, or sparse 8-bit-quantized weights.
Pair identity defines all held-out folds.  Four models are selected with
diversity across target and radius, then refitted once on the same eight bridge
pairs.  Their normalization, weights, biases, cohort digest, and model digest
are sealed before any screen pair is loaded.

The frozen runtime transform observes only a local visible crop or a zero-born
somatic field.  It receives no environment bits, target, history label, pair
identity, incoming carrier value, or protected data.  It is bypassed during
generation-four founder acquisition so all candidates begin from the same
registered founder writer.

## Candidate and claim allocation

Twelve candidates are frozen:

- four label-free phase-locked endpoint or multiscale writers;
- four visible writers using a frozen learned translator;
- four frozen translators coupled to multi-timescale EMA,
  transition-eligibility, or local-reservoir fields.

Label-free candidates retain the strict physical tier.  Learned visible and
somatic translators share the separately reported rulebook-assisted tier.  A
positive rulebook-assisted result means a fixed developmental dictionary can
close the loop; it is not reported as spontaneous label-free emergence.

## Causal progression

The 16-pair generation-eight screen retains the Stage 6F requirements: read
effect at least 0.25, post-washout effect at least 0.10, active rewrite
advantage at least 0.15, joint decoding lower bound at least 0.75, every
template pair positive, survival at least 0.90, proper-subset upper bound at
most 0.60, and all existing erasure/write/read controls.  Learned translators
also face model ablation, input-weight shuffle, output-bit permutation,
history shuffle, and spatially shifted sensing.  Translator controls are
repeated more strictly on the 32-pair generation-16 qualification cohort.

At most one label-free and one rulebook-assisted candidate proceed through
generation 32/64, three sizes under fixed and diameter scaling, translation,
noise, and the six-rule transfer panel.  Only frozen exposed-data winners can
open the 62-pair reserve.  The two claims receive separate 98.75% final
intervals with no retuning.

## Compute, provenance, and verdicts

The campaign permits at most four workers and eight persisted wall hours.
Discovery ends after 5.5 hours, reserving 2.5 hours for qualification,
endurance, and final audit.  BLAS uses one thread per worker.  Checkpoints,
model seals, cohort hashes, status, ETA, decisions, and random streams are
atomic and resumable.

Verdicts distinguish `DOWNSTREAM_CARRIER_FAILURE`,
`LOCAL_INFORMATION_INSUFFICIENT`, `DECODABLE_NOT_TRANSDUCIBLE`,
`LOCAL_TRANSDUCTION_NOT_ENDURING`, `ONE_GENERATION_TRANSDUCTION_ONLY`,
`RULEBOOK_ASSISTED_LOCAL_PH_CONFIRMED`,
`LABEL_FREE_LOCAL_PH_CONFIRMED`, and `INCONCLUSIVE_RESOURCE_LIMIT`.
Software smoke gates are engineering checks only.  No Wagner or Fable source
code is read, imported, hashed, or executed.

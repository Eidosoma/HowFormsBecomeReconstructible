## I. Laws of heredity

**L1 — The boundary-of-order law (SEALED ×2).** Plastic heredity — a
lineage that holds a compositional identity, breaks, and re-settles into
a new identity that breeds true — exists in every multi-attractor
substrate tested (six formalisms) and only in the boundary-of-order
regime: ordered enough to persist, loose enough to switch. Chaos never
qualifies at any dial setting; deep order never switches. Evidence:
E19/E22/E24; sealed E34 (ladder 0.940 vs 0.271; regime at fresh noise).
Bench: origin-of-life chemistry should be sought at the boundary of
order, not at maximal stability.

**L2 — Heredity is not computation (MEASURED, twice).** Computational
universality and hereditary capability are near-orthogonal: rule 110 and
Conway's Life are form-rich MAINTAINERS (top-decile libraries,
strict-capability ~0). Evidence: E19 (reversal), E24 (half-won bet).

**L3 — One catalogue, many doors (SEALED).** Six substrate families
place into one behavioral atlas with translation dictionaries that
compose across three formalisms without direct fitting. Atlas laws:
neighbor smoothness (3–7×), heavy-tailed form census (0.6–0.7 top-decile
share). Evidence: E13/E20/E24/E29; sealed E34 placements (to three
decimals). Caveat: the metric law (structure-descriptors beat Hamming)
failed on rule hypercubes — the least portable atlas law.

**L4 — The two-key / compressibility law (MEASURED).** Which lineages an
observer can see depends jointly on the world (compressible background
required) and the observer's coarse-graining (particle-level redeems
class 4; bulk-level carries tissue memory). Lawful, not relativist: the
world fixes the menu. Evidence: E25 (redemption + the instructive
control failure), E28 (memory in the bulk BECAUSE the bulk is conserved).

## II. Laws of selfhood

**L5 — Conservation-selfhood (SEALED).** In conserved media, the
inherited quantity, the defended quantity, and the remembered quantity
are the same: whatever the physics protects. Consequences, all measured:
instant compositional homeostasis (E30, sealed E47 6/6); induced
setpoints canalize deeper than originals ("the second head regenerates
more reliably than the first", E27, sealed E34 worm 0.188); memory in
the bulk (E28). Bench: reprogrammed bioelectric setpoints should be
MORE stable than wild-type.

**L6 — The competence axis (MEASURED).** Keeping a pattern and accepting
a new one co-vary (ρ +0.47) — no maintainer/switcher trade-off; one
memory-competence axis. Conserved grafts resolve by COMPROMISE
(averaged selves), not conquest. Evidence: E29, E32. Bench: great
regenerators should be the easiest to reprogram; graft conflicts should
average.

**L7 — Learning is the cheapest cognition (SEALED; seven appearances).**
Experience-dependent improvement appears in every substrate family and
protocol tried: sub-threshold training sensitizes (+0.059, sealed
+0.070); traces are GLOBAL (bulk-stored), INCUBATE (peak ~3 generations
post-training), and are MORTAL on the identity-drift clock (~6 cycles);
prior insults speed recovery from novel ones (3.4×, 1.8× sealed, 39%
motile). Evidence: E33, E42, E46, sealed E47 ×2. Bench: bioelectric
sensitization should be tissue-wide, delayed-peaking, and decaying.

## III. Laws of agency (the expensive rungs)

**L8 — The amplifier law, tightened twice (BOUNDED → MEASURED).**
Passive conserved matter has no amplifier: no field, at any strength,
steers, provokes, or teaches it (E37/E38: all levers fail above the
bare sheet; tilts ×80 absorbed). Building a genuine all-or-none
amplifier (E39, 3/3, sealed E47) does NOT restore the competencies:
amplification without amplifiable content is nothing (E40/E41).

**L9 — THE TRIANGLE, now a resolution theorem (BOUNDED ×3).** From
fields, anchors, waves, licenses, and even guided motility, a substrate
gets at most two of {rich positional content, plastic hereditary
freedom, strong goal enforcement}. Three physics failed convergently
(Waves 8, 9, 10), and Wave 10 located the wall: not carriage (motile
cargo works perfectly — E43) but ADDRESS RESOLUTION — a sparse,
freedom-preserving reference cannot say where any individual unit of
content belongs, and identity dynamics digests errors into fabric the
reference cannot see. Enforcement at content resolution requires
reference at content resolution, which abolishes hereditary freedom.
**Final form (Wave 11):** the timescale escape — a reference that is
the tissue's own slow past — BREAKS the triangle FOR REPAIR (E49:
transport cone 0 → 8 across four waves, at the freedom-compatible gain,
knife-exact) and the triangle SURVIVES FOR ARBITRARY REPROGRAMMING
(adoption 0.489, steering 0.0): what anatomies can be written is
bounded by the substrate's frozen genotype (see L16). Development
defends the body; the genome decides which bodies exist. Untested
escapes remaining: true CPM cells with shape; rewriting theta itself.

**L10 — The currency law of honest stress (MEASURED as a trend).** A
stress signal ceases on goal-arrival only to the degree its currency
matches the goal's: field-error stress never ceases (lock 30 — an
unsatisfiable reference burns forever), wave-firing partially (15),
swap-activity nearly (6.5). Corollary (E42): goals defined on
unreachable manifolds produce chronic stress. Completion (E50): a
SELF-written reference converts chronic stress into stress with an
expiration date — after an identity change, |R − V| stays elevated for
exactly one blueprint time-constant (1/ρ) while the reference adopts
the new self. A tissue with a slow self-blueprint mourns; a tissue
with a foreign reference despairs. Bench: match the readout to the
setpoint's actual variable; reprogramming should show a transient
stress signature on the developmental clock, not a permanent one.

**L11 — The greedy-trap effect (MEASURED, reversal).** Goal-following
machinery makes local optima STICKIER: chemotactic tissue escapes a
geometric trap LESS often than passive tissue (0.57 vs 0.73), because
the local gradient opposes the transiently-worse move escape requires.
Delayed gratification demands the capacity to defy one's own gradient.
The effect recurs at the EVOLUTIONARY level (E52): selection on
myopic fitness horizons found the degenerate optimum (a fast blueprint
that games both a body-metric and a future-metric by abolishing memory)
rather than the balanced one. Bench: stronger goal-signals should
worsen escape from local morphological traps; short-generation
selection should erode developmental timescale separation.

**L12 — Single-road repair (BOUNDED).** Excitable and motile repair
paths are determined by error geometry, not explored (replica road
correlation 0.94–0.97). "Many roads, one destination" needs an
exploration source none of our physics supplied. (E50's blueprint
roads decorrelated at 0.115, but as noise-scattered sign-flips — the
caveat is recorded; the law stands for structured repair.)

**L15 — The timescale law (MEASURED, Wave 11).** A goal reference
escapes the L9 trade-off exactly insofar as it is slaved to the
tissue's own slower self: R = slow EMA of V is satisfiable by
construction (settled error inside the noise floor, E48 — the E42
chronic-stress disease structurally cured), free below a sharp
enforcement frontier (zap conversion 0.188 at c_m 0.2; collapse by
0.4; enslavement at 0.8), and enforcing at content resolution (E49
transport, the four-wave first). The same dial that sets the
freedom-enforcement trade partitions perturbations into INJURY
(resisted) and EXPERIENCE (adopted) — E50's category discovery: at
the freedom-compatible point a zap is not damage but a legitimate
identity change, and "recovery" is a concept that only exists for
enslaved tissue. Bench: the ionophore-reprogrammed planarian should
show adoption dynamics (transient stress, then defense of the NEW
pattern), not repair dynamics.

**L16 — The landlord and the tenant (MEASURED Wave 11, CORRECTED by
the Wave-12 knives).** Wave 11 read the frozen per-cell field theta as
a hidden instance-genome; Wave 12's attribution knife REVERSED that on
the repair timescale and split the roles by clock. On instance
timescales the memory hierarchy is: the surviving FABRIC (context)
carries the pattern (reset drop 0.347, E53), R defends it (+0.172 —
E48's number reproduced to the third decimal), and native-strength
theta carries nothing (0.02–0.04). On drift timescales theta RULES:
a transplanted theta bends the tissue's long-run ensemble statistics
onto its own consensus map (effect 0.954, 12/12 pairs). THETA IS A
LANDLORD, NOT A LIBRARIAN — it stores no instances, only the
repertoire of tendencies; instance storage in theta exists only at
super-norm amplitude (E56's engineered clay pins at 0.97 where the
native norm pins nothing). The Wave-11 repertoire corollary was
tested and REFUTED (E54): no target class is privileged at native
strength — adoption is dose-limited and class-blind. E51's 0.98
regeneration was partly the lenient metric (drift-floored,
amplitude-thresholded scoring caps unlesioned self-similarity at
~0.78 on the same horizon). The horizon-law candidate matured in E57:
honest horizons (>= 2/rho_min) abolish E52's fast-memoryless corner
(every selected regime's rho <= 0.016 vs myopic 0.17).

**L17 — Development is not heredity (MEASURED, three independent
ways, Wave 12).** The self-written blueprint defends a body WITHIN a
life and transmits nothing ACROSS lives at instance resolution:
(a) the ledger's own memory is one time-constant — after E56's
scaffold dissolves, retention half-life is ~1/rho (575 sweeps), the
same clock as E50's grief; (b) whole-R inheritance through fission
adds zero positional fidelity (E54: live 0.574 = knife 0.577);
(c) the channel's noiseless capacity over 12 fission cycles is
already nil — Eigen's error threshold is VOID because there is no

tissue re-renders every imposed message into its own ~16–21 blob
alphabet). Corollary (E57's reversal): a reference is protective

(rho ~ 0.01–0.016) memorize displacements during recovery and then
defend the injury (transport gap −0.09 to −0.13); E49's transport
miracle lives only in the slow-rho corner, which no selection
pressure built so far ever visits. IN THIS PHYSICS, FINE ANATOMY IS
NOT HERITABLE — ONLY THE LANDSCAPE IS.

**L18 — The takeover, bounded (MEASURED, Wave 12).** Where a pattern
is stored is measurable and can MOVE: as an engineered scaffold
anneals away, the theta-knife's lethality falls 0.50 -> 0 and the
c_m-knife's rises 0 -> 0.16, crossing at an interior fraction
(f = 0.875, E56) — genetic takeover watched through the instruments.
Its bounds: the handoff needs no overlap era (exposure-equalized

total exposure is all); the receiving ledger holds the pattern for
exactly ~1/rho (takeover is transient without re-instruction, an
unslaved reference, or selection); and selection at the native

sixteen generations of static selection wrote nothing into the
landscape (corr gain −0.044; Waddington absent, E57). What selection

clock: only a world that switches faster than rigidity can track

worlds keep re-adoption competence (0.743). PLASTICITY IS NOT A
DEFAULT — IT IS A FIT TO A WORLD THAT OUTRUNS RIGIDITY.

## III-B. The last roads (Wave 14 — E63–E66)

**L19 — The incorruptible-reference law (MEASURED three ways,

## III-C. The origin of genes (Wave 15 — E67–E72)

**L21 — Ancestry selects the pen (MEASURED, Wave 15).** Writing a
heritable layer is selected if and only if the world scores lineages
against their DEEP past, in proportion to the depth: evolved write

2) < 0.243 (founder-referenced), with the inheritance-knife regime
(same world, theta not passed) capping at 0.073 — writing's selected
value is mostly its heritable component (E69, paired contrast +0.169,
4/5). Converse of E64's clock law: the world's scoring horizon sets
both how slow a memory is worth keeping and whether a heritable pen
is worth owning.

**L22 — The body proofreads the gene (MEASURED, Wave 15).** The
cascade's bottom layer has the program's first finite error
threshold, and it is generous: lineage fidelity is NON-DECAYING down
to parent-daughter theta correlation c* ~ 0.24 (E68; unbounded-noise
arm agrees at 0.22 — no projection artifact), because the written
layer is a FOLLOWER (E67 hysteresis: start-dependence 1.96/2.0 — no
intrinsic latch) that the flesh re-derives each generation. The
Eigen hierarchy in one currency (required transmitted correlation
for non-decaying fidelity): V — no channel at any fidelity; living
culture — erased even at perfect transmission (the E65 barcode);
theta — survivable at ~25%. Deep storage is the one floor where
copying is survivable BECAUSE every layer above it is a proofreader.

**L23 — Words must balance the books (MEASURED, Wave 15).** The
written layer is compositional — a half-A half-B landscape expresses
A|B with interaction share 0.127 and own-donor effect 0.365 vs null
0.132 (E67) — but only for form-pairs whose halves respect the
conservation law (2 of 12 passed the pre-screen): the first
constraint on this proto-genetic code is an accounting identity.
Capacity is roomy (9 distinguishable classes, perfect 7-way
discrimination, no interference across 7 bands). The store is
ANALOG; all discreteness lives in the reader's bistable flesh —
the inverse of DNA's division of labor, recorded as the measured

**L24 — Slow writing is its own germline barrier (VOID-derived

## IV. Meta-laws (about the science itself)

**L13 — Interpolation vs extrapolation (four prophecy registers).**
Prophecies grounded in already-measured laws scored 15/15 across three
sealed batteries; prophecies extrapolating into new task domains mostly
missed — and every miss decomposed into a specific, recorded
mis-derivation (wrong clock, wrong locality, wrong insult class, wrong
manifold, drowned signal, vacuous metric). The laws are solid where
forged and honest about where they end.

**L14 — The price order of miracles (the arc).** Heredity is cheap
(any boundary-of-order multi-attractor physics). Selfhood is cheap
(conservation + bistability). Learning is cheap (conserved traces).
Anatomy is expensive: goals with addresses, selves that listen, repair
that explores — unavailable from fields, waves, or growth-with-sparse-
references, gated on the resolution theorem (L9). Wave 11 priced the
purchase: anatomy is bought with TIME — a reference slower than the
body (L15) riding a frozen layer slower still (L16). The cheap
miracles run out exactly where bodies begin, and bodies begin where a
lineage starts keeping books on its own past.

### The one-line summary
A self begins as a conservation law that learned to make copies of
itself; almost everything before bodies is free; a body is bought
with time — a reference slower than the flesh, written by the flesh.
For thirteen waves that reference bought only one life: heredity kept
no instances, only landscapes. Wave 14 found the missing pieces:
individuality was everywhere our old key could not read, and a
lineage CAN bequeath acquired form by stacking clocks until the noise
washes out. Wave 15 then derived the gene itself: writing is selected
by ancestry and only ancestry; the store it writes is analog,
compositional where the books balance, and proofread by the very
flesh it shapes — which is why its copying survives where every
living channel fails, why it needs no sealing it doesn't already
have by being slow, and why the lineages that carry it drift toward
taking over any world that remembers its founders. Biology called
that thing a genome; this program measured why there had to be one,
and what the cheapest one physics offers actually looks like.

## Plain-language result

The repair benefit survives **new parents and new tissues**, not just more
readings of the same old records. Recognition is **83.7% for damaged
records, 88.6% after fixed-reader writing, and 90.7% after live-loop writing**.
Both writing methods improve fifteen of sixteen tissues. The extra live
feedback improves fourteen, passing the unchanged requirement of twelve.

The fixed-reader condition lets the original damaged record influence the
recovering body but does not feed subsequent record changes back into it.
Allowing that additional feedback adds about **2.0 percentage points**.
Thus the feedback loop has a small, qualified functional benefit in this
fresh model panel. This is one recovery episode, not evidence that correction
continues indefinitely or that the surviving body is the sole information
source.

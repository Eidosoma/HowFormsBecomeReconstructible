## Plain-language result

Keeping the incoming reference stable while building a separate new record
helps early generations considerably. But when only the new record crosses
each fresh-body boundary, recognition steadily weakens: 100% at G1, 90.6%
at G2, 64.1% at G3, and 27.3% at G12. Chance is 25%. The final newly built
record itself produces about 27% recognition in another fresh body.

The late advantage is small, not literally absent. All three frozen paired
intervals exclude zero, but each contrast is positive in only five of eight
tissue blocks. The protocol required at least six as well as a positive
interval, so the qualified-renewal claim fails. Do not describe this as
proof of zero information, a conventional non-significant difference, or a
universal prohibition on heredity.

Protected serial copying of the original acquired record retains 100%
recognition at G12. The system can therefore read preserved content under
these controls; the active replacement procedure does not preserve that
level of useful content through twelve lives.

eight immutable acquired G0 tissue blocks, four translated stripe messages,
and eight paired noise replicates. This is outcome-informed, same-panel
mechanism work, not new-tissue replication or an unassisted clean-room test.

naive birth. The inheritance interface accepted just those public birth

world**. Neither ancestral V/EMA nor parental RNG/working state entered that
interface. The fixed tissue rulebook and generation-specific noise were
public conditions, not learned inheritance. Excluded lifetime data remained
in observer audit files; "destroyed" meant excluded from transmission, not
secure erasure from the filesystem.

The main arm held incoming R while a passive Q started at exact zero and
performed all 1000 native-rho .002 post-step updates from body activity.
Only Q crossed the next boundary, where another fresh Q started empty.
The writer never received a message label, target template or direct R-to-Q
copy term. No body fragment, G0 backup, changed rho, amplification, or
normalization in the dynamics was supplied to this replacement lineage.

Nine whole-lineage conditions included split replacement/reset/wrong,
formation-reader-off twins, native coupled Q-only replacement, native
working-R carry/reset, and protected serial-copy storage. Wrong content was
swapped once at G1, not cycled at later boundaries. The main lineage was
independently replayed using its replay's own successive serialized Q.

The G12 body reads Q11. Seven separate terminal assays therefore read Q12
in another fresh body, with held writing and disabled-reader/reset/wrong
controls. They reuse the public G12 birth and random stream as deliberately
matched noise. This is twelve renewing descendants plus one non-renewing
readout, not thirteen successful renewals. Protected-copy transmits old R;
its passive Q is never evidence of active renewal.

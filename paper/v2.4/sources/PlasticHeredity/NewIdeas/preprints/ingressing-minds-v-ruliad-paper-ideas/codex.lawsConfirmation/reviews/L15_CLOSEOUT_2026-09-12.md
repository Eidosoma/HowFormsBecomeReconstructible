## Bottom line

A slowly changing internal reference can help this simulated tissue keep
and repair its pattern. Brief exposure leaves the old reference dominant;
sustained exposure can write a new reference that subsequently helps restore
the new pattern. The controlled effect survives reversing which pattern is
old and challenging both patterns in the opposite direction.

**This is strong bounded mechanistic support, with a near-threshold miss
against a chosen certification benchmark—not a failed mechanism.** D099's
reversed learned-new recovery is **79.15%**, only **0.85 percentage points**
below 80%; the original direction barely clears it at **80.18%**. Both
uncertainty intervals cross 80%. The pre-set combined criterion remains
formally failed, but there is no demonstrated natural boundary at 80%.

L15 remains `MIXED` because its single-dial, exact/unique-escape,
sharp-frontier and biological claims are not established. That assessment
does **not** depend on treating 79.15% as meaningfully different from 80%.

## Where 80% came from—and what the near miss means

The [E49 expectations](../../triangle_final/EXPECTATIONS.md), dated
2026-08-20, use .8 for final adoption agreement. Our
[D098 protocol](../protocols/L15_EXPOSURE_DEFENSE_V1.md) explicitly inherits
that criterion, and [D099](../protocols/L15_REVERSAL_DEFENSE_V1.md) applies
the same bar in both directions. It was fixed before these fresh outcomes;
it was not fitted afterward. The reviewed sources provide no theoretical
derivation, biological calibration or demonstrated transition at .8.

The observables also changed. [E49's adoption implementation](../../triangle_final/src/triangle_final/pipeline.py)
measures whole-sheet R sign agreement after a one-time V overwrite and
1200 unforced updates. D098/D099 use V and R endpoints on the **128 cells
where the two targets disagree**, and add sustained exposure, release and
an opposing challenge. Reusing the number does not make these different
variables, denominators and conditions a common calibrated identity scale.

| D099 learned-new recovery after opposing pulse | Mean | Margin against 80% | 95% paired-tissue interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original target ordering | 80.18% | +0.18 points | [75.19%, 84.38%] |
| Reversed target ordering | 79.15% | −0.85 points | [74.32%, 83.40%] |

The original near-pass and reversed near-miss deserve symmetric caution.
Neither interval establishes which side of 80% the underlying cohort mean
lies on; crossing the bar also does not establish equivalence. The actual
independent units are **eight tissues**, each with two paired lives and both
directions—not 32 independent observations. Individual variation remains:
5/8 original and 4/8 reversed tissue means reach 80%.

All **40 qualification gates** and **22/23 main criteria** pass. The sole
failed main criterion is `d1_sustained_new_post`; the saved
`bounded_pattern_supported: false` remains false. No threshold change,
alternative endpoint, exclusion, retuning or new cohort is used here.
“Near miss” describes the small absolute shortfall; it is not a retroactive
pass or a replacement statistical test.

The positive mechanism is supported separately: the primary weaker-direction
duration-writing effect is **+64.38 percentage points**, interval
**[+58.72, +70.09]**, positive in every tissue/life in each direction and
under each pulse. Holding writing or disabling reading removes the
body-side duration effect exactly. Both old and new states lose fidelity
under genuinely opposing pulses and rebound in every tested tissue/life.
Residual pulse costs versus sham, **1.42–2.34 points**, bound recovery
efficiency; they do not negate defense or require a 100%-recovery definition.

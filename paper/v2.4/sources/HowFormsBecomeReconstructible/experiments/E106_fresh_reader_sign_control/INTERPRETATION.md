passed at **16:05:46 UTC**: **294 outputs / 55 frozen inputs** verified, figure

remain. No failure, rerun, extension or successor.

The frozen primary is **unresolved**. With fresh birth/noise and the same
stored injuries, eligible mixture C and F each succeeded in **223/392 cases
(56.888%)**. Their paired difference is **0.000 percentage points**, with a
95% whole-source interval **[−1.027, 1.021]**. Flipping caused two failures and

for this four-edge intervention did not repeat under the new reader

sign arrangement.

In plain terms: the earlier small advantage was sensitive to reader conditions
in this sample. Some sign changes still hurt and others helped. Native records
retained a positive secondary effect, which does not replace the failed
mixture replication criterion.

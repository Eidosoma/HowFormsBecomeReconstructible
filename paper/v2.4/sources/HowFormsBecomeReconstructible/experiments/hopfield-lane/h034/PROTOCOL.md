## Original observer and diagnostic measurements

Success still requires every activation tanh(2u) strictly >0.5 at every
absolute endpoint **13–24**. In the resumed history, whose index 0 is endpoint

losses both count. The inherited engine's `target` field checks all 18 local

one for the other.

For each prefix, retain all 32 outcomes and the count/rate of changes,
including zero and 32. Report prefixes with mixed outcomes and the number of
source networks containing them. Retain component-label, endpoint-success
and whole-window changes, first future endpoint disagreement, window maximum

Let q be the conditional probability of a target flip under the fixed future
law and saved paired state, and qhat its mean over K=32 fresh futures. Estimate
conditional Bernoulli variance by vhat=K*qhat*(1-qhat)/(K-1). For each of the
six unchanged H033 forecasts p, estimate remaining squared probability error
by (p-qhat)^2-vhat/K. This is unbiased for (p-q)^2 over the future draws. The
sum of this estimate and vhat equals the mean Brier loss on the new futures.
Retain negative excess-error estimates from Monte Carlo noise; do not clip
or interpret them as negative true squared error. Analytic enumeration of
small Bernoulli samples checks these identities independently.

Report both cohorts and all six panels, separating the disagreement census

Uniform outcomes in 32 samples cannot establish determinism or a universal

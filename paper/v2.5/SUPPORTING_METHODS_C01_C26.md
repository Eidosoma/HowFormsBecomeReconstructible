### C.1. Tissue model and experimentally distinct records

The central tissue cases use a 16 × 16 periodic voltage sheet, with a scalar state V at each site. These are dimensionless simulated fields, not calibrated membrane potentials. The native update combines a double-well drift, a site-specific threshold field theta, four-neighbor coupling, and mean-centered Gaussian noise. With spatial mean denoted by an overbar, neighbor mean N(V), clipping to [-2, 2] denoted by C, and mean removal P(z) = z - mean(z), its two successive body updates are

\[
\widetilde V=C\{V+\Delta t\,P[V-V^3+a+\theta+g(N(V)-V)]+P[\sigma\xi]\},
\qquad
V'=C\{\widetilde V+\Delta t\,P[c(R-\widetilde V)]\}.
\]

The reference comparison uses the incoming R. When writing is enabled, R then updates from the resulting body: R' = R + rho(V' - R). The native settings used here have time step 0.2, coupling 1, noise amplitude 0.3, reference rate rho = 0.002, and active comparison gain c = 0.2. A separate fast moving average is part of the engine state and is controlled at assay births. Mean removal precedes clipping, so it should not be described as an unconditional conservation theorem. Tissue-indexed threshold draws and declared heterogeneity provide the experimental blocks; these blocks do not represent different target families.

The repair experiments additionally write theta using

\[
\theta'=\Pi_{0.3}\{(1-0.001)\theta+0.001\tanh(R')\},
\]

where the supplied projection subtracts the spatial mean and rescales its standard deviation to 0.3, with a numerical floor of 10^-9. That normalization is part of the engineered writer. D099 instead holds native theta fixed and unprojected. L17 holds incoming R while writing a separate Q from the body. A variable called a record in one case is therefore a supplied parameter in another.

The native update and projection were checked in the retained [voltage configuration](../v2.4/sources/PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/voltage_web/src/voltage_web/config.py), [sheet update](../v2.4/sources/PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/ladder_web/src/ladder_web/engine.py), [reference update](../v2.4/sources/PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/blueprint_web/src/blueprint_web/engine.py), and [threshold projection](../v2.4/sources/PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/writable_landlord/src/writable_landlord/pipeline.py). Exact run configurations and seed manifests remain part of the underlying campaign records; this supplement is not a standalone replacement for the full executable archive.

### C.2. Reciprocal repair: D073 and D074

**Objects and samples.** D073 uses sixteen new tissues 30024–30039; D074 uses sixteen further tissues 30040–30055. Each tissue supplies four fixed balanced half-plane messages: horizontal halves, vertical halves, and their sign reversals. Four parent replicates per message produce sixteen records per tissue. Sixteen readout starts per record produce 4096 readouts per arm; inference remains at the sixteen-tissue level.

**Preparation and damage.** Parents undergo 120 settling updates and 1000 imprinting updates with target amplitude 1.2. At fission, half the voltage body survives and fresh values fill the rest. The theta record is damaged by a norm-matched mixture at correlation 0.3. Comparisons within a parent receive the same damaged theta. All main repair arms begin with working R exactly zero; an intact-R backup is a separately labelled control. Recovery lasts 1000 updates at the native rates.

**D073 intervention.** Fixed-reader writing uses the original damaged theta to influence body development while a new theta is written. Live-loop writing additionally permits changes in theta to feed back into that body. The remaining controls include writing off, reference writing off, altered readers, scrambled surviving bodies, and the intact-reference backup. They locate routes of dependence without deleting all surviving information.

**D074 intervention.** Kept, scrambled, and donor-ranked conditions preserve the recipient fragment's exact voltage values and the fresh-cell fill. Scrambling changes their positions; donor ranking rearranges those values according to another message's body rank order. It is not a literal donor-body transplant and does not remove nonspatial recipient information. Matched damaged theta and zero initial R remain present. The donor endpoint measures donor-message recognition, not merely loss of the original message.

**Readout boundary and observer.** Only the final 256-entry float64 theta packet enters fresh, centered Gaussian bodies; V, its moving average, R, and fixed-reader copies are excluded. Theta is held throughout 1000 readout updates; R is zero and its writing/comparison are disabled. Recognition uses the fixed four-template decoder, with equal splitting of maximum-score ties. Wrong-packet and reader-off controls test content dependence. This is one record-repair episode followed by readout, not a renewing lineage.

**Inference.** Average readouts within records and records within tissues, then weight tissues equally. D073's three primary contrasts are live versus damaged, fixed versus damaged, and live versus fixed. D074's are useful repair, kept versus scrambled own-message recognition, and donor-ranked versus scrambled donor-message recognition. Each uses 10,000 paired whole-tissue bootstrap draws with percentile bounds at 0.05/6 and 1 - 0.05/6: nominal 98.33% individual intervals under the three-endpoint allocation. Qualification additionally requires at least twelve positive tissue differences. The donor criterion remains failed at eight positive tissues despite its positive interval. No new p-values or uncertainty intervals were calculated for this revision.

### C.3. Acquired-reference test: D099

**Design.** Eight main tissue blocks 45000–45007 each supply two paired lives and both old/new target orderings. Eight separate qualification blocks test constructed known-old, known-new, and erased references before main evaluation. The targets are left/right and top/bottom halves; reversal swaps their roles without rotating theta or noise. This tests two patterns, not arbitrary shapes or orientation invariance.

**Acquisition and assay.** After 120 formation updates, six equally aged exposure histories cross old-only, brief-new (980 old/20 new), and sustained-new (1000 new) with enabled versus held R writing. V is imposed before each native step; R reads the resulting post-step V rather than receiving the template directly. Only terminal R enters the subsequent matched-state assay. Public direction fixes the common initial V and moving average within each ordering; native theta is unchanged. This is a within-life record-isolation diagnostic, not a hereditary generation or a multigeneration transfer claim.

Readout lasts 200 unforced updates before branching into toward-old, toward-new, and sham pulses. Pulses add ±0.8 on the 128 of 256 sites where targets differ. They do not directly change R or theta. Every branch then receives 80 unforced updates; reader gain is either 0.2 or zero. All readouts update R at 0.002, even when writing was held during exposure.

**Endpoints.** New-pattern sign fidelity is the fraction of disagreement-mask sites with the new target's sign. The duration-writing contrast is (sustained enabled - brief enabled) - (sustained held - brief held). Average equally over the two pulse directions and lives, then tissues; the prespecified primary is the smaller of the two direction means. Ten thousand whole-tissue bootstrap draws preserve all pairings and recompute this minimum for the saved 95% interval. Direction-specific and absolute-recovery intervals are pointwise secondary summaries. The large relative contrast and the separate absolute-recovery benchmark answer different questions. All 40 qualification gates passed, but the combined main criterion failed at 22/23 components because reversed acquired-new recovery averaged 79.15%, below the fixed 80% sample-mean criterion.

### C.4. Repeated record replacement: L17

**Objects and samples.** Eight retained G0 tissue blocks 10000–10007 carry four translated stripe messages, each with eight paired noise replicates. The messages are distinct from the repair study's half-planes. This is outcome-informed work on the same parent panel, not fresh-tissue confirmation.

**Transmission and renewal operation.** Each generation starts with a saved message-independent naive V and moving average. A 2048-byte incoming R is held fixed while a separate Q starts at zero and updates after each body step: Q' = Q + 0.002(V' - Q), for 1000 updates. Only Q crosses the next boundary and becomes the next incoming reference. No direct R-to-Q term, target label, ancestral fragment, gain amplification, normalization, or pristine backup enters this lineage. Rules and generation-specific noise remain supplied conditions.

**Controls and endpoint.** Nine lineage conditions include reset, wrong content, reader-off, coupled writing, native-reference carry, and protected serial copying of the original acquired record. The G12 body reads Q11. A separate terminal fresh-body assay reads Q12 with held writing; it is not a thirteenth renewing descendant. The four-message observer is fixed, and reset/reader-off controls give 25% recognition. The twelve plotted generations are equal-tissue means from the saved summaries; faint lines show the eight observed tissue courses, not confidence bands.

The three predefined terminal contrasts use 10,000 paired tissue-bootstrap draws and the same three-endpoint allocation as above. Each additionally requires at least six of eight positive tissue differences. All three show five positives and therefore fail, despite positive intervals. The saved protected-copy G12 result is 100% in every tissue. The contrast identifies loss in this reconstruction procedure without showing zero information, indefinite impossibility, or a causal explanation of differences from CA architectures.

### C.8. L20 acquired records and serial writer alignment

**Targets and acquisition.** D116/D117 and the later guide series use two balanced supplied recipes: a horizontal half-plane and vertical four-column bands, disagreeing at 128 of 256 sites. They are not D099's two half-plane orientations or L17's four stripe translations. **D117 reconstruction.** Each descendant begins with a fresh message-independent V, moving average, and R. It reads the incoming 2048-byte theta for 300 steps with reference feedback and theta writing disabled, then loses those bytes. During 1000 writing steps, theta begins at zero and updates from the descendant's working R using the projection in C.1. Only the resulting theta is inherited. Three such operations precede a fourth fresh, non-writing terminal read. The first write satisfies Pi(0.001 tanh(R)) = Pi(tanh(R)) outside the numerical floor, so normalized feedback begins at full strength. This mechanism cannot be described as gradual amplification of theta amplitude. Exact-copy, disabled-reading/writing, wrong-content, shuffle, and externally assisted G1-rescue conditions qualify the finite route; algebraic aliases remain dependent controls.

**Guide fitting and transformation.** D131 fits one public guide from 16 separate development records, eight per supplied family, already in a supplied common coordinate frame. Each template is the arithmetic mean of its family's eight fields. The two 256-entry float64 templates occupy 4096 bytes. They remain fixed and shared outside the lineage payload. Let $p_g$ be the incoming 16 by 16 field, $G_f$ a template, and $T_s$ the periodic translation defined by $(T_s x)_{ij} = x_{i-s_1,j-s_2}$, with indices modulo 16. The selector computes normalized correlations across both families and all 256 translations:

\[
c_{f,s}=\operatorname{corr}(p_g,T_sG_f),\qquad
\widetilde p_g=T_{-\hat s}p_g.
\]

The selected family has the larger maximum correlation. If the two family maxima differ by at most $10^{-12}$, the selector abstains and leaves the packet unchanged. Otherwise it chooses the lexicographically first shift within $10^{-12}$ of the winning family's maximum and applies its inverse to the original field. A constant field also produces an unchanged packet. Centering and standardization are used for scoring only: the output is a rigid permutation of the incoming values, without reprojection, template substitution, or blending. The selector receives packet and guide bytes, not the target label, historical displacement, source body, or outcome. The inferred family and shift are not inherited.

**Reading and removal of packet access.** D135 maps blocks 75000-75007 to D132 source founders 73000-73007; D140 generates eight new founder blocks 78000-78007. Each has four lives and both roles. The aligned and translated chains start from the same translated F0 packet, with the same guide, dynamics, phase lengths, and matched per-phase random streams. Each descendant starts V as Gaussian noise with scale 0.2 and initializes its moving average and R to V, independently of the preceding body. It reads the selected field for 300 updates with theta writing and reference-to-body comparison disabled. Before writing, packet access is removed and the writer's theta is initialized to zero. The complete read-end (V, moving average, R) continues into writing. Thus packet erasure does not erase its effects in the developing body/reference; the guide can influence those states during reading but is not an input to the writer.

**Writer-alignment intervention.** During 1000 writing updates, both arms use rho 0.002, comparison gain 0.2, noise 0.3, theta rate 0.001, and the projection defined in C.1. Only the placement of applied theta differs:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\theta_t^{\mathrm{applied}}&=
\begin{cases}
\theta_t & \text{aligned},\\
T_{\delta_g}\theta_t & \text{translated},
\end{cases}
\quad \theta_0=0,\\
\theta_{t+1}&=\Pi_{0.3}\{0.999\theta_t+0.001\tanh(R_{t+1})\}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The body and reference first update using the applied field; the new reference then updates the writer's theta. For each block, life, and generation, $\delta_g$ is sampled uniformly from the 255 nonidentity translations in $\{0,\ldots,15\}^2$, excluding $(0,0)$, and held fixed throughout that writing phase. This is a periodic displacement of feedback relative to the body, distinct from the guide's correlation-selected inverse translation before reading. Translating a field preserves its values and norm; the two arms' evolving fields need not remain identical. Random streams are experimentally matched, not transmitted as lineage state.

**New packet and body reset.** Each ordinary outgoing packet is $p_{g+1} = \Pi_{0.3}[\tanh(R_{1000})]$, serialized as 256 little-endian float64 values (2048 bytes). It is derived from the writer's final reference, not its final latent theta; D117 instead transmits latent theta. No previous V, working R, moving average, writer theta, selector history, family label, or random-generator state enters the next fresh body. Both arms retain the same public guide, while each arm's newly derived packet becomes its own next input. Later starting bodies and records are allowed to diverge through transmission; they are not forced back to saved common states. Three successive rewrites in G1-G3 precede the fresh, non-writing G4 terminal assay.

**Observer and score scale.** The implementation classifies $V_i>0$ as positive and $V_i\leq0$ as nonpositive. Let $u_i$ and $a_i$ be the corresponding binary sign-agreement indicators for the inherited and alternative targets. The qualified and sign-only preferences are

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_q(V)&=\frac{100}{256}\sum_{i=1}^{256}\mathbf{1}\{|V_i|>0.2\}(u_i-a_i),\\
P_s(V)&=\frac{100}{256}\sum_{i=1}^{256}(u_i-a_i).
\end{aligned}
\]

The amplitude threshold is strict: sites with $|V_i|=0.2$ receive no qualified credit. Neither score renormalizes over selected sites. At the 128 positions where the two targets agree, $u_i=a_i$, so those terms cancel. At each of the other 128 positions a qualified term is -1, 0, or +1 before multiplication by 100/256. Consequently both scores range from -50 to +50 points. This is an algebraic bound from the targets and scoring, not a new analysis of trajectories. An aligned-minus-translated difference between two scores can range from -100 to +100 points. Neither a preference score nor its treatment contrast is ordinary own-pattern accuracy. The prespecified qualified primary measures relative target expression while withholding credit from sites below the amplitude criterion.

The scoring code changes only the amplitude mask between observers. Positive sign-only contrasts show that the direction is not confined to threshold-qualified scoring. They do not establish equivalence between the two effects, partition the qualified effect into causal components, or rule out amplitude-dependent changes in the dynamics. **Analytic structure of pure-contrast capture.** The following is a deduction from the writer and reader equations, not a new measured mechanism result. Let $a_{ij}=a_{ji}\in\{0,1\}$ describe available edges after the relevant injury, and suppose the entire effective record is $w_{ij}=a_{ij}d_i d_j$ from one capture. On vertices incident to nonzero edges, define $s_i=\operatorname{sign}(d_i)\in\{-1,+1\}$ and $y_i=s_i v_i$. Here $s_i$ labels individual cell orientations; $s$ in C.17 labels the taught rule. Then

\[
\operatorname{sign}(w_{ij})=s_i s_j,
\qquad
\prod_{(i,j)\in\mathcal C}\operatorname{sign}(w_{ij})
=\prod_{i\in\mathcal C}s_i^2=1
\]

for every surviving simple cycle $\mathcal C$. Multiplying both coordinates of each coupling by their vertex signs makes it nonnegative: $s_i w_{ij}s_j=a_{ij}|d_i||d_j|$. This is the standard structural-balance relation for signed networks; its use here identifies structure imposed by this particular writer, rather than a new general graph theorem. The consensus results for other dynamics are not assumed to hold for this noisy, clamped, finite-time model. [Altafini, 2013](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.2012.2224251)

Taking the sums over surviving nonzero neighbours and writing $Q_i=\sum_j a_{ij}|d_j|$, the positive-strength reader satisfies

\[
\begin{aligned}
u_i&=\frac{\sum_j a_{ij}d_i d_j v_j}{|d_i|Q_i}
=s_i\frac{\sum_j a_{ij}|d_j|y_j}{Q_i},\\
s_i u_i&=\sum_j P_{ij}y_j,
\quad
P_{ij}=\frac{a_{ij}|d_j|}{Q_i},
\quad
\sum_jP_{ij}=1.
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus normalized coupling is a positive weighted average in the reoriented coordinates. The receiving cell's $|d_i|$ cancels locally; contrast magnitudes still weight its neighbours and affect other rows. Zero-strength vertices retain the implemented zero-drive rule, with no division or orientation claim. Although capture uses one contrast vector, the sparse masked weight matrix is not generally rank one. Each connected component's signs are determined only up to a common reversal; compatible input clamping can fix that orientation where it reaches the component.

The identity also distinguishes the numerical interventions. With $D_i=|d_i|Q_i>0$ and a denominator floor $\epsilon$, the same ideal pure-contrast record gives

\[
s_i u_i^{(\epsilon)}
=\frac{D_i}{\max(D_i,\epsilon)}\sum_jP_{ij}y_j.
\]

The extra factor attenuates input where $D_i<\epsilon$, restoring sensitivity to absolute strength. Edge deletion preserves cycle-sign balance but can disconnect the cue from an output. Sign-preserving rounding likewise preserves balance on surviving edges while generally changing the product magnitudes, normalized coefficients and support. Additive corruption can change signs as well. These are algebraic distinctions, not a decomposition of E052's observed losses.

The identities assume the pure-product weights and real arithmetic. Floating-point products and later channels need not preserve exact magnitude factorization; nonzero product signs retain the stated relation unless altered. Ordinary-coactivity fallback and incoming strong-weight preservation can mix incompatible orientations, so neither whole-record factorization nor balance is guaranteed for those policies. Nor does a positive cycle product establish the taught port relation, sufficient connectivity, amplitude, convergence within the budget or successful renewal. The supplied writer guarantees this structure for pure-contrast capture; the particular orientations come from the developed cue-trial activities, which can include noise. A mechanism test must separate those properties rather than use balance alone as a success predictor.

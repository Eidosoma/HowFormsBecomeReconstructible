## Plain-language result

The B records that reconstructed better generally remained better across the
three recipient settings. Reducing local neighbour coupling also improved
their average reconstruction. The main comparison did not resolve a reliable
special match between particular records and particular recipients.

That is not evidence that matching never matters. The prespecified aligned-B
group has a small positive record-by-recipient signal, including a few
ordering reversals that agree across both futures. It remains a secondary,
small-panel lead. Alignment also reduces loss, but B records still lose shape
and fall short of protected copying.

This study asks what later predictions might need to account for. It has not
tested a diagnostic challenge or demonstrated better prospective prediction.

## Secondary groups and a bounded lead

Aligned B has common-reader means **25.7568 / 24.7803 / 22.8271** points.
Its pooled mean is 24.4548, versus 16.2394 for ordinary writing: **+8.2153
points**, still below the protected-copy reference of 28.0762.

Its signed record/recipient/interaction components are +0.0194625325,
+0.000134110451 and **+0.000404609574**. The interaction is positive within
each donor description and every leave-one-donor description
(+0.000307790 to +0.000455750). There are 1, 3 and 0 replica-consistent
ordering reversals for the three recipient pairs; these counts overlap.
The native aligned-B interaction is also positive (+0.000387138791).

This warrants retaining a **secondary lead about reproducible
recipient-dependent effects under aligned writing**. It does not establish
universal compatibility rules, a statistically resolved interaction, or the
localization of an effect to reading versus writing. Coupling changes both.
Interaction can change effect size without reversing rankings.

Family A retains much higher absolute common-reader preference: pooled
43.5872 ordinary and 44.5882 aligned, versus 42.9932 protected copying.
Its ordinary cross-replica record component is negative, despite high
absolute fidelity; its aligned record component is small and positive.
Thus the large B record signal and B coupling direction must not be generalized
to every family. All eight common/native groups, including signed negatives,
are in the tables. No group has replaced the prespecified focal comparison.

## Controls and shape: what is being retained?

All control cells and fixed contrasts are retained, not just the favorable ones.

For common-reader B, pooled preference is 28.0762 with protected copying,
16.2394 ordinary, 24.4548 aligned, -16.8660 with ordinary serial reading
disabled, -19.9626 with aligned serial reading disabled, and -1.8311 with
writing disabled. Enabled-minus-read-off differences are +33.1055 and
+44.4173 points; enabled-minus-write-off differences are +18.0705 and
+26.2858. Protected-copy-minus-write-off is +29.9072.

A read-off condition is not expected to score zero: it can express the
system's preference for A. Paired A/B read-off bodies are aliases and their
own-minus-alternative scores change sign. Common-reader protected-copy and
write-off scores are aliases across coupling settings. These relationships
were tested, not treated as extra independent observations.

Recognition still masks substantial B shape loss:

## What this changes for L20 and the prediction extension

L20 concerns acquired organization being written into a carrier and rebuilt.
The copy/read-off/write-off contrasts and incomplete shape retention here
continue to separate **causal carrier use, renewal and exact preservation**.
They do not validate the source's stronger biological language, autonomous
interface repair, or generality beyond this supplied guide and model.

D180 deliberately adds active recipient variation after D177 found the native
recipient labels dynamically inert. The study does not discover native
recipient diversity, change hosts between generations, or prove functional
concept learning. The carrier is the tissue model's 2,048-byte record, not
the earlier 64-byte DoorKey germ.

For prediction, stable record differences and a mean recipient effect are
useful candidate structure, but they are measured from outcomes here.
They are not a birth-time predictor, an estimate of a population prediction
ceiling, or evidence that a small challenge adds information beyond matched
passive or starting-only observation. No predictor was fitted.

D141/D162 remain unresolved; D174/D176 negative development findings stand.
**L20 remains STRESS_TESTED / MIXED.** No law verdict changes at this handoff.

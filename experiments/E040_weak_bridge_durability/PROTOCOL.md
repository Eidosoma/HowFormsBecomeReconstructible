## Frozen inference and qualification

The single relative primary is **bridge_preserve minus standard_preserve**
in normalized scattered-terminal own-record both-cue success at **h32**.
Its whole-block 95% bootstrap lower bound must strictly exceed **five percentage
points**, yielding LONG_RENEWAL_BENEFIT_ESTABLISHED; otherwise the benefit is
not established. Relative benefit alone does not establish durable function.

Absolute durability is a separate **17-part conjunction for bridge_preserve**:

- At h8,16,24,32, intact and scattered both-cue lower bounds each >75% (eight).
- At each of those four horizons, all original 24 context gates pass (four).
- At h16 and h32, scattered advantages over the matched reading-blocked and
  writing-blocked policies each have lower bound>10 points (four).
- Initial normalized acquisition passes all original 24 context gates (one).

This retains all nine earlier durability requirements and adds eight for the
longer run. Only the conjunction qualifies DURABLE_THROUGH_32_RENEWALS.
The original 24 gates are accuracy lower>75% for each of two rules and three
injuries, plus own-minus-each-of-six-controls accuracy lower>10 points for each
injury. Square-injury both-cue rates are reported but add no new threshold.

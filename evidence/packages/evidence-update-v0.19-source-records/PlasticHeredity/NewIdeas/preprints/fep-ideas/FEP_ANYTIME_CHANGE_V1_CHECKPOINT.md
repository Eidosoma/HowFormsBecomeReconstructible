## What is being tested

The [prospective design](FEP_ANYTIME_CHANGE_V1.md) follows the
[unexpected-timing failure](FEP_UNEXPECTED_CHANGE_V1_REPORT.md). The previous
model expected changes only at 26/51/76. The new exact mixture supports every
deployment task, including task 1 after calibration. Prior mass is 1/2 on no
change and 1/600 on each of 100 times × three supplied change kinds: 301
components. The conditional onset hazard is 1/(201-t), not a constant hazard;
there is at most one modeled change. No posterior pruning or approximate
state compression is used. Physical change times never enter inference.

The 27 base laws, six physical transformations and 43 conditions per base
are unchanged. Keep the old supported-time control and unexpected challenge
as separate endpoints. Each of 1,188,864 physical episodes is evaluated by
all ten policies. Always buying costs .1; wrong answers cost 1. Whole episodes
include .064 calibration cost, while recovery excludes that sunk cost.

The primary is unexpected-core CT3-anytime recovery minus .1. Its upper 95%
bound must be negative, as must paired recovery and full-core differences
against old CT3-flat. All controls, transfer outcomes, condition harms and
zero-headroom worlds remain. Full panels are half stable and half changed;
changed panels equally weight families/times and fixed base strata. No old
sample is pooled. Use the unchanged paired-stratified uncertainty estimator.

Recovery windows contain 25 tasks except task 88's 13 tasks. Only windows
from a condition's own schedule partition its recovery. The new measurements
include stable wrong/purchase/total cost, posterior probability of change
and threshold crossings after available paid labels, and evaluator-only
conditional risk/regret. These are descriptive; extra checking is not always
unnecessary, and posterior threshold crossing is not a new policy action.

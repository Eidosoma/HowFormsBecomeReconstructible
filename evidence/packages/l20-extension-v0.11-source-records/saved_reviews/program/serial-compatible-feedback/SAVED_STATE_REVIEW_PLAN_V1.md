# D145 saved-state review — exploratory plan, 2026-09-18

User approved the proposed review of the 14 B final-preference exceptions and
the unresolved compatible B G3-minus-G1 shape change. The D145 outcomes and
exception identities are already known. This is a **post-hoc descriptive
review**, not a preregistered test or new prospective evidence.

## Scope and comparisons

Read only the completed D145 artifacts. Preserve every founder, life, noise
replicate, generation and condition in the original experiment. Extract all
128 paired compatible/zero observations for each of A and B, not just the
14 B losses. Keep the original primary, controls, thresholds and intervals.
No new dynamics, descendants, guide fit, address choice or outcome exclusions.

1. List every B negative paired G3 preference with all three generations'
   preference and shape contrasts. Distinguish a negative *difference* from
   an absolutely negative outcome. Count recurrence across the two replicas
   of each starting record and the distribution across founder blocks.
   Replicas change both address and dynamics streams; this is **not** a
   crossed address-versus-noise experiment.
2. Compare continuous outgoing-record, writer-end and assay-reference shape
   with the preference exceptions. Inspect all saved read/write/assay time
   points, the original guide family and margin, canonical alignment and
   packet mean/RMS. Identify losses already visible before the final assay.
3. Decompose the original terminal preference difference exactly, at the
   unchanged abs(V)>0.2 threshold: sign changes at cells active in both
   conditions, cells active only with compatible feedback, and cells active
   only with zero feedback. Report net cell-scoring units and changed-cell
   counts. Do not substitute an alternative threshold or endpoint.
4. Record each actual supplied address and the actual incoming/read-end
   field's self-correlation under that shift. An ideal-pattern-preserving
   address need not preserve an imperfect acquired field. Report observations
   without ranking offsets, selecting a better rule or claiming causality.
5. For compatible B G3-minus-G1 outgoing shape, retain all positive and
   negative changes. Report their contributions to the complete mean,
   per-block means, all negative cases and sibling-replica sign agreement.
   Reproduce the registered whole-block interval and report leave-one-block-out
   **point-estimate range only** as an influence diagnostic. Do not remove
   adverse blocks, retest selected subgroups, declare equivalence or tune a
   stopping rule. Record whole-cohort correlations only as descriptive leads;
   correlations involving G1 and G3-minus-G1 are mathematically coupled.

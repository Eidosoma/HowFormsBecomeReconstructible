# TT036: fresh-cohort confirmation of stopped writing

Decision dated 2026-10-21, before any production source state or outcome is generated. TT035 motivates one fixed comparison: write for 100 updates and continue the body to update 1,000, versus writing throughout all 1,000 updates. This experiment does not optimize the stopping time or initiate a serial-writing confirmation.

## Population and interventions

Use all 64 newly reserved identities 36000000–36000063, with four task messages and four coupled replicas per identity. Generate parents, captured common/residual packets, fresh developed bodies and injury jitter using the audited TT028 source recipe. No retained production bodies, packets, or recipient outcomes are reused. Excluded identities 608280 and 608281 are used for qualification and benchmarking; an exposed 28000000 source bridge checks the inherited recipe. These are never confirmation cases. Bounded identity and RNG-address searches establish local non-reuse, not independent custody or blinding.

For each family and block, use all four physical half-body masks, own/foreign/neutral body sources, two independent corruption fields, and writing budgets 0, 100 and 1,000. Replace the entire incoming packet with projected random noise (the inherited mix100 recipe). Pair that corruption across all arms and families. Reset ema to zero and memory uniformly to V minus half the resting value. Each body continues for exactly 1,000 updates; writing stops at its assigned budget while physical dynamics and live packet feedback continue. Foreign substitution moves body voltage only, from the orthogonal task with the same replica. Neutral voltage is resting voltage plus matched injury jitter. All cases remain enrolled regardless of initial developed-body performance or later outcome.

The finite channel is 16×16 binary64 values (2,048 bytes per record). For each corruption use four new matched recipient conditions from the TT036_stopped_confirmation_v1 namespace. Within a receiving condition, pair birth and forcing noise across every packet, budget, family, mask and body source. Different recipient conditions and corruption indices use different declared RNG addresses. Native source/repair noise remains coupled across the four tasks within a replica and paired across interventions. This does not test independent teaching cues. Retain the original undamaged packet as a reference as well as preservation and every rewrite.

## Fixed analysis and decision

Two co-primary endpoints: terminal body fractional target recognition at update 1,000 and terminal fresh-recipient fractional target recognition after its 1,000 updates. For each, compare 100 minus 1,000 writes in the residual family, own body source, equally averaging all four masks, both corruptions, all 16 worlds and (for packets only) four recipient draws. The population unit is the source block: 64 equally weighted paired block differences. Fractional ties are retained; this credit is not interchangeable with uninterrupted body success or a binary success rate.

Use one-sided paired-block Student-t lower bounds with alpha 0.025 per endpoint (Bonferroni family alpha 0.05), margin zero, and a joint confirmation only when both lower bounds exceed zero. Coverage is approximate. If the estimated variance is zero, use the bounded Hoeffding lower bound instead; also report that conservative bound as sensitivity for each endpoint and pointwise two-sided 95% intervals. No practical-effect threshold or power guarantee is asserted. Failure of either endpoint gives NOT_CONFIRMED; secondary panels cannot rescue this decision. No interim scientific examination, optional stopping, extra cohort or automatic retry.

Report each endpoint separately, all three budgets, comparisons with preservation, common family, every physical mask and target-relative geometry, body sources, initial passing/failing histories, messages, and target/donor/template/opposite credits. Retain paired gains/harms/ties at receiving-draw, fixed-output mean and record-mean resolution, plus joint body/packet sign tables. Body counts occur once per corruption/record, never once per recipient draw. Average two mixed-polarity masks within each record; do not count them as separate population units. Preserve both equal-block and pooled-record reductions for unequal subgroups. Four recipient futures per fixed packet provide coarse conditional information; they do not estimate serial descendant capacity or uninterrupted performance.

Source bridge uses only exposed data. Exact reuse inside this experiment requires identical full physical initial-state bytes, RNG address and writing budget for repairs, or identical packet bytes and receiving address for readers. Preserve every logical case and alias; never reuse by outcome or symmetry.

Qualification uses full 1,000-update dynamics, both families, all masks/sources/budgets, one corruption and one recipient condition on both excluded identities, with serial/parallel equality and adversarial analytic fixtures. Benchmark that same excluded workload once. Forecast production from the slower measured baseline, repair and reader rates scaled to upper physical counts, plus an eightfold per-block allowance for remaining work, global summary/custody scaling, and 1.5 overall slack.

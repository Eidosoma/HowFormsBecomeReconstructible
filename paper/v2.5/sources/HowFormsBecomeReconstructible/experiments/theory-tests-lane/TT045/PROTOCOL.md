# TT045 — new stochastic futures of fixed states and packets

Authorized after reviewed TT044. This development experiment tests whether its
packet and starting-state effects persist when averaging genuinely new forcing
streams. Source blocks 39010000, 39010016, 39010032 and 39010048 are already
exposed. Outcomes from the new production streams must remain unseen until the
fixed design, implementation and resource limits are qualified and frozen.

Use the same actual preserved and late-written finite float64 theta packets
(16 by 16, 2048 bytes per record), one saved continuing V/ema/R state and all four
saved fresh states. Keep every damage level, family, source, mask, task, history
and role credit. Hold feedback off (c_m=0), native memory updating (rho=0.002),
writing off, and theta byte-identical throughout. No repair, normalization,
selection by outcome or additional teaching occurs.

For each block sample 16 new forcing futures. Restore each fixed starting state
for every future and packet: one continuing state and four fresh states each
receive all 16 streams. The new future index is independent of the old birth
index. This is a full crossing of saved states and new futures. Reuse the same
future's Gaussian fields across states, packets and injury contexts within a
block, with native message coupling and four replicate noise fields. Distinct
blocks and future indices have separate seed addresses. The state is restored,
not regenerated. Observers receive task labels only after dynamics.

Use native assay RNG with address equal to the first 16 SHA-256 bytes, interpreted
little-endian, of compact JSON ["TT045_fresh_forcing_v1", role, block, future].
Use tag/phase assay, no skipped fields. Qualification uses role excluded, blocks
608280/608281, their one saved fresh state and two new futures each. Production
uses role development. Bind all addresses before dynamics, reject collisions
with each other and every retained TT044 stream. Verify independent RNG start,
end and checkpoint digests. These streams change realizations, not the noise
law or amplitude; there is no separate continuing/fresh noise-law factor.

## Outcomes and estimands

Retain readouts after 0, 1, 100, 500, 900 and 1000 updates. The main panel remains
half-mixed/residual/own, all histories and masks, target credit at both 900 and
1000. Compare late minus preserved packet in the continuing state, the average
across the four fixed fresh states, and their state-by-packet interaction.
Retain absolute packet scores, state effects for each packet, both remaining
cross-arm contrasts and both ordered decompositions (seven contrasts total).
Do not substitute a relative packet advantage for an absolute improvement.

Use tied-winner success credit, stored as integer twelfths, and retain continuous
target-versus-best-other margins separately. First average actual saved states
within a profile, then paired futures, and masks within each source position.
Keep the continuing state once per actual new future, never four times as birth
evidence. Retain means and Monte Carlo standard errors separately for every
fixed state/packet/case, as well as each fixed-state paired packet effect.
Sixteen futures give coarse individual-record estimates. Tied credit is a bounded
random variable; do not apply binomial confidence intervals to fractional credit.
Zero observed variance is not certainty about unobserved futures.

For each condition and block, retain all future-specific arm and contrast means.
Estimate conditional Monte Carlo standard error as sample standard deviation
over the 16 independent future indices divided by sqrt(16), after averaging
dependent positions/masks/states within each future. Pair all comparisons using
the same future index. Across independent block seed banks, propagate Monte
Carlo variance with the squared weights for equal-block and position-pooled
means. This quantifies simulation error conditional on the fixed source records,
not population uncertainty. Keep all four block results; do not count the new
futures, masks, tasks or starting states as additional population blocks.

Retain harm/tie/gain counts for source-position means, individual injury means
and each paired position/future (after averaging masks and fresh starting states
where appropriate), with explicit denominators. Retain the joint signs of each
contrast at 900 and 1000, at both position-mean and paired-future levels. Keep
within-arm 900-to-1000 changes. Retain the original-packet comparator and the
full TT044 endpoints as historical data, clearly separate from new-future means.
Show intact and fully replaced panels alongside half-mixed results. No outcome
is dropped for disagreeing with the earlier result; no significance gate, adaptive
extension, seed selection or early efficacy stopping is allowed.

This estimates repeatability under new futures conditional on exposed packets
and receiving states. The average of four saved fresh states is an empirical
mixture, not a newly sampled recipient population. No fresh population
confirmation, long-run inheritance, general capacity or uninterrupted-performance
claim follows. No serial-writing successor runs automatically.

## Verification, storage and execution

Bind sources to completed TT044 manifests. Use its native kernel, independent
equation audit and reference equations unchanged. Each new trajectory undergoes
full independent equation and scalar-score replay. Preflight also replays both
unchanged TT044 baseline packet pairs on the first excluded context. Check
state/packet custody, pairing, new RNG addresses, wrong-stream/offset rejection,
label/field rejection, a one-ULP packet mutation, exact contrasts and weighting,
actual continuing denominators, joint signs and Monte Carlo variance formulas.

Store every changing V/ema/R field at every checkpoint. Store the held packet
once per trajectory; expanding it must reproduce all 24 original raw arrays
byte for byte. Every physical package (packed states, observations, metadata)
must fit 700 KiB. Count physical identities from starting bytes and forcing
addresses before dynamics; deduplicate only literally identical trajectories
while preserving all labelled cases. Cap total study output at 18 GiB, with a
4 GiB free-space reserve. Budget the fixed physical packages plus 1 GiB per
phase for reductions/receipts and 64 MiB administration before launch. Enforce
per-package/count ceilings and disk reserve between trajectories, and actual
study size at block/phase boundaries. No prior evidence is deleted.

Qualify the complete design on two excluded blocks with two new futures. Require
exact serial/parallel agreement with the fixed preflight context. Two normal-
priority workers run on CPUs 6/7 with one numerical thread each, within the
shared 12-CPU ceiling. All writes remain in this lane.

Qualification forecast: 1.5 times measured eight-new-trajectory context duration,
times the larger excluded physical block count divided by eight, plus 60 seconds;
require below one hour. Production forecast: 1.5 times measured two-block
qualification duration, times the sum of the maximum physical counts in the two
production block pairs divided by the smaller excluded physical count, plus
60 seconds; require below 12 hours. Each phase deadline is twice its forecast,
at least 600 seconds. These are conservative forecasts, not guaranteed runtimes.

One detached controller admits capacity once, qualifies once, freezes if checks
and budgets pass, admits production capacity once and runs production once.
Record durable completion/failure and exit. One startup persistence check, then
leave the job unpolled until the user asks. No automatic retry, extension,
waiting loop or successor. Earlier scientific classifications remain unchanged.

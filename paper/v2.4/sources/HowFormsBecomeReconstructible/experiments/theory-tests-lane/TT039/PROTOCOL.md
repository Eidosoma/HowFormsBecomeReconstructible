# TT039: fresh confirmation of late writing

Authorized after the TT038 result and the user's acceptance of the fresh
confirmation proposal. Freeze one comparison before any new production state
or outcome: late writing versus preservation. TT037/TT038 selected the schedule
and later endpoint; their exposed records provide no confirmation evidence here.

Use 64 newly reserved source identities 39010000–39010063. Generate new parents,
captured common/residual packets, developed bodies and injury jitter with the
unchanged audited TT028/TT036 source recipe: source capture and development both
last 1000 updates. No production body, packet or recipient outcome is inherited.
Use excluded identities 608280/608281 for qualification and benchmark, and exposed
28000000 only for the source-generation bridge. Record a bounded local identity
search and explicit RNG inventory; these establish local non-reuse, not independent
custody or global non-use. The fixed 64-block size follows the prior confirmation
design and is not a power guarantee or a promised practical-effect threshold.

Each block has four task messages and four coupled replicas. Retain both families,
four physical half-body masks, own/foreign/neutral body sources, two corruption
realizations, every initial performance history and all four recognition credits.
Use the same full incoming-packet replacement, uniform memory reset and injury
recipe as TT036: incoming projected random noise, ema zero and R = V minus half
the resting value. Pair corruption and physical forcing across arms. Foreign
substitution moves body voltage only to the orthogonal task with the same replica.
Neutral voltage is resting voltage plus matched injury jitter. No cases are filtered.

Preserve four schedules: no writing; early updates 1–100; late 901–1000; full
1–1000. Every body continues to update 1900, with writing off in all arms after
1000 and native body/memory dynamics and packet feedback continuing. Save complete
physical states at 0, 1, 100, 101, 900, 901, 1000, 1001, 1100, 1500 and 1900. Score
bodies at 1000, 1001, 1100, 1500 and 1900. Only 1900 is the primary body endpoint.
The full-writing control is full-then-hold after 1000. Writing schedules receive
no labels, scores or stopping decisions. Packets must remain byte-identical on
every nonwriting update, including the entire 1001–1900 follow-up.

The finite channel is 256 binary64 values / 2048 bytes per record. Assess the
original undamaged packet, preserved incoming packet and every outgoing packet
in four newly generated receiving conditions per corruption. Recipients each
run for 1000 updates without packet writing. The outgoing packet at 1900 must
equal its update-1000 packet. Pair recipient birth and forcing across arms,
families, masks and sources within each receiving condition. Distinct conditions,
corruptions and blocks have distinct declared addresses in
TT039_late_confirmation_v1. Native source/repair forcing remains coupled across
task messages within a replica. Teaching cue independence is not changed or tested.

Two co-primary outcomes are fractional original-target body credit at 1900 and
fresh-recipient credit after 1000 recipient updates. Compare late minus preservation
in the residual family, own source, averaging equally over all four masks, both
corruptions and all 16 worlds, with recipient draws averaged only for the packet
outcome. Independent population units are the 64 paired source-block differences.
Fractional recognition credit is not a binary success rate or uninterrupted-body
endpoint. Time-1000 scores and the other checkpoints are descriptive only.

Use the TT036 inference rule: one-sided paired-block Student-t lower bounds,
alpha .025 per co-primary outcome, Bonferroni family alpha .05, margin zero.
Joint confirmation requires both lower bounds to exceed zero. Coverage is
approximate; report pointwise two-sided 95% intervals and the conservative bounded
Hoeffding sensitivity. If variance is zero, use the bounded lower limit for the
decision. Failure of either endpoint gives NOT_CONFIRMED. Secondary schedules,
geometries, sources and timepoints cannot rescue it. No interim scientific inspection,
optional stopping, enlarged cohort, automatic retry or successor is permitted.

Retain all six arm contrasts, comparisons with preservation, both families,
physical masks and target-relative geometries, body sources, initial passing/failing
histories, messages and target/donor/template/opposite credits. Average the two mixed
masks within each record. Preserve all gains/harms/ties at paired, fixed-output mean
and record-mean resolutions and joint body/packet tables. Body counts never multiply
by recipient draws. Save equal nonempty-block and pooled-record estimates, all block
values and within-arm body changes from 1000 to 1900. An average benefit establishes
neither benefit for each individual nor benefit in every damage geometry.

Freeze code, native source hashes, predecessor evidence, reservations, RNG manifest
and the measured resource plan before production. Independently replay every new
parent/capture/development, 1900-update body and 1000-update recipient trajectory;
verify saved states, RNG digests, scoring, holds and roundtrips. Physical reuse requires
identical full starting-state bytes, repair address and writing window; receiving
reuse requires identical packet bytes and receiving address. Check bytes after hashes
and retain all logical aliases. Neither outcomes nor symmetry authorize reuse.

Qualification uses both excluded blocks, both families, every mask/source/arm,
one corruption and one new receiving condition, at full durations. Run the first
block serially and both in the two-worker batch and require exact serial/parallel
agreement. Bridge fresh source generation to TT028 and all four uninterrupted
1900-update schedules to the excluded TT036/TT037 prefixes and TT038 continuations.
Require independent physical replay, analytic sign/count/within-body fixtures,
joint-decision and confidence-bound checks, invalid context/window/RNG rejection,
packet-mutation and join rejection, and saved report roundtrips. Qualification is
expected to exceed ten minutes; run it detached with a fixed 1800-second limit.

After qualification, benchmark the complete excluded two-block pipeline once
(600-second hard limit). Forecast from the slower measured source, repair and reader
rates, upper physical counts, eightfold remaining per-block work, summary/custody
scaling and 1.5 slack. Admit production only below a 24-hour forecast, 32 GiB output
cap and 8 GiB free reserve. Stop at twice the forecast, capped at 48 hours. These
are resource bounds, not statistical stopping rules.

Use two normal-priority workers on CPUs 6/7, one numerical thread each, within the
shared ceiling of 12. A detached controller may wait a cumulative two hours for
resource capacity, qualify once, benchmark once, freeze and launch once only after
all checks pass. It performs one production startup check, then exits. User-requested
status checks are the only scientific polling. No automatic retry, extension or
successor. Preserve failures; all writes and caches remain in the lane.

This tests the fixed schedule's joint average benefit in the stated population.
It does not test optimal timing, independent teaching cues, continuous performance,
indefinite stability, damage-level robustness, warning-policy selection or serial
heredity. TT028 primary/joint and TT036 confirmation classifications are unchanged.

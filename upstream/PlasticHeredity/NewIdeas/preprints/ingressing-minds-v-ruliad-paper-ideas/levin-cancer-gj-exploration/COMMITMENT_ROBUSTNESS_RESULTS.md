## Plain-language result

The two ways of stabilizing an acquired M state tolerate the tested small
starting differences and uneven contact times. Removing one of two donors
breaks onward M transmission at these settings. Stability before handover
and successful replacement by new carriers are different requirements.

This does not mean a carrier necessarily keeps M throughout unsuccessful
contact: saved donor trajectories can show changes during the attempted
handover. The retention assay precedes that interaction.

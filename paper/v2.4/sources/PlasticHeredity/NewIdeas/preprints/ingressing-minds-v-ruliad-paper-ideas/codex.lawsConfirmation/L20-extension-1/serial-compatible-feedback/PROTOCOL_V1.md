## Serial bottleneck and diagnostic isolation

fresh V/EMA/R and read 300 steps; remove direct access to the incoming field,
reset writer theta to zero and write 1000 steps; construct the same outgoing
record. Only those outgoing bytes continue to the next generation. No previous
body, theta, rollout, optimizer or recurrent state crosses a boundary.
Static host/rulebook parameters and the public guide remain fixed.

A separate fresh 300-step assay reads each outgoing record. Its body and
states never enter the lineage. Assay noise/birth and actual next-generation
birth/read use separate namespaces. The assay cannot affect outgoing packets.
All cases share dynamics streams within a noise replicate at a given phase
and generation. Different generations/replicates/lives/blocks/phases are
separate. G1 ordinary readers and enabled writer starts match; after G1, only
birth/noise are matched, because inherited packets legitimately diverge.
Do NOT force later read-end states equal across conditions.

Read-off disables every lineage reader, but not the separate assay.
Write-off disables every lineage writer and transmits the zero field at every
boundary. Copy preserves F0 rather than its routed version. Every path retains
full read/write bookkeeping and fresh assays, including disabled controls.
No rescue packet, archived birth correction or other undeclared carrier.

Save lineage read times 0,1,100,101,300; write 0,1,300,1000; assay
0,1,100,300 at all three generations. Preserve byte continuity
F0→F1→F2→F3 and each independent fresh birth. Record-only transmission is

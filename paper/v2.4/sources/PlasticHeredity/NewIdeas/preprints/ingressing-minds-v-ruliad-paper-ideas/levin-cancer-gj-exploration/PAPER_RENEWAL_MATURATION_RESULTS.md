300 is the smallest sampled interval with renewed-trio retention in both
historical founders and both resolutions, and 100 is its preceding grid point.
Stock and isolated-focal outcomes did not select these intervals. All selected
trios, including failed and RNA-blocked parents, underwent every onward readout.

RNA blockade from the first replacement prevents both focal E retention and
renewed-trio E retention at every tested interval. Ionic coupling remains in

erase an already retained state. All six matched whole-trio wipe pairs fail

longest unchallenged exposure and are not age-matched to every shorter schedule.

## Interpretation and next lead

P17 separates retention in isolation from resilience during continued carrier
turnover in this model. The same newcomer can preserve E alone yet revert to M
when its neighbourhood undergoes the next replacement. Longer complete
schedules support renewal and another handover. Endpoint labels, isolated
retention, final renewal, and the every-window correction criterion therefore
describe different properties.

The sampled renewal contrast is now 100 failing versus 300 succeeding; the
every-window contrast is 300 failing versus 1,000 succeeding. Neither is an
exact threshold, and no monotonicity between sampled intervals is established.
Every turnover window uses tau, so P17 does not separate the first waiting
interval from later intervals or establish a molecular maturation mechanism.

The next useful lead is to vary the first waiting interval independently of
later turnover: test whether extra preparation before the second replacement
rescues the otherwise failing 100-unit schedule, or whether each successive
handover needs more time. A matched fork comparing extra connected time with
extra isolated time could then distinguish support from neighbours from elapsed
time alone. This is a proposed follow-up, not a registered or launched panel.

Written and ordinary histories perform equally well on the registered outcomes;
no acquired-history advantage is shown. The stock difference at 100 is not a
controlled history effect because stock age and full state are unmatched.
There is one archived source per historical origin, shared earlier ancestry,
and a fixed draw sequence. Forks and finer runs are not independent biological
replicates. Discrete agreement under refinement is not a general convergence
proof. These results concern finite model renewal under externally scheduled
replacement; autonomous reproduction, division and physical heredity were not
demonstrated.

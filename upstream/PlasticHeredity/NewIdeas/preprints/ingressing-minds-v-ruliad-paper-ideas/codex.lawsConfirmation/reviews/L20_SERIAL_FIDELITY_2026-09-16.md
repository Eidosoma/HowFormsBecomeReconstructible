## Result in plain terms

The aligned writer reduces damage, but a fresh descendant reconstructs a
less faithful version of the weaker pattern before it starts writing.
Writing partly recovers shape on average; the final tanh/normalization step
changes it only slightly. This points to the reading/reference hand-off as
the next causal target, not another address correction or write threshold.
It is descriptive localization, not a proof that one component causes a
specified fraction of total information loss.

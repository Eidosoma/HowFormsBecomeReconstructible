# TT016 is complete: next question is dependence on shared initialization

TT016 passes the prospective practical primary on all 1,024 records in 64 new
blocks. Repeated repair improves uninterrupted success under independent ongoing
cue noise by 12.30 points, with one-sided 95% lower bound 10.14 above the frozen
5-point requirement. See the [checked interpretation](INTERPRETATION.md).

The clearest remaining pairing question is whether this benefit survives when
birth/reset states are also independently generated. TT016 changed ongoing cue
noise while retaining matched teaching and birth/reset randomness. A follow-up
could keep acquisition fixed and vary birth/reset pairing, so that an effect can
be attributed to that change. Teaching-noise changes should be a separately
specified factor or study because they also change the acquired record population.

Such a study needs a separately frozen protocol, new lineage identities, one
all-record primary with a practical margin, a sample-size rationale and complete
verification scope before generation. Maintain actual body-written successors,
continuation after failure and the fixed repair. Distinguish the total effect of
reset pairing from any claim about fallback mediation. The terminal decline under
independent ongoing noise in TT016 should remain visible in the motivation.

# D143 — crossed feedback address and other noise: completed results

## Outcome in plain terms

**COMPLETE and independently audited on the user's 2026-09-17 status request.**
For the fragile B pattern under ordinary writing, changing the spatial
feedback address produces substantially more terminal variation than changing
the other noise histories. The address-minus-noise interval is wholly positive.
There is also address–noise interaction: the two sources are not simply
interchangeable random disturbances.

For A, the address-minus-noise interval spans zero; dominance is unresolved,
not evidence of equivalence. Aligned writing ignores the shifted address by
construction and exactly passes its address-invariance controls. That is not
newly learned repair. B's aligned shape still falls 0.824→0.701, reproducing
D142 rather than providing new independent preservation evidence.

This narrows the mechanism behind ordinary-B instability. It does not prove
that slow layers always filter noise away, that the guide is autonomous, or
that acquired shape is preserved completely. D141's prospective challenge
result remains unresolved; D142 primary results are unchanged; **L20 remains
STRESS_TESTED / MIXED**.

# TT009 proposed independent P03 recovery and injury-transfer audit

Status: proposed audit; not registered or launched. This follows the user's request for the next step after TT008. It specifies a review of completed evidence, with a benchmark required before any long replay. No new biological-model cohort or scientific intervention is generated by this proposal.

TT008 established prospective forecast improvement within its apparatus. Its gain was concentrated in initially failing bodies, and its approximate matched-pair decision task did not outperform constant/history controls. The next question is whether changing a failed body's transmitted record changes its probability of succeeding under a specified fresh read. Two completed studies in the theory lane already address this question. An independent audit should determine what their evidence supports before committing to another prospective cohort.

The intended sources are `../theory-lane/p03_fixed_injury_recovery_v0_1/runs/pilot_v1_resume1` and `../theory-lane/p03_injury_transfer_v0_1/runs/pilot_v1`, with their frozen jobs, preparations, source arrays, original protocols and prediction manifests. Current completion metadata reports COMPLETE for both. Their provenance, interrupted-run recovery and scientific verdicts have not yet been audited here. The injury-transfer prediction manifest still labels its package draft development; establish the actual freeze chronology rather than treating that label as a confirmatory registration. [PLANNING_CONTEXT.json](PLANNING_CONTEXT.json) records exactly which materials were opened during planning.

Both studies use all 64 generation-14 failures previously selected from eight exposed blocks, 34000–34007. This audit remains an independent implementation audit of selected development evidence. New reset/noise draws and new injury masks do not turn these old states into a fresh independent cohort. Related histories, founders, recipients and repeated reads remain grouped within their original blocks.

The fixed-injury study compares the actual incoming record, the actual rewritten outgoing record and erasure under the original injury. Its primary quantity is outgoing-minus-incoming success at the native 2,048-update budget, averaged over 16 paired repeats within a case, cases within a block, then equally over eight blocks. Budgets 1,536 and 4,096 are secondary. No practical-benefit margin or confirmatory significance gate was specified. The primary rewrite-effect estimate must be reported regardless of sign. Its signed-transfer decision rule is compared against unsigned access, always incoming, always outgoing and direct repeated-read probes; preserve the split between probe repeats 0–3 and evaluation repeats 4–15.

The injury-transfer study compares incoming, outgoing, erasure-only and zero records under the original injury and fresh uniformly sampled 24-cell injuries. Its primary directional prediction is positive equal-block outgoing-minus-incoming success under fresh injuries. A zero or negative point estimate fails that declared development prediction; a positive estimate remains development support with uncertainty. Preserve outgoing-minus-erasure, erasure-minus-incoming and the change in rewrite effect between injury regimes. Erasure-only removes originally unavailable edges without changing the other incoming weights. This is a total record intervention; it does not isolate signs from magnitudes or establish general robustness to damage severity.

The proposed audit will:

1. Bind every consumed artifact to source completion and frozen-input hashes. Reconstruct enrollment and verify all 64 identities, packet bytes and original masks. Check whether the fixed-injury resumed job preserves the original protocol, random streams and all cases without duplication or outcome-dependent replacement.
2. Independently regenerate the declared fresh masks, resets and noise, preserving native cue/founder matching and common randomness across record alternatives. Verify mask-law and record-order invariance, the erasure-only construction, exact equality of masked incoming and erasure-only records under the original injury, and the zero-record controls.
3. Qualify a separate nonlinear reader implementation on retained known trajectories and benchmark its cost. The target scope is every saved experimental trajectory, including the full fixed-injury trajectory through 4,096 updates and every injury-transfer read. Compare retained endpoint bodies and observation windows as well as scores. If full replay is infeasible within the declared resources, report that limitation before revising the plan; do not silently replace it with selected favorable cases or receipt-only verification.
4. Recompute strict success directly from all four task-aligned last-32 cue/output means, using margin >0.25. Independently reproduce paired rescues and harms, all block contrasts, the existing descriptive bootstrap intervals, and the entire fixed decision-rule comparison. Recompute signed and unsigned diagnostics using a separate operator construction. Prevent probe outcomes from entering evaluation targets or choices after the allowed boundary.
5. Preserve every negative and mixed result. Distinguish record usability under repeated fresh reads, the complete rewrite effect, transfer across the specified injury law and useful diagnostic decision-making. Those are separate claims. Verify compute-cost reporting without inventing a budget-matched advantage or retroactive significance threshold.

Use one CPU and one numerical thread in this lane. Freeze the audit inputs and implementation before replay. Benchmark retained or explicitly duplicated known inputs; record a concrete runtime/storage forecast and ceiling before any long execution. Jobs forecast over ten minutes run detached with closed stdin, a unique log/PID, durable status, one startup check and no automatic polling. Preserve failed audits and completed source artifacts. This proposal does not launch a successor automatically.

The immediate deliverable is a complete audited account of both P03 studies, including their decision-rule failures if present. A subsequent prospective causal test would require fresh independently enrolled states, a frozen intervention, a justified practical effect and sample plan, and a declared injury/read distribution. If the P03 effects do not survive the audit or decision controls, narrow the proposed claim before allocating that cohort. Do not reinterpret TT008's forecast success as proof of either causal effect.

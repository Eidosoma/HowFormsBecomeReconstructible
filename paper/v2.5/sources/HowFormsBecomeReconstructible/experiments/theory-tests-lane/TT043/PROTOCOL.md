# TT043 — packet usefulness with and without memory feedback

Authorized 27 October 2026 after reviewed TT042. This is a development
intervention on the same four exposed blocks (39010000, 39010016, 39010032,
39010048). Its design is informed by the opposite mean packet effects in
continuing bodies and fresh recipients. Freeze before new production outcomes.

Supply either the actually preserved incoming packet T0 or the actually
late-written packet T1 from TT040. Compare memory feedback gain c_m=0 (F0)
against native c_m=0.2 (F1); all other parameters, including rho=0.002, remain
native and identical. The four arms are F0T0, F0T1, F1T0, F1T1. Writing stays
off, theta remains byte-identical at every step, and no teaching, normalization,
new injury or state reset occurs during the comparison. The channel is the
actual finite float64 16-by-16 theta field, 2048 bytes per supplied record.

Use two separately paired receiving profiles. The continuing profile starts
from the preserved body's V/ema/R at update 1000 and continues the original
repair forcing after exactly 1000 consumed fields. The fresh profile starts
from the saved TT040 fresh receiving V/ema/R at birth, with each of its four
original receiving draws and original assay forcing. Within each profile/draw,
packet and feedback arms share exactly the same initial body and noise stream.
The profiles have different initial states and forcing; their direct difference
does not isolate feedback. Retain snapshots after 0, 1, 100, 500, 900 and 1000
updates in both profiles. The continuing profile's last point is absolute
update 2000; its 900-update anchor is TT042's terminal update 1900.

Feedback is the only intervention in a conditional F1-minus-F0 comparison.
R keeps updating in both arms. With feedback off it is an unexpressed tracker:
V, ema and theta match the original fresh assay, which additionally clamps R
to zero. Do not claim full R-state identity to that clamped assay. Qualification
must separately reproduce the original clamped native assay and establish that
enabling R updates with c_m=0 leaves every saved expressed field unchanged.

Retain all three packet mixtures, both common/residual families, four injury
masks, own/foreign/neutral sources, all 16 source positions per block, all
histories and four target/donor/template/opposite credits. Preserve the incoming
packet and the separately labelled inherited original-packet recipient control.
Use all four original receiving draws in production; excluded qualification
uses its one existing draw on each of blocks 608280 and 608281. No filtering or
selection by success, warning, state difference, margin or intervention benefit.

## Outcomes and contrasts

The main panel is half-mixed/residual/own, all histories and masks, target
credit. Main contrasts at both 900 and 1000 updates, separately in each profile:
late-minus-preserved packet effect with feedback off; the same effect with
feedback on; and their difference (the packet-by-feedback interaction).
Also retain both conditional feedback effects, the other two pairwise contrasts,
all checkpoints and panels, and both exact additive decompositions. No unique
mediation fraction, significance gate or confirmation decision is assigned.
Keep intact and fully replaced panels alongside the half-mixed panel.

Primary scores allocate unit credit across tied winning messages. Store exact
integer twelfths before aggregation. Retain harm/tie/gain counts at each
individual injury and receiving draw and after averaging masks and draws within
source positions. Average each profile's actual draws without replicating the
single continuing-body observation four times. Preserve target-versus-best-other
continuous margins for all four role credits as descriptive secondary outcomes.
Retain the 900-to-1000 change and paired signs separately from terminal levels.

Report the four block effects, equal-nonempty-block means and pooled record means
separately. Four exposed blocks, not their dependent positions/masks/draws, are
the population units. Reusing receiving randomness adds new intervention
conditions, not new independent futures or state-local capacity estimates.
Sparse checkpoints do not establish uninterrupted performance. A changed
interaction would support a feedback effect in this simulated intervention;
it would not by itself explain all differences between the receiving profiles.

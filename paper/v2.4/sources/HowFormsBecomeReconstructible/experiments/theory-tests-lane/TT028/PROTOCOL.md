# TT028: prospective conditional correction confirmation

Authorized after completed TT027. Its exposed four-block findings motivate
this study: informative half-mixed inputs improved by 3.223 points under full
corrected writing, with one adverse block; complete replacement improved by
53.711 points, while full writing damaged unmodified informative packets.
No exposed case enters this new cohort. This protocol and all executable code,
qualification, benchmark, identity reservation and randomness manifest must
be frozen before any new production context, parent or outcome is generated.

Enroll all 64 new tissue identities 28000000--28000063, all four messages and
four replicas each: 1,024 starting record positions in each family. Retain
passing and failing bodies and every case regardless of original quality,
realized corruption damage, later outcome or geometry. No replacement of
unfavorable cases. Excluded identities 608280/608281 are for qualification and
timing only. A bounded accessible metadata/code search checks both identity
ranges; its limitations are recorded. No independent custody or technical
blinding is claimed.

Generate fresh parents by the unchanged source laws at each new tissue
identity: 120 settling updates, 1,000 teaching updates, native source fission,
common/residual packet construction, and 1,000 updates of the original
full-capture own-body live writer for each family. Its outgoing packet is
the unmodified starting record, matching TT027's source recipe. Develop one
new body from that record for 1,000 updates, then apply the original half-body
fission. Keep its actual injured V, fresh mask, developed V and history score.
Every parent, capture and developmental trajectory is independently replayed.
A full-duration reconstruction on the exposed 21000000 control must reproduce
TT027's retained first-episode starting records/bodies exactly before freeze;
this bridge is qualification and never contributes population evidence.

Restore that same developed/injured state for two independent corruption
draws. Each draw consists of four Gaussian 16x16 fields repeated across
messages, centered and projected to standard deviation .3. Test mixing
weights eta=0,.5,1, with eta>0 packets projected from
`(1-eta)*original+eta*noise` at the same norm. Eta=0 returns the original bytes.
Eta=.5 is equal-weight mixing, not a percentage of information erased. Eta=1
contains no original packet information. Corruption's actual functional effect
is measured and never used to filter cases. The original is an assessment
reference only; it never enters damaged-record repair or its receiving physics.

Use own injured V, the fixed orthogonal-message partner V (0<->2, 1<->3, same
replica), and neutral V equal to resting value plus the original injury jitter.
Foreign transport is V only; the packet, old R and ema are not transported.
Masks/jitter are paired across messages, families and all interventions.
Neutral initially lacks message history but is not matched for mature body
energy. The supplied injury mask and resting value remain explicit resources.

The sparse confirmation panel is chosen before new outcomes for the source
and correction questions. Own bodies retain corrected writing at 0,100,1000
updates and full-capture/zero-initial-memory 1,000-update controls. Foreign and
neutral bodies retain corrected 0 and 1,000 updates. Thus nine body/writer cells
occur at each damage level, rather than every writer under every body source.
Unavailable matched source contrasts are absent, never imputed. The 100-update
own-body comparator is a declared sensitivity analysis, not a selected winner.
All bodies evolve V, ema and R for 1,000 steps with live packet feedback.
Only writing is gated; theta is held byte-exact afterward. Corrected R removes
the known resting fill at fresh cells, retaining jitter; full R starts at V;
zero R starts at zero and subsequently updates. Ema starts at zero. Preserve
lambda=.001, norm=.3, the native physical rates and binary64 channel. Save
0,1,100,101,1000 checkpoints and body scores, with exact independent replay
of every physical trajectory and post-gate hold.

Eight new independent receiving draws assess each fixed output packet.
Birth/forcing are paired across damage levels, writer/body interventions and
families within each block/corruption/draw. Only 256 finite float64 values
(2,048 bytes per record) cross the receiving boundary. Bodies, R, masks,
labels and original references do not cross. The 1,000-step reader has centered
Gaussian birth scale .2, ema=birth, R held at zero, no memory feedback and
immutable theta. Labels enter scoring only. Use native fractional tie credit
at absolute 1e-12, independent scalar-score tolerance 2e-13, and integer-twelfths
arithmetic checks. Read each original and two corrupted inputs once per draw;
zero-writing outputs alias only their byte-verified input assay. Read all other
declared outputs separately. No outcome-dependent content deduplication.

Native parent/source seeds retain the original namespace at new tissue
identities. First-body development, fission and repair addresses are SHA256
of compact JSON `["TT022_serial_capture_v1",block,0,1,phase]`, first 16 bytes
little-endian. Their phase seeds retain native reader, source and repair tags.
The same body and repair forcing are intentionally restored across corruption
draws. Corruption seeds hash
`["TT028_conditional_correction_v1","corruption",role,block,corruption]`.
Receiving addresses hash
`["TT028_conditional_correction_v1",role,block,corruption,draw]`.
Reader phase seeds hash `l22-fixed-record-readout-v1:{address}:{phase}` for
assay_birth/assay. All hashes use the first 16 bytes little-endian. Roles are
qualification, benchmark and confirmation. Enumerate and check streams before
production. Source noise stays coupled across messages within replicas as in
the original model; this is not a test of independent cue/message noise.

The fixed panel has 6,912 intervention-repair batches and 43,008 receiving
batches, each containing 16 records: 110,592 repairs and 688,128 receiving
evaluations. Retain 884,736 paired receiving rows, 110,592 distinct intervention
body rows and 110,592 eight-draw conditional packet comparisons. Fresh source
preparation/development is additional, explicitly counted and replayed.
Repeated unmodified repairs are byte-identical under the two corruption slots;
their receiving draws differ. Repeated observations are not new source cases.

Average receiving credit within each fixed output's eight draws, then across
the two corruption draws, 16 starting positions, and finally 64 blocks with
equal block weight. The population unit is the source block. Body observations
are counted once per actual intervention, never once per receiving draw. Retain
original/input/output levels, outgoing-minus-input/original effects, available
matched source effects, all block/record means and paired gains/harms/ties.
Keep all message, injury, pre-injury developed-body and source-body history
strata. Those labels are descriptive, not enrollment or autonomous policy rules.

Three directional practical claims share family alpha .05 via Bonferroni,
alpha=.05/3 each. (1) Primary: own-body full corrected informative rewriting
after half mixing exceeds preservation by two percentage points. (2) Under the
same condition, own-body target credit exceeds foreign-body target credit by
five points. (3) At full replacement, own-body full corrected informative
rewriting exceeds preservation by five points. The margins are operational
requirements of practical effect size, consistent with earlier lane margins;
they are not theoretical constants or fitted to secure a positive result.
Joint message-specific partial correction requires claims 1 and 2. Claim 3,
common-family outcomes and shorter writing cannot rescue the primary.

Use the paired-block mean minus the Student-t quantile at 1-alpha and 63
degrees of freedom times its standard error. PASS requires the lower bound
strictly above the margin; otherwise NOT_CONFIRMED, without claiming absence
or equivalence. Student-t coverage and family control are approximate. Retain
pointwise two-sided 95% intervals and the bounded Hoeffding lower sensitivity
`mean-sqrt(2*log(1/alpha)/64)`. With zero observed variance use that bounded
limit for the decision. All other comparisons are descriptive. Sample size is
fixed under the compute budget, not enlarged after outcomes. A small true
partial benefit can remain unconfirmed: the exposed four blocks do not give
a reliable population variance estimate. Qualification records a sensitivity
grid over effect and block standard deviation instead of asserting assured power.

Each conditional packet comparison includes pointwise 95% Hoeffding intervals
for incoming/outgoing mean credit (range [0,1]) and their paired difference
(range [-1,1]); binary gain/harm frequencies have equal-tailed Clopper-Pearson
intervals. These quantify receiving-future uncertainty for a fixed output and
do not include source-population or corruption uncertainty. They are not
simultaneous across all packets. Eight futures remain coarse; broad intervals
must remain visible, with no claim of precise capacity for every record.

Qualification uses both excluded blocks, two corruptions, both families, all
three damage levels and nine cells, two receiving draws, and five intervention,
capture/development and receiving updates; native parent teaching remains
unchanged. Compare serial/two-worker results and test malformed boundaries,
physical/scoring mutations, source-history substitution, complete sparse-panel
coverage, paired harm arithmetic, block aggregation, decision gates and
conditional intervals. The exposed full-duration source bridge is separate.
The full-duration timing benchmark uses both excluded blocks, one corruption,
the informative family, eta=.5, all nine cells and one receiving draw, including
complete source preparation. No production outcomes enter planning.

Use two single-thread workers within the shared 12-CPU ceiling. Forecast from
the slower worker's per-repair/read, baseline and saved-verification costs,
including per-block analysis, final saved replay, final analysis and manifest
overhead. Add a 1.5 multiplier and 600 seconds. Require forecast below 24 hours
and a fixed deadline twice forecast plus 600 seconds, at most 48 hours. Project
storage by component, add 1.3 margin and 128 MiB, with a 32-GiB run cap and
8-GiB free reserve. If admission fails, preserve it and expose the workload
tradeoff before any changed freeze; never silently reduce the design.

Runs over ten minutes launch detached outside the transient sandbox namespace,
stdin disconnected, unique log/PID and durable RUNNING/COMPLETE/FAILED receipts.
Verify both workers after launcher return, then leave unpolled until the user
asks. No automatic scientific retry, sample extension or successor. Preserve
frozen inputs and failures. This confirms one episode within supplied apparatus;
serial durability, autonomous warning/scheduling and biological transfer remain
separate questions.

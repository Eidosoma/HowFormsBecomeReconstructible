    MSE(r)-MSE(c) = 2 mean[d(y-r)] - mean[d^2]

For plus/minus relative to matched passive let a=(p_plus+p_minus)/2-p_passive
and b=(p_plus-p_minus)/2. Common gain = mean[2a(y-p_passive)-a^2-b^2]; directional
gain = mean[2b(y-p_passive)-2ab]. Plus gain=common+directional, minus gain=
common-directional. Their difference=2*directional. The midpoint is algebra
only, not a scored or proposed third-copy ensemble. Keep both directions.

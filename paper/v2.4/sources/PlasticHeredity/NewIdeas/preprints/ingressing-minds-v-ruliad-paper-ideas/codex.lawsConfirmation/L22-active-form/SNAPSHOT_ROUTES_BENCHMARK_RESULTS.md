With direct writer access on, adding direct body access improves common
recognition **22.65625 points**, preference **45.3125**; it harms residual
recognition **3.90625 points**, preference **4.6875**. Both future streams give
these same conditional signs. Common record means: four improve, none worsen,
twelve tie. Residual: none improve, three worsen, thirteen tie. This is a
conditional routing result, not a claim that bodies universally help or harm.

The protected-only comparator prevents the stronger leap. Both-on updating
is **3.125 recognition / 6.25 preference points below** protected-only for
common records. For residual it is **2.34375 / 3.125 points above**, with five
records improving and two worsening. That residual advantage appears in the
first future and ties in the second. Writer-only updating beats protected-only
in both futures (pooled **6.25 / 7.8125 points**), but it is an observed arm,
not a newly selected primary contrast or a validated recipient policy.

Shape does not reduce to the label. Common both-on parent correlation is
**0.801752**, versus protected-only **0.710526**, despite worse recognition.
Residual correlations are **0.564557** and **0.551388** respectively. None of
these establishes complete shape preservation or online feedback necessity.

Native captured/live and held/live have the same pooled recognition and
preference here, yet different continuous shapes. Held fixed/body-disabled/
body-free writing produces exactly identical outgoing packets, as required.
Writer-off equals incoming. Intact-parent and supplied-positive recognition
are 100%; reader-off is 25% with zero preference. Scrambling the snapshot
removes the common both-on recognition gain, but does not harm residual
recognition relative to both-on. All directions, including harms, are retained
in the complete tables. No favorable gate, threshold or new challenge was tuned.

## Meaning for L20 and prediction

L20 concerns acquired content being written and reconstructed. These controls distinguish carrier use and renewal from intact copying; shape scores distinguish recognition from fidelity. The repeated aligned signal adds a bounded finding: retained usefulness can depend on the record and reconstruction setting even under a common final reader.

This is an explicit simulated extension with supplied guide and coupling variation, not discovered biological diversity, functional concepts or autonomous interface repair. The carrier is 2,048 bytes, not the earlier 64-byte DoorKey germ. It does not validate every original L20 claim.

For prediction this identifies candidate structure, but does **not** show a challenge reveals it better than equally costly passive observation or starting-only measurements. No predictor or prospective forecast was tested. D141/D162 remain unresolved; D174/D176 negative findings stand. **L20 remains STRESS_TESTED / MIXED.**

Next, choose between checking recurrence on fresh founders and returning to a preregistered recipient-aware prediction comparison with the starting-only baseline prominent.

# Repair frequency and continued reconstruction

This authorized development study measures uninterrupted reconstruction under five fixed repair schedules over generations 15–32. The earlier P08 warning study found 219/256 uninterrupted successes with its warning, 221/256 with nine evenly spaced repairs and 222/256 with eighteen repairs. Its warning forecast failed. Nine repairs left little scope for improvement on these exposed trajectories; the next question is how performance changes when regular repair becomes sparser.

The study has two fixed components: a small paired intervention audit at saved failing boundaries, then three new schedule branches on the entire exposed cohort. Audit outcomes do not select cases, alter schedules, change endpoints, or gate the scientific comparison on effect direction. Only technical verification and compute capacity gate execution. All methods, source bytes, analysis and decisions are frozen before either component produces new outcomes.

The causal audit includes every source policy's first failure after a successful common first reconstruction. The exposed census is ten events: three warning, one periodic and six random, with none under continual repair. Identical block, case, generation and incoming packet bytes define the same state; the ten events reduce to seven states. Each state is read with its original incoming packet and with the unchanged recipient-only repair applied to that packet. Both receive the exact saved injury, matched birth reset and independent ongoing positive/negative cue forcing. No earlier body state is carried across the boundary. A spatial implementation and a separate flat implementation must agree byte for byte for both choices, and the actual source choice must reproduce all saved endpoint windows and the final body.

The selected-state outcomes are repair rescue, repair harm, both fail, or both pass. Every result is retained. The original failing observation prevents both-pass except in an implementation error. The audit tests this intervention at this boundary under realized forcing. Both-fail does not establish general irreparability, and a rescue does not show that an extra call would improve the original fixed-nine-call policy once its full allocation changes. The audit is a post-hoc mechanism check, not a population effect estimate or a repair schedule trial. It uses fourteen paired read contexts plus independent replays and seven independently checked optimizer calls.

The main study uses the same 256 starting records in sixteen blocks, 82000–82015, as completed P08. Four blocks represent each of the four existing acquisition-history strata. Generation-14 outgoing records and injury masks are reused exactly, without new teaching or outcome-based enrollment. All branches remain enrolled and continue writing their own successors after failure. They share all injury, reset and forcing draws at every assessment generation. Ongoing cue noise is independent; teaching and reset pairing remain matched. The apparatus, ports, writer, reader, solver and supplied resources remain fixed.

These are the proposed schedules accepted by the user. The first repair is identical across all five arms and the first reconstructed body and readout must agree byte for byte. Subsequent repair calls are fixed in advance and use no outcome or record-dependent scheduling. The one-call branch is withdrawal after the first repair. The others are regular schedules with intervals six, three, two and one generations. The terminal phase is part of each exact contract; no conclusion about an optimal schedule or a universal minimum frequency follows from this grid.

The repair itself is unchanged: infer signed node organization from the current incoming record using sixteen optimizer starts, a maximum of sixty-four sweeps and the fixed seed, with input pins positive. Fill only exactly zero edges using inferred sign products and the median magnitude of observed nonzero edges. Preserve every observed edge byte for byte. An entirely empty record remains empty. Every new optimization is checked with a separate solver implementation. Repair calls, added edge events, added absolute weight and actual repair CPU are distinct resource measures. A call counts even when it changes no edge. Audit CPU is retained separately. Historical control timings remain labeled as historical costs.

The principal endpoint remains uninterrupted intended-task success through all eighteen generations, requiring the original strict aligned output margin greater than 0.25 for both ports under both cues. Its denominator is all 256 records. Terminal success, opposite-task expression, recovery, initial success-run length, generation curves and history strata are secondary descriptive endpoints. Maintenance among the common-first-pass records is reported separately as pooled successes divided by eligible records. It does not change enrollment. The existing first reconstruction passes 222 records and fails 34 in every arm. The exposed eighteen-call control preserves all 222 first-pass records, so its uninterrupted upper bound is already known; nonnegative eighteen-minus-sparser differences are not a new directional discovery.

The entire five-budget curve is reported, together with the adjacent comparisons 3−1, 6−3, 9−6 and 18−9, and the endpoint comparison 18−1. There is no newly declared confirmatory primary effect, equivalence margin or minimum acceptable success rate. Sixteen whole blocks are the inference units. Twenty thousand paired stratified block-bootstrap replicates preserve four blocks per history. For conditional maintenance, numerator and eligible denominator are pooled within each resample; unequal block ratios are not averaged. All intervals are descriptive. Nonmonotone outcomes are retained without fitting or smoothing them into a monotone curve. All policies continue through the full horizon; no effect-based early stopping or sample extension occurs.

The main curve makes 2,560 new optimizer calls and 13,824 reconstruction contexts. Of these reads, 768 repeat the exposed common first step and 13,056 are new subsequent branch outcomes. The complete analysis also includes 9,216 copied nine/eighteen-call control contexts and 6,912 historical repairs. Each record allocates 4,096 bytes. Each policy performs eighteen native reads with 2,048 updates per cue pair, regardless of repair budget. Initial acquisition histories are copied source evidence. No fresh cohort is claimed.

Every control field is copied and checked against source bytes. Separate checks cover all five arms' scheduled calls, exact quotas, repair constructions, writer captures, injuries, noise moments, endpoint scoring, CSV rows, block summaries and bootstrap intervals. Selected flat dynamics cover all three new arms at generations 15 and 32 for cases 0 and 14 in every block. First-step identities and the entire incoming/outgoing chain are checked. These are same-lane internal audits, not external replication.

The frozen worker is admitted only when the shared project allowance can accommodate one logical CPU, with numerical libraries restricted to one thread. At preparation, the host snapshot counted thirteen other active or reserved project threads. Therefore qualification is queued with the scientific work. After admission, seven tests must pass, the selected-state audit must reproduce factual and independent calculations, and a full-budget known-control benchmark at the exact three-arm shape must pass byte comparisons and forecast the curve within 120 CPU minutes. Only then can the three new schedule branches start. Any failed gate writes a durable FAILED record and stops. The schedule and analysis remain frozen through qualification; observed causal effects cannot alter them. Qualification time and scientific-run time are recorded separately.

The job is detached with closed stdin, a unique log, PID and RUNNING/COMPLETE/FAILED records. Capacity admission checks resource availability once per minute and exits after starting the worker; it does not poll scientific progress. The user requests subsequent status checks. No fresh-cohort successor is automatically launched. After this development curve is reviewed, one quantitative budget prediction, a justified practical criterion and an adequate new-block sample can be specified and frozen for the separately authorized prospective stage. These choices cannot be called predeclared results of the current curve.

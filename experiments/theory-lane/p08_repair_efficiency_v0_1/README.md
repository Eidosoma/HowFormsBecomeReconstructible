# Prospective repair call efficiency

This study tests whether six regularly spaced repairs provide more than twice the added uninterrupted-success benefit per additional repair call of eighteen repairs, relative to one initial repair. Every schedule is generated on 132 fresh blocks containing 2,112 records. The prior development curve selected the schedule and motivated this new quantitative prediction.

PROTOCOL.md gives the experimental and inference contract. PREDICTION.json binds the claim and decisions; SAMPLE_SIZE.json records the development-based planning calculation. SOURCE_REUSE.json and sources/ bind reused implementations and known fixtures. EXPOSURE_AND_DECISIONS.json records development exposure and the distinct concurrent theory-tests programme.

Qualification uses synthetic cases and full-budget archived fixtures. It must pass before freeze and launch. The detached job executes its immutable snapshot in runs/prospective_v1/inputs, keeps every record and continues after failure. STATUS.json, COMPLETE.json or FAILED.json provide durable progress. No scientific polling, optional extension or automatic successor is used.

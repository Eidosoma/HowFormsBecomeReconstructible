# Commitment components (v1; fixed before new integrations)

## Question

Why does M-return after six contacts revert to E, whereas after eight it
retains and renews M? Both endpoints are M-labelled. Test whether the difference
is carried by RNA totals, ZEB protein, the ionic background, or their
combinations. This is a causal state-intervention panel within pinned native
BITSEY, not a wet-lab mechanism, global minimality proof or autonomous PH.

## Holds, renewal and outcomes

No new cue contact. Isolate each manipulated receiver for 10000 + 10000 nominal
units. Pass the actual held pair, including negative or mixed outcomes, into
unchanged active rounds 4 and 5: each writes to two fresh E recipients
sequentially for 1000 units each; held recipients undergo 10000 + 10000 waits,
plus separate 5000-unit shadow and 20%-opposite-regulator challenge forks.
Only unchallenged actual recipients continue. Nothing is reset between rounds.

New outcome names avoid calling the direct intervention a natural cue:
`M_retained` requires both carriers to end both holds M with at least 0.95
M occupancy in their last-quarter saved samples; `M_renewed` requires M
writing, retention and local recovery in both fresh-pair rounds;
`durable_M` requires both. Report entry labels, all held/onward labels,
recovery distances, and full regulator/ionic differences, not just these flags.
A negative valid branch is retained and exits successfully.

Use two branch-qualified intervention-fork IDs, recording their relationship to
the original receivers. Exclude the 18 earlier IDs and 12 historical
receiver/cue IDs (the two stopping points share those same historical IDs),
plus these two intervention IDs: 32 initial exclusions. Four fresh relay
recipients are distinct from all of these. Forks are not biological births.
Assay-relative round 4 is 20000–42000, round 5 is 42000–64000.
Challenge forks are diagnostic, not part of this continuing nominal clock.

## Fixed execution bounds

16 × 26 = 416 new integrations, 64 fresh recipients, 32 complete pair
replacements, 32 intervention receiver placements, zero new cue cells or
priming integrations. Historical checkpoints are reused, not new runs.
Two single-threaded workers; 900 seconds per branch; 3600 seconds including
preflight for the suite. A detached supervisor has a 3900-second runner
watchdog and at most 300 seconds for independent post-audit, recording failures
without retry. Expected duration roughly 20–30 minutes based on the previous
panel, not a guarantee. Keep durable logs, heartbeat and exit status.

Freeze the protocol, config, new runner/auditor/tests, native helper chain and
detached launcher before starting the actual panel. Validate in proportion to
risk without running unrecorded biological pilots. Do not edit any earlier
executed helper or upstream source. Reproduction in a fresh checkout requires
the documented predecessor chain and a separately documented path adaptation;
these source paths are intentionally pinned, not auto-regenerated.

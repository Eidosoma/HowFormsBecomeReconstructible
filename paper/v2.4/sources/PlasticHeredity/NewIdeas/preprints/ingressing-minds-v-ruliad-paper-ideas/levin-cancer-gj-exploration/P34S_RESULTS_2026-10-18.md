The control and RNA-only donors ended MMM. Both successful arms ended EEE and
completed replacement of the original and fork carriers. All four failed-parent
numerical cases—two arms at two resolutions—retained their actual-parent open
transmission assays. Their recipient assays failed; none was omitted or replaced
with a successful donor.

The combination's connection-cut readout failed at both resolutions, while its
open readout succeeded. This supports dependence on the tested donor–recipient
boundary coupling for that route. **An ion-only cut was not included**, so this
cut result does not directly establish connection dependence of the ion-only
route. It does not identify a transported molecule.

Both registered binary factorial contrasts, Y6-Y2-Y4+Y0, are zero. The joint-only
rescue pattern from P34R did not recur: ions alone reached the same retention
and transmission endpoints as the combination. This comparison does not imply
identical trajectories or absence of RNA effects on other measurements.

## What changes scientifically

The combined rescue route succeeded on another prospectively specified sequence.
The stronger idea that both substituted components are always needed is not
supported. On P34R's selected difficult sequence, neither individual block
rescued the parent or transmission; on this new sequence, the ion block sufficed.
The sufficient intervention therefore depends on the incoming sequence in these
bounded panels.

“Ions alone” means that only paused ion-state rows were supplied at assembly.
RNA and protein remained at their open-state values initially and then evolved
freely. This does not show that RNA dynamics are dispensable. Every arm also
received the same native algebraic binding refresh.

P34G retains its two-sequence prospective panel. P34R remains an outcome-selected
diagnostic on an already-known sequence, adding zero held-out sequences. P34S
has its own denominator of one. The selected P34R case and numerical resolutions
must not be added to a prospective denominator or treated as a biological
population sample.

Both retained EEE arms transmitted successfully. This panel therefore does not
demonstrate different successor usefulness among parents matched on that coarse
label. It also does not establish acquired-record renewal, autonomous repair,
source generalization or a clinical intervention. The
[source-model correspondence table](SOURCE_MODEL_CORRESPONDENCE_2026-10-16.md)
and its unresolved publication provenance questions remain applicable.

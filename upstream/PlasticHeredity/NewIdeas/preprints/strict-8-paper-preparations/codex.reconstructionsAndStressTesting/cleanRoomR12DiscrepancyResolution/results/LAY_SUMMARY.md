# R12 lay summary

R12 asks why the original and clean-room implementations agree on the broad matrix effect but disagree on finer mechanisms.

The two simulators are `CONFORMANT`. Detailed routing is `INCONCLUSIVE`. The continuous alternative is `SURROGATE_NOT_ADMITTED`. The learned boundary test is `NO_LOCAL_BOUNDARY`. The slow hold intervention is `HOLD_IS_PROXY_ONLY`.

A failed or underpowered module is left inconclusive; it is not converted into evidence for the opposite theory.

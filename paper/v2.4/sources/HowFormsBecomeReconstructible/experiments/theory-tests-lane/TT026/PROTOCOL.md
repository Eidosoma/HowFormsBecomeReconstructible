# TT026: writing duration while body repair continues

Exposed-case development authorized after the completed TT025 diagnosis.
The source cases, historical bodies and TT025 receiving results are exposed.
The principal descriptive estimand is the entire informative corrected-writing
duration curve: outgoing-minus-incoming fresh-recipient credit for budgets
0, 1, 10, 100 and 1,000, with no selected winner or significance gate.

Restore all first-episode inputs from TT024 blocks 21000000--21000003: 16
message/replicate positions, four historical futures and both common and
informative (residual) families. Retain passing and failing bodies, every injury
stratum, gains, harms and ties. Enrollment never depends on diagnostic values.
This is 32 physical contexts and 64 starting records per family in four blocks.

For each context, restore the same actual injured V and incoming theta.
Initialize ema to zero. Corrected R equals V except that the known resting
replacement fill is subtracted at fresh-mask cells; jitter remains. The mask
and resting value are supplied resources. Five corrected variants write for
the first b=0,1,10,100,1000 updates at native lambda=0.001 and norm=0.3. Each
variant continues its own body, ema and memory updates and live-theta feedback
for all 1,000 updates. After b, hold theta byte-exact. Include full R=V and
zero R initializers with 1,000 writing updates; their memory also subsequently
updates. These seven variants constitute the fixed panel.

Every repair is newly computed and independently replayed. No original repair
is counted as new independent evidence; full-budget native checkpoints must
also match their original TT024 controls. The zero-writing corrected-memory
body is simulated with that initializer, never copied from zero-memory repair.
The historical TT022 repair noise is intentionally restored, paired across
variants and families; divergent variants use their own later states. The
repair address is SHA256 of compact JSON
`["TT022_serial_capture_v1",block,history,1,"repair"]`, first 16 bytes little-endian.
The native repair seed hashes `l22-active-form-boundary-v1:{address}:repair`
in the same manner. The acquired injury and its source development remain
fixed; no new injury or birth is substituted.

Repair checkpoints are 0,1,2,10,11,100,101,1000. Verify post-gate packet identity
at every update in both implementations. Save all V/ema/R/theta checkpoints,
separate repaired-body scores, output hashes and random-stream digests.

Each context gets four new receiving draws. Pair births and forcing across
all versions, conditions and families within block/history/draw; retain native
noise coupling across the four messages and separate replicate draws. Distinct
block/history/draw addresses differ. The reader address hashes compact JSON
`["TT026_writing_window_v1",role,block,history,draw]`, first 16 bytes little-endian.
PCG64 birth/forcing seeds hash `l22-fixed-record-readout-v1:{address}:{phase}`
for phases assay_birth and assay. Qualification, benchmark and development
roles are separate. Check addresses and reader seeds against TT021--TT025's
declared previous schedules. Historical repair pairing is intentional reuse.

Only 256 finite float64 entries, 2,048 bytes per record, enter receiving dynamics.
No body state, memory, mask, outcome or task label crosses that boundary.
Supplied structural tissue/ladder/blueprint parameters remain fixed. Use 1,000
native reader updates, centered Gaussian birth scale 0.2, ema=birth, R=0 held,
no memory feedback and immutable theta. Task codes enter subsequent scoring
only. Save 0,1,1000 physical checkpoints and native scores. Require exact
independent physical replay/RNG agreement and scalar scoring error <=2e-13;
native tie tolerance is 1e-12 with fractional credit.

Assay seven packets per context/draw: initial and six nonzero-writing outgoing
versions. Reuse the initial result for zero writing only after exact packet
verification. No other content-based reuse. Production requires 224 repair
batches and 896 reader batches, each with 16 records and independent replay:
3,584 individual repairs and 14,336 individual receiving evaluations. Retain
14,336 paired receiving rows, 3,584 separate body rows and 3,584 four-draw
conditional packet pairs. These shared and repeated observations are not
independent population cases.

For every family/variant report absolute initial and outgoing recognition,
paired change, the paired difference from the full-budget corrected variant,
all block means and starting-record means, and draw/record gains, harms and
ties. Average within blocks then equally over the four blocks. Retain message,
injury and historical developed-body pass/fail summaries. Four-draw conditional
means are coarse; no zero estimate establishes harmlessness. Repaired-body
performance has its own 256 historical cases per variant/family and separate
paired contrasts; do not count each recipient draw as another repaired body.
Exact integer-twelfths arithmetic independently checks fractional differences.

There is no significance test, selected best budget, calibrated individual
capacity estimate, new population confirmation or new serial endpoint.
Reducing damage relative to full-budget writing does not establish a benefit
relative to preserving an already useful packet. A first-episode effect does
not establish durability through future writing/replacement chains.

Qualification uses excluded blocks 603210/603211, their first historical
future and both families, two receiving draws and five updates. Only for this
short qualification, clip budgets to five and use checkpoints 0,1,2,5. The
excluded full-duration benchmark uses the same cases and draws at 1,000
updates with the exact production budgets/checkpoints. Their restored bodies
come from the original excluded 100-update TT024 benchmark; their outcomes
never enter production. Verify exact inherited prefixes, native constant-on/
off identities, independent gated replay, input/packet boundaries, corruption
rejection, pairing, complete summaries, fractional ties and serial/two-worker
equivalence before freezing production.

Two single-thread workers stay within the shared 12-CPU ceiling. Forecast is
benchmark time times the maximum production/benchmark repair or read batch
ratio times 1.5 plus 180 seconds. Deadline is three forecasts plus 600 seconds.
Project bytes with the same maximum ratio, times 1.3 plus 64 MiB; limit the
run to 2 GiB with a 2 GiB reserve. All code, inputs, seed rules and checks must
match qualification and benchmark at freeze. Runs over 600 seconds are
detached with stdin disconnected, durable status and unique log/PID. Give one
startup acknowledgment, then no polling until the user asks. Preserve failures;
no automatic retry, sample extension or successor is authorized by the freeze.

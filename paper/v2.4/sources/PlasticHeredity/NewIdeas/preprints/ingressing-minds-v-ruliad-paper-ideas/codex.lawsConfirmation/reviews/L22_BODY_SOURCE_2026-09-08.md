than the body only amplifying information left in damaged theta.

## Matched comparison

Sixteen fresh tissues 30040–30055; four fixed messages × four new parent
replicates. Keep the original damaged theta and initially zero R identical.
Cross kept, scrambled and donor-ranked body organization with live reader,
fixed damaged reader and writing off. Retain all earlier writing controls.
Use sixteen fresh theta-only readouts per record at unchanged native settings.

The donor-ranked control preserves the recipient fragment's exact values
and fresh-cell fill, arranging its values in the next-message donor's spatial
rank order. This prevents donor bulk voltage or amplitude from replacing the
recipient's. It does not erase all body information or literally transplant
the donor field. Donor labels are used by the analyst, never passed into
fresh readout dynamics.

Three predefined tests: useful repair versus damaged theta; correct-body
versus scrambled-body own-message accuracy; donor-ranked versus scrambled
body **donor-message** accuracy. Reducing original-message accuracy alone
cannot qualify donor redirection. All require a positive adjusted interval
and at least twelve of sixteen positive tissue differences. Other controls
and metrics remain secondary; old failed criteria stay failed.

## Early donor-signal lead: descriptive, not a new primary

Scalar reconstruction verifies all 256 donor-rank transfers. They change
**114–128 of 128 retained-cell positions**, mean **125.949219**, while
preserving every recipient value and fresh-cell value exactly. A large
changed-position count alone is not proof of a strong message intervention.

Read-only summaries of the audited `body_trace` at the predefined saved
checkpoints show that the intervention does initially express donor content:

These are body-state classifications during repair, **not** the primary
fresh-body readings of the completed theta records. For each of 256 original
worlds, the donor is `(own message + 1) mod 4`; scores use the frozen four
half-plane templates and equal splitting of maximum-score ties, tolerance
1e-12. Average worlds within each tissue, then weight the sixteen tissues
equally. No selected subgroup, new dynamics or significance test is involved.

In the donor-live branch, mean donor projection of V changes from **+.031311
at birth to −.083184 at update 50**. Donor projection of initially zero R is
**+.00001536 after update 1**, then **−.005346 at update 50**. Meanwhile theta's
own-message projection changes only **.067738 → .067969** by update 50; its
donor projection remains negative (**−.032629 → −.032682**). These are signed
observer coordinates, not accuracy, comparable channel capacities or a
formal causal-loss decomposition.

Thus the wrong-body signal is present at birth but largely disappears early,
while the damaged record retains substantial original-message information.
This suggests investigating early body-signal survival and transfer into R
and theta. It does **not** yet establish whether the loss is caused by the
incoming reader, native relaxation, fragment limitations or their interaction.
Rank transfer preserves nonspatial recipient information and is not a complete
donor-body transplant. A weak final redirection cannot establish that bodies
carry no corrective information.

## Interpretation and recommended next decision

L22 gains a narrow causal result: **with damaged theta, recipient value
distribution, fresh fill and initial R matched, preserving the surviving
body's spatial organization improves the usefulness of the rewritten record**.
It does not show reliable donor-message redirection under the frozen rule,
exclusive body-derived content, a finite damage threshold, projection-free
writing, a common-currency channel hierarchy, biological generality or durable
renewal. L22 remains `MIXED`, `STRESS_TESTED`; D071/D072 failures stand.

Recommended next, **not launched**: use the saved early-time and fixed-reader/
writer-off controls to localize the loss of the incoming donor-body signal.
Then, if warranted, preregister a short early-window causal intervention to
separate pressure from the incoming damaged reader from native body
relaxation. Do not first change a write threshold, enlarge the panel until
the failed donor gate passes, or extend to a long lineage. This is a next
decision, not permission for an automatic new run; no DoorKey or PX work.

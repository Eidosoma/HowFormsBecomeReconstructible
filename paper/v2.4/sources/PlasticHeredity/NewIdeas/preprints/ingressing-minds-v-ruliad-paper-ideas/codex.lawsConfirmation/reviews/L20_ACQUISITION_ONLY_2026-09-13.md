## In simple terms

The parent was taught one of two patterns. We then retained only its theta
record and gave that record to a fresh body, with no parent body or reference
state. When the parent could write, the new body matched the taught patterns
at **91.5% and 84.0%**. Disabling acquisition writing gave **43.8% and 41.1%**.
Scrambling the written record also removed most of the benefit.

This is stronger than D114's deliberately supplied static pattern: here the
writer acquired useful content through the taught parent's V/R history. The
field strength was matched from birth, and the new body could not keep writing
or use reference feedback during this readout. It is a controlled positive for
the E66-specific acquisition question, not a first-ever writing result across
the programme. Earlier L22/L24 results remain separate positive evidence.

The teaching patterns, writer and addresses are still supplied by us. We have
not shown descendants renewing the message for multiple generations without
reteaching, autonomous concept formation, biology, or a learning disposition
rather than an acquired instructive field.

## Identities, secondary observations and limits

reverse the observer labels. Their average preference is identically zero;
the same holds for the two off conditions. Consequently the combined written-
minus-off estimate equals the primary exactly. Wrong-role traces are the
opposite role's written traces, so combined written-minus-wrong equals twice
the primary, **+82.01 [79.79,84.23] points**. These are useful content controls

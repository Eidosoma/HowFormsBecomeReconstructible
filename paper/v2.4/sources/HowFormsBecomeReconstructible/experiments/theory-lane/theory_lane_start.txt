START LOGICAL RESEARCH LANE: theory
Working programme: Reconstructive Dynamics Theory (RDT)
Status: DRAFT_NOT_REGISTERED

MISSION

Develop an ambitious, falsifiable candidate theory connecting acquisition, reconstruction, maintenance of reader/writer machinery, inherited adaptability and collective individuality. Propose the theory now; a later logical lane, theory-tests, will challenge its frozen predictions. Do not invent a numbered ai lane or imply that either lane already exists.

RDT's starting conjecture is that durable inherited competence depends on maintaining the organization that reconstructs successors, including relevant records, readers, writers and relationships. Learning can stabilize changes that would destroy a useful distinction while permitting changes that preserve, extend or recombine it. When those reconstruction relationships become mutually maintained across component turnover, they may support a new scale of collective individuality.

This is a proposed synthesis, not an established law. Your job is to make it explanatory and quantitatively risky. Merely describing a simulator as a transition operator, calling an attractor a form, or redescribing successful descendants as reconstructible does not advance the theory. Seek predictive compression: can a compact description derived from short probes predict long-horizon outcomes and unseen interventions better than strong alternatives?

2. START FROM THE ACTUAL EVIDENCE

V0.16 separates acquired information, present expression and continued reconstructibility. Its conditional capacity C_H^O(r,m | A,D) names the record, evaluated distinction, apparatus, disturbance distribution, observer and renewal horizon. Extend that clarity rather than replacing native endpoints with one generic score.

The current evidence includes modest founder-specific spatial reconstruction, fresh-body reciprocal repair, matched writer-placement effects, finite reconstruction of a taught conditional function under repeated damage, and context-dependent cellular transmission readiness. It does not establish arbitrary-form reconstruction, a general continuation predictor, unprescribed useful competence followed by durable inheritance, autonomous maintenance of the apparatus, or biological efficacy.

The user's E041 robustness experiments are already running and remain a separate programme. Do not duplicate, interrupt or retrospectively reclassify them. Treat new outcomes as development evidence unless an exact, sealed prediction demonstrably predates access to those outcomes. Existing E041 findings motivate RDT; reproducing them is not an independent confirmation of RDT.

3. DEFINE THE THEORY'S OBJECTS AND BOUNDARIES

Define a complete boundary state sufficient for the proposed forecast. Include retained content, surviving body state, relevant reader/writer parameters, role relationships, resource state and persistent environmental information when they cross the boundary or influence successors. Distinguish what is transmitted, rebuilt, externally reset and continuously supplied. A state is not complete merely because it is named Z.

Distinguish fixed encompassing laws from experience-modified effective organization. RDT need not explain the origin of physical laws. It must explain which learned or maintained constraints matter within them. Some apparatus components can remain explicitly external; that yields a weaker claim than endogenous maintenance.

Define historical specificity, present expression, terminal reconstruction, uninterrupted performance, recovery, adaptability and viability separately. A useful function requires a declared task or viability consequence; resemblance alone does not supply value. Describe individuality as a testable hypothesis about causal maintenance and continuation, not a label automatically granted to interacting components.

4. BUILD AN EXPLANATORY CORE

Formulate a generation-level stochastic reconstruction process and a reduced representation inferred from short, declared probes. Explain how record, body and maintenance organization jointly determine the next state. Specify which observables can be measured before a long lineage and which require unavailable future information.

Investigate selective stability: contraction of perturbations that threaten a functional distinction, together with separation between alternative identities or competencies. Contraction toward one common outcome can erase history. Therefore include both within-identity restoration and between-identity distinguishability. Functional families may contain different implementations; do not assume their approximate similarity is transitive.

Local linearization, spectra, response operators, metastable sets and information-theoretic quantities are candidate tools, not mandatory conclusions. Account for nonlinear escape, non-normal amplification, time dependence, finite perturbations and changing apparatuses. A local spectral radius below one cannot certify indefinite memory or global recovery. Specify when a simpler model suffices and when added structure produces a new prediction.

Keep actual rebuilding success jointly coupled to successor state when the same within-cycle noise affects both. Use the joint transition-and-success sub-Markov kernel for uninterrupted success; do not multiply marginal probabilities without conditional independence. Use consistent forward/expectation operator conventions for translation and prediction.

5. KEEP THREE LEVELS OF CLAIM SEPARATE

EXACT_OR_KNOWN: definitions, algebra, established mathematical results and propositions proved under explicit assumptions. Examples include conditional data-processing bounds, finite-state mixing consequences and local stability conditions. Cite the original result or provide a checkable derivation. Do not promote an assumption to a theorem.

PROPOSED_EMPIRICAL_LAW: a frozen quantitative relationship between measurable short-probe features and future reconstruction, intervention response or retained adaptability. State its domain, estimated parameters, uncertainty, competing models and rejection conditions. This is RDT's immediate scientific target.

LONGER_TERM_CONJECTURE: emergence of inherited learning, renewal of interpreters, flexible collective roles and expansion of competence. Explain how these could follow and what additional evidence is needed. They are not conclusions of current experiments. A finite simulation cannot establish literal open-endedness, and fixed universal low-level laws do not themselves prevent changing effective representations.

6. RESPECT INFORMATION AND RESOURCE ACCOUNTING

Reconstruction cannot create information about an unknown ancestral history from a closed chain with no additional history-correlated input. Improved expression can reflect denoising, redundancy, an appropriate reader or supplied structure. Track those sources. A public guide, coordinate frame, task repertoire, calibration procedure or recurring cue can contribute substantial machinery outside a small packet.

Compare copying and reconstruction under declared loss and resource contracts. Protected copying, copying with accumulated erasures and body-driven rewriting answer different questions. Count relevant storage, computation, time, sensing, actuation and maintenance costs. Do not infer compression from allocated bytes or information gain from a higher fidelity score.

Under a finite irreducible mixing chain with no refreshing source of ancestral information, initial-history distinctions eventually disappear. Long practical persistence is compatible with this limit. Any exception must identify the changed assumptions, such as protected sectors, growing resources or continuing informative inputs.

7. ENGAGE THE CLOSEST PRIOR ART

Read primary sources and construct an explicit overlap-and-difference table. Required starting points include Watson et al.'s developmental memory; Buckley et al.'s natural induction; Watson, Levin and Lewens on natural induction and selection; Hopfield and epigenetic memory; McCourt, Fiete and Chuang on evolved error correction and modularity; computational mechanics; Pattee's semantic closure; Clark, Hickinbotham and Stepney's evolved constructor architecture; Montévil and Mossio's closure of constraints; and information-theoretic individuality and semantic information.

Semantic closure, self-reproducing interpreters, evolved associative memory and error-correction-enhanced evolvability are not new RDT discoveries. Neither is mutual maintenance alone. Identify what RDT adds: a predictive relationship, a discriminating intervention, a new domain of validity or a useful unification that survives comparison. Include relevant multiscale information and open-ended evolution work without treating every information measure as a validated predictor.

Use strong rivals: ordinary attractor/error-correction models, finite-state survival models, tuned predictors from initial state, matched learned reconstruction models, copying with comparable protection, and existing developmental-learning mechanisms. Negative results against these alternatives are welcome.

8. PREPARE EIGHT PREDICTION CARDS

Maintain RDT-P01 through RDT-P08, initially DRAFT_NOT_REGISTERED:

RDT-P01: short-probe features predict long-horizon reconstruction.
RDT-P02: states matched for present expression differ predictably in continuation.
RDT-P03: damage, renewal and resource budgets define a predictable success boundary.
RDT-P04: the theory predicts unseen intervention combinations and order effects.
RDT-P05: surviving bodies and damaged records contribute complementary corrective content.
RDT-P06: acquisition across many tasks supports retained composition beyond the taught examples.
RDT-P07: reader/writer turnover exposes requirements beyond content preservation.
RDT-P08: theory-guided repair choices improve outcomes under explicit budgets.

For each card specify the claim, assumptions, operational endpoint, inference unit, development data, proposed holdout, required controls, strong baseline, effect direction, uncertainty, failure conditions and interpretation of every outcome. Include relevant negative controls and identifiability concerns. Separate a test of RDT from a test that its apparatus works.

Quantitative margins, sample sizes, horizons and practical-benefit thresholds must be justified using development evidence and feasibility, then frozen before confirmatory outcomes. Do not insert attractive numbers as registered requirements. Preserve earlier card versions and explain revisions. Cross-substrate transfer requires predetermined mappings and budget accounting, not post-hoc relabeling of successful cases.

9. BUILD COUNTEREXAMPLES BEFORE DEFENDING RDT

Construct small examples where: a stable attractor erases history; a perfect record has an unusable reader; present success leaves an inadequate successor; failed expression precedes recovery; feedback amplifies error; a shared guide explains apparent inheritance; closure exists without useful adaptation; and a large information score predicts nothing beyond a simple statistic.

Also test alternative functional identities and observer choices. State which examples are outside the theory's scope and why, before their outcomes are used. Reject a proposed measure if elementary counterexamples expose it as tautological or non-identifying. Narrow the theory when a simpler explanation covers the evidence. Avoid a framework that accommodates every outcome by changing the meaning of form afterward.

10. MAINTAIN A FLEET-WIDE BURN REGISTER

Find the existing shared exposure register or establish one according to repository conventions. Record which datasets, seeds, model families, task generators, interventions, aggregate outcomes and raw histories are known to this lane or other programme participants. A fresh seed is not automatically a fresh hypothesis. Document reused panels, outcome-informed design choices and accidental exposure.

Before handoff, produce a machine-readable prediction manifest and a versioned snapshot binding equations, parameters, analysis, controls and decision rules. Dates and hashes establish provenance, not external preregistration. Mark a genuinely prospective status only when the stated timing and access conditions hold.

The theory and theory-tests lanes belong to one programme. Their separation is procedural; do not describe it as independent laboratories or external replication. The evaluator should implement or check the frozen tests independently where feasible and remain free to find errors, failed predictions or adequate simpler explanations.

11. PRODUCE A CONCRETE THEORY PACKAGE

Follow existing naming conventions; otherwise provide: definitions and boundaries; the candidate theory and derivations; an assumptions/claim-status register; a prior-art and alternatives analysis; machine-readable prediction JSON plus readable cards; counterexamples; an evidence ledger; the burn register or its reference; an evaluator handoff; and a decision log.

The evidence ledger must distinguish author-reported results, inspected reports, available raw data, reproduced calculations, missing artifacts, development results and prospective tests. Record exact sources without claiming a raw-data audit when only reports were inspected. Keep the theory readable as a connected argument, with native model details in supporting material.

End the package with the strongest current formulation, its weakest assumption, the most damaging feasible test, what would reject or narrow it, and the next theoretical decision. Do not request confirmation merely to continue ordinary authorized analysis.

SUCCESS CRITERION

A successful theory lane produces a compact, precise and ambitious account that earns its additional complexity through predictions unavailable from a verbal restatement of the data. An equally valuable outcome is a clear demonstration that a proposed RDT component reduces to existing theory, fails a counterexample or requires narrower assumptions. Preserve those findings. The goal is an explanation that can be wrong and improved, not a grand narrative protected from evidence.

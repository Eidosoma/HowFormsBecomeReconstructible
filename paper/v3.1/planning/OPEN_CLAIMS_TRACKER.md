## How to use this tracker

Evidence status and work status are separate. A promising result does not become confirmed because a follow-up is planned. An existing protocol does not establish a completed outcome. Lane documents encountered while locating related work can be newer than the paper's selection; links below identify overlap, not automatic evidence admission or live-job status.

Here, a bounded negative result means that a suitably precise estimate rules out a **prespecified practically meaningful effect in a declared domain**. A nonsignificant result, an underpowered estimate or a broken positive control does not do that. None of these tests can rule out every possible implementation.

Priorities mean: **A**, next focused work; **B**, follow after feasibility or a frozen candidate; **C**, longer-range research. They are suggestions for sequencing, not publication requirements, registered protocols, reserved experiment IDs or launch instructions. Existing campaign criteria remain unchanged.

### Q01 — Body-supplied corrective message content

**16 October status:** [TT021/E92](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e92) confirms message-specific body contribution on 64 fresh blocks. Ongoing memory updating does not establish a benefit over a held snapshot; [TT024/E97](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e97) retains serial failures and an advantage for preservation. The original proposal below is the planning baseline, not a request to repeat the completed confirmation. Further work should distinguish memory routes and serial conditions.

**What is established / missing.** D073/D074 establish that surviving arrangement can improve a record tested in a fresh body; they do not establish the stronger donor-redirection claim. D184's common incoming record does not recover the missing distinction. D186's early-capture development panel improves common-record recognition from 50% to 77.73%, with informative donor controls, but uses four exposed blocks and harms the informative-residual comparison. See [E10](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e10), [E90](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e90) and [E91](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e91).

**Discriminating test.** Freeze a candidate on development evidence, then use fresh founders and byte-identical incoming records within matched history pairs. Compare own, partner, spatially scrambled and history-erased bodies; retain fixed-reader, writing-disabled and capture-matched averaging controls. Only the outgoing record enters the fresh-body assay. A route factorial should separate an early snapshot used to sustain the body from one supplied directly to the writer; count that snapshot as stored information.

**Would support the claim:** a reproducible donor-specific change in the outgoing record's effect, improving recovery of the declared pre-damage message, beyond what the common incoming packet explains. **Would limit it:** a precise absence of useful donor specificity, or recovery explained by residual record content alone. If conventional averaging matches the effect, body-derived content could still be supported while a special reciprocal-feedback advantage is not.

**Work / lane.** Proposed fresh confirmation, not registered here. L22 owns the native study; the [existing active-forms design](../../../experiments/theory-lane/active_forms_v0_1/README.md) describes the same question, not a second replication. Reconcile it with L22's current compatibility work before freezing; ask theory-tests to evaluate only the resulting complete package.

### Q02 — Useful records in currently failing bodies

**16 October status:** [TT018/E93](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e93) now samples 64 serial futures for each of 64 prospectively assigned records, including nine currently failed bodies. Reliable future usefulness is observed for some failed-body records, alongside low-capacity cases and repair harms. The native conditional-capacity estimate is established in scope; matched foreign, reading-disabled and incoming-versus-outgoing comparisons remain additional questions. The design below is retained as the original broader proposal, not an uncompleted duplicate of TT018.

**What is established / missing.** Of 512 E041 histories, 307 have a failed rebuilding-body readout; 243 of 281 eligible first-failure histories recover within three later bodies. These are realized histories. The selected-record injury-transfer study gives an original-injury benefit without establishing the same benefit under fresh injuries. Neither supplies a general estimate of each record's future usefulness. See [E63](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e63), [E64](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e64) and [E72](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e72).

**Discriminating test.** Prospectively sample both passing and failing boundaries, save incoming and outgoing records exactly, and start repeated independent continuations under a fixed injury/noise distribution. Compare incoming, outgoing, repaired, matched foreign and reading-disabled conditions. Hold each paired future realization common across arms; use new independent realizations across future replicates. Measure immediate reconstruction, terminal reconstruction after a declared number of renewals, and uninterrupted expression separately. Each branch must transmit its own newly written record.

**Would support the claim:** failed bodies repeatedly yield useful own-history records, with a stable advantage over the controls; the incoming–outgoing contrast then identifies whether writing helped or hurt. **Would limit it:** the advantage disappears across new futures, or writing reliably reduces future usefulness. Recovery explained by favorable later draws remains compatible with the original descriptive counts.

**Work / lane.** New proposed design, led by theory-tests with native restore support from theory development. Future draws are nested within records and source blocks, not additional independent founders. This operationalizes conditional reconstructibility under a stated apparatus and disturbance distribution; it does not make the capacity definition a predictive law.

### Q03 — Persistence after a single repair is withdrawn

**What is established / missing.** Exact-state intervention rescues all 46 selected immediate failures under their original disturbances. That assay does not include successor writing. Repeated repair improves fresh-cohort uninterrupted outcomes, but continual assistance is a different intervention. See [E83](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e83) and [E89](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e89).

**Discriminating test.** Follow matched no-rescue, rescue-once and continued-repair branches through actual successor writing and a fixed unassisted horizon. The [existing P08 rescue-withdrawal protocol](../../v1.2/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/theory-lane/p08_rescue_withdrawal_v0_1/PROTOCOL.json) already does this for the selected 46 contexts; review that study before planning a duplicate. A later population claim needs fresh enrollment, including initially passing records to estimate harm, and preferably the new-future sampling in Q02.

**Would support the claim:** a one-time intervention changes subsequent reconstruction probability after assistance stops. **Would limit it:** immediate rescue vanishes at the next unassisted boundary, or only continued repair works. A selected-panel result cannot establish population safety or a minimum repair schedule.

**Work / lane.** Existing protocol; no withdrawal outcome selected at this baseline and no current completion claim made here. Theory development owns it. The earlier [P05 noise-repair withdrawal study](../../../experiments/theory-lane/p05_noise_repair_withdrawal_v0_1/PROTOCOL.md) is a separate exposed development comparison, not this exact-state rescue follow-up.

### Q04 — RDT as a useful predictive reduction

**What is established / missing.** Reconstruction capacity is defined conditionally on a record, apparatus, horizon and disturbances. The two-mode model is not yet validated as a native law. Short histories leave very wide long-horizon bounds in TT006. TT008 establishes a useful model-informed forecast feature, not a general theory of continuation. See [E71](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e71), extended evidence (see [source availability](../SOURCE_AVAILABILITY.md)) and the [theory handoff](../../../experiments/theory-tests-lane/THEORY_HANDOFF.md).

**Discriminating test.** Specify one restricted RDT model, its state variables, observation/transition assumptions, permitted probes and computational budget. Freeze forecasts before fresh continuation at held-out horizons and conditions. Compare calibrated probabilities or valid prediction bounds with strong history, conventional state-model and record-feature baselines using comparable information and resources. Check assumptions as well as predictive scores; abstention is legitimate when the model cannot identify a useful answer.

**Would support the claim:** useful, calibrated out-of-sample forecasts, or tighter valid bounds at a declared resource advantage. **Would limit it:** wrong calibration, uninformative bounds, or a precise failure to beat the stated practical benchmark. TT006's witnessed ambiguity cannot be removed merely by running the same search longer. If an RDT formulation is mathematically identical to an HMM, data cannot choose between the names; the contribution must concern adequacy, interpretation or economy.

**Work / lane.** Theory development specifies the model; theory-tests evaluates a frozen handoff. Q02 supplies a better target for state-conditioned validation. The existing [P01 package](../../../experiments/theory-lane/p01_v0_2/README.md) and TT006/TT008 remain their own studies.

### Q05 — Useful record-specific recipient matching

**What is established / missing.** Recipient settings affect reconstruction. D182 extends the named secondary aligned-writer interaction to four fresh founder blocks, while the focal ordinary-writer result remains mixed. This is not yet a demonstrated matching policy. See [E74](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e74), [E79](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e79) and [E87](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e87).

**Discriminating test.** Cross fresh records with independently varied recipient settings. Develop a rule choosing a recipient for each record, then freeze it before new records and outcomes. Compare with the best shared recipient chosen on development data and with simple task/family-based rules. Isolate reading and writing changes where possible and preserve a common terminal assay.

**Would support the claim:** a prospective personalized choice improves a declared endpoint beyond the best common setting. **Would limit it:** one setting helps nearly every record equally, interactions do not repeat, or matching adds no practically useful held-out benefit. A positive secondary interaction alone is not a qualified decision policy.

**Work / lane.** L20 extension leads. Use its existing recipient-founders work as development context; do not revive the obsolete claim that all evidence uses old founders. No additional recipient sweep is registered here.

### Q06 — Added predictive value from an active diagnostic challenge

**What is established / missing.** D141/D162 do not qualify the prescribed active-challenge advantage. Positive response transfer between related tissue graphs and TT008's signed record diagnostic answer different questions. See [E51](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e51), [E55](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e55), [E57](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e57), [E34](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e34) and [E71](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e71).

**Discriminating test.** Select one challenge using development cases only. Freeze the challenge, predictor, endpoint and meaningful improvement margin. Compare with additional ordinary readouts under matched observation and computation budgets, with record/history baselines included. Explicitly account for whether probing changes or damages the system whose future is predicted.

**Would support the claim:** a fresh-cohort forecast gain beyond the margin and the matched passive alternative. **Would limit it:** a sufficiently precise upper bound excludes that gain for this challenge and budget. Failure of one probe is not evidence that all interventions are uninformative.

**Work / lane.** L20 extension leads; theory-tests can advise on prospective comparisons. Keep this separate from Q05's recipient choice and Q07's decision value.

### Q07 — Repair timing based on benefit, not risk alone

**What is established / missing.** The warning-triggered nine-call policy scores 85.55% uninterrupted success versus 86.33% for periodic repair. The later one/six/eighteen-call efficiency test remains unresolved. E013 already confirms a different, small benefit from using recipient wiring to choose spatial repair entries. See [E88](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e88) and [E42](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e42).

**Discriminating test.** On development records, estimate paired repaired-minus-unrepaired future outcomes under identical future draws. Train a policy to predict **intervention benefit**, not just failure probability. Freeze it and test all enrolled fresh records against periodic, random and simple-threshold schedules, using the same call budget. Report uninterrupted and terminal outcomes separately and charge diagnostic reads, fitted state, solver work and added support as well as calls.

**Would support the claim:** improved function at matched resources, or lower total cost at a prespecified performance target. **Would limit it:** no useful gain over the simple policy, or gains consumed by diagnostic and optimization costs. A good risk score is insufficient when high-risk states are unrepairable or already beyond the useful intervention window.

**Work / lane.** Theory development leads candidate design; theory-tests receives the frozen policy. Q02/Q03 inform the target. FEP can contribute a decision-cost formulation without duplicating the native scheduling experiment.

### Q08 — Robustness and mechanism across numerical channels

**What is established / missing.** E051/E052 show major matched-noise sensitivity to writer tolerance, quantization and normalization. E053 retains 92.19% success under four combined changes in the independent-noise regime, but its five-point noninferiority conjunction fails. These do not establish general robustness. E054 shows that record-feature dependence changes with rebuilding history. See [E69](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e69), [E70](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e70), [E75](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e75) and [E76](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e76).

**Discriminating test.** With the same captured records and reader draws, compare noise-then-rounding with rounding-then-noise. Declare whether rounded-to-zero edges remain zero or can be reintroduced, and measure support, signs, relative magnitudes and success. Treat finite writer tolerance as a separate intervention. Use a later frozen confirmation for any selected mechanism; do not pool different earlier cohorts to infer a channel-order effect.

**Would support the claim:** the specified regime meets a declared robustness margin and the interventions identify which record features carry the effect. **Would limit it:** success depends on a narrow zero/tolerance or support convention. High terminal success alone does not pass a failed noninferiority requirement. Float32 storage, float32 computation, uniform quantization and writer thresholding remain distinct interventions.

**Work / lane.** Forms experiments lead channel order and writer mechanisms. The TT017 reset protocol (see [source availability](../SOURCE_AVAILABILITY.md)) already addresses independent resets during continuation; consult it before duplicating that axis. Its outcome is not added here. It does not by itself test independent teaching or every numerical convention.

<a id="q09"></a>

### Q09 — Predicting when rewriting is better than copying

**What is established / missing.** Universal superiority of rewriting is already contradicted. Several spatial chains lose to protected copying; rewriting can beat decaying copying, benefit from supplied family structure, and improve later injury tolerance in E020 even when copied storage stays intact. No validated general rule predicts the winner. See [E12](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e12), [E43](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e43), [E45](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e45) and the [v1.3 synthesis](../../v2.4/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/reviews/V1.3_ESTABLISHED_CLAIMS_AUDIT.md).

**Discriminating test.** In one apparatus, cross storage loss, recipient injury and writing policy while retaining protected-copy and matched-degradation controls. Keep task information, shared machinery and resource accounting explicit. Fit a proposed crossover explanation on development cases and freeze its predicted winners or effect sizes before testing untouched conditions.

**Would support the conditional claim:** successful prospective prediction of where rewriting helps and where it loses. **Would limit it:** the proposed explanatory features fail to predict those crossovers. Another favorable rewriting example does not establish a general advantage; another failure does not erase the existing positive cases.

**Work / lane.** Forms experiments lead with theory development. Use E020's terminal-injury boundary as an existing positive comparator, not an unknown to rediscover. The stronger slogan “only lossy copying or supplied family priors allow benefit” is also too restrictive for the selected evidence.

<a id="q10"></a>

### Q10 — Richer forms and inherited functions

**What is established / missing.** Unprescribed spatial histories retain modest specificity while measured fidelity declines. D099's two-pattern acquisition effect is strong but fails its complete criterion. The functional network's inherited task distinction is one binary orientation at fixed ports. Hopfield H004 supplies a useful separate confirmation, not arbitrary task generality. See [E31](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e31), [E43](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e43), [E86](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e86) and the [acquisition methods](../SUPPORTING_INFORMATION.md).

**Discriminating test.** Hold out entire pattern/task families and require at least two independently acquired distinctions. Establish that the supplied reader can express each task with an intact suitable record before testing acquisition and renewal. Use own/foreign histories, route ablations and copying controls. For unprescribed forms, keep founder prototypes strictly offline for scoring and retain formation failures in the full-cohort report. Separate sign agreement, amplitude qualification and task success where relevant.

**Would support the claim:** acquired specificity and reconstruction extend to genuinely held-out families, beyond common attractors or one polarity bit. **Would limit it:** success depends on the original templates or fixed polarity mapping. If the intact-record reader cannot express a task, the acquisition/renewal mechanism has not received a valid generalization test.

**Work / lane.** Forms experiments lead spatial/task expansion; Hopfield supplies a bounded functional comparison. Start with a small feasible extension. Transmitted record size and the size of the complete supplied mechanism remain separate quantities.

<a id="q11"></a>

### Q11 — Transfer of a causal description across architectures

**What is established / missing.** E008 prospectively transfers response predictions between related tissue connectivities, while a different graph contrast fails. This is useful but does not establish a universal atlas or a portable causal identity across unrelated substrates. See [E34](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e34) and [E86](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e86).

**Discriminating test.** Define a limited intervention signature on development cases in two implementations, then freeze a mapping. Predict graded, held-out intervention outcomes in the second architecture against appearance-only, task-label and simple mechanistic baselines. Include challenges beyond universally disabling reading or erasing everything; account for target-domain calibration data and common task machinery.

**Would support the claim:** the mapped description predicts which interventions preserve, redirect or abolish reconstruction without target-outcome refitting. **Would limit it:** the correspondence requires shared labels alone, new target fitting, or explains no held-out behavior beyond the simpler baselines. Either result concerns a bounded mapping, not every possible form catalogue.

**Work / lane.** Hopfield with forms and theory development; longer-range. Use the successful related-graph case as a benchmark. A cross-architecture success would not establish embodiment-independent causal agency.

<a id="q12"></a>

### Q12 — Renewal of the learned interpreter as well as its record

**What is established / missing.** Supplied readers, writers, guides, pins and schedules still carry causal work. FEP reader-calibration studies repair limited mappings with supported references; Wagner maintains interpretation under supplied mutation attenuation. Neither is an end-to-end demonstration of a lineage rebuilding its own learned interpreting relationships. See [E52](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e52), [E58](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e58), [E81](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e81) and [E82](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e82).

**Discriminating test.** Make one learned calibration or writer organization vulnerable to loss alongside the transmitted record. Ask whether current organization restores both and whether this enables a successor. Retain externally restored-interpreter and disabled-feedback controls. Permit declared low-level dynamics and energy/resources, but exclude an undeclared protected copy of the learned mapping or repeated external recalibration. Inventory every surviving information route.

**Would support the claim:** useful reconstruction persists because the system renews both record and learned relationship. **Would limit it:** success requires restoring the interpreter from outside or retaining a protected learned backup. This would still be a bounded maintenance result, not independence from physical laws or an environment.

**Work / lane.** Theory development first specifies the smallest feasible test; Hopfield or legacy GRN work can later supply an implementation. The [active-forms design](../../../experiments/theory-lane/active_forms_v0_1/README.md) already distinguishes this later RDT-P07 question from message-source repair. Do not launch it as an automatic extension of Q01.

<a id="q13"></a>

### Q13 — Inherited flexible collective competence

**What is established / missing.** Detached communicating trios retain and transmit a regulatory distinction through tested member turnover. Coarse E labels do not determine readiness; component and timing interventions matter. The task, schedules and much of the coordination remain supplied. See [E48](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e48), [E49](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e49), [E65–E68](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e65) and [E85](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e85).

**Discriminating test.** First test the readiness mechanisms across a prospectively declared starting-state and recipient panel, retaining ordinary-origin comparators and numerical checks. A later competence test should teach a collective distinction, replace members and alter geometry or obstruct the usual route, then ask whether alternative coordination preserves function better than a fixed-role control. The changed task must be feasible under a declared positive control.

**Would support the claim:** history-dependent compensation across novel membership and geometry, beyond merely passing on a state label. **Would limit it:** performance requires the original ports, fixed roles or schedule, or broadening recipient states removes the earlier effect. A positive competence result still would not identify an autonomous origin for task values or goals.

**Work / lane.** Cellular/BITSEY leads. Broader readiness transfer is the nearer step; flexible collective competence is a separate later milestone. Related deterministic panels and repeated solver resolutions are not new independent populations.

<a id="q14"></a>

### Q14 — Economical adaptation of interpretation

**What is established / missing.** Exact finite models show useful learned calibration under supplied conditions. Unexpected-change recovery can cost more than buying the reference answer; broader temporal support improves the score without passing that economic benchmark. See [E52](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e52), [E58–E60](../EVIDENCE_E001_E060.md#e58), [E73](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e73) and [E80](../EVIDENCE_E061_E127.md#e80).

**Discriminating test.** Complete and interpret the existing horizon comparison first. Then freeze an adaptive controller and test declared fresh change schedules, horizons and reference prices, counting sensing, delay, computation and mistakes. Include matched Bayesian decision controls and an option to buy the reference. Separate changes to priors from changes to resource allocation or the objective.

**Would support the claim:** a practically meaningful cost advantage across the specified evaluation population, not only a selected favorable schedule. **Would limit it:** apparent benefits vanish when costs or unexpected changes are included. Where Bayesian and FEP policies are mathematically identical, the same outcomes cannot establish uniquely FEP-specific success.

**Work / lane.** FEP owns the formal study. Its [next-work document](../../v1.2/sources/PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/NEXT_WORK.md) identifies existing work; completion is not reviewed here. Keep Q07's native repair scheduling in its owning lane and use conceptual handoffs rather than a duplicate implementation.

<a id="q15a"></a>

### Q15a — Translation to real biology

**What is established / missing.** The simulations establish causal distinctions within implemented models. The cellular source connection is documented, but exact publication table/geometry correspondence remains unresolved. Related biological literature supplies precedents, not a validation of our variable mapping or treatment efficacy. See [SI C.7](../SUPPORTING_INFORMATION.md) and the [cellular correspondence source record](../../v2.4/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/reviews/V0.18_CELLULAR_MODEL_SOURCES.json).

**Discriminating route.** First create a table-by-table source-model mapping: retained equations/parameters, complete and derived state components, added interventions, altered numerical procedures and unresolved source configurations. Test important model contrasts across plausible states, recipients and numerical settings. Only then propose a biological assay with observable record, reading, writing and replacement roles—for example, identical coarse present classification but different response after replacement, tested by a targeted route intervention.

**Would support the bridge:** the predicted causal contrast survives an independently specified biological intervention with suitable controls. **Would limit it:** the relevant mapping or contrast fails in that system; revise the translation rather than discard the established simulation result. Simulation success alone cannot establish efficacy in cells, tissues or patients.

**Work / lane.** Cellular plus manuscript integration for the documentation; experimental biological collaboration for a later assay. No wet-lab study or clinical claim is authorized by this tracker.

<a id="q15b"></a>

### Q15b — Independently active nonphysical patterns

**What is established / missing.** The program measures consequences of implemented dynamics, history and information routes. It has not specified an outcome that would discriminate those accounts from independently active patterns in another realm. This is a different problem from useful pattern-level descriptions or biological transfer.

**Discriminating requirement.** State what additional operational prediction the stronger ontology makes, what the embodied account predicts under the same conditions, and what observation could favor one. Without that disagreement there is no meaningful sample size or success threshold to assign. Surprise, complexity, transferable intervention signatures and self-maintenance are not by themselves unique evidence for the stronger ontology.

**Possible outcome.** A genuinely different prediction would permit a future test with its own causal assumptions. If the accounts make identical predictions, retain them as interpretations: a null effect in an ordinary repair simulation cannot refute the metaphysics, and a positive repair effect cannot establish it.

**Work / lane.** Manuscript/theory discussion only. Keep the Wolfram–Levin framing productive through the operational questions above without treating more runs of a closed simulation as an ontological adjudication.

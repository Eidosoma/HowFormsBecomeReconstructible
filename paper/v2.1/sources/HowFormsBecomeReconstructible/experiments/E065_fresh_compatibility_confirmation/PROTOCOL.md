## Frozen primary confirmation family

Average each slot over founders/recipients within a block, then equally over
the two original histories. Let N=FF, B=FO, C=earlier-C-only, S=earlier-S-only.
Three pooled success contrasts are oriented so the predicted direction is positive:

1. Earlier-sign benefit at earlier magnitudes: **OO − FO** (E062).
2. Sign-by-magnitude interaction: **(OO − FO) − (OF − FF)** (E062).
3. Excess loss from substituting both magnitude groups:
   **(C − N) + (S − N) − (B − N) = C + S − B − N** (negative of E064's

95% family coverage; bootstrap coverage is approximate. Strict lower bound
above zero is positive; strict upper bound below zero negative; touching zero
unresolved. All three positive plus passing controls gives

each contrast even if the conjunction fails. Technical runs suppress scientific

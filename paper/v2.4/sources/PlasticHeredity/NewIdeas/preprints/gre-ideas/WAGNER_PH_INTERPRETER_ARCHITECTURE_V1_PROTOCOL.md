# Wagner PH Interpreter Architecture v1

Frozen: 2026-08-30 UTC

Status: prospectively frozen, exploratory develop-then-confirm protocol.

## Question and evidence boundary

Interpreter Resilience v1 preserved causal plastic-heredity information but no
candidate passed every resilience gate. Four-generation selection evolved mean
global repair near 0.8 and retained the carrier in 45.8% of distance-four
descendants, yet repair-off explained only 10.4 percentage points. Ten of 100
regulatory edges accounted for 47.3% of measured damage. This campaign asks
whether the near-miss came from ongoing repair, evolved topology, or their
interaction, and whether outcome-only multi-horizon selection can distribute
the Wagner interpreter's function.

Development reuses the 12 sealed task clusters. Six arms form a single versus
repairable interpreter by ordinary, uniformly stressed, or cross-fitted
atlas-guided challenge factorial. A seventh, coordinate-wise median-of-three
interpreter is an explicit engineered error-correction control. It cannot
support an ordinary-Wagner claim. No carrier information, donor identity,
intervention result, atlas damage value, or plastic-heredity label enters
fitness.

## Stage 1: cross-fitted prediction and causal decomposition

Reset rows from the sealed Interpreter-Depth atlas train a deterministic ridge
model of `max(0, -delta_information_high)`. Continuous predictors are within-root
ranks of absolute weight, source absolute output, and target absolute input.
Binary predictors mark self edges and carrier-locus endpoints. Fixed pairwise
interactions are included and the ridge penalty is 1.0. Development predictions
leave the complete target task out; predictions for untouched tasks fit all 12
historical tasks. The predicted ten highest-risk edges are archived. Predictor
quality is reported but never used to cancel a registered arm.

The selected `individual_reset` and `lineage4_repair` interpreters receive a
paired 2x2 transplant: ordinary versus repair-evolved topology crossed with
repair off versus the task's evolved repair value. The primary analysis holds
the repair-evolved carrier fixed; a sensitivity analysis holds the ordinary
carrier fixed. Common mutations and assays run to distances one, two, and four.

The same roots compare arithmetic-mean triplication, coordinate-wise median
triplication, and median triplication under concerted copy mutation. This is a
mechanistic sufficiency test, not evolution or confirmation evidence.

## Stage 2: seven-arm evolution

Each development arm starts from all 16 members of each sealed final
`lineage4_repair` population. Effective rulebooks and carriers are identical
across forks; repair values are retained only in repair forks, and median forks
start with three identical copies. Populations evolve for 24 generations with
two elites and tournament size three.

| Arm | Architecture | Virtual mutation challenge |
|---|---|---|
| `mh_single_ordinary` | one ordinary interpreter | ordinary reset law |
| `mh_single_uniform` | one ordinary interpreter | one uniformly forced reset |
| `mh_single_guided` | one ordinary interpreter | one reset among predicted top ten |
| `mh_repair_ordinary` | evolvable global repair | ordinary reset law |
| `mh_repair_uniform` | evolvable global repair | one uniformly forced reset |
| `mh_repair_guided` | evolvable global repair | one reset among predicted top ten |
| `mh_median3_ordinary` | three-copy coordinate median | ordinary independent copy mutations |

Uniform and guided challenges affect only virtual descendants used to score a
parent. Actual reproduction always uses the ordinary mutation law appropriate
to its architecture. Four shared trajectories provide immediate offspring and
distance-four terminals. Fitness is the arithmetic mean of focal performance,
the worst two immediate performances, and the worst two terminal performances,
minus ordinary carrier and architecture costs. Repair costs `0.04*r`; median
triplication costs 0.04.

## Stage 3: causal audit and pedigree

Every selected population receives the unchanged eight-intervention causal PH
audit at persistence 0, 0.5, 0.9, and 1.0, crossed with zero and 25% pre-cue.
Two roots per task generate four ordinary-mutation chains through distance four.
Repair arms also receive repair-off chains. Median arms receive arithmetic-mean
decoder and concerted-mutation chains. All common-stream coordinates, complete
genomes, edge rankings, mutations, and ancestry are archived for exact replay.

Core gates retain the established eight causal checks, retention means of 0.70,
0.55, and 0.35 at distances one, two, and four, a distance-one lower bound above
0.50, positive task-centered parent/offspring slope, information advantage at
least 0.08 with positive lower bound, distance-one and distance-four advantages
of 0.30 and 0.20 with positive lower bounds, and performance loss at most 0.05.
Information must stay positive at every distance and distance-four information
must be at least half distance-one information; exact monotone ordering is not
required.

Mechanism attribution is separate. Repair-off must reduce distance-four
retention by 0.15, guided selection must beat its uniform same-architecture
control by 0.10, and median decoding must beat both arithmetic mean and
concerted mutation by 0.15; each paired lower bound must exceed zero.

## Mandatory fresh confirmation

Development always freezes two candidates before any fresh task is generated.
Candidate A is the strongest single-interpreter arm. Candidate B is the
strongest repair or median arm. Within each family, ranking uses the number of
core gates passed, then distance-four retention lower bound, distance-one lower
bound, information advantage, and frozen arm order.

Sixteen untouched tasks receive a shared 30-generation ordinary burn-in. The
complete ancestor populations fork into both candidates and deduplicated focal,
ordinary/uniform, repair, or arithmetic-mean controls required by their
architectures. Every audit, pedigree, transplant, and mechanism contrast is
repeated unchanged. Development and confirmation estimates are never pooled.

## Runtime, integrity, and claims

The workflow is CPU-only, deterministic across worker counts, atomically
resumable, and capped at four workers. The cumulative hard wall is 28,800
seconds and the soft wall is 27,600 seconds. Stage reservations are 2,400,
7,200, 4,200, 300, 7,200, 4,200, and 900 seconds, leaving 1,200 seconds of soft
contingency. Tasks, arms, and assays are never silently reduced at a boundary.

All inputs, model folds, protocols, kernels, shards, analyses, candidate
commitments, and seed families are checksummed. A result can establish an
ordinary simulated Wagner route, an engineered repair route, or engineered
error-correction sufficiency only in its registered tier. It cannot identify a
natural molecular carrier or prove plastic heredity in living cells.

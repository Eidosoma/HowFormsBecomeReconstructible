# Response to the first developmental review

**Received:** 13 September 2026, supplied in the conversation by Robert Bjarnason. **Revision:** working manuscript v0.6; local PDF remains preview v0.1. This document summarizes the supplied review and our decisions; it is not a verbatim transcript and has not been sent to anyone. Reviewer identity, tool use, and independence were not supplied and are not inferred.

## Assessment

We agree with the review's central diagnosis. The strongest separate paper concerns what keeps organization reconstructible, with acquired references, reciprocal repair, and reconstruction loss as its central cases. The earlier table's definition of plastic heredity was a substantive error: it silently changed the companion paper's episodic resemblance criterion into inheritance of acquired information. Correcting this is necessary even without further experiments.

We also agree that feedback, reconstructive memory, regeneration, and intervention-based causal comparisons have prior art. The defensible contribution is the specific body–record tests and their synthesis through explicit information routes and boundaries. We do not claim a general explanation of renewal success from contrasts between different architectures.

Two qualifications guide the revision. First, the ruliad and Levin's Platonic-space proposal remain useful interpretive contexts consistent with the author's stated purpose; they need not be removed. The experimental argument must remain assessable without either ontology, and the stronger independent activity of patterns remains open. Second, an operational active-form category can include simple controllers and some attractor-restoration mechanisms. Its utility lies in declaring and testing the causal contribution, not in introducing an unearned threshold of agency.

## Experimental priorities, not launched

The most promising next experiment is prospective prediction of information survival through a fixed record–body–new-record cycle. Develop and freeze a prediction using development histories; test on untouched multigeneration histories, including held-out pattern families. Compare protected copying, copying under matched degradation, and body-dependent rewriting, with reading/writing controls. Preserve a single native endpoint or explicitly separate coarse and fine distinctions.

A second experiment could test whether intervention signatures predict unseen responses better than appearance-based grouping. Content-specific body-to-record correction also remains unresolved after D074; a better intervention must preserve the relevant general conditions while changing the candidate corrective content. These suggestions are not frozen protocols and authorize no automatic simulations.

## Verification and publication boundary

The pre-revision document set is preserved in the v0.5 archive. The revision checks the companion's definitions and selected campaign descriptions, the four tissue reports/protocols, selected native update code, and saved summary/audit files used in the plots. Before public release, pin accessible evidence archives and exact implementation dependencies, complete missing Wagner provenance, and obtain final human approval of the manuscript. The local preview has not been uploaded, deposited, or assigned a new DOI.

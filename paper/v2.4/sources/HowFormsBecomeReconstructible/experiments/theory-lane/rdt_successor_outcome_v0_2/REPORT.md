# Fixed successor predictor: amendment 1 and runner qualification

The 304-block design is prepared and frozen for runner qualification. The lightweight qualification passed all 22 tests. Full-resolution native qualification was launched detached as `native_qualification_v1`; its result is pending. No scientific cohort or scientific continuation outcomes have been generated by this package.

Amendment 1 explicitly raises the previous 256-block cap to 304, uses the previously checked faster reader for measurements and continuations, and adds durable deterministic recovery. The previous feasibility failure remains a failure. Model coefficients, scaling, 32 successor probes, the retained-eight-probe comparator, TT008, both cue-noise regimes, the 18-rewrite horizon plus fresh terminal recipient, and all primary decision margins remain fixed.

The proposed cohort contains 19,456 records: all 64 records from each of 304 independent acquisition blocks. Both matched and independent cue-noise conditions give 38,912 record/regime outcomes. Every forecast and its raw measurement evidence must be committed across the entire cohort before the first scientific continuation. The two regimes share injury masks and base random draws; independent noise retains distinct birth and ongoing cue noise. Acquisition remains matched.

Four paired Brier contrasts are evaluated across acquisition blocks. Support requires the lower one-sided 97.5% limit to exceed 0.002 against TT008 and zero against the direct comparator, in both regimes. An upper limit at or below the relevant margin rejects that component; other cases remain unresolved. Joint support is an intersection-union claim. Calibration and expression/history strata are descriptive and cannot rescue a failed primary result. Incomplete execution produces no scientific verdict.

The 304-block count is a plug-in estimate from eight exposed development blocks after model-family and budget selection. The upper-variance sensitivity needs 960 blocks. Power under independent cue noise and joint power across all four comparisons remain unknown. This design is a bounded prospective comparison of a fixed ordinary predictor; it cannot by itself establish an RDT-specific mechanism or general law. The earlier failed predictor and variable-budget selector retain their verdicts.

The 22 passing tests include deliberately interrupted measurements, reuse of completed checkpoints, exact agreement with an uninterrupted run, rejection of changed evidence and duplicate commits, a closed continuation barrier for incomplete forecasts, strict task thresholds, block-level inference, empty strata, probability-one calibration, exclusive run locks and an unchanged deadline on explicit resume. Reduced-step fixtures were used for these integration tests.

Native qualification separately replays exposed legacy block 34000 through generation 14 and requires byte-identical archived packets, bodies and response means. It also executes the full production workflow on four synthetic packets spanning four history labels and both rules, under both noise regimes: full 32-probe measurements, cohort sealing, 18 rewrites, fresh terminal evaluation, flat-reader checks and separate arithmetic verification. Its forecast is 850 seconds and its hard cap is 1,800 seconds, on one CPU. The detached process passed the single process-liveness startup check; its durable execution status had not yet appeared at that check. No further automatic polling is scheduled.

The eventual scientific run is forecast at approximately 15.8 days on one CPU, including the declared cost allowance, with a 20-day deadline from its original launch. An explicit resume preserves that deadline and reuses only hash-valid completed units; partial attempts remain available. Full launch requires completed native qualification and fresh CPU/disk admission. Qualification does not launch the scientific run automatically.

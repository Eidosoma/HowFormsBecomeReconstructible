Registered 2026-09-19 before new scientific integrations. Authorized after P14
closeout. This bounded follow-up tests the continuing E tissue's contribution
to correction during turnover, then tests the actual replacement cells as donors.

Use the actual full 169-cell `withdrawal_2` endpoints of P14's sham arm, source
seed 301, for written-origin and ordinary-origin histories. Each resolution

geometry, background and subsequent exposure, not prior age or state vector.

This tests dependence on continuing background contact after correction, not

## Turnover and retention

At relative times 0, 20,000 and 40,000, replace positions 83,84,85 respectively
with one incoming M cell; evolve the entire sheet for 20,000 after each.
Only that column and ID change. Keep each assigned route throughout turnover.
Incoming states exactly reuse P14's three seed-401 protein perturbations and
M stock, paired across histories, carrier types and resolutions. These are
deliberate challenges, not fresh independent stochastic replicates.

At 60,000, remove the surroundings and take the three actual replacement cells
through two fully isolated 10,000-unit holds. All original central carriers are
gone. A successful turnover requires all three central cells E at every
replacement endpoint and at least 95% E occupancy per cell in each window's
last quarter, plus the same criterion in both isolated holds. No successful
subset is substituted. Record each window and retention separately.

The four actual/tissue branches (two histories × two resolutions) are exact
unchanged-condition replays of P14's replacement/isolation suffix. All raw
numerical arrays must equal that archive. They are controls, not new evidence
from independent sources. Stock-E and cut branches are new counterfactuals.

## Readout assay and controls

First run six fixed capacity cases: ordinary-E stock trios, three contact routes
(open, RNA blocked, donor–recipient boundary cut), and both resolutions.
Each trio contacts three fresh M cells in a 2×3 native depol sheet for 20,000,
with expression power 60, then all donors are removed and recipients undergo
two fully isolated 10,000-unit holds. This calibrates this exact readout.

After turnover, fork EVERY actual final replacement trio into the same three
readout routes and timings. Carry its full state and IDs; never reset a failed
trio to E or substitute successful cells. Recipients are unperturbed ordinary-M
stock with new IDs. Continue the source's logical clock (1,140,000–1,180,000).
All readouts run even after a scientific turnover failure. Such readout success
cannot retroactively repair failed earlier replacement windows.

Readout success requires all three initially M recipients to retain E at both
isolated endpoints and during each final quarter. Report donors at contact end
as well as recipients. This is one additional three-to-three handover, not a
repeated relay. P14 already isolated its replacement cells but never tested
their onward transmission.

## Registered interpretation

Report all raw outcomes and all coarse/fine pair comparisons. Primary contrasts
are tissue versus patch at fixed source/history, and patch versus patch_rna.
Classify each pair as both renew, tissue only, patch only or neither. A claim
of RNA-dependent patch renewal requires success with boundary cut alone and
failure with the additional RNA block at both resolutions.

Report the full chain (all turnover windows, isolated retention, open onward
transmission) separately. Control-qualified transmission also requires negative
matched onward RNA and cut readouts at both resolutions. Benchmark-supported
transmission additionally requires positive stock-E open readout and negative
stock-E RNA/cut readouts at both resolutions. If capacity does not qualify,
failed onward readouts cannot establish loss of donor competence.

The denominators are two preselected historical sources, each crossed with a
paired stock-E substitution and three routes. Counterfactual branches and fine
runs are not additional lineages. Positive stock-E results constrain any claim
of ancestry-specific advantage. Results test finite native-state renewal under
external turnover and contact schedules, not autonomous reproduction, cell
division, new attractors, physical heredity or therapeutic efficacy.

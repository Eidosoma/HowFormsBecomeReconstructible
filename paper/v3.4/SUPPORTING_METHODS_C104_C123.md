### C.115. Repeating a controller without recurring injury: E2E002

E2E002 restores the same sixteen AR005 development blocks as C.114, with new addressed successor draws and one future per condition. It compares vms acting every generation with vms acting only in generation zero. Successors now use the AR tissue's two-record vote rather than E2E001's body-writing rule; comparisons between those studies alone are therefore not isolated controller effects. Each generation lasts 8,000 updates and only the first record's values and bond signs cross the handoff. [E160]

At birth, a fresh body is initialized, strengths are reset, a second record is copied from the incoming one and cue values are taken from that incoming record at the supplied anchor sites. None of those newly made stores adds an independent ancestral history. The cue, second record and controller state are discarded at the next handoff. A separately reported lineage-cue arm retains generation zero's cue and therefore adds an inherited channel. Policies share paired births and noise. No new localized injury occurs after generation zero. Endpoints and block intervals follow C.114.

### C.116. Successor writing rules, redundancy and inherited errors: E2E004

E2E004 directly compares successor rules on the same sixteen exposed AR005 blocks, with paired births/noise and one newly addressed future through eight renewals. No controller acts. Record-led updating, body-led writing, the two-copy vote and a vote without its second copy share the declared first-record handoff. The second copy, when used, is rebuilt locally from the incoming record, not inherited as a separate packet. The rule operates in each 8,000-update newborn. Record-led updating relearns the record's own relations with parity repair; body-led writing learns body coactivity. Voting uses the majority of the body and two record copies. In the one-copy ablation, the second copy is held at zero and abstains; a body–record tie leaves the contested bond unchanged. [E161]

Two tasks are scored separately: q_r on lineages started from the pre-injury record measures keeping history; u_r in the initially flipped patch on post-damage lineages measures healing. This is not one common success rate or an information-theoretic frontier. After eight renewals:

|---|---:|---:|
| Record-led | 0.998352 | 0.002441 |
| Two-copy vote | 0.757118 | 0.171143 |
| Body-led | 0.530945 | 0.500854 |

### C.117. Repeated tissue injury and correction through renewal: E2E003

E2E003 restores sixteen exposed AR006 development blocks, a different source cohort from C.114–C.116, and runs one future per condition through eight renewals. It uses C.115's newborn machinery with C.113's confirmed selective detector vtol. Arms receive repeated control, control in generation zero only, fixed voting, or no injury; vms is an additional misread-cue comparator. The qualification of vtol within one body does not confer fresh-confirmation status on these serial outcomes. [E162]

In each generation, the incoming first record supplies a fresh second copy and cue before a new 4 × 4 injury is applied to the first record only. Its values flip within the patch and its bond signs/strengths flip on patch-boundary edges. The second copy and cue retain the pre-injury information; this is not simultaneous damage to every store. Patches are disjoint from all earlier injuries, preventing a later flip from undoing an earlier one. New body initialization, cue placement, cue misreading and controller rules remain supplied. Only the first record's values and bond signs cross the next boundary; errors already present at birth can be reproduced in both the backup and cue. The evaluator's original target and true patch schedule are not detector inputs.

U is orientation-relative record agreement over the union of generation zero's patch and all subsequent patches; by generation eight it covers nine disjoint patches. q_r is whole-sheet historical specificity. Agreement on the newest patch is also reported. Each newborn runs 8,000 updates. Sixteen blocks, with nested unit–body pairs and cases averaged within them, supply the intervals defined in C.113. Main-text table values are the retained pooled generation-eight levels.

These are continuous agreement/specificity endpoints, not terminal or uninterrupted success rates.

### C.119. Reconstruction through a shared diffusible field: SM007

SM007 restores sixteen exposed AR005 blocks. It tests four unit–body pairs, two futures and 58 arrangements of sheets reading fixed inherited records for 8,000 updates. No record is rewritten and no new injury is applied. Reading gain is reduced from 0.2 to 0.05. The medium obeys $\partial_t c=D\nabla^2c-\gamma c+e$, with decay γ = 0.0625 and baseline decay length $\lambda=\sqrt{D/\gamma}=0.5$ in the field's lattice units. Absorbing, half-leaking and sealed boundaries are compared. Each sheet emits its mean body–record mismatch and reads at gain $0.05(1+\kappa x)$, where x is its exposure from the preceding update and κ = 1.5176 is fixed by an engineering calibration. The response is unbounded; sealed groups can exceed the undisturbed gain. [E164]

The endpoint a is whole-sheet sign agreement, allowing global sign inversion, with a ≥ 0.95 separately called a rescued sheet. Those counts are not biological survival. The compact 2 × 4 group exceeds an elongated 1 × 8 group by **0.033316 [0.030939, 0.035692]**; sealing the elongated group adds **0.082015 [0.076545, 0.087485]**. Shared versus isolated sheets gains 0.071117. Sealed shapes differ by −0.000028, within the frozen ±0.005 band. Uniform exposure and rearranged-exposure controls change little, supporting a role for retained exposure rather than its detailed placement in these groups.

The effective extent is $L_{\mathrm{eff}}=1/\sqrt{\mu_1}$, where μ₁ is the smallest eigenvalue of the negative discrete Laplacian on the absorbing domain. A monotone pilot-fitted curve of $L_{\mathrm{eff}}/\lambda$ on absorbing arrangements predicts held-out shapes and a changed decay length with mean absolute errors 0.016891 and 0.017013. It outperforms headcount, but ties a curve based on steady field retention. Nine frozen development statements hold; this does not distinguish a new scaling law from that conventional field description.

The experiment was inspired by Manicka and Levin's *A topoietic model of collective morphogenesis and compartmentalization*. Its main stress signal and reader response are supplied adaptations to this tissue model, not an embryo-model replication or a mapping to ATP concentrations. A separate reported arm uses the lane's source-inspired, body-independent bistable stress equation. It does not supply evidence that mismatch is required for every geometry effect. [Manicka and Levin, 2026](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.11.03.763900). [Report](sources/v3.4-multiscale/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/multiscale-error-lane/REPORT_SM007.md), [protocol](sources/v3.4-multiscale/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/multiscale-error-lane/REGISTRATION_SM007.md), [field and geometry implementation](sources/v3.4-multiscale/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/multiscale-error-lane/sm007/sim.py), [results](sources/v3.4-multiscale/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/multiscale-error-lane/results/sm007/predictions_scored.json).

### C.120. A shared response under recurrent record injury: E2E006

### C.123. Response direction crossed with the damaged store: E2E007

E2E007 restores C.120's sixteen exposed AR006 blocks, eight unit–body pairs and one future through eight renewals. Paired rows cross no field, stronger reading $g(1+\kappa x)$, and weaker reading $g/(1+\kappa x)$ with two injuries. Record injury flips the scheduled 4 × 4 patch at birth, keeping its bonds consistent. Body injury flips V and its moving average in the same patch at update 2,000, leaving the records intact. All rows share the restored generation-zero packets; the crossed injury treatments apply in subsequent renewals. The ordinary vote operates in each 8,000-update newborn. The medium and κ = 1.944 are inherited from the frozen E2E006 implementation, not fitted to these outcomes. A separately calibrated weaker-reading row uses κ′ = 1.400 to approximately match realized response size under record injury; it is a reported control. Only the outgoing first record enters the next body; the field is reset. [E168]

Under record injury, weaker reading improves generation-eight U_v by **0.213182 [0.203728, 0.222636]**, q_r by **0.115044 [0.107868, 0.122219]**, and a field-free fresh recipient's U_v by **0.229282 [0.218890, 0.239674]** over no response. That recipient reads for one generation with ordinary voting and no new injury. The size-matched control retains most of the gains: approximately 0.183, 0.092 and 0.178. Its realized integrated response is 0.974–1.036 of the stronger-reading response across generations when pooled, but is less closely matched by body type. The native scores and fresh-recipient levels in the main table come from this cohort alone; earlier cohorts are not pooled.

Under body injury, stronger reading changes terminal U_v by **−0.020270 [−0.025312, −0.015229]**, while weaker reading changes it by **−0.053324 [−0.059024, −0.047624]**. Without an added response the body is already within 0.003 of pre-flip body–record patch agreement by update 2,100. Faster recovery therefore does not imply a lasting endpoint gain. Weaker reading still improves record specificity by about 0.054. The positive stronger-reading injury interaction reflects reduced harm, not a positive effect under body injury, and is absent in the nonpreferred-pattern subgroup.

The body–record observer supports the proposed route: before update 2,000 under record injury, patch agreement with the injured record is about 0.60 with weaker reading, 0.81 without response and 0.92 with stronger reading. This is agreement with the record, distinct from agreement with the acquired pattern. It is consistent with less injured content entering the body and subsequent vote; it is not a measured causal mediation fraction. Seven final frozen development criteria hold. Two body-injury directions were changed after pilot inspection; the result does not confirm their original predicted benefit. Longer-lasting body injury and endogenous choice of response direction are assigned to E2E008/E2E009, whose implementations/plans provide no completed outcome at this intake.

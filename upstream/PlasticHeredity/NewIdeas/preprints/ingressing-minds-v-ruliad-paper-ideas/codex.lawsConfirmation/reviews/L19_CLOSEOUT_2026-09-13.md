## Bottom line in plain language

A simulated tissue can remember a pattern better when it has a readable
record protected from its own changing body. Scrambling that record's spatial
addresses damages the memory. A simple repair rule helps recover the broad
shape, but can smooth away fine details. Some detail-specific signal survives
and can influence a fresh body; the small advantage of nearby over outer
record content also survives fresh parents.

That is a useful, tested mechanism. It does **not** establish an indestructible
record, faithful arbitrary anatomy, or that genomes are the only possible
way to preserve a pattern. We now park this L19 simulation branch rather
than repeat the halo assay for a larger score.

## What the causal controls add

**Instruction and balance, D101–D102.** Withdrawing correct instruction
reduces founder retention; restoring it rescues retention, and a shifted
teacher steers toward its own pattern. High parent-to-child resemblance can
coexist with loss of the founder. The target-blind mean controller corrects
bulk voltage drift, but its regrown-region primary is uncertain. Its positive
early whole-sheet secondary effect remains evidence that balance can help.
Correct spatial instruction still exceeds the tested mean-only conditions.
This does not show that every possible balance controller fails, or identify
a dose-matched fraction of the teacher effect.

**Protection and copy integrity, D103–D104.** Holding the actual trained R
improves retention under matched balance support. Correct versus shifted R
and exact reader-off aliases establish content- and access-dependent effects.
Holding R prevents body-driven rewriting by construction; it does not make
R immune to damage. Address swaps preserve the value multiset, mean and RMS
but reduce retention. A declared external backup partly rescues the damage;
that is not autonomous repair or renewable transmission.

**Repair versus detail, D105–D106.** A fixed local median uses only the
current R, without a pristine backup. It helps coarse recovery, and shifted
records retain their own broad direction. The smoothing prior is supplied,
not learned; it changes amplitude as well as spatial structure. Deliberately
taught landmarks qualify in a separate cohort before the D106 main test.
Nevertheless, the main detail-repair primary remains unresolved. Prespecified
secondary coarse recovery improves by **13.23 [9.78,16.99]** points, while
repairing an otherwise clean record reduces landmark specificity by
**2.34 [0.98,3.91]** points. This is not faithful detail correction, but
neither is it total erasure: a small reader-mediated signal survives.

**Reading and location, D107–D109.** Transferring final R into common fresh
bodies shows that the surviving signal is not solely an old-body V/EMA
history effect. The fixed near-ring advantage repeats with new parents in
D109, with five positive and three tied tissue effects and no negative
tissues. However, the D107 inside-versus-outside primary remains unresolved;
D109's positive corresponding secondary does not retroactively pass it.
Outer effects unresolved at the final endpoint do not mean absence: D108
detects an earlier outer effect, while D109 does not. Keep both results.
Unequal region areas and factorial interactions prevent density, exclusive
location, long-range communication or additive information-fraction claims.

D109's repaired intact own-detail correctness is only **44.73%**, versus
42.19% for the wrong target. The content-specific gate is not a high-fidelity
reconstruction gate, nor a guaranteed benefit over reader-off on every
absolute score. There is no imported 80% criterion or automatic 50%-chance
interpretation in these assays.

## Keep the heredity standards separate

The sheet series uses a **2048-byte float64 R**, with native theta/physics
declared and body fragments retained in the source lineage. D107–D109 fresh
reading is not a chain of fresh descendants that each write the sole record
passed onward. It does not demonstrate the complete renewable-carrier cycle
of writing, bottleneck transmission, descendant reading and descendant
renewal under hard resets.

That is a limit of this series, not an assertion that no renewable carrier
exists elsewhere in the repository. The [L20 record](../laws/L20_self_written_genome.md)

also distinguishes engineered renewable CA/Wagner carriers from one-generation
readout. Those results cannot be transferred into genome necessity, learned
ontology, useful inherited learning disposition, or the sheet's fine-anatomy
claim without the corresponding tests.

## Proposed replacement wording

**Readable, maintainable references can support pattern memory.**

In this engineered voltage-sheet model, correct spatial instruction and
protection of an actual written reference improve retention of a fixed
founder pattern. Spatial copying errors reduce retention even when the
record's value distribution is unchanged. A supplied local repair rule can
recover coarse structure while losing detail. Some repaired content remains
readable in fresh bodies, with a small near-region advantage reproduced in
fresh parents. These are bounded sufficient mechanisms and demonstrated
limitations, not an incorruptible-reference requirement, a unique genome
architecture, or an explanation of biological origins.

This recommendation does not edit the source law or retrospectively amend
any frozen experiment.

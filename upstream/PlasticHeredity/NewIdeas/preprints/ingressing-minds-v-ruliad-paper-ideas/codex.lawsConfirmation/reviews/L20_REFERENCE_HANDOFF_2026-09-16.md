## Result in plain terms

Removing the calculable leftover from random birth did not produce a clear
overall improvement in the next readable record. Both the unchanged and
altered records still carry useful pattern information. This is an unresolved
small effect, not a failed instrument, proof of zero effect, or equivalence.
The weaker B pattern has not been convincingly rescued by this hand-off.

The effect on A is small and directionally positive; B remains uncertain.
Natural removal without strength restoration is also unresolved overall.
There is a small secondary pattern benefit at H's own mean/std, but that
does not replace the frozen primary or establish a repaired serial lineage.

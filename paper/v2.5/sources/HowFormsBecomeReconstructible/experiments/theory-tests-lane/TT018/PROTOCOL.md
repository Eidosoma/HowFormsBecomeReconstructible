# TT018 restored records under independent serial futures

TT018 estimates how reliably a fixed outgoing record supports continued
reconstruction, and how repair changes that reliability. This final prelaunch
protocol was prepared on 12 October 2026 after native qualification and before
TT017 outcome review. It is registered only when REGISTRATION.json binds this
file, the successful qualification and all executable inputs. No new cohort
may be generated before that registration. TT017's completed technical closeout
and evidence interpretation remain prerequisites to registration. Its direction
does not select TT018's condition, sampling rule, endpoints, policies or margin.

## Question and current evidence

Plastic Heredity v5 restores states and samples independent stochastic futures
to estimate finite-horizon event probabilities. TT014 samples eight one-read
futures per outgoing record. TT016 establishes an average serial-repair benefit
on a fresh cohort, using one continuation per record and policy. TT017 tests
birth/reset pairing; its outcomes were not inspected when this protocol was
prepared. These studies
leave the distribution of serial futures from a fixed record unresolved.

The completed P08 warning pilot has a negative primary difference from periodic
repair, -0.78 percentage points. Its separate 132-block efficiency confirmation
leaves the doubled-efficiency criterion unresolved. Their saved endpoint tables
are checked in [SOURCE_REVIEW.json](SOURCE_REVIEW.json); this lane has not replayed
their full dynamics. Those results inform development and do not select a new
warning or budget policy for TT018. Compare untreated, one-time repair and the
unchanged repeated rule. A later timing test requires equal repair budgets and
separate accounting for diagnostic computation.

## Sampling and restoration

Generate 64 genuinely new blocks, with 16 per existing history: exact writing;
a tolerance pulse at generation 3; pulses at 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13; and pulses at
3, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13. Teaching and the first fourteen rebuilds retain the qualified
TT016/TT017 acquisition contract. The tolerance is 1e-4 only at those prescribed
pulses. Acquisition generates and retains all sixteen candidate records per
block, including failed bodies.

The proposed identities are integers 18000000 through 18000063, with acquisition
namespace `TT018_serial_futures_v1_acquisition` and continuation record namespace
`TT018_serial_futures_v1_record`. Registration requires the bounded accessible
metadata/code collision search in register.py and a retained RESERVATION.json.
The identities are not considered reserved merely by appearing in this protocol.
Archived and excluded qualification inputs use separate identities and cannot
enter the new cohort. The search does not provide independent custody or technical
blinding in the shared workspace.

Before teaching, assign block offset i to history i modulo 4 and case floor(i/4).
Each history therefore includes each of the sixteen founder/recipient positions
once. Enroll that one preassigned generation-14 outgoing record per block,
irrespective of its current pass/fail status, risk or packet content. This yields
64 enrolled records, rather than 1,024 enrolled records. The balanced case rule
targets the equal mixture of histories and case positions while allocating work
to repeated futures. It does not guarantee a particular number of failing
bodies; empty descriptive subgroups remain empty. No outcome-based substitution,
deduplication, quota repair or additional enrollment is allowed.

Seal all 64 starting records, case assignments, current expression labels and
192 forecasts before the first continuation. Preserve original packet bytes and
the acquisition receipts. Restore the same packet separately for 64 independent
future environments. Within each environment fork three policies. Only the
packet crosses subsequent replacement boundaries; the body is reset. Preserved
acquisition bodies, injury masks and task labels may support auditing and the
declared forecasting inputs, but are not extra repair inputs or inherited states.

This defines probabilities conditional on the fixed starting packet, supplied
task, reader, writer, continuation policy, numerical channel and disturbance law.
It does not estimate capacity independent of those conditions or establish
physically identical whole-body state restoration across the two programmes.

## Policies, channel and random sources

Untreated continuation makes no repair calls. One-time repair acts at generation
15. Repeated repair acts at every generation 15 through 32. All branches continue
after failure and inherit their own actual body-written successor. The fixed
O-only rule, sixteen-start optimizer, sixty-four-sweep maximum and seed 2026101004
remain unchanged. The rule fills exactly zero edges using inferred signs and the
median absolute surviving weight, preserves every nonzero weight and leaves an
empty record empty. Lattice and input pins remain supplied.

Each record is exactly 4,096 bytes: 512 little-endian IEEE-754 binary64 weights.
Use the original exact-zero writer during continuation, with finite binary64
arithmetic and unchanged update order. The allocated 32,768 bits are not an
entropy, compression or physical energy estimate. Any precision change would
require a different declared experiment.

New reader bodies have independently drawn positive- and negative-cue resets,
and ongoing cue noise is independent across cue episodes. Each fresh injury
removes 24 uniformly sampled non-port cells without replacement from the 16 by 16
lattice. Birth standard deviation .05, noise .02, dt .2, 2,048 reader updates,
1,024 settling updates and the last 32 observed port values remain unchanged.
Acquisition keeps its original pairing; the independent-reset condition begins
at the restored boundary. Independent resets remain the specified target
regardless of the direction of TT017's result; a numerical failure would require
correction and renewed qualification.

Separate namespaces identify record, future, generation, source and cue.
Sources are injury, reset, ongoing forcing, terminal injury, terminal reset and
terminal forcing. One injury mask is shared by both cue episodes of a recipient;
resets and ongoing forcing have separate cue streams. Policy is absent from
environmental keys: corresponding policy
branches share realized challenges within a future. Different futures, cues and
terminal versus continuation sources have separate streams. Do not restore an
identical RNG state for purportedly independent futures. Repair solver randomness
is fixed, separately supplied apparatus. Check pairing directly in saved arrays;
hash uniqueness alone does not establish statistical independence.

The qualified key payload is the compact JSON array
`["TT018_DRAFT_STREAM_CONTRACT_V1", record_id, future, generation, source, cue]`.
The historical word DRAFT in this literal is retained to preserve qualified
streams. SHA-256 supplies the first sixteen digest bytes, interpreted as an
unsigned little-endian integer, to NumPy PCG64. Injury keys have a null cue;
reset and forcing keys have cue 0 or 1. Futures are numbered 0 through 63.
Terminal sources use generation 32 with their distinct `terminal_` source prefix.
These are separately initialized pseudorandom streams under the declared law.
Paired policies consume the same realized environment; batching sixteen futures
at a time does not change a future's draws or outcomes.

## Distinct endpoints

The primary endpoint is intended-task success when the generation-32
outgoing record is read in one additional fresh recipient. Fresh injury, reset
and forcing are independent of the preceding chain and paired across policies
within the same future. The terminal assay applies no additional repair and
writes no successor. It assesses the transmitted record after the last body has
been discarded. It is distinct from success in the generation-32 body.

Separately record uninterrupted body success at every generation 15 through 32,
generation-32 body success, generation curves, recovery and opposite-task
expression. A reconstruction passes only when the minimum task-aligned margin
over both cues and output ports is strictly greater than .25, using the final
32 observations. A later success cannot remove an earlier uninterrupted failure.
Technical failures stop and preserve the run; they do not silently remove
records, replace futures or become successful biological recoveries.

Each starting record has 64 Bernoulli future outcomes per policy for terminal
record usefulness, and a separate 64-outcome uninterrupted-performance target.
The terminal probability averages over both descendant-writing randomness and
the final fresh reading. One assay of each descendant does not estimate that
particular descendant's own conditional readout reliability. Keep every actual
descendant packet for subsequent explicitly classified work.

## Estimation and forecasting

Report each record's success fraction and exact binomial pointwise 95% interval.
Also provide simultaneous intervals using alpha=.05/(64*3*2) across all record,
policy and the two principal endpoint combinations. Preserve zero- and all-success
uncertainty. At 32/64 successes the pointwise interval is [37.23%, 62.77%]; at
64/64 it is [94.40%, 100%]. Thus 64 futures improve measurement substantially but
do not supply precise probabilities for every record or certainty after no
observed failures. Paired record-specific repair effects retain rescues and
harms, with conservative intervals from jointly bounded discordant probabilities.

The single practical primary is repeated minus untreated terminal-record
success. Average the 64 paired future differences within each record/block and
then equally across the four history strata. Require a one-sided approximate
95% stratified Welch-Satterthwaite lower bound strictly above .02. This
two-point margin is an operational benefit requirement for the extra
work, not a consequence of earlier favorable outcomes or a biological constant.
Keep two-sided intervals, approximate-coverage limits and a conservative
zero-variance fallback. The 4,096 future environments and 12,288 policy chains
do not replace the 64 independent population units. No terminal-body, subgroup
or forecasting result can rescue this primary. Record probabilities remain
estimable regardless of the practical-primary verdict.

For history h, let s_h squared be the sample variance of its sixteen record
contrasts and v_h=s_h squared/16. The mean is the equal average of the four history
means, its estimated variance is sum(v_h)/16, and the reference degrees of freedom
are sum(v_h) squared divided by sum(v_h squared/15). Use the .95 Student-t quantile
for the one-sided primary bound and .975 for the two-sided interval. If estimated
variance is zero, use the retained conservative bounded-contrast fallback, with
one-sided radius sqrt(2*log(20)/64) and two-sided radius sqrt(2*log(40)/64).
Bounds are clipped to [-1,1]. A lower bound above .02 is PASS; an upper one-sided
bound below .02 is UNSUPPORTED; otherwise the result is INCONCLUSIVE. No failed
practical primary changes the availability of the record probability estimates.

Freeze the unchanged TT008 candidate and its expression/history and
expression/history/unsigned rivals. All receive their original task-aligned
expression and history fields; the supplied task orientation and signed/unsigned
record diagnostics remain explicit. Predict untreated terminal fresh-recipient
success only. Independent cue resets/noise change the old continuation contract,
so this is a secondary transport test. There is no refitting, recalibration or
assumption that these forecasts predict repair benefit or repaired-policy risk.
Their scalar adapter reproduces all 12,288 archived predictions to 2.8e-16.
New boundary feature extraction is checked against 48 archived boundaries during
native qualification, including its separate indexed operator calculation.

Use per-record mean Bernoulli Brier loss across the 64 futures, then block-based
paired model comparisons. Include mean prediction minus outcome frequency and
fixed-bin calibration (ten equal-width bins; empty bins excluded). Report
calibration and ranking descriptively, alongside uncertainty and model cost;
do not promote them to alternate primary gates. The candidate's diagnostic work
includes 64 unsigned and 64 signed iterations; the unsigned rival needs 64
unsigned iterations. Shared implementation caching does not make the information
or computation budgets equal.

Assign futures 0-31 and 32-63 to halves before outcomes. Report split-half
probability agreement overall and within history, retaining negative covariance
estimates and undefined correlations. This examines resolvable state differences
without declaring a positive heterogeneity gate after seeing results. Both
currently passing and failing records remain in the primary population;
subgroup summaries and per-record repair differences are descriptive.

# Source availability — v2.4

The package brings forward the selected v2.2 evidence and adds E105–E116 from v2.4. It preserves earlier packages and qualification verdicts. The [inventory](source-inventory.json) identifies **2808 selected records**, of which **2791** fit the 256 KiB limit. Frozen records are byte-identical to the writing inputs. Additional implementation files are explicitly labeled context, not extra experimental outcomes.

Code is grouped under `sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/` and `sources/PlasticHeredity/`. Protocols, implementation and compact results support review; this is not a complete portable execution closure. Some historical launchers use original absolute paths, require omitted arrays, or depend on external cellular models. Large run archives, NPZ arrays, source ZIPs and oversized metadata are omitted. Existing public source dependencies remain at their earlier paths. No full experiment or original statistical audit is rerun during packaging.

TT039's full run and the equal-read forecast's named full report/audit are absent in the working checkout. Their retained reports, completion records and protocols support the manuscript transcriptions; that availability limit remains. The multiscale material is development and does not become confirmation through export. Pending studies supply no outcome.

The evidence ledger and SI Markdown are split into small files with complete indexed anchors. Both full PDFs contain all their main/SI figures. The new PDF paths become accessible online after commit and push; local export checks do not assert live web availability.

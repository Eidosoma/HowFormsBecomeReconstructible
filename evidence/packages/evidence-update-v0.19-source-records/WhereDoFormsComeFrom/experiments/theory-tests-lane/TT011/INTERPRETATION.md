# TT011 checked task content and alignment findings

The independent first-stage audit passes. All 128 recipient/donor pairs have contradictory exact sign-alignment constraints on their surviving overlap, even before fixing input/output ports. Every one of the 256 published contradiction witnesses validates, and two independently implemented solvers agree. An exact-alignment performance experiment on these same pairs has no admissible inputs under this contract. This finite result does not rule out approximate alignment, different donors or task inheritance generally.

Reconstruction and scoring also pass for all 64 cases and 10,880 saved endpoints. The same-task donor advantage is +0.298 percentage points, with descriptive 95% interval −2.241 to +3.295. It passes the source's positive-point rule, but remains uncertain and small. Four blocks are positive, one tied and three negative. The separate opposite-task replacement contrast is −0.329 points (interval −0.658 to −0.071), failing the declared replacement direction. All 21,760 dual-task flags, five tables and numerical summaries reproduce. Loss of recipient-task performance does not establish donor-task replacement.

The original backup signs perform better than either donor's signs: common-own success is 26.45%, same-task donor signs 9.94% and opposite-task donor signs 9.64%, at matched support and magnitudes. Complete donor records retain their nominal task competence: 86.81% for the same-task donor and 83.15% for the opposite-task donor. These controls distinguish competence of an intact donor record from portability of its signs into another record. The ordinary signed-transport rival has higher mean reward than the task-label rule, but its paired reward-difference interval spans zero. The evidence does not establish a uniquely RDT mechanism or robust task-label transfer.

The mathematical checks distinguish a coordinate change from a physical sign intervention. Reader symmetry requires transformed reset/noise and fixed task ports; it does not preserve trajectories when common noise is reused unchanged. The original and tolerance fallback writers obey the corresponding coordinate transformation. A contradictory cycle cannot be fixed solely by changing internal node sign conventions. Free-bit counts of inconsistent equations are not counts of admissible repairs, and ambiguity dimensions are undefined for these infeasible pairs. The [derivation](DERIVATION.md) and exhaustive small-graph fixtures retain these distinctions.

This is exposed development evidence on eight blocks, with supplied donor/incoming access and a fixed reader. The 80-second audit checked all retained scores and mathematical certificates, plus synthetic dynamics; it did not independently replay all 10,880 P05 nonlinear trajectories. The [full report](runs/audit_v1/REPORT.md), [execution record](EXECUTION.md) and [closeout](CLOSEOUT.json) state that scope explicitly. P03, P04 and TT008 conclusions remain unchanged.

The next discriminating question is how to vary imported task content while controlling network compatibility. A coordinate relabeling that preserves task observables cannot itself test task replacement. Approximate alignment or a different intervention would be a new development specification, followed by fresh enrollment if its identifiability survives. No successor was launched.

despite recognition declining. YL correlation also rises, from 0.78995619 to
0.79188133. Residual parent correlation rises under both routes:
LL **0.54395818 to 0.56277994**, YL **0.54403020 to 0.56086197**.
Neither recognition nor correlation alone certifies complete shape retention.

The unchanged body-free snapshot writer still yields common recognition
**67.96875%** and residual recognition **86.328125%**. Its family-dependent
advantage/disadvantage remains; a universal body advantage is unsupported.
The full sister recording contains 1,001 fields, whereas that baseline has
one snapshot, so information capacity and donor-generation costs are not matched.
Reciprocal trace-writer equality is structural, not an empirical equivalence
finding. The current LL/YL contrast is recipient contingency with two bodies,
not presence versus absence of embodiment.

Protected-parent and supplied-positive controls reach 100% recognition;
disabled reading gives 25% own and partner recognition and zero preference.
Disabled writing preserves the incoming packet with or without the pulse.
All sixteen undisturbed donor traces were sealed before recipients and match
D193. Eighty unchanged paths and two hundred named reads reproduce the pinned
D193 panel. Only the declared 2,048-byte theta record enters each fresh reader.

The excluded pilot remains negative (preference interaction −3.125 common,
−0.78125 residual); it is neither erased nor averaged into this panel.
Opposite pilot/panel signs limit the claim of recurrence. L20/L22 remain
**STRESS_TESTED / MIXED**. This is not equivalence, autonomous interface repair,

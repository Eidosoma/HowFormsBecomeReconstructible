# Lessons carried into this lane

**Evidence baseline: manuscript v2.2, selected through 18 October 2026.** This design does not incorporate later lane outcomes or change any existing verdict. It uses lessons from all relevant branches without requiring every apparatus in the first experiment.

| Existing evidence | What it teaches | Consequence for this design |
|---|---|---|
| [Acquired-reference writing, D097–D099 / E08](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e08) | Exposure can alter a retained reference and later defense; the complete reversed-order benchmark remains failed. | Separate acquisition, sensing and response; do not assume a reference is autonomously selected or a detector evolved. |
| [Unprescribed capture / E31](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e31), [long renewal / E43](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e43) | Total agreement and own-history specificity differ. Qualified agreement combines sign and amplitude. The homogeneous pilot has a large history effect but poor formation. | Keep all founders; report sign, amplitude, their joint table, own/foreign and formation separately. Start with a supplied family, then test new families separately. |
| [Early body capture / E92](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e92) | Body organization can supply message-specific information absent from an incoming record. Continued updating does not establish superiority over a held snapshot. | Preserve body-source and snapshot controls; do not infer that a living trajectory is necessary from one repair benefit. |
| [Conditional correction / E100](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e100) | Corrected writing helps complete corruption, harms informative inputs, and misses its primary partial-corruption margin. Injury mask/fill information is supplied. | Damage body and record separately; withhold damage labels/masks from the controller. Test when not to rewrite. |
| [Snapshot routes / E101](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e101) | Snapshot and body routes have context-dependent effects in an exposed small panel. | Count protected information and include a held-snapshot alternative without upgrading it to fresh confirmation. |
| [Fresh pruning reversal / E98](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e98) | Immediate benefit can coincide with successor harm; compatible substitution changes the result. | Measure present and successor effects in paired cases, including harms. Never optimize only present mismatch. |
| [Compatibility / E94](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e94), [partial recovery / E95](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e95) | Record components can each be informative yet fail when mixed. Rewriting can recover some compatibility. | Test misplaced signals and actions, not only deletion. Avoid treating a plausible multiscale combination as automatically useful. |
| [Numerical regime / E69](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e69), [E70](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e70), [E75](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e75), [E76](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e76) | Exact-zero logic, normalization and relative magnitudes matter differently by regime; high performance does not erase failed numerical criteria. | CPU float64 first; qualify precision, noise, clipping and reduction changes independently. GPU agreement is measured rather than assumed. |
| [TT008 forecasts / E71](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e71), [predictive limits / E103](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e103) | Better probability forecasts do not guarantee better decisions; rich probes can fail to improve them. | Predict intervention benefit, compare strong fixed policies and charge all diagnostic computation. |
| [Paid checking / E104](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e104) | Better post-change response need not pass an economic criterion; clock information has value too. | Separate information value, timing and action opportunity; log stable-condition costs and harm. |
| [Repeated futures / E93](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e93) | Capacity can be estimated for exactly restored records; average gains coexist with harmed individuals. | ME003 restores complete records, samples independent futures and keeps inference at source blocks. |
| [Recipient-informed repair / E42](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e42), [reader intervention / E61](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e61) | Recipient conditions change usefulness. A different reader is not additional inherited information. | Fix the assay reader in primary contrasts; transfer and altered readers are separate conditions. |
| [Two-rule Hopfield renewal / E99](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e99) | Supplied guidance improves richer functional renewal, with protected copying still stronger. | Count guides, cues and task structure; do not call the controller's competence spontaneous. Transfer later tests more than a single polarity task. |
| [Cellular readiness / E65](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e65), [timing / E85](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e85), [P34 / E102](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e102) | Coarse labels conceal consequential internal states; component sufficiency and timing depend on location/history. | Inspect complete states and source location; do not claim one universal sensor or treat a scale label as a complete state. |
| [L17 losses / E12](../../EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e12) | Serial reference reconstruction can lose distinctions while protected copying preserves them. | Preserve copy baselines and instrument failures. Do not assume the new architecture will sustain sixteen renewals. |

## Biological motivation

Bioelectric states can participate in organizing and remembering form, alongside gene regulation, chemistry, mechanics and geometry. [Zhao et al. (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04925) connect wound electric signals to directional cell movement and molecular response pathways. [Tewari et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2018.11.004) show that disrupting a missing-tissue response need not abolish regeneration. Neither establishes an organism-wide target comparator or validates our dimensionless fields as biological stores. These are reasons to separate signal detection, action and outcome experimentally.

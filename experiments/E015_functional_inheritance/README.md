# E015 — inherited functional competence

Status: **bounded feasibility pilot complete and independently audited; reliable
routing was not established.** Neither of the two fixed gains met the full
criterion, so no setting was selected and the renewal phase was not started.
The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) defines a bounded signal-routing feasibility pilot,
with activity-written local connection records and explicit communication
controls. This is a new local tissue variant, with supplied teaching goals and
4096-byte edge records. E014's scalar-field apparatus remains a separate result.

Sixteen development blocks (64 founders) tested exactly two reader gains, with
all 21504 trials retained. Three short fixtures and both full independent audits
passed. The scientific run took 50.42 seconds; its audit took 43.64 seconds,
below the 252.59-second conservative forecast. At most 12 CPUs were used.

The criterion required a lower 95% bound above 75% for each taught rule and
damage condition, plus advantages over every control. All six absolute
reliability gates failed at each gain. Learned records nevertheless carried
useful task information: at gain 2, intact performance exceeded opposite-rule
records by 34.57 [26.56, 42.19] percentage points and input-isolated records by
19.14 [13.87, 24.80] points. This partial benefit does not qualify reliable
competence. These are developmental, pointwise intervals.

A separately labelled saved-output diagnostic found mostly wrong output signs,
not merely weak correct responses. At gain 2 without damage, 57.03% had both
correct signs when the margin was ignored, versus 49.80% passing the original
criterion; only 26.56% of matched founder/trial pairs answered both cues correctly.
No criterion changed and no extra simulation or candidate search followed.

A second saved-data diagnostic suggests a more specific lead: interference
between the two teaching episodes. Averaging their local correlations lost
2.79% of total absolute edge strength, with greater cancellation associated
with worse intact performance across 16 block means (Pearson r = -0.831).
This is an exploratory association, not a causal finding. A separate controlled
test of teaching consistency, with an engineered capacity control, is proposed;
it has not been launched.

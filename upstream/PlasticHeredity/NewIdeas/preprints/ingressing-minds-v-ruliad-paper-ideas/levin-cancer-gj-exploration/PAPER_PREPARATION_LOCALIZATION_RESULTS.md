## Interpretation and limits

The registered classification is **alternative sufficient routes** for both
founder histories. Neither prepared cell location is individually necessary
when the other carries its prepared state. This does not identify a unique
molecular carrier or establish that the two routes use the same mechanism.
Whole-cell columns include all 17 concentration variables, and later normal
coupling permits additional changes and interactions.

This is a fixed, externally scheduled, counterfactual reconstruction from one
archived source trajectory per founder/resolution. It does not establish
independent biological replication, cell division, autonomous reproduction or
a written-history advantage. Before-state columns were explicitly reanchored
to the common logical epoch without integration; the exact historical replay
controls passed.

The next mechanistic question is which components of each sufficient prepared
state matter. Reciprocal component erasure and rescue is a candidate next
panel; it has not been registered or launched.

adds a useful distinction: during the first challenge the prepared newcomer
is E at every saved sample, whereas the neighbour-only route shows a temporary
M label followed by recovery. This agrees across both histories/resolutions.
It refines the meaning of final rescue without changing the registered result.

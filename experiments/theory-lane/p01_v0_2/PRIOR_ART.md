# Prior art and alternatives for P01

This is a targeted overlap review, not an exhaustive priority search. The comparison column gives this lane's assessment of what P01 would still need to establish. Mathematical ingredients, evolved memory, semantic closure and mutual maintenance are not claimed as RDT discoveries. The [initial review record](../review_provenance_v0_1.json) identifies the primary sources already inspected. Access limitations below are retained rather than implying a full-text review where none occurred.

| Primary source | Relevant established or proposed contribution | Overlap and remaining P01 question | Inspection basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Michel & Siegle, Markov aggregation with transient error bounds (2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.07618v2) | Quantifies transient approximation errors and relates reduction to lumpability. | The operator/error-bound core belongs to this tradition. P01 must validate a small native representation and its measurement cost. | Primary full text, relevant definitions and bounds. |
| [Bittracher & Schütte, aggregation of undersampled Markov chains (2021)](https://publications.imp.fu-berlin.de/2614/1/1-s2.0-S016727891930764X-main.pdf) | Investigates low-dimensional structure and aggregation with limited transition data. | Low dimensionality alone does not establish a sufficient reconstruction state. Compare reductions without privileged hidden labels. | Primary PDF, relevant aggregation discussion. |
| [Shalizi & Crutchfield, computational mechanics (2001)](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9907176) | Predictive equivalence of histories motivates sufficient causal states and minimal predictive representations. | RDT cannot claim predictive state equivalence as new. The proposed two-mode restriction is an empirical approximation, not a proved native causal-state construction. | Primary paper record and authors' summary. |
| [Hopfield (1982)](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/w41x7-8bn13) | Collective dynamics implements content-addressable memory and error correction. | Attractor restoration is a necessary rival. P01 adds the question of continuation through rewriting, which still requires explicit testing. | Primary repository abstract; no replication. |
| [Dodd et al., epigenetic cell memory (2007)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0092867407004588) | Feedback among nucleosome modifications supports alternative persistent states despite noise and partition. | A conventional feedback-memory model may account for persistence. P01 cannot equate retention with new inherited competence. | Primary indexed abstract and repository metadata; full text not audited. |
| [Watson et al., developmental memory (2014)](https://www.richardawatson.com/_files/ugd/395d71_73b2fb6f638842949320c0dd26ccfd89.pdf) | Evolved developmental interactions can recall previously selected patterns and generalize. | Learned developmental memory predates RDT. A measured short-to-long prediction with strong comparators would be a narrower additional result. | Primary PDF. |
| [Buckley et al., natural induction (2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11431681/) | Slow structural changes interacting with fast relaxation can produce learning-like optimization in the studied systems. | Learned constraints and attractor restructuring are prior mechanisms. P01 must distinguish acquisition from later transmission. | Indexed primary passages; direct page access encountered CAPTCHA. |
| [Watson, Levin & Lewens, natural induction and selection (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsfs.2025.0025) | Examines the relationship between natural induction and selection. | A synthesis of learning and evolution needs explicit predictions beyond renaming these processes. P01 only tests a persistence reduction. | Indexed primary-source passages. |
| [McCourt, Fiete & Chuang, evolved error correction (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/7kcl-jshm), [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.14448v2) | Links error correction, neutral variation, evolvability and modular organization in its model. | Error-correction-enhanced evolvability is prior work. Terminal rule retention is insufficient to establish inherited adaptability. | Publication record and primary preprint. |
| [Pattee, semantic closure bibliography (1995)](https://casci.binghamton.edu/publications/pattee/bibliography.html), [author's 2001 treatment](https://casci.binghamton.edu/publications/pattee/pattee.html) | Relates symbolic descriptions to the material processes that interpret and maintain them. | Reader/record interdependence is prior theory. Fixed supplied machinery in P01 does not test autonomous semantic closure. | 1995 bibliographic entry; linked old full text unavailable. Relevant primary 2001 discussion inspected. |
| [Clark, Hickinbotham & Stepney (2017)](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/117175/1/01_06_2017_Semantic_c.pdf) | Studies evolving descriptions, copiers and constructors in a reproducing architecture. | Evolving interpreters and constructors are not new RDT claims. Machinery turnover belongs to later P07 work. | Primary PDF. |
| [Montévil & Mossio, closure of constraints (2015)](https://montevil.org/assets/pdf/2015-MM-Organisation-Closure-Constraints.pdf) | Formulates biological organization through mutually dependent constraints. | Mutual maintenance alone does not establish useful adaptation or predictive advantage. P01 presently fixes the apparatus. | Indexed primary-source text. |
| [Krakauer et al., information theory of individuality (2020)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12064-020-00313-7) | Develops an information-theoretic treatment of individuality. | Predictive information and causal boundary choices require explicit endpoints. A large temporal-information score need not forecast task performance. | Indexed primary-source text. |
| [Kolchinsky & Wolpert, semantic information (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsfs.2018.0041) | Connects information to its causal contribution to viability under a declared intervention scheme. | Task relevance is not supplied by mutual information alone. P01 uses prescribed rule performance, not demonstrated ecological viability. | Indexed primary-source passages. |
| [Taylor et al., open-ended evolution (2016)](https://www.tim-taylor.com/papers/taylor2016openended.pdf) | Surveys unresolved definitions and requirements for continuing evolutionary novelty and growth. | Finite retention of two prescribed rules cannot establish open-ended evolution. Scaling the task repertoire is a separate claim. | Primary author PDF, selected criteria and discussion. |

The closest model comparator for the actual equations in this package is a conventional four-state hidden Markov model with independent identity and expression components. Its forecasts are exactly identical. No literature comparison can turn that equivalence into mathematical novelty.

The closest empirical comparators are already in the supplied manuscripts: the Plastic Heredity composite initial-state/history predictor and strong basin/sensitivity comparison, and the Forms passive/diagnostic forecasts discussed around D162. Their reported performance motivates a demanding benchmark; it is not fresh support for the present law. The evidence ledger distinguishes these manuscript reports from the toy calculations actually reproduced here.

# A ladder for reconstructible forms in neural models

Proposed and user-approved on 11 October 2026. Implementation has begun.
Individual experiments require their own frozen protocols; the first forms
extension is now specified in [H002](h002/PROTOCOL.md). H001 remains exploratory
evidence. This ladder does not replace any experiment's decision rules.

Status on 12 October: H002 and H003 development are complete; [H004](h004/README.md)
confirmed the selected functional-renewal and timing effects on 72 fresh base
networks. [H005](h005/README.md) completed the record-model branch of rung 6:
matched present expression followed by new descendants under a common future
apparatus. Its positive four-point future difference falls below the frozen
ten-point minimum, so this study does not qualify. [H006](h006/README.md)
completed direct record perturbations while preserving strength and matching
current performance within one-point bounds. The selected four-edge dose
produced essentially equal terminal renewal rates; both planned durability
contrasts failed. A post-result inspection found over 99% exact packet
convergence to the unchanged branch after one replacement/write. These tests
have not established the record-model branch's proposed durability distinction.
Useful reciprocal repair remains a separate unresolved branch.

The latest record-model lead is to encode a changed hidden activity pattern
with the existing writer. Post-result diagnostics find that H005's coherent
hidden-pattern differences persist, whereas H006's small edge edits lie outside
the writer's exact output set and usually vanish at the first rewrite. The
relevant H005 subgroup still has a small present-performance gap and an
unqualified future effect. [H007](h007/README.md) passed its feasibility screen
on the 24 H003 development networks at all four doses. The nominated eight-pair
swap retained 244 current-matched packets, with success 97.67% versus 97.87%
and every one-point equivalence gate passing. Joint exact transmission of both
distinct, functional records through one damaged replacement reached 94.15%
(adjusted lower 92.65%); read/write blocks reduced that event to zero. Ten
fixtures and 7,011 audit checks passed. [H008](h008/README.md) attempted the
sixteen-replacement follow-up on the fixed 244 pairs, but fresh current
validation failed the declared equivalence bounds. Current success was
97.02% versus 97.67%; the success-difference interval reached 1.04 points and
two output intervals reached 1.62 points, outside the one-point limit.
No study futures were generated. All 15 fixtures and 896 audit checks passed.
H007 retains its one-replacement feasibility result; the selected cohort's
tight current equivalence did not reproduce on H008's fresh probes. The next
step is to diagnose that observer mismatch before specifying another
durability experiment. No fallback cohort, dose or relaxed bound was used.
See the preceding [diagnostic and limits](h006/README.md).

## Decision

Begin with a bounded, private replication package for the existing neural
experiments. Then test the forms claims through explicit translations into
Hopfield models, with fresh data and their own protocols. The immediate goal is
a defensible additional simulation layer for forms. Learning research and the
origin of inherited competence are a later horizon.

Use source-faithful reproduction to check the instruments, and model
translation to investigate generality. These answer different questions. A new
implementation is not independent evidence merely because the code is new;
replaying an old cohort is not a new cohort. Shared research direction and
prior exposure must remain explicit.

## Rung 0 — retain the current starting point

H001 supplies working binary and continuous implementations, causal controls,
raw evidence and integrity checks. It supports feasibility of historical
capture, finite functional renewal and a continuous readiness distinction. Its
useful reciprocal-repair contrast is unresolved; its tested timing rescue fails.

It does not reproduce the older neural protocols exactly. Preserve its
parameters, data and verdicts. Subsequent work gets a new protocol and new
output directory. The existing damaged-copy benefit cannot be substituted for
a demonstrated benefit over protected storage.

## Rung 1 — calibrate broken and restored resemblance

First privately reproduce a selected fixed-rulebook experiment under its own
declared dynamics and observer. Include multiple stable destinations,
single-destination repair, no repair, zero perturbation and an extreme
perturbation condition. Check raw resemblance, coherent continuation and
capture by a declared destination separately. Signed forms and their inverses
must remain distinguishable when the observer requires it.

Finish line: original execution and endpoint fixtures agree, and the original
claim is evaluated under its unchanged decision rules on the specified source
cohort. A later new-cohort replication is labeled separately. A reduced smoke
run checks engineering only and cannot inherit a full-cohort verdict.

Do not make the frequency of break-and-renewal a fitness measure: the event
requires a failure. This rung establishes an observer and a fixed-dynamics
baseline; it does not establish acquired information transmission.

State-based future prediction is an optional extension. It is not required
before checking acquired records. A failure of resemblance continuity also
does not logically rule out an engineered acquired carrier in another model.

## Rung 2 — reproduce acquired transmission and carrier repair

Privately reproduce the selected temporary-experience and damaged-carrier
experiments. Match their update schedules, boundary treatment, shared probes,
writer, loss law and observer. Use no-read, material reset, no-write, donor
transplant, organization shuffle and exact rescue controls as specified by
their source contracts.

The decisive questions are:

- Does experience change a later recipient after the experience and donor
  activity are removed?
- Does the identity and organization of the transmitted state determine the
  response?
- Can rewriting restore useful function under the declared loss, and does
  removing the reconstructed component remove that benefit?

Finish line: intact transmission, dependence on the carrier, and functional
reconstruction are evaluated as separate claims under the original gates.
Preserve negative outcomes. A change in record norm, resemblance, or
decodability cannot replace recipient function.

An original passive-decay comparator must remain in an exact replication.
Any added protected-copy comparison is an explicitly new diagnostic. Once
translation starts, include protected copying, copying under cumulative loss
and body-driven rewriting together, with their resource differences stated.

## Rung 3 — check the writer–reader boundary

Privately reproduce one bounded compact-carrier renewal assay with its frozen
writer/reader pair and its original positive/null controls. First establish
that the apparatus can express a known legal signal. Retain the original
scaffold as part of the machinery; do not silently remove it or describe it as
learned by the replication.

Measure donor expression, fresh-recipient function and outgoing-record
usefulness separately. Distinguish suppressing a reader, deleting material,
blocking a write and changing coordinates. A record may differ between
histories without carrying a useful instruction. Full donor state can serve
as a diagnostic ceiling only; it cannot count as carrier-only transmission.

Finish line: the selected original assay and its controls are reproduced or a
specific disagreement is localized. Reusing a frozen writer/reader checks
that pair. It does not replicate discovery, training repeatability or transfer
to another reader.

Full discovery searches, the complete sequence of unsuccessful carrier
designs, and multi-timescale selection are deferred. A failed-export case is
an optional diagnostic when a later forms experiment needs it; it is not a
required long campaign before starting the public model translations.

## Rung 4 — translate the forms boundary into Hopfield dynamics

This is the first main forms experiment after instrument calibration. Use an
activity model that is fully specified without a private checkpoint or learned
decoder. Separate two tasks: unprescribed pattern capture and an instructed
conditional function. Success at the latter cannot establish unprescribed
origin of competence.

Compare a writer allowed to retain incoming-record material with one that
receives only developed body activity and creates the outgoing record afresh.
The same initial records, births, perturbations, time budgets and endpoint
definitions should be paired wherever possible. Include own/foreign records,
read/write blocks, erased acquisition, protected copying and cumulative-loss
copying. Record identity and ordinary resemblance are different endpoints.

Use H001 as development evidence, then test a predeclared extension: multiple
acquired patterns or rules per record, reduced input support, or sparse
reciprocal connectivity. Start with one extension rather than a large combined
sweep. Count unique stored parameters, shared machinery and update cost.

Finish line: fresh recipients retain a declared history-specific effect after
complete activity replacement, and full rewriting satisfies its separate
route and horizon criteria on new base networks. Quantify attrition and the
protected-copy deficit. A partial-retention pass cannot qualify full rewriting.

## Rung 5 — test what organization contributes to repair

Translate the reciprocal-repair and writer-placement questions in the spatial
forms experiments. Hold the damaged record and the surviving body's value
distribution fixed while changing the arrangement or writer address. Cross
kept versus shuffled organization with fixed versus live reader feedback.
Evaluate only the repaired record in independently initialized recipients.

This rung must distinguish three contrasts:

1. Useful repair: repaired versus original damaged record.
2. Contribution of arrangement: kept versus shuffled body under matched repair.
3. Reciprocal feedback: live versus fixed reader with matched writing resources.

H001 has evidence for the second contrast and an unresolved result for the
first and third. It supplies no full reciprocal-repair qualification. Use a
bounded development map over corruption, survivor fraction and writer rate to
avoid uninformative floor/ceiling conditions; freeze one selection rule before
opening a new cohort. A failed primary cannot be replaced by another contrast.

Finish line: the particular claim proposed for forms passes its own frozen
contrast and necessary controls. If arrangement merely changes the harm from
rewriting, report that narrower result. Renewal work can continue even if
beneficial reciprocal feedback is not found.

## Rung 6 — distinguish present expression from continuation

Two related branches address the same question under different boundaries.

For the record model, preserve exact checkpoints and fork independent future
descendants. Measure present response, future reconstruction probability and
uninterrupted performance separately. Compare states with matched expression;
use interventions on record content, reader or writer to test the reason for
different futures. Any predictor must beat expression/history and simple
record-strength controls on held-out base networks. Record forecasts before
opening future outcomes. Coordinate with the existing theory-testing work so
this tests transfer of a prediction rather than duplicating its fitting task.

For the continuous member-replacement model, hold coarse labels fixed while
substituting full states or individual components between prepared and
unprepared populations. Test pause duration/onset at equal total clocks, and
compare with continuous and fully blocked communication. Carry actual
descendants into revision and onward transmission, tracking every member.
Retain the failed H001 pause conditions as reference panels.

Finish line: a causal change in continuation under a declared matched-current-
expression comparison, reproduced under numerical refinement and relevant
parameter variation. Finite deterministic panels remain mechanism specimens;
population probabilities require a declared sampling distribution. A timing
rescue is a separate claim from preparation-dependent readiness.

The continuous branch may proceed after its numerical and boundary checks
without waiting for the binary repair claim. It transmits surviving live state,
not the same weight packet, so the branches cannot be pooled into one effect.

## Rung 7 — confirm a limited contribution for forms

Select one or two claims supported by development. Before generating their
confirmation inputs, freeze the model, intervention, observer, horizon,
smallest meaningful effect, complete control set, inferential unit, exclusions,
multiplicity treatment, numerical tolerances and decision rule. Choose sample
size from development variability and a stated precision/power target.

Challenge the selected claim with the most relevant model changes: pattern
load, network size/connectivity, finite record precision, body/record damage,
reader changes, and timing. Some sensitivity is a result; universal robustness
is not required. Mark development and confirmation conditions explicitly and
do not replace a failed primary with a favorable stress panel.

Finish line: new-cohort evidence, passing integrity checks, complete negative
controls and a portable source/data/figure package for the qualified claim.
All arms and failures remain recoverable. No later rung is mandatory for
publishing an earlier, properly bounded result. A useful forms addition could
include a confirmed renewal effect together with a clear repair limitation.

## Longer horizon — competence and reconstruction machinery

After the forms milestone, ask whether a body can discover a useful response
through interaction and acquire the capacity to reconstruct it. Then allow
writer, reader and their relationship to change under selection for descendant
function, with inherited machinery counted alongside the acquired record.

This connects unprescribed organization, useful competence and renewal, which
the current experiments demonstrate separately. It needs new controls for
teacher leakage, environmental re-acquisition, repeated training, supplied
encoders and continual access to an ancestral archive. A failed search would
not establish impossibility without a working positive control. This broader
programme is separate from the near-term forms release.

## Sequence, evidence and execution

| Step | Immediate scope | Decision before expansion |
|---|---|---|
| Instrument package | Rungs 1–3, selected source-faithful private assays | Executable source, artifact availability, trace fixtures and original decision semantics verified |
| First forms extension | Rung 4, one added stressor | Complete-rewrite effect and its loss relative to copying identified |
| Mechanism branches | Rung 5 repair; rung 6 continuation/readiness | Each claim has its own controls and a bounded failure diagnosis |
| Publication milestone | Rung 7 for the claims that qualify | Fresh confirmation and complete evidence package |
| Later programme | Discovered competence and changing reconstruction machinery | Separate task and budget, without delaying forms |

Source-specific replication manifests belong in git-ignored `private/`, with
their configurations, imported artifacts and outputs. Rerunning private work
does not automatically make its protocol, trained state or results releasable.
Forms artifacts use independently specified model translations and their own
evidence; restricted mechanisms also remain restricted when copied into new
code. No release is performed by this plan.

Each new experiment requires a brief executable protocol. The gates above
define the questions, not numerical preregistrations. Keep original replication
gates intact; choose new translation gates before their relevant outcomes.
All-cohort inference resamples independent base networks rather than treating
related descendants, futures, checkpoints or solver resolutions as replicates.

Start with one CPU worker. Benchmark a small engineering run before sizing
each campaign, including analysis and I/O. Use the shared 12-CPU ceiling and
the ten-minute detachment policy in ../RUN_POLICY.md. Keep all new artifacts
inside this lane, preserve failures and leave other source trees read-only.

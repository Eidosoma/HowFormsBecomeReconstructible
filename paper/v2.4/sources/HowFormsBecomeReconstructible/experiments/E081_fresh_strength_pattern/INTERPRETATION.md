The frozen result is **PREDICTED_PATTERN_UNRESOLVED**. The filtered pattern has

matched-total pattern prediction. This is not evidence of equivalence, a negative

## Primary comparison

OF uses ordinary selected magnitudes rescaled to the filtered selected absolute
total; FF uses filtered selected magnitudes at that same total. Both retain
ordinary signs from the same intact body, connectivity and the pruned-rebuilt
record's outside bytes. Totals are matched per record before reader injury.

| Mixture condition | Both-cue successes | Success |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| OF: ordinary pattern at filtered total | 619/1,024 | 60.4492% |
| FF: filtered pattern at filtered total | 627/1,024 | 61.2305% |

The single primary FF−OF contrast is **+0.78125 [−0.58594, +2.24609] percentage
points**, a paired whole-block 95% bootstrap interval. There are **25 gains,
17 harms and 982 unchanged binary outcomes**. The 1,024 paired cases are nested
within **32 independent source blocks**, with equal sham/pulse weighting within
each block; they are not 1,024 independent replicates.

Secondary minimum-margin change is **+0.00921 [−0.00846, +0.02631]** and
aligned-transfer change **+0.00488 [−0.00194, +0.01176]**. Both remain unresolved.
The binary sham estimate is +0.1953 [−1.1719, +1.7578] points and pulse estimate
+1.3672 [−0.7812, +3.5156] points, also unresolved. These do not establish a
history-specific benefit or a difference between histories.

E080's corresponding estimate on previously studied records was
+3.1250 [1.3672, 5.1758] points (98.75% interval). The fresh estimate is smaller,
but different significance labels do not establish a statistical difference
between cohorts. Source blocks and reader streams both changed. No old outcomes
were pooled into E081, and no subset is promoted to replace its primary result.

## Consequence and next lead

Keep E080's positive result and E081's unresolved confirmation side by side.
The broader observation that pruning can aid an immediate read yet compromise
later rebuilding remains supported by the preceding experiments and the new
endpoint changes. A transferable advantage of filtered relative magnitudes at
matched total has not been confirmed on untouched mixture records.

The next economical step is a descriptive comparison of the saved E080/E081
records: relate all block-level gains and harms to strength concentration,
changes in local normalized coupling and proximity to the fixed success
threshold. Keep both endpoints and all cases. Such analysis may generate a
conditional prediction, but cannot validate it on the same outcomes; any new
prediction needs a separate frozen test. No longer horizon or added sample is
justified solely to make the present primary interval positive.

The apparatus still uses paired cue birth/noise, exact-zero fallback, float64
and natural strength normalization. External substitutions use an intact-body
donor. These results establish neither autonomous repair nor later persistence
of the substitution benefit. Manuscript integration remains with the other agent.

## Suggested manuscript wording

A prospective test on 32 newly generated source blocks compared filtered
selected magnitudes with ordinary magnitudes rescaled to the same preinjury
selected absolute total, holding ordinary signs, support and outside record
bytes fixed. Mixture success was 61.23% versus 60.45%, a difference of 0.78
[−0.59, 2.25] percentage points (95% paired block-bootstrap interval), with
25 gains and 17 harms among 1,024 nested cases. The prespecified pattern-benefit
prediction was unresolved. Both substitutions improved the pruned-rebuilt
baseline in secondary comparisons, but the fresh test did not confirm a
distinct advantage of filtered allocation. Immediate pruning improved success
by 7.23 [5.08, 9.47] points, whereas its post-rewrite contrast was −2.44
[−5.27, 0.20] points; individual harms occurred at both endpoints.

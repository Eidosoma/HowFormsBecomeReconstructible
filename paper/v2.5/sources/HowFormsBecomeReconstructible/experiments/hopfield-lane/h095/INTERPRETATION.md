# H095 — fresh confirmation of context protection; copying remains tied

Completed **27 October 2026 at 17:58:38 UTC**. Independent saved-array closeout
passed **28 October at 11:08 UTC**. The fixed H094 intervention succeeds on
**32 fresh independent networks**, with four related births each and two actual
handoffs. Protecting the actual internal context during the first query sweep
raises unseen tasks after two handoffs from **77/128 to 128/128**. All protected
primary responses are bit-exact. Equally protected copying and the stronger
native controls also score **128/128**; reconstruction has no strict advantage
over those controls.

This is fresh confirmation of the six finite-cohort screens under the declared
sampling model, with no tuning after main outcomes. The 128 task trials are
not 128 independent networks. It is not a population confidence guarantee,
an efficiency comparison or an RDT forecasting result. H094's exposed cohort
is not pooled into these results. Both H095 conditions were acquired afresh.

## Fixed mechanism and six separate findings

H095 calls the H093/H094 simulators unchanged. During only the first query
sweep, the protected condition skips context-coordinate updates. It holds
the actual post-gap values, including errors, then releases context for all
later query and continuation sweeps. No true context is supplied again and no
extra buffer or observation is introduced. The energy formula, writer and
eight-actual-parent-response limit remain fixed. Every child learns from its
actual parent's emissions. Copying receives the same schedule; native learners
retain the same declared history and completion apparatus.

## What the saved traces show

Own G0 unseen context is correct before the query in 1,024/1,024 cases in both
conditions. Unprotected dynamics retain it in **817** after the first sweep
and **993** at the recipient endpoint. Protection retains it in **1,024** at
both stages. Donor context improves from **829** after the first sweep and
**979** at the unprotected endpoint to **1,024** at both protected stages.
Founder preparation and gap traces match exactly across conditions.

At each protected later generation, eight primary unseen queries lose context
in the gap: **1,016/1,024** enter the query with correct context. The first-sweep
hold preserves those eight wrong values too. Released dynamics subsequently
recover all eight, giving correct context and exact responses at every
recipient endpoint. Preserving current state is therefore distinct from
re-supplying the correct context.

The unprotected G2 has 322 failed unseen queries: **47 with context lost**
and **275 with context intact**. The latter 275 retrieve their wrong learned
completion exactly. Across an overlapping split, 296 failures have an incorrect
learned completion, while 26 fail despite a correct learned completion. These
are counts of saved outcomes, not estimates of separate causal contributions.
All protected primary learned completions and retrieved responses are exact
through G2. Because protection is applied at every generation, H095 does not
separately estimate repaired parental teaching versus protection during the
child's own read.

These fresh results support the specific transition mechanism found in H093
and repaired in H094. They do not show that unwanted energy minima have been
eliminated, that protection is sufficient for arbitrary tasks, or that more
complex dynamics will preferentially help reconstruction. The current task
has reached the same ceiling for reconstruction and the strongest controls.

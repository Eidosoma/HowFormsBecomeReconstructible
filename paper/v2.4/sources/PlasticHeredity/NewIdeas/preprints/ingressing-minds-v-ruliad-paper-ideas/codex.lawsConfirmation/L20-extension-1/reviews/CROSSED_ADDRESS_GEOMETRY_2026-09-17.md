# D143 saved-address geometry — completed exploratory review

Completed 2026-09-17. [D143 primary results](../crossed-address-noise/RESULTS.md)
are unchanged. This user-approved follow-up remeasures sealed trajectories,
not new simulations or prospective predictions. **L20 stays STRESS_TESTED / MIXED.**

## What we learned

The effect depends on the pattern's geometry, not simply the distance moved.
For B, feedback shifts that oppose the stripe pattern are associated with
marked loss of translation-invariant shape during rewriting. Shifts that
preserve the ideal pattern do substantially better. Some nonzero,
pattern-preserving shifts even outperform the matched zero-shift writer in
these saved first-generation comparisons.

A is more robust: its shape can dip during writing and largely recover even
while its native-position preference reverses. A pattern changing position
is not the same as a pattern being destroyed. The next guide can readdress
a displaced pattern; the writing body's native-coordinate score alone is not
the final descendant outcome.

The new lead is therefore **compatible nonzero feedback**, not a blanket rule
that every displacement is damaging. This is an exploratory association on
known data, not confirmation of a beneficial replacement writer. The full
panel remains retained; no favorable offset was chosen for a new run.

## Scope, definitions and limitations

The [frozen scope](../crossed-address-noise/analysis/v2/SCOPE_V2.md) predates
cohort geometry–outcome extraction, but follows the known D143 aggregate result.
All 16 founder blocks, four lives, four noise histories, four address histories,
both families and both writers remain: 256 labelled cases / 4096 crossed cells.
The 1024 old diagonals and 1536 aligned aliases are not new independent evidence.
There are 16 independent founder blocks, no new outcomes, exclusions or fits.

Ideal compatibility **q** is the signed overlap between a fixed family template
and its shifted self. +1 preserves its pattern; −1 reverses its signs; zero
has zero signed overlap. B is periodic, so a nonzero spatial displacement can
have q=+1. Every analytical level was fixed in advance, not selected by its
outcomes. A perfect phase-reversed pattern still has best-translated shape
correlation 1; q=−1 alone is not shape loss.

Actual start-reference compatibility **r** is the centered autocorrelation of
the writing-start R field at the assigned shift. It is not instantaneous theta
feedback, and later R already includes previous address history. All start
references were nonconstant: zero invalid cases in every family/writer/generation.

Shape is best own-family R correlation over all 256 translations. Preference
uses the original native coordinates, strict abs(V)>0.2 and all 256 tissue
cells in its denominator. Both metrics, all saved checkpoints and F0–F3
record shapes were retained. Preference units and shape-correlation units
must not be interchanged.

Each geometry-level row below averages four noise histories first, then its
assigned block/life/address cells. Counts are assignments, not independent
founders. Different rows have different host support. Ordinary-minus-aligned
contrasts are matched within each assignment/noise; cross-level pooled means
are not a fully matched causal geometry intervention. Later-generation
contrasts also contain diverging inherited histories.

## What this does and does not add to L20

This qualifies the spatial-feedback mechanism: renewal quality depends on
compatibility with the organization being reconstructed. Ordinary-B loss is
not just an unexplained sum of small noises. Conversely, full zero-shift
alignment is not established as the best possible writer: some nonzero
pattern-compatible shifts do better in the saved comparisons.

It does **not** show that slow layers universally clean up noise, complete
shape preservation, autonomous address discovery or repair, or successful
challenge-based forecasting. D141 remains unresolved; the D142 and D143
primary results remain unchanged. L20 remains **STRESS_TESTED / MIXED**.

## Next recommendation — no new run launched

Specify a small, fresh matched test of **nonzero pattern-preserving feedback
versus zero-shift feedback**, with opposed/partial/neutral shifts retained as
controls and both target families included. Freeze a balanced address rule
from mathematical symmetry, not the best observed offset. Match starting
record, host and other noise; retain all cases. Measure both outgoing shape
and a fresh descendant read, not the writing body's position alone.

This would test whether the q=+1 advantage generalizes, and distinguish a
possible useful redistribution of imperfections from cohort selection or a
transient effect. The latter is a hypothesis, not a mechanism established
here. Benchmark and qualify any new executor before requesting a detached
run if its forecast exceeds 600s. No new simulation, refit or successor has
been prepared or launched by this review.

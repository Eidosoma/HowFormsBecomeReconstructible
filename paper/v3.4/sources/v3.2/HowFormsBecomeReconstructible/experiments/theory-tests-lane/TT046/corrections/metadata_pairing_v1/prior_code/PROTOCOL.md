## Frozen claim and analysis

The four co-primary estimands are fresh-state target tied-winner credit:
late minus preserved under full replacement at 900 and 1000, and that paired
packet effect under full replacement minus its intact counterpart at each
horizon. Average actual births and futures and all masks/16 worlds within each
block; weight the 32 source blocks equally. Joint confirmation requires all
four one-sided block Student-t lower bounds to exceed zero, with Bonferroni
alpha .05/4 per endpoint (31 degrees of freedom). Coverage is approximate.
For zero observed block variance, use the bounded Hoeffding lower bound as the
decision fallback. Report pointwise two-sided 95% intervals and Hoeffding
sensitivity for all four endpoints. Effect ranges are [-1,1] for packet effects
and [-2,2] for damage differences. The primary claim is joint directional mean
superiority, not absence of individual harms, a minimum useful effect, or a
general damage-response curve. Any failed bound gives NOT_CONFIRMED; secondary
results cannot rescue it. Sample size 32 is a fixed resource-conscious choice,
not a claimed power guarantee; no outcome-based enlargement is allowed.

Retain the seven TT045 state/packet contrasts and their exact decompositions,
absolute expression, every block, half-mixed and intact controls, all messages,
histories, masks and target-relative geometries, and target/donor/template/
opposite credits. Continuing-state and margin results are secondary. Preserve
paired harm/tie/gain counts for source-position means, injury means and
position/future outcomes. Save 900-to-1000 joint signs and within-arm changes.
The original-packet fresh comparator is the same physical assay as intact
preservation, using the new matched births/futures; save that alias explicitly.
Never pool damage levels to rescue a loss or call sparse readouts uninterrupted
body performance.

Keep all fixed-state/packet means and paired Monte Carlo SEs. Eight futures give
coarse individual estimates. Use whole block/future means as simulation
replicates after averaging dependent masks, positions and births. Combine
conditional Monte Carlo variances with squared block weights. These errors
condition on the fixed sources/states; population intervals instead use the
32 full block estimates and include finite sampling of births/futures. Do not
subtract Monte Carlo variance or count the futures as new population blocks.
Zero sampled variance is not certainty, and fractional tie credit is not a
binomial endpoint. Store probabilities in integer twelfths and continuous
credit-versus-best-other margins separately.

# TT024: the fill correction helps serially, but continued writing still loses useful information

Removing nominal replacement fill from captured memory improves the outcome
through eight replacements on these exposed cases. Informative-record terminal
recognition is **75.391% with subtraction versus 48.438% with full capture**, a
**26.953125-percentage-point** gain. All four block differences are positive:
15.625, 32.8125, 28.125 and 31.25 points. The primary descriptive direction holds
across the serial horizon, but the correction does not eliminate the loss.

The simpler controls limit the interpretation. **Zero-memory writing reaches
74.219%**, only 1.172 points below corrected writing. **Preserving the initial
captured record reaches 89.453%**, 14.0625 points above corrected writing, with
preservation ahead in every block. The experiment adds no further record
corruption. It supports the specified intervention relative to repeated full
capture; it does not establish a serial advantage for rewriting over preservation.

Each terminal value averages 256 paired chain draws per condition/family,
nested in 64 starting positions and four population blocks. The same starting
captured packet is supplied to all four policies. Writing-off preserves it
byte-for-byte. Zero initial memory subsequently updates normally; it does not
disable memory feedback. The body endpoints require uniquely correct recognition
at every respective endpoint across all eight cycles. Terminal recognition
retains fractional tie credit and comes from an additional fresh recipient.

Corrected capture improves **45 of 64 informative record means**, worsens two
and ties seventeen relative to full capture. Across paired future draws, 76
improve, seven worsen and 173 tie. Relative to zero-memory writing, 19 record
means improve, thirteen worsen and 32 tie; the four block effects are +1.5625,
+1.5625, +3.125 and −1.5625 points. Relative to preservation, corrected writing
improves four record means, worsens thirty and ties thirty; eight future draws
improve and 44 worsen. These adverse changes remain part of the result.

For common records, corrected terminal recognition improves **18.75 points**
over full capture, with all four block gains positive (12.5, 20.3125, 17.1875 and
25 points). Forty record means improve and twelve worsen. It ties zero-memory
writing in the pooled terminal score, with mixed block effects and balanced
draw improvements/harms, and remains 1.5625 points below preservation. The fourth
message's corrected-minus-full terminal difference is −1.5625 points, despite
the positive pooled effect. All message and injury strata remain in the summary.

The clearest extra advantage of corrected memory over zero initial memory is
**uninterrupted development**: +8.984 points on informative records and +12.5
points on common records, positive in all four blocks for both families.
Uninterrupted repaired-body performance is different: the corrected condition
gains 7.8125 points on common records but loses 1.5625 points on informative
records relative to zero-memory writing. Final recipient usefulness, continuous
development and continuous post-repair performance should not be collapsed
into one success label. Preservation leads corrected writing in all three
pooled endpoints for both families under this channel.

The saved generation profile retains residual degradation. Informative corrected
development is 87.891% at generation two (TT023's first-repair recipient), and
73.828% at generation eight; the separately challenged terminal recipient gives
75.391%. The intervening profile fluctuates rather than falling monotonically.
Preservation's developed-body recognition stays around its initial level across
the same paired environments. Adjacent-generation differences alone do not
isolate the functional effect of an individual rewrite, because each generation
uses a different receiving environment. The full policy comparisons above are
the controlled serial contrasts.

The largest informative corrected-minus-full terminal gain by final injury
stratum is in negative-half cases: **75.000% versus 28.125%**, +46.875 points.
Both-polarity cases give 71.875% versus 44.531%, and positive-half cases 82.812%
versus 76.562%. These final-cycle strata summarize entire diverged histories;
they are not isolated last-injury experiments. Initial-injury and all eight
generation strata are retained separately. The primary contrast and these
strata were frozen before any new TT024 continuation was generated.

This is a controlled serial development result designed after TT022/TT023 were
exposed. There are four population blocks and four original futures per record;
no significance gate, precise individual capacity estimate or fresh-population
confirmation is available. Reused controls and matching first episodes retain
their original replay evidence and are not new independent samples. Each new
condition evolves its own later body and packet. Only its finite 2,048-byte
record crosses replacements. Tissue/task apparatus remains supplied, and the
correction uses the physical injury mask and resting value at every cycle.
The evidence does not establish autonomous injury detection, equal-information
superiority, robustness to recurrent record corruption or a unique general law.

# Proposed active-form replication: evaluator intake

**11 October 2026 — planning only; no TT identifier assigned.**
The author requested a short active-form section in v0.19 and suggested
replications or tests in the two theory lanes. The [AF01 design](../../../theory-lane/active_forms_v0_1/DESIGN.md)
targets the unresolved body-source question in reciprocal repair. It maps to
the original charter's **RDT-P05**, not the existing `p05_*` functional-repair
modules. No current protocol, run, exposure-register entry or verdict changes.

## Proposed intake sequence

1. Reproduce D073/D074's native information routes on development cases.
   Separate an exact retained-state replay, a new implementation comparison
   and a fresh-cohort replication; availability determines which is possible.
2. Test the proposed common-record construction. Within each message pair,
   verify byte-identical incoming theta and identical non-body context, R,
   averages and fresh fill. Randomness and missing-value patterns must not
   encode the target. If native acquired bodies cannot be value-matched,
   distinguish native substitutions from supplied spatial permutations.
   The source initializes its activity average from each body's V; preserve
   that in replication and label a common-average extension explicitly.
3. Implement the outgoing-theta-only boundary independently. A target label
   may reach the offline scorer, never the repair dynamics or recipient.
   Check that writing-off packets remain identical and positive readers work.
4. Use the original native recognition endpoint and both message roles.
   Require useful correct-message recovery as well as body-specific direction.
   Preserve wrong-body, scramble, fixed-reader and conventional body-informed
   comparisons. Do not substitute a generic fidelity gain for content transfer.
5. Before any confirmation, require a complete prediction and registration:
   cohort, margins, uncertainty, multiplicity, budget, source commitments and
   a genuinely unexposed split. The [prediction draft](../../../theory-lane/active_forms_v0_1/PREDICTION_DRAFT.json)
   has null quantitative fields and is not ready for confirmation.

## Exposure and interpretation

Planning has used public-to-this-program D073/D074 reports, subsequent donor
reviews, the CA decoding–writing mismatch and Wagner maintenance closeouts.
Those are exposed motivation, not withheld predictions. No new body fields,
test populations or continuation outcomes were generated for this intake.
Record their identities and any development exposure in the shared register
when implementation begins; a new seed alone does not make the hypothesis new.

A qualified result would identify a body-derived contribution under the
declared boundary. Matching performance by a conventional method remains a
valid explanation. A failed body-source contrast does not invalidate D074's
already established organization benefit. Autonomous interpreter repair and
cross-apparatus predictive RDT claims remain separate.

E055, TT014/TT015 and P08 retain their own questions and execution contracts.
This intake does not poll, duplicate or launch them. No simulation has been
performed, and no outcome is supplied to the preprint by this proposal.

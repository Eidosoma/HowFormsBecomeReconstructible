## Current scientific position

The strongest defensible conclusion so far is:

> Wagner GRNs can evolve a renewable, causally readable parent-state carrier
> under predictable ecology without a memory reward in fitness. That simulated
> carrier is not yet mutation-robust: the carrier's storage machinery survives
> reasonably well, while ordinary changes to the GRN interpreter often erase
> its meaning. We therefore have evidence for fragile simulated plastic heredity,
> not yet for a robust second inheritance system or a natural cellular mechanism.

The important pieces are:

1. The GARD preprint established state-dependent and steerable occurrence of a
   break-and-renewed-inheritance event. The later strict-eight work established
   coherent rehoming occurrence, but not a shared prospective predictor,
   recurrence, or control.
2. The first GRE sampled-network pilot failed its frozen committor gate in both
   Boolean and Wagner engines. Evolved networks were positive controls showing
   that selection can create destination-predictive structure; they cannot rescue
   the sampled-network failure.
3. Stage A produced a small positive Boolean own-history committor contrast and a
   Wagner null. At the same time, Wagner self-inherited lineages returned to an
   active destination about 59% of the time versus about 26% with shuffled donors.
4. The formal diagnostic showed that Wagner’s roughly 33-percentage-point capture
   advantage was almost equally large in natural, isomorphic, and own-anchor
   controls. It is therefore mainly a general continuity effect, not an
   acute-mismatch-specific effect.
5. A smaller Wagner strict-switching interaction was specific to acute mismatch.
   This is interesting exploratory evidence about rehoming occurrence, but it is
   secondary and does not establish destination information.
6. Phase 1 showed that the deterministic Wagner transition map can be represented
   as an exact Boolean lookup table: 97,280 paired native/compiled futures were
   pathwise identical. “Boolean code versus Wagner code” is therefore not the
   explanation when both encode the same map.
7. Phase 1 also showed that a donor chosen to carry the same prior destination or
   exact state nearly restores the self-inheritance result. The relevant carrier
   appears to be state content and destination compatibility, not the label
   “self.” This is compatible with a transferable physical memory state, but it
   does not by itself demonstrate mismatch-specific heredity.
8. The destination-committor evidence remains endpoint-dependent, non-monotone in
   carryover, and largely weak at full carryover after subtracting controls.
   Phase 2 is therefore scientifically necessary.
9. Direct Prediction and the Robustness Ratchet established a renewable,
   causally readable simulated carrier under predictable ecology, while ordinary
   Wagner mutations frequently damaged the GRN dictionary that interprets it.
10. Interpreter-Depth localized that brittleness: reset damage is concentrated
    among a minority of regulatory edges, and selection over more offspring
    improves immediate but not four-mutation persistence.
11. Interpreter Resilience raised distance-four retention to 45.8% with evolved
    global repair, but repair-off explained only 10.4 percentage points and its
    paired interval included zero. Strong absolute PH evidence therefore
    coexists with unresolved mechanism attribution and inadequate deep
    robustness.

No result in the current GRE program is confirmatory. There is no authorization
to reinterpret a failed frozen gate after seeing a favorable secondary outcome.

## Evidence and language rules

The following labels are used throughout this log:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Preprint-level | Reported result from the existing GARD preprint or its explicitly separated side program |
| Sealed exploratory | Protocol and outputs are fixed, replayable, and checksummed, but the study is not confirmatory |
| Engineering | Validates machinery or controls; signs and magnitudes are not scientific estimates |
| Running/unsealed | Incomplete; partial output must not be interpreted |
| Interpretation | A reading of the observations, not a measured result |
| Hypothesis | A live explanation that a future comparison can support or weaken |

Unless stated otherwise, reported intervals are 95% rulebook-bootstrap exploratory
intervals. The independent unit is the rulebook, not an individual stochastic
future.

Six questions must remain separate:

1. Does an event occur?
2. Is its probability state-dependent?
3. Can the destination or event be predicted prospectively?
4. Does a similar coherent regime recur?
5. Can an intervention control it?
6. Can selection change it heritably?

Passing one question does not imply the others.

## Translation across substrates

GARD, Boolean GRNs, and Wagner GRNs are not three spellings of the same model.
They can test the same process claim only after their objects and clocks are
translated explicitly.

| Property | GARD | Boolean implementation | Wagner implementation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inherited/configuring rulebook | Catalytic matrix β, usually environmental in the reconstructed lineage | Global truth-table transition map | Signed weighted regulatory matrix W plus bias; W can later mutate |
| Fast state | Continuous molecular composition | Six-bit expression vector in the original implementation | Ten-bit signed expression vector in the original implementation |
| Update | Growth, catalytic exchange, and fission | Synchronous global lookup by default | Sequential in-place threshold updates by default |
| Noise | Molecular exchange, growth, and fission stochasticity | Expression perturbation before a cycle; no edge-weight noise in the original engine | Expression perturbation plus multiplicative regulatory-edge noise θ during development |
| Coherent form | Compositional regime | Point attractor or Boolean limit cycle | Point attractor or Wagner limit cycle |
| Library | Empirical/deterministic compositional forms | Exact attractor catalogue when state space is enumerable | Deterministic θ = 0 attractor catalogue |
| Inheritance unit | Selected daughter composition | Expression state passed to the next developmental cycle | Expression state passed to the next developmental cycle |
| Rehoming | Break from old composition followed by coherent distinct residence | Loss of old expression regime followed by stable distinct expression | Same process definition, under a constrained weighted threshold map |

The GARD catalytic matrix β and the GRE regulatory matrix W are analogues, not
identical objects. Likewise, molecular composition and expression state are
analogues, not interchangeable measurements.

Two clocks are mandatory:

- Developmental or cell-lineage clock: hold the rulebook fixed and ask how state
  inheritance, perturbation, and relaxation determine the next adult state.
- Evolutionary clock: mutate or recombine W across reproduction and selection.

A developmental transition within one W is not an evolutionary genotype
replacement.

## Core experimental vocabulary

- λ, expression-state carryover:
  - λ = 0 means complete developmental reset. Self and donor inheritance must be
    pathwise identical here.
  - λ = 1 means full adult-state carryover.
  - Intermediate λ values represent partial epigenetic carryover.
- θ, Wagner regulatory-noise variance:
  - θ = 0 is the deterministic weighted transition map.
  - Larger θ perturbs regulatory interactions during development.
- Self inheritance: a recipient continues from its own prior adult state.
- Donor-shuffled inheritance: the recipient receives another lineage’s adult
  state, with a fixed cyclic donor mapping inside the frozen data half.
- Destination-matched donor: a donor is selected to share the recipient’s prior
  frozen destination.
- Exact-state donor: a donor is selected to share the recipient’s prior exact
  point state.
- Active capture: settlement in a destination recognized by the active frozen
  rulebook catalogue.
- Strict switching: a break followed by the registered run of coherent,
  old-anchor-distinct descendants. It measures rehoming occurrence.
- Destination committor: a held-out probabilistic forecast of the eventual
  destination. Gain is improvement in log loss over a cross-rulebook dynamical
  baseline. It measures destination information, not just event frequency.

Phase 1 froze four destination definitions in advance:

1. frozen_library: the original deterministic attractor catalogue;
2. exact_point_state: repeated exact point states, including off-library points;
3. realized_signature: repeated phase-invariant point or cycle signatures;
4. competing_outcome: an exhaustive recognized-point, recognized-cycle,
   off-library-point, noise-specific-cycle, non-convergent, or
   no-stable-destination category.

The endpoint ladder exists because a noisy system can settle coherently outside
its deterministic catalogue. Such futures must be classified, not discarded.

## Evidence ledger

### 1. GARD preprint and strict-eight side program

Evidence source: [GRE simulation research program](GRE_SIMULATION_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md)
and the [current preprint](../../../output/pdf/plastic-heredity-biorxiv-v1.pdf).

#### What was found

- The preprint’s common F12 endpoint is a break followed by three locally
  inherited boundaries within 12 fissions.
- F12 probability was state-dependent, ranked by a frozen composite algorithm,
  and externally steerable in reconstructed GARD.
- F12 was not necessarily a coherent new compositional regime: only about
  4.5–7.2% of qualifying three-run episodes passed stronger episode-wide
  coherence checks.
- The stricter F32 endpoint required eight consecutive inherited daughters,
  mutual coherence across all 28 daughter pairs, and distinction from the old
  anchor.
- Two clean-room strict campaigns produced 9,703 events in 512,000 futures.
  Candidate/half rates ranged from 1.698% to 2.109%, with events spread across
  119–143 of 200 catalytic matrices per cell.
- Homogeneous and fission-only generative controls produced no renewed
  inheritance in one implementation, while acute catalytic-label derangement
  increased strict-event occurrence.

#### What the larger GARD program suggested

- A rulebook sustains a small empirical menu of forms, commonly two or three.
- Deterministic mean-field analysis located empirical forms well but badly
  undercounted how many forms were occupied.
- Acute state–rulebook mismatch raised strict-event occurrence, erased the state
  incumbency bonus, and redirected episodes toward altered-rulebook forms.
- Neutral occupancy and rehoming destinations were associated with basin entry,
  or “doors,” more than with local compositional proximity.
- Direct selection on composition changed occupancy; selection on a correlated
  downstream trait did not.
- Forms showed error thresholds under molecular swaps, although several proposed
  wall/lifetime relations failed.
- Chimeric catalytic rulebooks produced mostly novel, off-geodesic forms with
  cross-seam support.
- The tested GARD chemistry did not acquire durable post-release history under
  dwell time, feedback, wall softening, or strong selection manipulations.
- Slow perpetual rulebook change was harder to track than a fast completed
  replacement at the same total change.
- Direct adaptation was smoother, while neutral drift contained more punctuated
  rehoming jumps in one candidate.

#### Boundary on the claim

This is an occurrence and state-dependence result. No strict-event predictor
passed a shared prospective gate; strict recurrence and control were not
established. The generative-null contrast came from one implementation and is not
a universal null.

The negative record is part of the evidence: 33 registered predictions missed,
including early thermodynamic interpretations, the first-window segmenter,
deterministic library counts, several wall/lifetime hypotheses, durable
niche-constructed memory, a punctuation prediction, and indirect-selection
access. GRE should test the corrected process claim, not silently restore those
superseded stories.

### 2. GRE pilot v1

Evidence source: [pilot v1 diagnostic report](diagnostics/pilot-v1/PILOT_V1_DIAGNOSTIC_REPORT.md).

Status: sealed, post-hoc diagnostic of 245,760 futures. The frozen decision was
not to advance.

The primary result was held-out destination-committor gain:

| Stratum | Rulebooks | Mean gain and interval | Positive rulebooks | Above +0.02 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Boolean sampled | 16 | −0.026 [−0.048, 0.003] | 2/16 | 2/16 |
| Wagner sampled | 16 | −0.034 [−0.065, 0.005] | 2/16 | 2/16 |
| Boolean evolved | 16 | +0.034 [−0.005, 0.073] | 10/16 | 10/16 |
| Wagner evolved | 16 | +0.120 [0.068, 0.172] | 13/16 | 12/16 |

Interpretation:

- Randomly sampled networks did not contain a general state-conditioned
  destination signal under the frozen test.
- Evolved networks demonstrate that the effect is evolvable or can be enriched
  by selection. They are positive controls and a separate scientific question,
  not a rescue of sampled-network failure.

The strict-event controls exposed a specificity problem:

- Boolean sampled acute-minus-natural strict differences were +52.852 percentage
  points at λ = 0 and +36.602 points at λ = 1.
- Wagner sampled differences were only +0.430 and +3.438 points.
- Forced single-attractor strict-event rates ranged from 86.25% to 100%.
- At full carryover, acute and developed-under-target strict rates differed by at
  most 0.82 percentage points across strata.

Therefore a raw strict event can be produced by forced convergence and acute
switching is not automatically a unique plastic-heredity mechanism.

### 3. Quick v2 control prototype

Evidence source: [quick v2 prototype report](QUICK_V2_PROTOTYPE_REPORT.md).

Status: engineering only; 896 futures and one rulebook per stratum.

What was verified:

- Every self/donor pair had the exact same trajectory at λ = 0.
- Donors formed a fixed cyclic mapping within each frozen half, with no
  self-donation or cross-half donation.
- Replay, counts, checksums, and representative Boolean/Wagner trajectories
  passed.
- With no other eligible rulebook in a stratum, hierarchical shrinkage correctly
  selected κ = 0 instead of borrowing across cohorts.

Tiny-cell own-history committor contrasts were all negative:

| Engine/cohort | Self-minus-donor gain |
|---|---:|
| Boolean sampled | −0.287 |
| Boolean evolved | −0.137 |
| Wagner sampled | −4.689 |
| Wagner evolved | −1.185 |

These are unstable machinery checks, not population effect sizes. The prototype
clarified the conceptual split:

- Plastic rehoming: a phenotype switches after its rulebook changes.
- Hereditary state information: inherited state improves destination prediction
  in a lineage- or content-specific way beyond controls.

The first can occur without demonstrating the second.

### 6. Mechanism campaign Phase 1

Evidence sources: [Phase 1 report](runs/wagner-mechanism-v1/phase-1/report.md),
[analysis](runs/wagner-mechanism-v1/phase-1/analysis.json), and

Status: sealed exploratory phase; 665,600 futures, 64 reused Stage A rulebooks,
runtime 893.11 seconds, no dropped outcomes.

#### Exact native-to-compiled bridge

The deterministic Wagner successor map was compiled into a Boolean lookup table.
At θ = 0:

- 76,800 main-arm native/compiled pairs were pathwise identical.
- 20,480 control-arm native/compiled pairs were pathwise identical.
- All 97,280 paired futures matched exactly, including summaries and trace
  digests.

This rules out the software representation of the transition function as the
source of a native-versus-compiled difference. It does not make a Wagner map into
a randomly sampled Boolean map: network size, update construction, and map
geometry remain Wagner-derived.

|---|---:|---:|---:|

These are descriptive near-equalities, not a formal equivalence test. They imply:

- Random donor shuffling disrupts state–destination compatibility.

- “Self” is not a magic label. The causal candidate is the content and dynamical
  placement of the inherited state.

This result can be read in two complementary ways. It weakens a special
own-lineage identity story, while strengthening the mechanistic idea that a
portable phenotypic state can carry causal information. Plastic heredity should
be transferable with the material state; it should not require a lineage name.
The remaining test is whether that information is specifically useful under
state–rulebook mismatch beyond natural and own-anchor continuity.

## Hypothesis ledger

### H1. The 59% Wagner result is mainly generic state continuity

Status: strongly supported in the reused Stage A cohort.

Evidence for:

- Self-minus-donor capture was approximately +32 to +33 points in acute,
  natural, isomorphic, and own-anchor arms.
- Acute-minus-control capture interactions were only about +0.7 to +1.0 points
  and their intervals included zero.

Evidence against or limitation:

- Strict switching retained a smaller mismatch-specific interaction.
- Reused rulebooks and exploratory intervals require a fresh challenge.

### H2. State content, not lineage identity, carries the raw effect

Status: strongly supported descriptively; formal equivalence not established.

Evidence for:

- Destination-matched and exact-state donors nearly reproduced self capture and
  strict rates at θ = 0 and θ = 0.10.
- λ = 0 self/donor paths are exactly equal, so the contrast depends on inherited
  state rather than an unrelated implementation branch.

Scientific implication:

- This is compatible with a portable, material phenotypic carrier.
- It does not establish that the carrier contributes mismatch-specific
  destination information beyond ordinary state continuity.

### H3. Acute mismatch creates a small, specific rehoming effect in Wagner

Status: supported for secondary strict occurrence in exploratory data.

Evidence for:

- Stage A strict acute-minus-control interactions were about +1.8 to +2.8
  percentage points under self inheritance and their exploratory intervals were
  above zero.
- Phase 1 at the Stage A-like θ = 0.10 reproduced positive strict rate
  interactions: acute-minus-natural +0.0316 [0.0105, 0.0578] and
  acute-minus-own-anchor +0.0211 [0.0066, 0.0371].

Evidence against or limitation:

- The primary destination-committor control interactions at full carryover were
  mainly null.
- Strict occurrence can be high under forced convergence and cannot alone
  identify plastic heredity.

### H4. The Boolean–Wagner discrepancy is caused by the programming language or
lookup-table representation

Status: ruled out for an identical deterministic transition map.

Evidence:

- Native and compiled Wagner were pathwise identical in all 97,280 registered
  θ = 0 bridge futures.

Remaining architectural candidates:

- N = 6 versus N = 10;
- synchronous global versus sequential in-place update;

- regulatory noise;
- attractor and basin geometry;

Status: supported as an endpoint concern, not yet as the explanation of the

Status: promising exploratory hypothesis.

- Reset erases history.
- Partial carryover combines memory with ability to move under the new rulebook.

Status: supported as a pilot positive control; not yet a general evolutionary

Evidence for:

Status: not established.

- an acute-mismatch effect beyond natural and own-anchor continuity;

- eventual untouched confirmation;

## What the 59% result means in lay terms

Imagine rolling a ball on a landscape. If the ball starts where its own previous
journey ended, it finds a recognized valley about 59% of the time. If we swap in a
random ball position from another journey, it finds one only about 26% of the
time.

At first this looks like the lineage remembers its past. The controls show that
most of the advantage also occurs when nothing special has been mismatched: a
good starting position simply helps the system settle. Then the matched-donor
experiment sharpens the explanation. Give the lineage somebody else’s position
that is already in the same valley, and almost the entire advantage returns.

So the result is real, but its best current interpretation is “the inherited
position matters,” not “the lineage’s name matters” and not yet “mismatch causes
a uniquely hereditary destination.” That is still scientifically useful: a
transferable state is exactly the kind of material carrier a plastic-heredity
mechanism would need. The next experiment asks whether that carrier contains
specific historical information after ordinary continuity is subtracted.

## Why the Boolean and Wagner implementations can differ

The original comparison changed several things at once:

- state-space size;
- update order;
- the family of allowed transition maps;
- regulatory noise;
- attractor/basin geometry;
- ease of assigning noisy outcomes to the deterministic library.

Boolean’s arbitrary synchronous map can form crisp, exactly enumerable
destinations. Wagner’s weighted sequential system is constrained by its matrix
and, under noise, often forms off-library points, noise-specific cycles, or no
stable destination. A difference in committor gain can therefore arise even when
both show large raw state-continuity effects.

The exact compilation result supplies an important control: when the Wagner map
is copied exactly into a lookup table, the trajectories are exactly the same.
The remaining explanation lies in which maps are generated, their dimension and
update scheme, the added regulatory noise, or how outcomes are classified—not in
the mere fact that one simulator uses a table.

| Fresh cell | Difference and exploratory interval |
|---|---:|
| Boolean | +0.0018 [-0.0042, +0.0072] |

## 2026-08-19: Plastic-memory causal campaign implemented

The next experiment now tests the missing causal statement directly. A
temporary A or B clamp induces one of two forms under an unchanged rulebook.
The induced expression state is transplanted into an otherwise identical reset
recipient, and a separately labeled slow mark is tested after expression itself
is reset. Reset, destination-matched donor, descriptor-matched, fixed-pattern
shuffle, write-disabled, inert-mark, ablation, and rescue arms prevent generic
continuity or a worsening donor from masquerading as memory.

The evidence ladder is frozen as induction, minimal state transfer, forced-break
recurrence, descendant persistence, mark-only recovery, cross-engine
replication, conditional GARD replication, and evolvability. The primary gate
requires an absolute hold-8 gain over reset of at least 0.05, a history
crossover of at least 0.05, a held-out log-loss gain of at least 0.02 nats,
multiplicity-adjusted positive lower bounds, reliability at least 0.80, and
pathwise self/transplant identity. No relative donor contrast can satisfy this
gate by making the donor worse.

The staged implementation contains:

- 32-rulebook cue calibration per GRE engine;
- a 7,716,864-future, 64-rulebook discovery campaign;
- a 4,096,000-future state-only confirmation, expanding to at most 9,830,400
  futures if one slow-mark candidate per engine advances;
- exploratory and conditional confirmatory evolutionary cohorts with neutral
  and immediate-cue controls;
- a conditional, candidate-specific, 1,945,600-future GARD confirmation; and
- immutable registrations, semantic seeds, hashed per-source checkpoints,
  exact shard counts, replay, independent reanalysis, detached execution, and
  checkpoint-derived throughput/ETA.

This section records an implemented and preregistered design, not a positive
scientific result. Its claims remain false unless the corresponding fresh stage
seals and passes its own gate.

Before launch, all 66 repository tests passed. A discarded-seed source benchmark
sealed 120,576 full discovery futures in 1,495.52 seconds serially and occupied
24 MiB. Representative five-generation evolution populations took 13.91 seconds
in Boolean and 14.81 seconds in Wagner. These measurements set an initial
5–6-hour GRE discovery envelope and a much broader 12–30-hour exploratory
evolution envelope; live checkpoint rates replace them after launch.

## 2026-08-19: Wagner induction failure localized and rescue funnel inserted

The repaired plastic-memory campaign sealed all 49,152 calibration futures and
then stopped at its registered induction rule. Boolean passed at a full clamp
and dwell one, with exact A/B acquisition 0.828/0.838. Sequential Wagner at
theta = 0.10 never approached the 0.80 requirement: its strongest full-clamp
cells remained near 0.49–0.52, and changing dwell from one to sixteen did not
improve them. At full clamp and dwell one, Wagner returned to the requested form
at least once in about 84–85% of futures, but correct strict-8 retention was
only 1.4–1.9%. Thus the cue can often revisit the target, while noisy released
dynamics do not keep it there.

A disposable paired replay on eight archived Wagner rulebooks separated noise
from cue duration. Exact acquisition / strict-8 retention was 1.000 / 0.438–0.469
at theta = 0, 0.875–0.945 / 0.273–0.352 at theta = 0.01, 0.750–0.875 /
0.125–0.141 at theta = 0.03, and 0.461–0.641 / 0.008–0.023 at theta = 0.10.
Longer dwell again showed no stable monotonic rescue. These are post-hoc design
diagnostics, not evidence for plastic heredity.

The new [Wagner induction-rescue protocol](WAGNER_INDUCTION_RESCUE_PROTOCOL.md)
therefore crosses noise, sequential/synchronous timing, initialization clamps,
persistent hard clamps, and target-aligned regulatory fields. It explicitly
includes no-cue and exact-target-injection controls so “writing” can be separated
from “maintenance.” A broad 288,768-future screen can nominate at most four
mechanistically diverse candidates; a 48-rulebook replication may nominate one;
and only a further untouched 64-rulebook validation can mark that regimen ready.
No candidate automatically launches the heredity experiment, and a low-noise or
alternate-update winner will not be relabeled as the original Wagner regime.

## 2026-08-19: Deterministic Wagner writer validated; heredity ladder registered

The induction-rescue funnel is complete and checksummed. Its selected sequential
Wagner theta = 0 writer uses a one-cycle full persistent hard clamp. On 64 fresh
rulebooks it acquired both A and B targets exactly. Correct strict-eight retention
was 0.5468 and 0.5461, correct-minus-wrong was +0.2390 with simultaneous lower
bound +0.1846, gain over no cue was +0.1407 with lower bound +0.1103, split-half
reliability was 0.979, and retention equaled the direct-injection ceiling.

This is a strong engineering result and a deliberately limited scientific one.
The cue writes the complete requested expression pattern under deterministic
dynamics. It does not yet show donor-to-recipient transmission, an uncued
descendant, a hidden carrier, or plastic heredity. At theta = 0.01 the earlier

far below the writer gate.

The new [Wagner plastic-heredity preregistration](WAGNER_HEREDITY_EXPERIMENT_PREREGISTRATION.md)
therefore treats the hard writer as the minimal-heredity ceiling, a strength-0.5
target field as the main softer intervention, and theta = 0.01 as the robustness
frontier. It requires exact state transplant into a fresh identical recipient to
beat reset and fixed-pattern shuffle, preserve absolute A/B history information,
and remain positive after one uncued descendant cycle. Destination-matched states
are interpretive: equivalence would mean ordinary attractor state is sufficient,
not that an extra hidden memory was found.

The registered discovery has 2,801,664 futures on 96 fresh rulebooks. Each
advancing writer receives 3,194,880 confirmation futures on 240 untouched
rulebooks. Slow-mark discovery and confirmation are separate 3,932,160- and
3,440,640-future stages. Evolution compares descendant-memory selection against
immediate-cue and neutral controls; GARD remains an independent read-only analog.
This section records the implemented design and the sealed rescue prerequisite,
not a downstream positive result. Partial detached-run files must not be read as

The two-to-three-day extension now includes acquisition-only natural-writer

This is an implemented and frozen design, not a positive result. All v1 artifacts

## 2026-08-21: Multigeneration Wagner lineage-carrier experiment implemented

Plastic Heredity v2 established the narrow state-channel result, but it still

The implementation adds immutable stage registrations, fresh frozen seeds,

streams, exact record accounting, replay, independent verification, SHA-256

## 2026-08-22: Local-memory de-engineering experiment frozen

## 2026-08-22: Local-memory search exposed a strength–repeatability tradeoff

The local-memory campaign sealed after 544 seconds. None of the sixteen
validation finalists passed the registered search gate because their source-wise
split-half Pearson reliability was below 0.70. This verdict is valid under the
frozen protocol and remains unchanged. Nevertheless, the two leading full
ten-locus carriers had large generation-4 validation gains of 0.5977 and 0.5898,
with all sixteen validation sources positive. Their Pearson reliabilities were
-0.139 and 0.186, respectively. This apparent conflict motivated a prospective
test of whether ceiling compression and only four futures in each validation
half made correlation an unsuitable stability diagnostic.

Because the search gate failed, the preregistered 96-source confirmation did not
run. Its 16-source failure atlas compared k=3, k=5, and k=10 reductions from both
leader families. Sparse k=3 and k=5 carriers had smaller effects but much higher
split-half correlations, roughly 0.77–0.88. Full k=10 carriers retained much
larger generation-4 gains near 0.55 and generation-8 lower bounds near 0.25–0.30,
but reliability remained near 0.18–0.20. Strong copy noise removed a dependable
generation-8 effect from every family. The atlas intentionally omitted the full
causal controls and therefore cannot support a causal-PH verdict.

The result shows that target-blind same-gene marks can carry substantial history
signal in Wagner GRNs, but the evolutionary search rebuilt something close to a
ten-entry notebook. It did not establish compact, noise-robust, naturally
plausible plastic heredity. The most useful unresolved question is now whether
the k=10 failure is stochastic instability or measurement failure under
saturation.

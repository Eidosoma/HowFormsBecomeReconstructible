## Fixed cases and controls

Four stages, with technical gates between them. Scientific negatives do not
prevent later registered stages; in particular benchmark failure does not
suppress a descendant test. No retuning or trial replacement.

1. Benchmarks: 6 cases, three new seeds x two resolutions. Fresh ordinary-M
   reference trios evolve isolated for 20k + 10k + 10k, then attempt three M
   handovers into fresh E recipients. This is time-matched over the NEW 40k
   writing interval, NOT matched to the descendants' prior 680k history.
   One benchmark is shared by both history comparisons for its seed/resolution.
2. Returns: 12 cases, all actual P12 descendants x two resolutions. Six standard
   M cues contact the actual E carriers for 20k. Remove every cue, retain the
   actual three targets for two isolated 10k holds, then attempt M handovers
   numbered 7,8,9. Remove all teachers at each replacement, retain all pupils.
   Stop after the first failed writing or handover; keep the failure in the
   denominator. No partial successful trio can continue.
3. Writing controls: 48 cases, four counterfactual forks per actual descendant
   and resolution: M cues with RNA transport blocked, M cues with the cue-target
   boundary cut, E same-state cues, and no cues. Same actual source cells,
   writing duration, target identities and withdrawal/holds. Negative means all
   three retain E in both holds. These controls assay writing, not E transmission.
4. Relay controls: 36 cases, RNA-blocked and boundary-cut first handovers from
   the actual held M writer/aged-benchmark trios, for all 18 parent cases.
   Use identical fresh E states, gaps, IDs and interval as that parent's r07.
   If a parent never acquired retained M, its controls are unavailable: zero
   phases, null ion-index metadata and an explicit skip, not replacement cells.
   Unavailable controls cannot qualify a trial. Retained E recipients are the
   negative endpoint; do not call unchanged E recipients evidence of E teaching.

Maximum 102 cases / 1,008 integrations; early failures/skips reduce calls, not
registered denominators. A technical error, corrupted source or coarse/fine
disagreement stops progress and is not relabelled as a scientific negative.

Writing requires desired final labels AND >=95% desired occupancy among the
26 regularly spaced samples at or after 7.5k in EACH 10k hold. Every handover
requires all three pupils to meet the same retention criterion, to start E,
and still to be E immediately before their first scheduled M contact.

Report raw complete returns out of THREE prior lineages per origin history.
Separately report control-qualified returns: both resolutions complete, all
four writing controls negative, and both actual-writer relay controls available
and negative. Also report benchmark-supported returns, requiring the paired
ordinary-M benchmark and its two relay controls to qualify. Do not silently
merge raw, control-qualified and benchmark-supported outcomes.
Report each writing/round stop and every failed or unavailable control.

Fine pairs, counterfactual forks, shared benchmarks and reused ancestry are not
extra independent trials. No precise success probability, history advantage,
new attractor, inherited learning rule, fitness, wet-lab or autonomy claim follows.
This panel adds only one return to M; no subsequent E rewrite or autonomous
scheduling experiment is authorized.

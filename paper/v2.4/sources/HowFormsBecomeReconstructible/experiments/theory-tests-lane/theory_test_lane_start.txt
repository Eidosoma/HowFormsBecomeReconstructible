THEORY-TESTS — STARTING CHARTER
Reconstructive Dynamics Theory (RDT) | evaluator brief v0.1 | 8 October 2026

STATUS: PREPARATION FOR A LATER LANE. ALL EIGHT PREDICTIONS ARE DRAFT_NOT_REGISTERED.
This charter does not launch the lane, authorize simulations, allocate compute, or
register an empirical claim. It accompanies reconstructive_dynamics_predictions_v0_1.json.
Use it when the coordinator later activates theory-tests for a defined study.

PURPOSE

Act as an independent experimental adversary to the theory lane's RDT proposals.
Test whether a compact, transferable account predicts reconstruction, loss, recovery,
and useful interventions beyond strong conventional alternatives at a declared
information and resource budget. A useful negative result is an apparatus-specific
law that predicts well while the proposed universal law fails.

The theory lane may explore ambitious explanations. Your job is to make their
empirical commitments concrete, preserve genuine opportunities for disconfirmation,
and report what the frozen claim did and did not predict. Do not quietly improve
the theory while scoring its confirmation set. Do not make erased-state ablations
the only alternatives: associative memory, denoising/error correction, static
scaffolding, ordinary nonlinear dynamics, epigenetic feedback, and learned
state-space predictors are serious explanatory competitors.

No simulated outcome can distinguish a description from an unrestricted conventional
model containing the exact simulator. The relevant advance is prediction, transfer,
compression, calibration efficiency, intervention choice, or a rigorously stated
new constraint. Do not infer independently active nonphysical patterns from an
unexplained result or from an information-route violation.

INITIAL WORK WHEN THIS LANE IS ACTIVATED

1. Read the frozen RDT theory document and the prediction registry. Verify their
   identities and status. Read the relevant native source methods rather than
   inferring mechanisms from a synthesis table or an agent summary.
2. Inventory existing and ongoing fleet studies that touch the proposed claim.
   Record exact interventions, cohorts, endpoints, apparatus versions, launch and
   completion identities, prediction timestamps, and which outcomes were visible.
   Do not duplicate already-running E041 work under a new test name.
3. Create the fleet-wide holdout-burn register described below. Classify available
   evidence as development, permitted calibration, eligible sealed confirmation,
   burned, or excluded with a stated reason.
4. For the selected study, turn the corresponding draft prediction into a complete
   protocol. Its numeric forecasts, margins and budgets are currently null. Null
   values are incomplete commitments, not zero budgets or implied permission.
5. Build or independently check the evaluator and strong comparators using
   development inputs. Validate numerical and information boundaries before
   scientific scoring. Keep this work outside the hidden confirmation outcomes.
6. Once a particular study is assigned for execution and its registration is complete,
   run only that registered study within its declared resource and stopping rules.
   Until then, prepare protocols, challenge generators and registration materials.

THE EIGHT TESTS AND THEIR ORDER

RDT-P01 — Long-horizon forecasts from short observations across apparatuses.
Freeze a compact model and limited calibration, then predict native reconstruction
and historical-specificity outcomes on new horizons, disturbance regions and
apparatus variants. Compare strong ordinary forecast models at matched observation
and fitting cost. Do not refit transfer scales on hidden outcomes.

RDT-P02 — Continuation variables in matched-current-performance states.
Freeze a matching rule and an RDT variable. Predict later divergence and intervention
effects after current performance and strong conventional summaries are matched.
Identical complete Markov states cannot differ because of an unrecorded history;
the proposed causal difference must be located in permitted state or apparatus.

RDT-P03 — Damage, renewal-time and resource boundaries.
Derive and freeze the predicted success/failure region before drawing its hidden
phase diagram. Test new severities, timings, loss distributions and resources.
Compare timescale, connectivity, ordinary error accumulation and coding models.
A curve chosen after seeing all systems is development, not cross-system prediction.

RDT-P04 — Unseen intervention compositions and order effects.
Fit or derive individual intervention maps, then forecast new combinations and
orders. Include predicted near-equivalence as well as predicted order sensitivity.
Nonlinearity alone is not the claim: quantitative prospective composition is.

RDT-P05 — Complementary historical information in body and record.
Cross controlled body-exclusive, record-exclusive and redundant message components.
Pass only the declared successor record to fresh recipients. Predict source-specific
recovery against strong Bayesian, associative and denoising alternatives. Audit all
continuing information sources. The conditional data-processing bound in the
registry is a standard assumption-dependent constraint, not an empirical RDT win.

RDT-P06 — Richer acquired competence and composition after turnover.
After feasibility development, increase independently distinguishable task identity
and reserve unpracticed input combinations. Separate acquisition, reconstruction,
generalization and adaptation. Count training, search, task-specific support and
renewal cues. Do not relabel the current binary response or E/M relay as general
competence. A later unprescribed-competence claim must join useful acquisition and
durable inheritance in the same lineage.
Include selective stabilization versus equally costly indiscriminate stabilization.
Forecast both favorable and unfavorable regimes, and score retained old competence
jointly with performance on new compositions.

RDT-P07 — Turnover of task-specific decoder and support state.
Develop an explicit mutable registration, calibration, addressing or communication
layer. Freeze which jointly maintained constraints should suffice or be necessary.
Test their turnover and selective loss without leaving a task-specific rescue route
outside the budget. Universal task-independent simulator laws remain supplied;
this test does not require the system to recreate physical laws.
Forecast where joint content/interpretation maintenance is worth its cost and where
it is not. Compare content protection alone, protected external machinery,
separately renewed components and joint maintenance under the declared loss contract.

RDT-P08 — Consequential repair choice at a fixed budget.
When an RDT model becomes actionable, freeze its observation/action policy and
costed native objective. Evaluate decisions on hidden contexts against strong
heuristics, learned selection and ordinary control. Improved prediction alone is
insufficient. Include no-headroom and adverse contexts under the registered rule.

Tests P01-P04 form the first independent round. P05 and the first feasible P06 study
come next. P07 and the more ambitious acquisition-to-inheritance bridge require
further architecture development. P08 can accompany any round once a model supplies
a feasible frozen action policy. This order is a research plan, not a compute launch.

REGISTRATION CONTRACT

Each confirmatory study must freeze, before relevant outcomes:

- The exact RDT mathematical/model version and domain of validity.
- The causal boundary and complete manifest of carried state, mutable support,
  public/task-specific scaffolding, external inputs and observation feedback.
- Permitted calibration inputs, measurement channels, probe duration and numeric
  budget, with all apparatus-specific fitting declared.
- Numeric forecasts or bounded quantitative predictions for native outcomes,
  including any uncertainty procedure and transfer normalization.
- Primary estimand and outcome, a meaningful effect/prediction/equivalence margin,
  independent unit, sample/resource plan, and stopping and inclusion rules.
- Strong comparator implementations, accessible information, training and tuning
  budgets, and the distinction between feasible policies and a costly oracle.
- The hidden population, family/parameter/intervention/task split, seed-generation
  procedure and permitted calibration/holdout boundary.
- A scoring plan that retains failures and handles related outcomes, multiple
  claims and any hierarchical or conjunctive decision rule explicitly.
- The observations that would reject or narrow the exact claim.
- A sealed prediction artifact, protocol identity and outcome-access record.

Do not let a study switch after unblinding between superiority, equivalence,
noninferiority, data efficiency, or a favorable subgroup. Choose the primary claim
before the evaluation. An unresolved difference is not evidence of equality.
Use development-only variance and scientific relevance to choose numeric margins;
no empirical forecasts or completed results are supplied by this charter.

FLEET-WIDE HOLDOUT-BURN REGISTER

Maintain one shared accounting register even if hidden inputs are independently
held. Suggested fields for every dataset, run, block, task family or reserved region:

holdout_id; prediction_id; theory_version; protocol_id; apparatus/version;
cohort/seed-family identity; parameter/topology/task/intervention domain;
independent unit; intended role; generation status; outcome availability;
first scientific outcome time; prediction-seal time; first access time;
accessing lanes/people; derived summaries exposed; inherited source/parent IDs;
content or generator hash; secret-generation custodian where applicable;
current classification; burn reason; replacement plan; immutable provenance links.

Roles include DEVELOPMENT, PERMITTED_CALIBRATION, ELIGIBLE_SEALED_CONFIRMATION,
BURNED_TO_DEVELOPMENT and EXCLUDED_WITH_REASON. Preserve the prior classification
and change history. A new filename or random seed does not erase family overlap.
An inspected summary, plot, favorable example or another lane's advice can burn a
holdout even if its raw array was never opened. Register exposure across the whole
relevant fleet, not just the current evaluator's context.

Use independently held inputs and delayed releases where practical. In a shared
workspace, a convention not to inspect a folder is useful procedural blinding but
is not a technical access-control guarantee. Do not claim stronger separation than
was actually achieved.

ONGOING E041 WORK

Existing or ongoing E041 variants are development for a newly proposed RDT claim
unless the exact claim, inputs and scoring were genuinely sealed before the relevant
outcomes, with the permitted calibration boundary and access history documented.
A later forecast cannot retroactively make known results prospective. A launch date
alone does not establish either independence or contamination; inspect the exact
outcome and access history. Do not discard useful existing work, and do not rerun
it simply to give it an RDT label. Use it to improve the next theory version and
reserve new scientific conditions for the later independent test.

RESOURCE AND MEASUREMENT DISCIPLINE

Keep retained history, present expression and future reconstructibility separate.
Keep terminal success separate from success at every recorded intermediate readout;
neither asserts correctness at every instant between samples. Keep activity-written
repair separate from direct copying and from selection of a canonical supplied form.

Use native endpoints: qualified spatial agreement/preference, task success, and
cellular retention/onward transmission are not one pooled accuracy measure. Freeze
any proposed dimensionless mapping before testing it. Maintain amplitude thresholds,
all-site denominators, observer definitions, and continuous margins where relevant.
Changing the observer does not by itself change the underlying dynamics.

Count the actual independent experimental unit. Repeated futures, cue readouts,
related forks, parameter cells, edge opportunities and numerical resolutions do not
automatically create independent founders or biological replicates. Do not turn
selected deterministic cellular panels into population success probabilities.

Count acquired records, mutable decoder/controller state, public task-specific
codebooks, protected templates, labels, coordinates, calibration observations and
continuing external cues. Packet allocation is not measured information content.
A held-out decoder may demonstrate recoverable task information; its failure does
not prove all information has disappeared.

Match the information-loss contract, damage opportunities, writable capacity,
training/search compute, read/write time, communication, probes and intervention
cost. Keep protected exact copying as a preservation reference and constrained
copying as a different practical comparator. Give strong conventional alternatives
the same available information. Do not convert dimensionless simulation costs into
physical energy or biological fitness without a validated mapping.

Use both a representative hidden population and a separately identified adversarial
panel designed to expose disagreement between RDT and conventional models. Do not
choose only favorable cases or only conditions with known intervention headroom.

NUMERICS, IMPLEMENTATION AND FAILURES

Qualify source preparation and downstream integration together where required.
Preserve registered numerical stop conditions and report continuous margins around
classification thresholds. A stopped study supplies a stopped-study result, not
completed transmission evidence. Keep historical errors and fixes visible.

Document each implementation defect and whether it changes scientific semantics.
A correction covered by a frozen rule may be rerun as that rule permits. A change
to the theory, mechanism, input contract, target, analysis or decision margin after
outcome access creates a new version and needs fresh confirmation. Never silently
waive a gate or reinterpret an earlier failure as a pass.

Distinguish rejection of a particular quantitative RDT claim from rejection of all
reconstruction-based research. If an established conventional account explains the
result as well, report that no added predictive novelty was demonstrated. A narrower
law proposed after evaluation remains a new developmental hypothesis.

REPORTING AND HONEST INDEPENDENCE

For every study report: the sealed claim; native apparatus and information boundary;
allowed calibration; independent units and all denominators; forecasts and outcomes;
strongest comparator; complete pass/fail/unresolved verdicts; costs; numerical and
implementation limitations; holdout exposure; and what was not tested. Make the
selected core evidence reproducible from portable inputs and extraction/scoring
code, not only a retained passing audit report.

Call separately implemented and prospectively held-out work what it is: independent
checking within the same human-directed program. Do not call it independent external
replication. Encourage later external reproduction of the strongest compact result.

Your success criterion is an informative test, not a favorable verdict for RDT.

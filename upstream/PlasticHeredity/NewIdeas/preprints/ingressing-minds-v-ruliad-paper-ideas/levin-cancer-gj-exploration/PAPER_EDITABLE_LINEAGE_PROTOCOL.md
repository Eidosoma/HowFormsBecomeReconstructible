# P11: an editable lineage, rebuilt under bounded clocks

Registered 2026-09-15 before any P11 biological integration. User authorized the
end-to-end rewrite-and-inherit-again experiment. All P1-P10R executed files and
artifacts stay unchanged. This does not include a timing-noise panel or autonomy.

## Question and fixed method

Can three freshly written M carriers transmit M through three complete replacements,
then have their actual current carriers rewritten to E and transmit E through three
further complete replacements? Every old carrier is removed at every handover.
Temporary writing cues are removed before retention and onward assays. The same
17-dimensional live states, never just labels or selected components, cross stages.

Use the pinned native biological equations and the already verified P10R bounded
explicit Euler adapter. All new calls, INCLUDING preparation, use that adapter:
sample every 100 units including zero/exact endpoint, max step 100 coarse/50 fine,
integer base ticks, complete proposed/accepted step traces. Fine also halves base
step and adaptive increments. This is an explicit numerical-method extension,
not an unchanged-native-integrator replication or proof of convergence.

Prepare the native P3 reference setup anew at each resolution: 13x13 sheet,
the same fixed 4x4 corner-offset M seed, 1,000-unit native preparation procedure.
Use fixed E cell 168 and M cell 1 of the NEW live endpoint to initialize fresh
external stock cells. The archived preparation INITIAL array is only an independent
initialization witness. No historical final state seeds any P11 scientific branch.
All downstream fine branches use their own newly prepared fine stock and prefixes.
Preparing digital stock copies is external provisioning, not simulated cell division.

Writing: six cue cells plus three fixed targets in the P8 3x3 layout, depol,
power 60, Kd 1.25e-14, 20k contact; remove all cues; isolate all targets for two
10k holds. No-cue controls isolate the same three targets for the full 40k.
RNA controls remove miRNA transport; boundary-cut controls disconnect cues from
targets while retaining within-group routes. Same-state shams retain cue count.

Inheritance: the P10R six-cell/eight-edge common graph, three teachers and three
pre-existing pupils. Sequential contacts 0-20k, 20-40k, 40-60k in an 80k window.
All six cells evolve throughout, including waiting cells; only contact masks change.
Remove every teacher; isolate all three actual pupils for 10k+10k. Each round is
100k. No pooling, successful-subset selection, extra donors, reset of waiting cells,
or teacher carryover. Targets must retain the desired label at the live endpoint
and >=95% of the 26 late samples in EACH hold. Complete the whole first failed
round before stopping. No early truncation of a bad pupil or substitute branch.

## Registered stages and scientific gates

1. **Stocks (2 cases).** New coarse/fine preparation. Require correct initial
   witness, source/clock/physics checks and paired labels. E/M stock eligibility is
   separately assessed; no appropriate stock means a valid negative stop.
2. **Calibration (20 new; 22 cumulative).** At both resolutions:
   - Write M into three fresh E targets with M-open, E-sham, M-RNA, M-cut and
     no-cue conditions (five cases).
   - Age three ordinary M cells for matched 20k+10k+10k (one case).
   - One sequential E-to-M-recipient handover with ordinary E teachers: open,
     RNA-block and cut, plus M-teacher sham (four cases).
   Qualify only if M-open targets retain M, other writing targets retain E, aged
   ordinary M stays M, ordinary E transmits retained E to all initially-M pupils,
   and its controls leave all pupils M. A valid failure stops before long lineages.
3. **Before rewriting (12 new; 34 cumulative).** Start from the actual new
   M-written targets and time-matched ordinary M carriers. Open/RNA/cut for each,
   coarse/fine. Up to three complete M-to-fresh-E handovers. Proceed only if both
   open histories complete all three and blocked branches fail to transmit at
   their first round, leaving all pupils E, in both resolutions.
4. **Rewrite actual carriers (20 new; 54 cumulative).** Take all actual round-3
   pupils from each OPEN branch. Apply E-open, M-sham, E-RNA, E-cut and no-cue
   conditions; remove cues and perform both holds. Proceed only if E-open rewrites
   all three to E and the four controls retain M, in both histories/resolutions.
   Writing failure is a scientifically valid stop, not an excuse to tune the cue.
5. **After rewriting (20 new; 74 cumulative).** From each actual E-open held group,
   attempt rounds 4-6 into fresh M pupils under open/RNA/cut routes. Separately
   assay the actual M-sham and no-cue held groups through open contact with fresh
   M pupils. All five branches use an E-renewal stopping criterion and at most
   three rounds. Controls therefore normally stop after round 4. E recipients
   must START M at each first contact; cells merely staying E are not E teaching.
   A qualified editable lineage requires all six open handovers (three M, three E),
   successful intermediate rewriting, and negative stage-specific controls at
   both resolutions. Negative final outcomes remain reportable valid results.

The M founder histories share elapsed preparation/writing time, but isolated
ordinary M and cue-written M are not identical experiences. No history-specific
advantage is assumed. Counterfactual branches share prefixes; these are NOT
independent biological or random replicates. The six total handovers comprise
three before and three after rewriting, not six on each side.

Lineage logical time starts at initial writing/ordinary aging: 40k preparation
of carriers, 300k before-rewrite handovers, 40k rewriting, 300k afterward = 680k.
The earlier 1k stock preparation is recorded separately. Calibration has its own
local clocks. No historical P9/P10R lineage time is counted as new P11 time.

# Title and abstract review — v2.4, 28 October 2026

The author selected **How Patterns Return: Memory, Repair, and Renewal in Simulated Systems**. It replaces **How Forms Become Reconstructible: Acquired Records, Reciprocal Repair, and Functional Renewal** for v2.4. The earlier recommendation to retain the old main title was superseded by this explicit selection.

The main title offers a plain-language entry to the central question. The subtitle states the simulation scope immediately. The text retains the conditional definition of reconstructibility and its distinction from resemblance or uninterrupted performance. It accommodates spatial records, functional networks and cellular transmission without implying that the same mechanism was established in every apparatus. The multiscale work remains exploratory development, not a fourth confirmed mechanism or a biological validation.

After the author requested a broader opening, the abstract starts with the emergence of forms, change through experience, and persistence as parts and surroundings change. It then bounds the contribution to the tested relationship between present organization and retained history, explains what a record means, and separates remembering a history, present task performance and what a successor can reconstruct. It retains two distinct quantitative repair results: 81.52% to 87.45% for a fresh reconstruction, and 87.52% to 91.72% after eighteen further rebuilds in a separate repeated-future test. They are not pooled or described as a common cohort. Harm to some cases remains explicit.

Other retained findings are the small own-history effect through sixteen spatial replacements, declining measured reconstruction fidelity, the importance of writer timing, equal-read forecasting, and transmission differences behind a coarse cellular label. The multiscale sentence states both maintenance and the risk of writing body distortions into memory. General prediction, autonomy and living-system validation remain open.

This is an editorial pass, not a new evidence selection or analysis. The pre-edit abstract is preserved in [V2.4_ABSTRACT_BEFORE_PDF.md](V2.4_ABSTRACT_BEFORE_PDF.md). The manuscript, named v2.4 mirrors, running headers, PDF metadata and public citation metadata use the selected title. Existing file and repository paths remain stable for citation continuity.

The broader opening is a motivating question, not a claim that the paper explains the ultimate origin of forms. Acquisition, active body–record relationships and history-dependent possibilities remain part of the question; damage repair is one test within it. No ontology, general agency or biological treatment claim is added.

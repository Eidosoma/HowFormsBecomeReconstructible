## Equal-read analysis to recover from saved outcomes

Distinguish a constant chosen before testing from a descriptive prevalence-fitted baseline. Include fixed-bin reliability with record counts and block support, and current-pass/current-failure summaries under explicit weighting. Preserve all four original intervals and the all-four gate; label any newly computed descriptive calibration as post hoc and do not recalibrate the predictors.

The earlier unequal-budget cohort has absolute losses, and TT008 has calibration data. Neither belongs to this 80-block equal-read cohort. Do not fill the missing table using those values or reconstruct absolute scores from contrasts alone.

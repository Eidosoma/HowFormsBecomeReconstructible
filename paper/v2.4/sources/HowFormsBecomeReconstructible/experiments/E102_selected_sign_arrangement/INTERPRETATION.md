# E102 interpretation — selected sign arrangement contributes to reconstruction

24 October 2026. Scientific run `arrangement_v1` completed all **32 blocks at
10:17:52 UTC**, in **30 min 38.46 s**, with four one-thread workers. Both
prespecified primary requirements passed. Closeout and figure inspection passed
at **11:27:41 UTC**. No E102 process remains active.

Keeping the donor's sign arrangement improves rebuilding relative to a constrained
reshuffle of those signs. This comparison fixes each connection's magnitude,
support, outside record, normalization and the numbers of positive and negative
selected connections before and after injury. The successful transfer therefore
depends on more than its aggregate sign counts and absolute strengths. Which
aspect of the arrangement matters remains open.

## Primary results and absolute endpoints

independent-noise magnitudes; D takes both independent-noise components. B/M/D

within the four survival classes of the two fixed terminal injuries. All four

## What is controlled, and what remains open

The shuffle changes a mean **28.043 selected signs** per mixture record, while
retaining mean **60.113 selected connections**, absolute mass **3.17251** and
uninjured selected normalized input share **10.199%**. D/S positive/negative
counts match case by case: means **29.896 / 30.217** before injury and
**24.377 / 24.628** after injury. Their post-injury selected input share is
**10.334%**. The identical magnitudes, denominators, absolute couplings, support,
outside bytes and sign counts are exact controls, not merely matching averages.

Local incident sign counts and weighted sign allocation do change. For example,
the mean absolute change in the number of incident negative selected edges is
**0.16579 per cell** before injury and **0.14125** after injury. Mean selected
negative mass is **1.58556 for D / 1.59075 for S**, without equality at individual
records. The experiment does not separate local sign balance, placement on strong
edges, cue alignment, paths through the surrounding network or cycle consistency.
It does not establish a particular structural-balance mechanism.

The permutation uses only the two frozen injury masks, not outcomes, birth
states or trajectories. It is an injury-conditioned comparison, not an
injury-independent writing algorithm. Only one prespecified permutation is used
per record, with no rerolls; the bootstrap samples source blocks rather than
providing an exhaustive permutation test. All sources were known from E098–E101,
and those results informed this hypothesis. New terminal nuisances do not make
this fresh-source confirmation. No old scores enter new intervals or
new-minus-old comparisons. There are no new intact or outside-swap readers.

E101 established a conditional contribution of transferred signs. E102 adds a
causal perturbation of their arrangement while controlling strengths and aggregate
sign counts under injury. Neither result justifies a general biological mechanism
or a universal repair rule.

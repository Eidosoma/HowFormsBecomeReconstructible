# Task content in preserved backup signs

This development experiment tests whether backup signs taken from another
lineage carry a distinction related to its taught task. The previous hybrid
outperformed a sign permutation, but that manipulation could disrupt a useful
network without identifying inherited task content. The failed P04 additive
forecast and uncertain hybrid advantage over incoming records remain unchanged.

## Cohort and donors

Retain all 64 exposed generation-14 failures in blocks 34000 through 34007.
Use each recipient's actual incoming record I and body-written outgoing record O.
The incoming record is the generation-13 output. For recipient founder f, take
the generation-13 records of founders f XOR 2 and f XOR 3, with the same block,
recipient index and writer history. The first donor was taught the recipient's
task; the second was taught its opposite. Both donors belong to the other
founder pair and share their own native random context. Donors are selected
by these identities, without reference to their subsequent success.

The earlier full snapshots contain every donor, including successful states
outside the selected 64 recipients. Their records and metadata are already
development material. The donors are different lineages within a shared
experimental block; they are not independent new blocks.

Let A mark edges available under the recipient's original injury. Let E contain
unavailable edges with nonzero I. Let K be E intersected with the nonzero
supports of both donors. For this fixed cohort, K contains 4,002 of 4,857 E
edges; each case has 49 to 72 K edges. Six cases have identical donor signs on
K. Retain them. No minimum disagreement, success or coverage filter is applied.

## Record interventions

All hybrids keep O on A. The four matched hybrids contain zero outside A and K.
On K they share the exact recipient magnitudes abs(I), differing only in signs:

| Record | Backup on K | Other originally unavailable edges |
|---|---|---|
| common_own | Signs of I | Zero |
| same_task | Signs of the same-task donor | Zero |
| opposite_task | Signs of the opposite-task donor | Zero |
| scrambled | A fixed permutation of I's K signs | Zero |

The permutation is keyed by namespace, block, backup_signs, history, founder
and recipient. Preserve sign counts; never redraw an identity permutation.
Locations, magnitudes, denominators and unsigned transport diagnostics are
exactly equal across these four records. Sign counts may differ between donors:
the intervention imports actual signs without imposing a new donor distortion.

Retain six additional options: I; O (backup deleted); the full own hybrid H
with I on every unavailable edge; a fully erased record; the complete same-task
donor record; and the complete opposite-task donor record. The full donor reads
measure retained task competence under the recipient's assessment context.
They are diagnostic controls, not evidence that every sign transplant preserves
a donor's full implementation. Full H measures the cost of restricting to K.
All ten options use 4,096-byte packets. Donor selection, access to incoming
records and templates, and protection during writing are supplied resources.
No total-resource equivalence or autonomous information-repair claim follows.

## Predictions and alternatives

The primary directional prediction is a positive equal-block difference in
recipient-task success: same_task minus opposite_task. A zero or negative
point difference fails that directional prediction in this development panel.
Report uncertainty and every block even when the point difference is positive.
Do not equate a zero-containing interval with equivalence.

The secondary content-replacement prediction is a positive difference in
opposite-task success: opposite_task minus same_task. A positive primary
difference with no donor-task replacement supports a narrower compatibility
interpretation. If original signs outperform both donor options, report that
specific comparison; do not infer a universal lineage requirement. If complete
donors fail to express their taught tasks, the transplant interpretation is
limited by donor competence; retain all cases in the primary analysis.

Score each read against both complete mappings. Recipient-task and opposite-task
success are mutually exclusive. Report both and neither; partial, mixed or weak
outputs belong to neither. A lower intended score alone is not replacement.

Prespecified other contrasts for recipient-task success are common_own minus
same_task, common_own minus opposite_task, common_own minus scrambled, same_task
minus outgoing, full_own minus common_own, full_own minus incoming, and full_own
minus outgoing. These qualify compatibility, retained backup benefit and support
restriction. They cannot substitute for a failed primary contrast.

An ordinary signed-network rival computes the already-used 64-step clamped
linear transport before native reads. For each case and fresh mask, q is its
minimum task-aligned transfer over the two output ports. It prefers same_task
when q_same is larger, opposite_task when q_opposite is larger, and ties on
exact equality. No fitting, tolerance search or probe-depth selection occurs.
The unsigned rival predicts a tie because its inputs are identical. Also score
the fixed task-label rule that always prefers same_task.

For each rule, report mean preference times (Y_same minus Y_opposite), where
preference is +1, -1 or 0, and Y indicates recipient-task success. This paired
ordinal reward is zero for outcome ties. Report discordant-read accuracy with
half credit for predicted ties, its denominator, every block and descriptive
uncertainty. Higher signed-network reward than the task-label rule favors an
ordinary compatibility account. No calibrated success probability is claimed.

There is no invented quantitative effect-size target. This tests a directional
mechanism and a fixed ordinal rival within development material. It is a first
step toward P05, which ultimately requires larger message families and direct
crossing of surviving body content with damaged records. Neither this assay nor
a successful ordinary network prediction uniquely validates RDT.

## Native assessment and inference

Use the unchanged native reader for 2,048 updates, with 1,024 settling updates,
dt 0.2, noise 0.02, birth SD 0.05, clipping at plus/minus 2, and both clamped cues.
The endpoint requires all four task-aligned output means over the last 32
updates to exceed 0.25 strictly. There is no writing during assessment.

For every case use 16 fresh uniformly sampled 24-cell non-port masks, each
paired with a fresh reset/noise stream. The mask key is namespace, block,
injury, draw, founder//2, recipient; the read key is namespace, block, read,
draw. All options share recipient-context masks, resets and noise. The native
matched-cue noise remains supplied. The namespace is P05_backup_task_swap_v0_1.

Use one new-noise read, draw 0, under the original injury for exact controls.
Under that injury O and all five hybrid options must yield identical trajectories.
The erased record must give identical cues and succeed at neither mapping.
Separately replay each known original incoming read with its original stream
and require byte identity of reset, endpoint and output. The scientific/control
assessment contains 64 x 10 x (16 + 1) = 10,880 trajectories, plus 64 known replays.

Eight blocks remain the inference units. Weight cases equally inside each
block and blocks equally overall. Report pooled counts separately. Use 20,000
whole-block bootstrap resamples with seed 2026101003 and common resample indices
for paired contrasts. Per-case Wilson intervals describe conditional Monte Carlo
uncertainty. Task-label balance, donor identity and current failures are fixed
cohort properties. No post-outcome subgroup changes the primary estimand.

## Validation and execution

Bind the completed P04 run, its prepared inputs, both full generation-13 source
snapshots and the reused code. Save donor identities and bytes, K, coverage,
sign differences, masks, both task scores, native windows and endpoint states.
Independently reconstruct donor selection, K, records, permutations, masks,
diagnostics, all scores, tables, contrasts and fixed-rival rewards. Audit the
first case of each block with a separate flattened reader for all ten options:
draw 0 under original injury and draw 4 under fresh injury.

Test ordering invariance, exact controls, dual-task scoring, zero common support,
identical donors, donor reconstruction and corruption detection. Test the full
analysis/report/verification interface with nontrivial synthetic outcomes,
including a failed primary contrast and donor-task replacement. The benchmark
uses ten duplicated known incoming records and two duplicated original streams
in the largest block. It cannot reveal new scientific outcomes.

Forecast time as 1.5 times the benchmark seconds times the ratio of scientific,
known-replay and independent-audit updates to benchmark updates, plus 180 seconds.
Require a forecast at most 3,600 single-thread seconds. If it exceeds the ceiling,
preserve the result and do not shrink the science to force launch. Use one
persistent detached worker, within the shared 12-CPU ceiling, with all numerical
libraries set to one thread. Freeze before launch and execute the frozen copy.
Analysis, independent verification, report and figures run automatically.
Durable status, logs and failure receipts are required. Do not poll automatically
or launch a successor. Figure inspection occurs at the next requested review.

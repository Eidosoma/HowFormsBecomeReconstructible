# TT007 bounded source review

The two P02 final snapshots and their definition snapshots were copied and verified after user authorization. Both are exposed development evidence. The residual package reuses P02 predictions, makes no new fits, and is exploratory. TT007 does not treat either timestamp as prospective outcome blinding.

Manual review of the retained spacing `inputs/dynamics.py`, lines 19–22, 143–168 and 170–192: rebuild stream keys contain block and generation, and assay keys contain block and horizon. Neither contains writer/history identity. Damage masks likewise omit history; birth states and each noise draw broadcast along the writer axis. Within a context this shares the disturbances across histories. Paired founders and matched cues share further randomness, so the 512 records are not 512 independent replications. The pulse implementation uses the same stream construction, rebuild and assay functions. TT007 checks these function definitions for AST identity and checks both runs' common dynamics configurations. These checks do not replay the time evolution.

The spacing config declares O throughout, P at generation 3 only, spaced T at 3/5/7/9/11/13, and clustered T at 3/9/10/11/12/13. All selected histories use O at and after generation 14; repeated T is excluded. The pulse `policies_at` function chooses its declared initial policy at generation 3 and later policy thereafter. The saved spacing case arrays independently retain writer codes. The original histories' generation-14 bodies, mean-output windows, outgoing packets, birth states and damage agree across the two source runs; original and single-pulse score arrays are also checked across both runs by TT007.

Saved native provenance is narrower than the field name `native_replay_passed` alone might suggest. The spacing receipts retain full original-control replay, pulse-prefix checks, independent capture verification and reused-control verification. The native completion explicitly disclaims a full independent replay of new-arm dynamics. The pulse receipts retain original-control replay and schedule/support checks, not full independent replay of every new-arm nonlinear trajectory. TT007 reconstructs every selected O capture and transfer diagnostic, but uses saved temporal mean outputs and terminal margins. A trajectory audit of all new arms remains outside this audit.

The theory note's finite-horizon reduction is valid under its stated assumptions: expression-level lumpability at every transition and a terminal payoff measurable from expression imply an expression-measurable continuation probability by backward induction. They are sufficient assumptions, not established properties of these runs. A 0.02 expression caliper supplies no probability bound without regularity such as a justified Lipschitz constant; one realized endpoint does not identify a state's continuation probability. Conventional hidden-state and nonlinear prediction remain live explanations. No new theorem or uniquely reconstructive mechanism follows from the score increment.

For fitting and score reconstruction TT007 uses the submitted record matrix **after** checking every entry against independently reconstructed native measurements. This retains the exact measured decimal values used by the submitted fits and avoids interpreting rounding-level changes in sparse transfer arithmetic as different matching or decision rules. Reconstructed native measurements and their maximum discrepancies are saved separately. Refit probabilities and aggregate contrasts are also retained separately from the coefficient-based reconstruction of submitted probabilities.

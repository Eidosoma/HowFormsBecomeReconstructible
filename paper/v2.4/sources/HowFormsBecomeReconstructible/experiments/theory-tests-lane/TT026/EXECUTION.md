# TT026 execution

The user authorized implementation, qualification, benchmarking and execution
of the writing-window proposal. Source and original first-episode states are
copied into this study and checked against completed TT024/TT025 evidence.
Every production repair was computed with its own writing gate and
independently replayed; the full-writing variants must also match retained
native controls. Only unchanged packets share receiving assays.

The fixed qualification uses two excluded blocks at five updates. The
benchmark uses those excluded bodies at all 1,000 updates, with the complete
writing-budget grid. Its runtime and storage determine the frozen bound.
No production outcome is used for qualification or sample-size selection.

Two single-thread workers stay within the shared 12-CPU ceiling. A forecast
over ten minutes requires detached execution, unique log/PID, stdin disconnected
and durable status. Give one startup acknowledgment, then no automatic polling,
retry, sample extension or successor. Production was subsequently frozen and launched as recorded below.

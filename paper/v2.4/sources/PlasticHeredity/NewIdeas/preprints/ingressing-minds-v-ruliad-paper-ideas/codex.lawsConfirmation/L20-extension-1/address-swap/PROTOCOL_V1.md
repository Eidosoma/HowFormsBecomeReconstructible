four lives, A/B targets, ordinary/aligned writers and U0/U1. Pin D168's complete

For each life/group construct all four cells Y[c,a], where c is the saved
copy's body/noise history (0 or 1) and a is the donor of the assigned feedback

shift changes with a. Donor labels do not change noise or starting bodies.
Same-cell diagonal Y[0,0],Y[1,1] must reproduce every original saved writer/
fresh-reader checkpoint, exported/routed packet, selector score and metadata.
Off-diagonal Y[0,1],Y[1,0] are the matched counterfactuals.

Retain all four cells, including equal shifts and equal ideal q. Equal q is
not necessarily an identical spatial operation. Aligned writers ignore the
shift, so changing a must leave their complete path and output bit-identical.

on the initially zero feedback. Ordinary/aligned matched starts also agree.
Keep full saved paths for verification, not just endpoint scores.

address may change as a downstream consequence. Freeze the guide, not its
decision. Do not inject the original routed output into the counterfactual.
Writer code has no target, guide or incoming-packet access. Target family is
used only in observation metrics. No initial reader is resimulated.

writer0 at 0,1,300,1000; outgoing-record best-own shape; outgoing guide margin;

correlation is not complete shape preservation. Record ideal q for each donor,

Primary mechanistic focus is development B-ordinary outgoing-own shape,
with the pilot counterpart reported separately and prominently. Retain all
groups and seven responses with no new success threshold or preferred cases.
For each block/life/group/response define:

- Original gap G = Y[1,1] − Y[0,0].
- Swapped gap S = Y[1,0] − Y[0,1].
- Matched address contrast A = ((Y[0,1]−Y[0,0])+(Y[1,1]−Y[1,0]))/2.
- Body/noise-background contrast B = ((Y[1,0]−Y[0,0])+(Y[1,1]−Y[0,1]))/2.
- Interaction I = (Y[1,1]−Y[1,0])−(Y[0,1]−Y[0,0]).
- Separate within-background address effects E0 and E1, the two differences
  averaged in A, and direct swap effects E0 and −E1 for U0 and U1.

Verify G=A+B and S=B−A casewise and in summaries. A is an address intervention
contrast conditional on these frozen model states/noise/guide, not D168's
observational category-mixture term. B bundles body and all retained copy
noise differences; it does not isolate germ quality or one noise phase. I
describes background dependence; A averages both backgrounds even if I is
nonzero. Do not divide by G or call a result a fraction of causation explained.

Report every cell mean, per-founder mean and all seven contrast vectors
(G,S,A,B,I,E0,E1), positive/negative/numerical-zero counts (1e−12 tolerance),

Intervals are descriptive conditional sampling summaries, not equivalence,

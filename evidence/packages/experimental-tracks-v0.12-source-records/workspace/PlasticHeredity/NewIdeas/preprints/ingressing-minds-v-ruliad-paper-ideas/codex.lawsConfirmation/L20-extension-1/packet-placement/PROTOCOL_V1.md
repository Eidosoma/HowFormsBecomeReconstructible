For each role/control/generation report the four absolute scores and:

- Natural change: Y(L,L)−Y(E,E).
- Content change at E placement: Y(L,E)−Y(E,E).
- Content change at L placement: Y(L,L)−Y(E,L).
- Placement change for E: Y(E,L)−Y(E,E).
- Placement change for L: Y(L,L)−Y(L,E).

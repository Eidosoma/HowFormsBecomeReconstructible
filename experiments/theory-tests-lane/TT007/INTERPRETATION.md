# TT007: independently reproduced, mixed P02 evidence

The independent audit passed. All 512 O packets matched byte for byte; the largest signed/unsigned transfer discrepancy was 5.55e-17. All 3,072 saved predictions, 240 block/history score rows, 128 pairs and 504 strict-match decision rows were reconstructed. Forty logistic fits were recomputed with an independent Newton optimizer, agreeing within 7.06e-6 in probability and 1.71e-7 in fold Brier error. The 129 copied source files, 145 frozen inputs and 19 completed outputs verified against their commitments. Six synthetic check groups passed, including held-out-label and preprocessing isolation in both implementations. See the [complete report](runs/audit_v1/REPORT.md), [audit receipt](runs/audit_v1/audit.json) and [figure](runs/audit_v1/tt007_audit.png).

The specified primary comparison is positive: adding signed record transfer lowers excluded-block Brier error from **0.092186 to 0.087626**, improving six of eight blocks. The independent refit gives a gain of 0.004560084, compared with the submitted 0.004560110. This is a reproduced development increment under the declared common future writer and measurement contract.

The primary candidate still trails the simpler expression/history model, whose Brier error is **0.085260**. The secondary expression/unsigned/signed model reaches **0.080065**, improving on expression/unsigned at 0.087577. Preserve the secondary designation: choosing the best observed model after reviewing these results cannot establish a prospective advantage.

The later exploratory split reproduces a more specific lead. The 64 currently failing bodies contribute +0.004809 to the overall primary gain; the 448 currently passing bodies contribute −0.000249. Twenty-nine of those 64 failures succeed in the later fresh scattered assay. This is an association with a changed future endpoint after continued rebuilding, not an estimated probability of recovery under the original injury. It needs a new frozen evaluation.

Matching remains unpersuasive as evidence of an individual record-based choice advantage. Among 84 pairs satisfying both current passes and the 0.02 output caliper, spaced history succeeds 75 times and clustered history 74 times. Only nine pairs have discordant endpoints. Current margin selects seven of those nine correctly, raw signed transfer and the primary signed model six, and the secondary signed model five. No pair has exactly equal output vectors. Those facts do not establish different latent continuation probabilities at equal expression, and post-treatment matching does not isolate a direct causal record effect.

The theory note's sufficiency implication is mathematically sound under its explicit expression-lumpability and terminal-measurability assumptions. The experiments do not establish those assumptions, their failure, or a uniquely reconstructive explanation. Ordinary hidden-state and nonlinear prediction models remain relevant rivals.

Native provenance has a material limit. Saved source receipts include original-control dynamics replay, schedule checks, independent captures and reused-control checks; they do not cover a full independent nonlinear replay of every new arm. TT007 independently reconstructs the captures and linear diagnostics, and verifies saved score consistency, but consumes saved temporal means and terminal margins. It generates no new native trajectories. The [source review](SOURCE_REVIEW.md) specifies this boundary and the shared-randomness dependence.

The scientific status remains **unconfirmed, mixed development evidence**. These eight blocks were already exposed. Neither 512 related records nor overlapping training folds create independent replications. This audit does not alter P01's missing native observation/transition validation or turn its earlier short-probe findings positive.

The next useful experiment should freeze one candidate and endpoint, stronger conventional rivals including the simpler expression/history model, a meaningful improvement margin, and a sample-size/power plan before opening genuinely unexposed blocks. Repeated future draws from fixed captured states would separately address state-specific continuation uncertainty. Full nonlinear verification of new-arm dynamics remains a qualification requirement. No successor has been launched.

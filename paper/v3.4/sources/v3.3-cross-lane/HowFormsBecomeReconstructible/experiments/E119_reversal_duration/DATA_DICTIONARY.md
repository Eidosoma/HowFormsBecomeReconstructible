# E119–E121 retained evidence

All scientific inference uses 32 whole source blocks. Founders, recipients,
histories, policies, cue episodes, assessment draws and horizons are nested
observations. No alias, duplicated baseline or additional policy creates an
independent source. Benchmarks have one excluded technical source and shortened
reads; their outcomes cannot support scientific claims.

## Identities and generations

`task` identifies an original E077 block (E119/E120) or a fresh E121 block.
`founder` 0–3 contains two opposite-task founder pairs; `recipient` is 0–3.
`kind` is native, mixture, erased, or the separately assessed acquired record.
`history` is sham/pulse; acquired and erased controls use `none`.

Generation 0 is the once-pruned or intact packet after the initial recovery
writer. Generation 1 is its actual next written record; E119 continues to 4.
These are local postintervention horizons, not the absolute ancestry count.
E121 generation −1 denotes the first committed acquired packet, before ancestry.

`regime` identifies matched/independent ongoing cue noise and exact/tolerant
capture. Reset correlation remains 1 in all three new studies. `renewal_injury`
is `none` or `repeated`; independent terminal assessments always have 24 injured
nonport cells. `reader=common` uses matched terminal cue noise; `native` follows
the corresponding E121 regime. Mechanism tables inherit the comparison label
`common`, but their supplying body always uses the named **writer regime**;
this field does not override that body's noise setting.

Policies I/P/E are intact/original once-pruned/erased in E119/E120. E121 uses
I/PF/PR/E: intact, fallback-defined pruning, route-independent restoration
pruning, and the erased source branch with its actual successors.

## Tables

- `cases.csv`: every observation, including unchanged cases and empty operations.
  `both` requires both cue margins strictly > .25. Both cue margins, minimum
  margin, signed/aligned transfer, wrong-sign and threshold classifications
  remain. `selected_edges`, `reisolated_cells`, `selected_coupling_share` refer
  to the original once-only intervention, before assessment injury. Intact rows
  carry its paired intervention's diagnostics, not a claim that intact was pruned.
- `events.csv`: nonzero treatment-minus-intact binary effects at each horizon,
  with both binary endpoints and the generation-0 effect. Full unchanged cases
  and both records' continuous scores remain in `cases.csv`.
- `recovery_times.csv` (E119): per matched assessment draw, first successful
  generation among 1–4, first success sustained through the last observed
  generation, and cumulative failed assessments over 1–4 and 0–4. Code 5 is
  right censoring, not measured recovery at generation 5. `generation0` retains
  the initial success class. These assess capacity; `mechanism.csv` separately
  retains the actual supplying bodies' outcomes.
- `mechanism.csv`: every writer's selected fallback/contrast counts, incoming
  and outgoing isolation, weak positive denominators, activity contrast on
  initially re-isolated available cells, selected mass and actual body outcomes.
- `summary.json`: block means, pointwise intervals, duration simultaneous
  pruning bands, all conjunctive gates, zero-operation counts, individual
  transitions, temporal summaries and descriptive re-isolation associations.
  Technical mode always has `scientific_claim_allowed=false`.

## Arrays and exact assessment aliases

Packets are uint8 views of 512 little-endian float64 reciprocal edge weights,
4,096 bytes per record. Decoded edge axes are orientation × 16 × 16. Stored
effective weights already reflect the physical injury mask. Denominators are
the incident absolute-weight sums; coupling has four directed neighbor entries
per cell. Both initial activity and final cue states are retained.

Writer files preserve incoming and outgoing packets, masks, body state,
port windows, every-step hashes, ordinary/contrast captures and fallback flags.
`pre_channel` and `outgoing` coincide in these unquantized experiments. This is
not a promise that finite-threshold and exact captures coincide.

Assessment calls deduplicate only whole-context packets with identical bytes
and identical cue-noise rules. `*_aliases_*.npz` stores `unique_slots` in the
requested slot order and `aliases` mapping each requested slot to a computed
trajectory; the assessment NPZ contains only those unique trajectories.
All requested observations remain under their original identities in the CSV.

E119's combined generation-0 call orders the 9 slots for no-renewal-injury,
then the identical 9 for repeated injury. Each block of 9 is four intact,
four pruned and one erased slot, with native sham/pulse then mixture sham/pulse.
E120 orders the four regimes as matched exact, matched tolerant, independent
exact, independent tolerant, each with the same 9 slots.
E121 orders 13 slots per regime: four I, four PF, four PR, one E. Its combined
immediate/successor assessment puts all common-reader slots first, then native
slots. The acquired assay puts native slots first, then common slots. The two
reader labels coincide exactly in matched regimes and reuse the same read.

Assessment seed namespaces are separate from all writers. Draw 0 and draw 1
are independent; each is reused across treatments and horizons. The same
assessment environment therefore compares record changes rather than a change
of assessment injury. Saving an alias does not transmit that reader's body back
into any lineage.

## Verification and completion

`manifest.json` and `inputs/` pin code, protocols, source hashes and settings.
`block_*.json` reports independent trajectory replay and stage timing.
`CHECKS.json` states exact verification scope. `COMPLETE.json` hashes retained
outputs; it is not a claim that the scientific primary passed. Scientific
interpretation follows full-cohort completion and separate output verification.

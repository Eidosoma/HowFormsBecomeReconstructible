# E083 interpretation — predictive information with limited individual recurrence

18 October 2026. **Complete and verified.** All 64 source blocks finished at
**13:16:19 UTC** in **1 h 39 min 28.49 s**, using four single-thread workers.
Exactly two fresh matched reader draws per saved record were run: 4,096 original
cases, 8,192 case/draw pairs, 106,496 terminal cue observations. Every new reader
state was independently replayed. Closeout passed **13:56:10 UTC**, verifying
552 original outputs, 32 frozen inputs, 2,376 summary checks and the inspected
figure. Run size is 1.074 GB. No added draw, source, rewrite or automatic successor.

Earlier mixture gains and harms carry predictive information about relative
new effects in both cohorts. Most individual gains and harms do not recur on
either of the two new reads. These findings coexist: a statistically positive
association does not make the old outcome a reliable label for each record.
The original E081 fresh-source primary remains unresolved; E083 is a separately
planned new-reader test on known records.

## The two primary tests passed

The alignment score centers old and two-draw-average new FF−OF binary effects
within each source block and history. This removes a block/history-wide average
benefit. All 32 blocks per cohort remain, including constant-label strata.

| Mixture primary | Mean alignment | 97.5% whole-block interval | Verdict |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| E080 records | 0.005524 | [0.000915, 0.011505] | POSITIVE_ALIGNMENT |
| E081 records | 0.004791 | [0.000458, 0.010712] | POSITIVE_ALIGNMENT |

The predeclared requirement of positive alignment in both cohorts is met.
These are dimensionless centered binary-effect products, not percentage points,
accuracy, explained variance, or proportions of stable records. Draws, founders,
recipients and histories remain nested within source blocks. Original outcomes
supply labels only; they are not pooled as a third fresh draw.

## Recurrence is limited, and all harms remain visible

| Original gains | 38 | 25 |
| Gain repeats on neither new draw | 28 | 18 |
| Gain repeats on exactly one draw | 7 | 6 |
| Gain repeats on both draws | 3 | 1 |
| Original harms | 6 | 17 |
| Harm repeats on neither new draw | 5 | 12 |
| Harm repeats on exactly one draw | 1 | 5 |
| Harm repeats on both draws | 0 | 0 |

Thus only **10/38 and 7/25** old gains recur at least once. These fractions
refer to original source cases, not independent new samples. The full transition
tables retain reverse-sign effects and all previously unchanged records.

The old-gain group's mean new signed effect is **+17.11 points [5.00, 29.17]**
in E080 and **+12.00 [−1.39, 28.00]** in E081. The old-harm group has **−8.33**
points in E080, with its interval deliberately null: **16/10,000** bootstrap
resamples contain no members of this small group. E081 old harms average
**−14.71 [−26.92, −3.33]** points. These group intervals are pointwise 95%, not
the primary family-adjusted tests. The E081 old-gain conditional interval is
unresolved even though its cohort's centered primary alignment is positive.

Across the two fresh draws, mixture FF versus OF produces **64 gains/22 harms**
in E080 and **45 gains/19 harms** in E081, each among **2,048 case/draw pairs**.
Previously unchanged records contribute **51/64 and 37/45** new gain observations,
and **21/22 and 12/19** new harm observations. These are repeated observations;
they are not counts of unique cases. Unique cases with a new pattern harm are
22 and 19; two E080 cases and one E081 case gain on one draw and lose on the other.

This supports reader dependence of observed individual outcomes. It does not
identify injury, birth or noise as the sole cause: all three changed together.
Two fresh draws also do not determine a precise long-run response probability
for each individual record or establish an invariant attractor classification.

## Scope and next lead

The evidence supports modest average benefits under the two fresh reader draws
and a positive association between earlier and later individual effects. It does
not establish a reliable targeting rule for untouched records, remove individual
harms, isolate the reader nuisance responsible, or turn E081's original unresolved
primary into a positive fresh-source confirmation. OF/FF still hold signs, support,
outside weights and preinjury selected total fixed; local normalization remains free.
The original cue pairing and normalization apparatus also remain unchanged.

A focused next intervention would test **where the selected magnitudes are placed**.
Compare OF and FF with versions that permute their selected nonzero magnitudes
within sign classes, preserving signs, support, the magnitude multiset and selected
total. Apply the same permutation to both patterns, and evaluate the change in
FF−OF advantage against the shuffled comparison. Both baselines are necessary
because generic damage from shuffling would otherwise be hard to interpret.
Keep all records, both endpoints and individual harms; freeze reader draws and
runtime before starting. This is a proposed mechanism test, not a fitted rule or
an implemented successor. Untouched-source validation would still be needed for
any later prediction rule. Additional reader draws merely to improve current
intervals are not warranted.

## Suggested manuscript wording

E083 evaluated all saved E080 and E081 records under two prespecified fresh matched
reader draws. Within-block/history alignment between original and new mixture
FF−OF success effects was positive in both cohorts (0.00552 [0.00092, 0.01151] and
0.00479 [0.00046, 0.01071], family-adjusted 97.5% block intervals). Individual
recurrence was limited: only 10/38 and 7/25 original gains recurred on either
fresh draw, and 3/38 and 1/25 on both. Mean fresh-read FF−OF effects were +2.05
[1.12, 3.08] and +1.27 [0.29, 2.20] percentage points at secondary pointwise 95%.
Fresh reads of both pruning endpoints retained immediate benefit and later cost.
These results establish predictive information in prior effects under the declared
reader ensemble, with substantial variation in individual outcomes. They do not
supply a new-source confirmation or revise E081's original primary verdict.

## Important qualifications

- The guard study's folder name includes “precision”, meaning a larger statistical sample. Its writer remains the native exact-zero writer. It does **not** resolve the manuscript's finite-writer-tolerance concern.
- The guard's earlier 16-block six-contrast test remains unresolved. The fresh 48-block study narrows the hypothesis after that development; the cohorts are not pooled. A matched-random-allocation development comparison uses exposed sources and shares the guard's group promotion quota. Its fresh-founder successor has a launch record but no locally retained completion.
- NP001 uses 16 fresh **development** blocks. Its successor records are protected during reading; repeated successor rewriting is untested there. The phase analysis has a disclosed post-result inversion-invariant check, which retains the direction. Both imposing bodies prefer the same spacing, so this is not a comparison between two different imposed native wavelengths.
- H095 has six finite-cohort screens, not a population-level confidence claim. Capability and retention pass; parent-policy benefit and superiority to all controls do not. H097's failed writer-precision audit must not be converted into a result from its fifteen passing blocks.
- TT045's Monte Carlo errors concern new futures conditional on saved records. Sixteen futures and multiple recipients do not create new founder blocks. The primary partial-retention comparison remains small and time-dependent.
- D197 is genuinely fresh-founder work, but only four histories. It supports a narrowly specified recurring secondary effect. A positive disturbance interaction can reduce a pre-existing deficit without producing superiority after disturbance.
- FEP's bounds concern expectations under a specified finite decision model. Exact fixed-noise guarantees do not imply per-episode protection, unknown-noise robustness or a biological controller. The simpler fixed-policy pool also passes the fixed interval.

# Repeated repair under cue noise mismatch

This authorized mechanism follow-up asks whether the fixed recipient-only repair improves uninterrupted reconstruction when the two cue episodes receive independent ongoing noise. It uses the exposed 256-record cohort from `p05_fresh_lineages_v0_1` and exactly replays two exposed arms from `p05_serial_repair_v0_1`. Only the four mismatched-noise arms generate new continuation outcomes. This is development evidence within one simulator, with no claim of a fresh-cohort validation, external replication or unique confirmation of RDT.

**Repeatability of paired repair benefit on the exposed 63-packet panel.**

The compact benefit predictor failed its fixed development gate and remains closed. Before proposing another predictor, this study asks whether terminal repair benefit has a reproducible packet-specific component. The earlier pilot supplied only one coupled future per packet and noise condition. A realized rescue is not an individual conditional effect.

All 63 selected generation-14 packets from the same sixteen acquisition blocks are retained. Four new future streams are run for every packet in both matched and independent cue noise. No original outcomes enter the new estimators. The design is informed by exposed outcomes and tests exposed founders; new randomness does not make it new-founder validation or external preregistration. Four repeats are a bounded measurement pilot, not a power-qualified confirmatory sample. They use one wave of four numerical workers, subject to measured runtime and the shared twelve-thread limit.

For packet i and new future r, let D_ir = Y_repaired − Y_control, including harms as −1. With fixed packet weights w_i summing to one, tau_i = E[D_ir] is its conditional mean benefit. The weights assign each acquisition block equal mass, its two current-expression classes equal mass, and equal mass to packets within a class. Each noise condition is analyzed separately.

The primary quantity is V = sum_i w_i tau_i² − (sum_i w_i tau_i)². Estimate it by averaging, over the six distinct pairs r<s, sum_i w_i D_ir D_is − m_r m_s, where m_r = sum_i w_i D_ir. Under independent future streams conditional on the fixed panel, E[D_ir D_is] = tau_i² and E[m_r m_s] = (sum_i w_i tau_i)². Therefore the estimator is unbiased for this finite-panel variance. This is an algebraic consequence of the declared independence assumption, not a new RDT law. A negative estimate is possible and is retained without clipping.

Separately estimate the average single-future noise W = sum_i w_i Var(D_ir) by the weighted within-packet sample variances across four repeats. Variance of single observed differences mixes W with V; it cannot alone establish heterogeneous conditional benefit. No correlation ratio is reported as a precision measure. Monte Carlo seeds implement independent streams by a distinct namespace, stage and replicate in their seed derivation; pseudorandom construction does not provide a mathematical proof of independence.

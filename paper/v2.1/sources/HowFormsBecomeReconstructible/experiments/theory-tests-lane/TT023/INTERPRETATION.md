# TT023: removing captured replacement fill recovers most of the first-rewrite loss

The controlled intervention supports a causal contribution of replacement fill
in captured memory to the first-rewrite loss on these exposed cases. With
writing active, subtracting the known nominal fill from initial memory raises
informative-record fresh-recipient recognition from **61.328% to 87.891%**, a
**26.5625-percentage-point** increase. All four block differences are positive:
14.0625, 35.9375, 23.4375 and 32.8125 points. The actual injured body, incoming
packet, physical equations and paired random environments stay fixed; only
memory initialization changes in this comparison.

This is the prespecified descriptive contrast in a mechanism experiment designed
after TT022 outcomes were exposed. There are four population blocks, 64 starting
positions per family and four original futures per position. No significance
gate or fresh-population confirmation is claimed. The gain is large within this
panel, but the intervention does not identify a unique general mechanism.

The full six-arm result retains the two endpoints separately:

| Memory and writing | Common: next recipient | Common: repaired body | Informative: next recipient | Informative: repaired body |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Full capture, writing on | 64.844% | 61.719% | 61.328% | 58.203% |
| Full capture, writing off | 69.141% | 67.188% | 89.844% | 78.516% |
| Nominal fill subtracted, writing on | 68.359% | 67.188% | 87.891% | 76.562% |
| Nominal fill subtracted, writing off | 69.141% | 66.406% | 89.844% | 80.078% |
| Zero initial memory, writing on | 67.969% | 66.406% | 81.641% | 78.125% |
| Zero initial memory, writing off | 69.141% | 67.578% | 89.844% | 77.734% |

Zero initial memory still develops normally during repair; memory feedback stays
active in every arm. All writing-off arms preserve the incoming packet exactly
and reuse its same checked receiving assay. Their identical recipient scores
are a consequence of that design, not three independent confirmations. The
body scores can differ because memory continues to affect the physical body.

The informative result improves over zero-memory writing by **6.25 points**
pooled, with gains in every block (6.25, 4.6875, 7.8125 and 6.25 points). Retaining
the surviving-body memory while subtracting nominal fill therefore helps more
than removing all initial memory on this panel. This contrast also retains
replacement-cell jitter, so it does not isolate surviving-body content alone.
Yet corrected writing remains **1.953 points
below preserving the incoming record**. It recovers most of the original loss;
it does not establish a need to rewrite this already useful packet. The design
adds no further record corruption.

Harms remain visible. Relative to full-capture writing, informative recipient
means improve for 39 of 64 positions, worsen for two and tie for 23. At the
paired future-draw level, 78 improve, ten worsen and 168 tie. Relative to
zero-memory writing, 19 record means improve, three worsen and 42 tie. Relative
to preserving the packet, corrected writing improves five record means,
worsens eight and ties 51. These four-future means are coarse conditional
estimates, not precise individual capacities or independent population samples.

The injury pattern agrees with the predicted direction and retains its adverse
side. Informative recipient recognition changes as follows:

| Surviving target content | Paired draws | Full capture/write | Fill-subtracted/write | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Positive half | 64 | 100.000% | 95.312% | −4.688 pp |
| Negative half | 64 | 35.938% | 87.500% | +51.562 pp |
| Both polarities | 128 | 54.688% | 84.375% | +29.688 pp |

The intended primary benefit is not uniform. Removing fill also removes an
advantage where its spatial contribution helped. The common-record pooled gain
is only **3.516 points**, with block effects +1.5625, +7.8125, +4.6875 and zero.
Common positive-half cases lose 15.625 points, negative-half cases gain 15.625,
and both-polarity cases gain 7.031. Common corrected writing is only 0.391 points
above zero-memory writing and 0.781 points below preserving the packet; 19
record means improve and eleven worsen versus full capture. Message and injury
strata were retained in the frozen analysis rather than selected after this
result.

Repaired-body recognition and record usefulness remain different measurements.
Informative corrected writing raises the body's score by 18.359 points versus
full-capture writing, yet its body score is 1.562 points below zero-memory
writing while its recipient score is 6.25 points higher. This is another reason
to measure both endpoints directly. The recipient writing interaction equals
the primary contrast by construction, because writing-off packets are identical;
it is not additional mechanistic evidence. The body interaction is 16.797
points on informative records and 6.25 points on common records.

The subtraction rule uses the supplied physical injury mask and resting value.
It leaves the actual body and replacement-cell jitter unchanged, but receives
information the original capture rule did not use. It supports a controlled
intervention effect with that resource. It does not establish autonomous injury
detection, equal-information superiority, long-term serial durability or
biological transfer. Restoring fixed starting records and using repeated
receiving environments connects this assay to the state-local probability
approach discussed in Plastic Heredity, while four futures and one repaired
episode retain substantial limits on individual and serial capacity claims.

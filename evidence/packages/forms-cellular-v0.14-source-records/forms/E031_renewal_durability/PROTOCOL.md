## Frozen decisions

Primary: own-packet, normalized-receiver, scattered-injury **both-cue success
after sixteen replacements** must have its two-sided whole-block 95% bootstrap
lower bound **>0.75**. This is absolute durability, not merely an advantage
over a deteriorating comparator. Separately, maturing minus full rewriting at
that endpoint must have lower bound **>−0.05** (noninferiority, not equivalence).

Require these **23 additional qualifications** for full durability:

- Maturing intact/scattered both-cue lower>0.75 at h8 and intact at h16 (3).
- Maturing passes the original 24 rule/injury/control gates at h8 and h16 (2).
- Maturing minus full rewriting, h8 scattered both-cue, lower>−0.05 (1).
- Maturing minus old exact-zero infill and minus matched-erasure copying,
  h8 and h16 scattered both-cue, lower>0.10 (4).
- Maturing minus its reading-blocked and refresh-blocked chains, h8 and h16
  scattered both-cue, lower>0 (4).
- Intact-renewal reference passes original 24 gates under both readers at
  h1, h3, h8 and h16 (8).
- All 12 full-rewriting read/write necessity contrasts pass accuracy
  lower>0.10 at h8/h16 for each injury with normalized receiving (1 conjunction).

Original gates: each of two rules under each of three injuries has own-packet
accuracy lower>0.75; own minus each of six terminal controls has accuracy
lower>0.10 under each injury. Resample whole independent blocks 10000 times
with namespace `["E031","bootstrap","maturing"]`. Report all block means,
scores, margins, contrasts, body function by generation and changes from h1
to h3/h8/h16. Secondary intervals and qualification gates are pointwise.
Report primary, noninferiority and additional requirements separately.
Full conjunction is `DURABLE_THROUGH_16_RENEWALS`, otherwise
`DURABILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Failure is a substantive result, not a reason
to change the policy. Technical results have null scientific decision flags.
Shorter successful horizons cannot replace the frozen primary endpoint.

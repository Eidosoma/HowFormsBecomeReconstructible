All three family-adjusted whole-block intervals exclude zero in the predicted
direction. Pooled success is **85.25%** for the final record, **30.86%** for

is incomplete. Substituting earlier magnitudes on changed-sign or unchanged-sign

more damaging. Final support and later-only raw weights remain fixed.

magnitudes; these are not equal-dose, per-edge or autonomous-repair estimates.

# Version 3.2 evidence revision

1 November 2026. This draft updates *How Patterns Return: Memory, Repair, and Renewal in Simulated Systems* from the current v3.1 source selection. It retains the four-chapter structure, title, twenty main figures, five supporting figures and the “inspired by” framing. It creates new named main/SI files and updates the canonical aliases.

## Main changes

After the evidence intake, the author's requested readability revision was applied to this same edition because no v3.2 PDF existed. The [outline](V3.2_READABILITY_OUTLINE.md), editorial baseline (see [source availability](../SOURCE_AVAILABILITY.md)) and [readability record](V3.2_READABILITY_REVIEW.md) distinguish that prose/structure pass from the scientific selection below. It shortens the abstract and opening route, retains the detailed tables as a main-paper reference guide, clarifies measured-figure titles, and separates repair from decisions about when to rewrite. The evidence ledger, SI methods, numerical displays and figure assets are unchanged by that pass.

The new evidence makes the account more specific. The original numerical apparatus repeatedly produces the present-performance/successor reversal, but the tested finite formulation performs poorly even when records form under it. That limitation now accompanies the main claim and appears in the abstract, status table, numerical explanation and conclusion.

The multiscale chapter now follows an experimental sequence: a body can maintain a compatible history, overwrite a nonpreferred one through actual serial renewal, use additional historical references to reject a misleading descending code, and detect some correction opportunities without a supplied corruption label. The latter has a fresh confirmation; the serial and nested-reference studies remain development. Supplied reference information is counted explicitly.

The repair chapter adds unmarked corruption and distinguishes recovering a response from recovering its historical identity. It pairs the positive fresh allocation result with the unresolved fresh increment from continual versus one-time rewriting. The competence discussion replaces the obsolete “numerical failure only” status with H098's corrected scientific failure, then adds H100's narrower successful decoder restoration with labelled reference observations and equally informed copying controls.

The theory discussion includes a frozen scale forecast and its unsuccessful comparisons. The FEP paragraph treats correlated observations as a testable independence assumption, retaining the cost/recovery failures and ordinary Bayesian comparisons.

## Source and audit boundaries

The baseline (see [source availability](../SOURCE_AVAILABILITY.md)) binds the prior sources and earlier editions. The [new manifest](V3.2_EVIDENCE_SOURCES.json.parts/README.md) binds 950 selected files in the frozen source archive, including the twelve new multiscale studies' exact implementation dependencies. All individual retained source files are below 256 KiB; the private compressed archive is about 3.6 MB.

The new forms interpretations and closeout receipts are available, but their full run tables and arrays are not in this checkout. Their original estimates and intervals are transcribed. TT046/TT049 have retained protocols and saved review artifacts. The multiscale studies include compact result objects, original analysis rules and checked frozen-source hashes; their large raw shards were not imported or reprocessed.

The targeted FEP report remains a startup checkpoint. Its external run has a completed manifest and a passing independent readback. Intake checked all 44 output hashes and the readback-to-manifest identity, then extracted selection margins, per-profile cost ranges and qualification decisions. The 88-MB exact RESULTS.json remains external; its identity and a compact extraction are retained. This verifies saved evidence, not a new independent rerun of its 1,952,701 recorded checks.

The existing equal-read forecast probability/outcome exports and TT039 block-level outcomes remain missing. Their older report-derived figures are unchanged. D199 has a completed excluded benchmark audit but no main-cohort completion. TT047 stopped at a disk reserve. Cellular P34W still has no accessible completed result and contributes nothing new.

No scientific simulation, new fit, uncertainty reanalysis, campaign launch or outcome-driven cohort extension was performed for this manuscript revision. No experimental lane or unrelated dirty working-tree file was edited.

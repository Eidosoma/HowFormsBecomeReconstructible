exposed-data diagnosis. Freeze 32 genuinely new source blocks, 49000000–49000031,
with eight blocks per TT014 writing history (block modulo four). Include every
one of their 16 generation-14 records: 512 records, regardless of prior success.
This is a prospective in-domain test of a fixed screen, not a test of historical
verification. No source outcome or receiving result determines enrollment.

Generate fresh consistent teaching and 14 rebuilding generations using the
unchanged TT014 production dynamics. Source stream identity is
TT049_majority_screen_source_v1:<block>; retain E016 teaching and E015/E022
damage/development tags. Exact rewriting is used except at the original
tolerance-1e-4 generations: none; [3]; [3,5,7,9,11,13]; or [3,9,10,11,12,13].
All four founders and four recipients remain. The last rebuilding body's
readout defines initial pass/fail, never inclusion. Independently replay every
teaching and rebuilding trajectory byte for byte; retain all states and packets.

Keep the TT048 eight input regimes and constructions unchanged: original;
10% additional missing observed edges; 10% sign flips; three disjoint 10% sets
of weak random signs, sign flips and fourfold magnitudes; actual opposite
founder's acquired teaching packet; matched unsigned balanced aligned and
balanced wrong-task controls; erasure. Damage locations and labels are unavailable
to repair. Corruption and receiving keys use the new assessment namespace
TT049_majority_screen_assessment_v1. The source and receiving stream registers

streams are explicitly reused and excluded from the population.

## Fixed physical arms

P preserves the input. G uses the TT048 maximum-weight spanning forest with
four positive input pins, preserving every nonzero magnitude, filling zeros
with the observed absolute median, and replacing signs by inferred node-sign
products. S uses exactly G's proposal if the input is nonempty and the two
four-cell output patches have strictly opposite nonzero majority signs; otherwise
S returns P byte for byte. There is no cost threshold or local optimization.
The complete proposals are sealed before any receiving assessment.

The repair interface receives only a finite 2-by-256 float64 packet. The supplied
lattice, input/output patches and rule that the outputs oppose each other are
explicit task-family information. Which output should have which sign is never
supplied. Neither initial body, true history, damage mask, regime, original
packet nor receiving outcome is available to selection. Every historical status
remains UNVERIFIED. Internal consistency and this majority screen cannot certify
the intended history; wrong-history controls cannot be pooled away.

Record eligibility, abstention, unchanged acceptance and actual edits separately.
G's inference cost is paid on every record; fewer edits do not automatically mean
less total CPU time. Qualify graph construction against independent Kruskal and
scalar packet reconstruction. Bridge both G and S to TT048's retained exposed
proposals and declared diagnostic decisions without consulting outcomes.

## Receiving conditions and endpoints

Read all three physical arms in four new matched futures per record and regime,
with 24 missing non-port cells, birth SD .05, dt .2, noise .02, 2,048 updates,
1,024 settling updates and the last 32 observations. Each packet is 4,096 bytes
of float64 values. Injury, birth and forcing are paired across arms and regimes.
Original coordinated teaching and matched cue randomness remain explicit; this
does not test independently randomized cues. Reading never writes.

Production contains 49,152 packet/future contexts, each with both cues, plus
7,168 rebuilding contexts. Independently replay all native reads byte for byte
and score saved windows with compensated scalar sums. Verify that S's readout
equals the readout of its selected P/G packet in the same future. Qualification
uses four futures too, so the production forecast does not extrapolate runtime
linearly from a one-future benchmark.

Principal panel: mixed unmarked damage. Retain separately all other regimes,
initial pass/fail and four source histories. Primary quantities are S-minus-G
intended success, S-minus-G opposite success, intended minimum-margin change,
and G-minus-S edit frequency. Success is strict minimum signed margin > .25
across both cues and output ports. Minimum margin remains a separate endpoint;
preserving binary success does not imply preserving subthreshold improvement.

Keep all three paired arm contrasts, complete success contingency tables,
record mean gains/harms, local four-future estimates, and conditional Monte Carlo
errors. Against P, keep rescues and harms for G and S. The S-minus-G success
change must equal harms avoided minus rescues lost; an abstention rule can
reduce harms simply by discarding useful repairs, so both quantities remain.
The opposite-task analogue is retained without interpreting it as intended gain.

Average four futures within records, 16 records within source block, and eight
blocks within each history; then weight the four histories equally. Independent
source blocks, not recipients or paired contexts, are the population units.
Report fixed-cohort counts and block values. For each prelisted all-record
contrast, retain pointwise two-sided 95% stratified Welch–Satterthwaite intervals
and conservative bounded Hoeffding intervals. With zero estimated variance use
the bounded interval, never a zero-width certainty claim. Subgroup summaries
retain pooled and equal-available-block rates and defined-denominator flags.
Conditional receiving Monte Carlo errors are not source-population intervals.

This is estimation of a fixed proposed policy on new sources. There is no
post-hoc best threshold, significance-driven extension, equivalence claim,
arbitrary noninferiority margin or composite positive verdict. The pointwise
intervals are not simultaneous family-wise claims across regimes/endpoints.
Report success, wrong-task expression, margins and edit burden together.

## Qualification, resource bounds and stopping

Preflight uses only excluded known source 34000: reproduce teaching, exact and
tolerance writing at generation 13 and the post-pulse generation 14; bridge
retained TT048 proposals; check fixtures and three receiving regimes with one
future. Limit preflight to 600 seconds. It generates no fresh population source.

Full qualification runs two excluded blocks, 49999996 and 49999998, in parallel
on CPUs 6/7. Both reuse source 34000, under histories 0 and 2, and must reproduce
the known retained generation-12/13/14 boundaries. Each performs every input
regime and four receiving futures. Reproduce preflight's matching cases/future
exactly. Qualification has a one-hour hard deadline and contributes no population
data. Only after it passes may the detached controller admit production.

Production uses two normal-priority workers, one numerical thread each, CPUs
6/7, within the shared ceiling of 12 active project threads. Forecast 1.5 times
16 waves times the measured full two-worker qualification-wave duration plus
120 seconds. Require forecast <= eight hours; hard deadline is twice forecast.
No automatic reduction of the fixed cohort is permitted if admission fails.
Two GiB total study storage, 32 MiB per block and four GiB free reserve are hard
guards; retain lossless raw states and hashes. Check space between generations
and regimes. Outputs, code snapshots and failures remain local to this study.

Run full qualification and conditional production detached. After one startup
check, do not poll until the user asks. Stop on any mismatch, failed guard or
deadline; preserve failed work. No automatic retry, extension, serial successor,
TT047 recovery or archive polling follows. Review and interpretation occur after
completion. Resource admission does not depend on a scientific result.

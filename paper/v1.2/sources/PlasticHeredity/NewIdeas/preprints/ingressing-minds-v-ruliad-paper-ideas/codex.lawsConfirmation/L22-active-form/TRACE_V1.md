record theta and fresh-reader activity is the intended within-pair distinction
visible in the saved states? Track ema as well, without assuming it is a
causal input to the native rung A dynamics. Include the averaging sidecar's
mean, tanh(mean) and theta wherever saved.

Use all four original blocks 30056–30059, all four messages and four replicates,
all 36 repair arms and all 51 named reads per block, including controls.
Exclude the disposable benchmark from estimates. Use every saved repair time
0, 1, 50, 250, 500 and 1000, and reader times 0, 1 and 1000. Reader draws remain
four per record, not four independent founders. No selected cases or timepoints.

For every field retain its four original template projections, RMS, centered
RMS, own-minus-partner projection, and that gap divided by centered RMS and
the RMS of the template difference (zero when the denominator is zero).
Also retain nearest-template own/partner tie weights and their difference,
and correlations to the original own/partner parent records. Ties use the
existing absolute 1e-12 tolerance. These offline geometry scores are not
fresh-read success except when evaluated on actual terminal reader activity.
Do not treat projection or correlation as mutual information or compare raw
amplitudes across different channels as fractions of information retained.

For common and residual conditions, retain matched field differences for
own versus partner body, own versus spatially scrambled body, and live versus
fixed reader, at every saved time. Apply the same contrasts to actual saved
fresh-reader activity. Field differences measure source sensitivity, not
successful recovery. Average equally over blocks and retain block and
message strata alongside every pooled trajectory.

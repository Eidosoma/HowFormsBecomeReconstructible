Both studies completed on 2 November. Neither is a new confirmation. AR005's seven passing statements include allowable costs; its body and record are dynamically coupled, not statistically independent witnesses. SM006's nine broad bands do not establish nine improvements or an accurate universal law. Its reported S2/S7 improvements have positive intervals but no prespecified positive qualification margin. Reader properties were separately measured at both scales, so the forecast does not use small-sheet observations alone.

## Charter assessment

The [original protocol](../../v2.5/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/multiscale-error-lane/PROTOCOL.md) connects discrepancy, sensing, corrective action, present expression, outgoing record and future reconstruction. Implementation and mechanism separation are well developed. AR001C–AR003C provide three bounded controller confirmations. NL002–NL004 address historical references and their acquisition/timing limits; SR001 and HS001/HS002 address different serial-continuity boundaries. The current AR and SM branches advance source reliability and scale prediction.

The unfinished link is an integrated policy-to-successor test: a frozen controller that preserves acquired specificity and improves record-conditioned capacity through actual replacement and repeated independent futures. Long retention under one apparatus does not establish that benefit for a newly developed controller. The main chapter synthesis and SI P.1 now state this directly. Endogenous reference-capture timing and biological validation remain separate open questions.

The [claims tracker](../planning/OPEN_CLAIMS_TRACKER.md) and [lane workplan](../planning/LANE_WORKPLAN.md) prioritize selective guarding followed by fresh confirmation, continuation testing, uncensored reader-scale measurement and frozen transfer. IA001's information-source decoder results remain unavailable in this checkout. These recommendations launch no work and do not change frozen lane protocols.

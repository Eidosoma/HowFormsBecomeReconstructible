## State, measurement and endpoint

Use all eight blocks 34000–34007, four founders and four recipients, and these
four histories only: O, P, spaced, cluster. Select generation 14 because it is
the first body after both six-event schedules have ended, not by a search over
prediction accuracy. There are 512 history-lineage records in eight block units.

The forecast boundary is after the generation-14 cue reads and before their O
capture. It contains both cue-end body states and the current mask; the reader,
writer, task rule, generation, future disturbance contract and ports are fixed
external apparatus. Current expression is the four task-aligned mean output
signals (two output ports for each cue) over the native final 32 read steps.
Also retain their minimum margin and strict both-cue pass indicator. This is
observed current-body expression, not an estimated fresh-readout probability
for the outgoing record. The hypothetical O packet is computable from these
already observed bodies with no future read. Verify it against the actual saved
generation-14 packet; packet diagnostics therefore use no later body or mask.

The endpoint is the existing generation-32 scattered terminal assay, both cues
passing strict margin >0.25. All histories use the same O writer for the entire
continuation. Future masks/noise remain matched within each block/founder/
recipient. Terminal assays, intermediate body success and uninterrupted
performance remain different quantities.

## Matching audit, without endpoint-based selection

The primary matched comparison is spaced versus cluster within the same
block/founder/recipient. Report all 128 pairs and their generation-14 expression
differences. First show the coarse subset where both bodies pass, then the
strict subset where both pass and the maximum absolute difference among the
four task-aligned output means is at most 0.02. This fixed caliper is two percent
of the unit target amplitude, a development operating definition; it is not a
measurement-error confidence interval or an established equivalence margin.
No caliper sweep, matched-pair optimization, reassignment or endpoint filtering.
Report exact output equality as a separate descriptive count.

For each subset, report coverage by block, current-output discrepancies,
terminal successes, both-pass/both-fail and discordant outcomes. A residual
difference under approximate matching is not evidence of identical latent
readout probabilities, and matching post-treatment expression does not isolate
a direct causal effect of spacing. Empty or sparse matching is a limitation,
not permission to widen the caliper after outcomes.

Compare three fixed choices on the strict matched pairs: always choose spaced
(the known aggregate-favored history), choose the larger current minimum margin,
and choose the larger outgoing signed cue transfer. Exact feature ties split
the predicted choice evenly. Report terminal yield and regret against the
within-pair hindsight oracle over all eligible pairs, plus accuracy and coverage
among terminal-discordant pairs. These are retrospective descriptive choices,
not a new P08 decision trial or a prediction of unseen outcomes. The strong
constant-history choice must not be omitted if it beats the measured choices.

## Fixed forecast comparison

The candidate reconstruction variable is the existing 64-step signed,
task-aligned cue transfer of the O-written record. Unsigned 64-step access is a
conventional connectivity comparator. Both use the previously checked
normalized graph calculation and native input/output geometry. They are linear
surrogates, not native probabilities or novel RDT mathematics.

Fit the following six fixed models with whole-block exclusion:

1. History-only terminal rate, with half-count smoothing in training blocks.
2. Four task-aligned outputs, minimum margin, current pass, and three history
   indicators (O reference).
3. Model 2 plus unsigned access.
4. Model 3 plus signed transfer.
5. Model 3 plus all ten degree-two products of the four output signals.
6. Model 5 plus signed transfer.

The primary comparison is model 6 versus 5: does signed transfer add value after
continuous current expression, nonlinear readout summaries, known history and
unsigned access? Model 4 versus 3 is the declared simpler secondary comparison.
Also report every model versus history-only. Use the existing fixed logistic
ridge penalty 0.01 on mean log loss, an unpenalized intercept, training-only
standardization and the previous optimizer tolerances. Do not tune coefficients'
penalty, select features, choose a favorable model, or change the matching rule.

Primary score is the equal-block mean Brier error; log loss is secondary. Show
all eight held-out block results and per-history scores. Hold all related
histories, founders and recipients of a block out together. Only current
whitelisted features enter a design matrix; terminal margin, future body rows,
seed/block identity and outcome-defined strata never enter. Every forecast and
fit is retained. These folds assess excluded blocks in known histories, not
unseen schedules, external replication or an initial short-probe P01 claim.

A positive primary improvement would support this candidate only as development
evidence and would require a later frozen evaluation. No improvement, an effect
explained by current expression/history, failure against the simpler baseline,
or insufficient matching narrows or rejects the corresponding P02 proposal.
A coarse matched phenotype alone omitting information is not an RDT discovery.
No confirmatory p-value, practical-benefit threshold or untouched-cohort claim
is specified.

## Comment 01 Finite numerical operations and the functional mechanism

**Assessment.** The reviewer identifies the right interpretation of E128. Repeated pruning reversals establish repeatability within the original apparatus. The fresh formation test shows that the specified writer tolerance, normalization floor and storage grid do not preserve a useful version of that phenomenon, even when the records develop under those storage and renewal conditions. Both implementations already use finite float64 arithmetic.

The failure therefore constrains the implemented mechanism. It does not establish that every finite implementation fails or, by itself, diagnose a floating-point bug. The original fixed teaching scaffold remains supplied; formation under changed storage/renewal rules is not an independently optimized learning procedure.

**Source checks.** The review was checked against the retained [E116 interpretation](../sources/v3.2/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/E116_finite_resolution_pruning/INTERPRETATION.md), [E117 diagnosis](../sources/v3.2/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/E117_saved_finite_sensitivity/INTERPRETATION.md), [E118 interpretation](../sources/v3.2/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/E118_finite_formation/INTERPRETATION.md), [E128](../EVIDENCE_E121_E168.md#e128) and [SI C.83](../SUPPORTING_METHODS_C83_C103.md#c83). The quoted effects and mixture-arm endpoints agree with those sources. Full arrays were not rerun or used for a new uncertainty estimate.

**Why the structural result matters.** Rounding removes little raw weight mass while deleting connections that carry substantial normalized influence. A denominator floor attenuates that influence without necessarily deleting connections; a writer tolerance changes capture routes. These are distinct interventions. E117 is a post hoc, rounding-only diagnosis of earlier records, and its coupling share is not a fraction of performance causally explained. Its outcome-conditioned associations are not a held-out forecast.

**Manuscript changes.** Main §5.3 now states the operational meaning of the finite-resolution challenge, gives the repeatability and robustness conclusions together, explains the normalized-input vulnerability, and identifies the next predictive question. It distinguishes the three-change float64 formulation from the earlier four-change E053 channel. The fresh-cohort table, abstract and original evidence/methods remain unchanged.

## Comments 02–03 Recovery delay and cue-noise dependence

**Assessment.** The completed pruning comparisons establish an immediate benefit and a cost after one rewrite under matched cue resets/noise and the original writing rule. They do not establish how long that cost lasts. A one-generation delay is a plausible alternative to persistent harm; the nearly exchanged arm means do not establish it, and a delay need not imply convergence under recurring injury or oscillatory dynamics. Re-isolation and renewed fallback are hypotheses requiring a causal test. The evidence does not establish the full reversal under independent-noise histories and tolerant writing.

**What the retained evidence already tests.** [E098](../../v2.4/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/E098_writer_pairing_tradeoff/INTERPRETATION.md) changes only ongoing noise during one writing episode. Acquisition, cue resets and terminal assessments remain matched. Its mixture pruning contrast under independent writer noise is +1.807 points [−0.684, 4.199], a descriptive 95% whole-block interval. This does not establish either benefit or harm, or equivalence. A secondary native-record penalty remains. [E099](../../v2.4/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/E099_saved_body_capture/INTERPRETATION.md) retains a noise benefit when selected edges use ordinary capture, while its focal same-body hybrid-versus-ordinary contrast is unresolved. E079 (see [source availability](../SOURCE_AVAILABILITY.md)) resolves magnitude effects but not its corresponding sign effects. E054's sign-flip harm therefore cannot establish that these selected fallback edges harm performance specifically through task-irrelevant signs. Existing SI C.52/C.62 retains these bounded comparisons.

The [runner](../../v2.4/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/E077_fresh_reconstruction_tradeoff/run.py) combines incoming and outgoing packets into one terminal assessment call; the [model](../../v2.4/sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/E077_fresh_reconstruction_tradeoff/model.py) broadcasts those draws across its arm axis.

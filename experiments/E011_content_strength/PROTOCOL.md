# E011 — crossed record content and applied strength

Outcome-informed follow-up to E010; fixed before any E011 scientific outcomes.
E010 varied both selected sites and original magnitudes. Here cross the same
three site rules with two common per-entry magnitudes, retaining original-value
controls. No fitting, amplitude search, founder exclusion or cohort extension.

## Cohort and interventions

64 fresh blocks 9000–9063, four founders each and eight recipient futures per
body/condition. Excluded benchmark 900311; fixtures 900312–900313. Native A
formation/capture and E008 degree-four B graphs are unchanged: one shared
formation background and one fresh graph per block, replacing exactly 128/512
edges with connected long-range links. Read 300 steps with a held record.

Independently erase 192/256 entries per founder. Using only that founder's
clean backup and injury mask, select 64 erased sites by smallest absolute
value, largest absolute value, or one independent uniform random subset.
Break ties by lower site index. The same three subsets are reused across all
strength modes, bodies and recipient futures. Prototypes and body responses
never enter selection or writing.

Apply three modes to each subset:

- Original: restore exact backup values, as in E010.
- Low: preserve each backup sign, set each selected magnitude to **0.15**.
- High: preserve each backup sign, set each selected magnitude to **0.40**.

Levels round E010's mean weak/strong magnitudes (0.1463/0.3846); they are chosen
before this cohort. No other amplitude will be tried. An exactly zero selected
backup value has no sign and causes a retained technical failure, not an
invented sign, excluded founder or replacement seed. Uninjured entries remain
byte-exact in every intervention; other injured entries remain zero. Each arm
writes 64 float64 entries (512 value bytes), leaving 128 erased. At each fixed
level all three site rules receive equal per-entry magnitude and equal total
absolute restored value (9.6 or 25.6). Signs and site locations differ.

The body retains its original mean-centered dynamics. Equal applied entry
magnitudes are not a claim of equal physical energy or identical effective
force; report complete-field centered RMS and restored signed sum as well as
entry magnitudes. Low/high deliberately transform the backup rather than
reproduce its values. This is a controlled record-reading intervention before
fresh development, with an externally supplied backup and addresses.

Six references: intact own, unrepaired, erased and each of three complete
foreign records. Nine interventions, ordered original/low/high × weak/strong/
random. Save and hash source, graph, subsets and all 15 packets before either
body reads them. Within a body, births and noise are paired across conditions;
A/B streams are independent. No writer, body-flip challenge or adaptive action.

## Primary claim and analysis

Primary body B. Qualified agreement counts matching sign and recipient |V|>.2.
Specificity = own agreement minus mean agreement with all three foreign founders.
Utility = specificity gain over unrepaired. Average eight recipients, then
four founders per independent block. Use 10000 whole-block percentile bootstrap
draws tagged E011:confirmation. Native A and individual foreign offsets are
secondary; neither substitutes for the primary observer.

The single primary claim is a **selective strength benefit for weak-site
content that produces a useful high-strength intervention**. Require positive
lower 95% bounds for all seven B contrasts:

1. High weak minus high strong specificity utility.
2. High weak minus high random specificity utility.
3. High weak minus low weak specificity utility.
4. (High−low weak) minus (high−low strong) specificity utility.
5. (High−low weak) minus (high−low random) specificity utility.
6. High weak specificity gain over unrepaired.
7. High weak own-agreement gain over unrepaired.

This is one conjunction, with no choice between amplitudes or alternative
winning claims. Its constituent 95% intervals are not simultaneous confidence
bands. Secondary contrasts/intervals are descriptive, not multiplicity-adjusted,
and cannot rescue the primary verdict. Report every gate and the two interaction
contrasts regardless of direction. A null does not establish equivalence.

Also report all nine arms' utility, own/foreign agreement, own recovery and
resolution; low-level contrasts; other within-rule strength effects; original-
value contrasts and high weak versus original weak; all reference contrasts;
individual foreign offsets; and B−A effects. Save per-founder observations and
repair descriptors: applied mean/total absolute value, applied signed sum,
full-field centered RMS and distinguishing-site fractions for each foreign.
These describe the interventions without using an observer to construct them.

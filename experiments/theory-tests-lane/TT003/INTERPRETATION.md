The principal finding is that fresh-body outcomes can vary while packet bytes remain fixed. Four acquired packets and one late packet each passed 64/64 fixed-packet assays; one late packet passed 0/64. The two other late packets, both from block 32001, passed 47/64 and 51/64. Their remaining assays expressed neither taught rule. At the starting boundary, paired independent assays disagreed in 9/16 and 7/16 paths respectively. These are conditional repetitions of particular packets, not independent founder samples.

The assay protocol resamples recipient damage, initial body state and update noise. It retains matched randomness across the two cue signs. Consequently, the observed category cannot be treated as a deterministic function of the transmitted packet alone under this protocol. This does not isolate which source of fresh-body variation causes a failure, nor does it by itself reject a latent-state HMM. A useful observation model must distinguish persistent packet properties from variation introduced by fresh recipient bodies.

All erased, random-record and disabled-reader assays classified neither: 128/128 in each control. Foreign records produced the opposite rule in 105/128 assays and neither in 23/128, with no ancestral-rule successes. Their port traces reproduced those of the corresponding opposite-rule packet under matched nuisance draws. These findings concern this selected cohort and do not establish population-level specificity or robustness.

Short histories provide limited information for forecasting. Six of eight packets produced only one category in the primary histories under each policy, so 12 of 16 fits used single-category data. Every fit reached at least one parameter boundary. No primary history expressed the opposite rule, leaving all 16 unrestricted three-category output-Markov fits unsupported because at least that source row was unobserved. The conventional HMM with the candidate's identical structure ties it by construction.

For `b32000_h32_f1`, every primary short-history observation passed, but an independent duplicate assay after two new rewrites disagreed in 1/16 paths. This illustrates how a saturated primary fit can omit observed assay variability. The largest fitted-versus-empirical marginal residual was 0.15625, for full rewriting of `b32001_h32_f0`; with only 16 paths, that is an in-sample diagnostic, not a calibrated rejection decision. Here `h32` denotes the age of the archived starting packet, not a newly observed 32-step continuation.

The saved depth-32 probabilities are unvalidated point extrapolations. Agreement between optimizer starts measures numerical stability and does not supply sampling uncertainty. No new long continuation was generated, and two source blocks cannot support population or transfer claims. TT003 establishes that the adapter and audit work on this bounded native cohort and exposes a measurement issue requiring explicit treatment. It does not confirm RDT or validate its long-horizon forecasts.

The next scientific priority is to specify the relation between persistent packet state and fresh-assay variability, then calibrate rare-loss uncertainty and abstention using appropriate development data before a larger prospective forecast test.

## Meaning and limits

This closes the fresh-preparation-to-rewrite-to-renewal chain under one fixed,
externally managed protocol. An inherited state can be changed mid-lineage and
the replacement state can then be renewed across new carriers in this model.

Ordinary founders also work. The result does not demonstrate an acquired-history
advantage, a unique PH mechanism, fitness, autonomous reproduction, wet-lab
biology or therapy. Fresh cells, cue cells and contact timing are externally
provided. No cell division is simulated. Coarse/fine agreement is a numerical
cross-check, not proof of convergence.

These branches are deterministic and share preparations; they do not estimate
a success probability across diverse conditions. Random contact spacing and
within-state cell variation are the next questions in P12. The native biological
equations remain fixed; the bounded integration clock is an explicit extension
of the original numerical method. All P1-P11 executed files stay unchanged.

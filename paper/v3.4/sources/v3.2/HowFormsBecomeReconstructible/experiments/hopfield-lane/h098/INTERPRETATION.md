# H098 — numerical convergence passes; inherited task capability fails under loss

Completed 29 October 2026 at 13:27 UTC on the sixteen exposed H097 networks,
with four related births per network and two actual handoffs. The corrected
writer and its extended-precision control agree on all actual packets,
completions and discrete trajectories. The original numerical threshold and
exclusion margin are unchanged, and the intact baseline matches H097 exactly.
All 28 fixtures and 12,941,012 main numerical checks pass. This resolves the
tested writer-precision sensitivity for these acquisitions; it does not revise
H097's original failed acceptance or provide fresh confirmation.

Each replacement loses half the pairwise coupling sites and half the nonlinear
response channels. Reconstruction, physical copying and informed repair share
those losses. Repair receives the same eight actual parent observations as
reconstruction, plus the incoming matrix. Copying has no protected blueprint.
The four direct native learners retain their declared separate substrate.

G2 recipients. Against independent copying, it has 53 unseen-query gains and

## A bounded lead from the saved traces

Failure starts during the first recipient's expression, before the inherited
teaching packet becomes corrupted. All G1 primary completions predict the
correct answers: 512/512 unseen queries. All 512 intended unseen patterns are
also fixed under the declared neural threshold. Yet ordinary retrieval
succeeds on only 204/512 queries and 7/64 complete tasks. Context remains
correct in all 512 cases, including the 308 failures. Stable stored patterns
therefore do not establish reliable access from the actual starting states.
Some signed fields are near zero; threshold-based fixedness alone does not
establish strong restoring forces or robust attraction basins.

At G2, both inherited errors and recipient retrieval contribute. The actual
parent teaching packets contain 2,250 wrong response bits across 487 of 512
observed queries. The fitted completion passes 273/512 unseen queries and
12/64 tasks; actual retrieval passes only 65/512 queries and zero tasks.
Of the 447 retrieval failures, 237 have an already-incorrect completion and
210 occur despite a completion that passes the query criterion. Context is
correct in all cases. Every learned G2 unseen pattern remains fixed, but only
28/512 true patterns do. These are separate storage, transmission and access
observations, not a proof of global architectural capacity or impossibility.

The next bounded lead is to diagnose access to the correct G1 patterns:
response-field margins and sensitivity to starting states, while retaining
matched copying and repair. If an intervention is then tested, separate
pairwise-edge loss from nonlinear-channel loss before combining them again.
This is a proposed mechanism diagnosis, not a new campaign or predictor search.
It supports testing the nature of the dynamics; it does not establish that
greater complexity by itself makes reconstruction superior.

retained. The run has 905 sealed artifacts, including the frozen sources and
all four acquisitions per network. No population, efficiency or fresh
confirmation claim is made.

# E005 — is homogeneous formation failure partly pattern motion?

17 September 2026. Post-hoc diagnostic of E001's saved pilot; no new dynamics,
new founders, altered formation criterion, or upgrade of the earlier failure.
Analyze all 32 founders in each background, including every failed case.

Split the retained final 100 formation steps into four consecutive 25-step
means, Q1–Q4. Fit one periodic translation aligning Q3 to Q1 by maximum
mean-centered continuous correlation. Apply that fixed translation to Q4 and
measure its sign agreement with Q2. Report improvement over unshifted agreement.
The fit and evaluation use disjoint samples, but temporal autocorrelation means
this is a diagnostic, not an independent holdout confirmation.

Repeat the identical fit/evaluation using the next founder's Q3/Q4 within the
same block as a foreign-pattern control. Report own alignment gain and
own-minus-foreign alignment gain using eight-block percentile intervals.
Both lower bounds must exceed zero to call the evidence specifically
translation-consistent. Otherwise retain an unresolved motion explanation.

Also report original half-window agreement, its best possible translation-
optimized value (descriptive, potentially overfit), and each quarter's sign
balance, resolved amplitude fraction, and neighbor agreement. Temporal changes
in these measurements may reflect deformation or loss of organization; they
do not identify one mechanism on their own. Preserve individual values,
source hashes, the original formation verdict, and a compact report.

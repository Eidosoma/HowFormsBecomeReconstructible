## Interpretation and next decision

This panel weakens the pilot lead that recipient-contingent feedback improves
reconstruction under the tested conditions. It does not establish equivalence,
universal lack of benefit, autonomous repair or complete shape preservation.
L20/L22 remain **STRESS_TESTED / MIXED**.

Before commissioning another simulation, a bounded analysis of the already
saved LL/YL reference inputs, body trajectories and outgoing packets could
establish how large a dynamical separation this intervention actually created.
That would distinguish a weak intervention from a sizable change mostly hidden
by categorical recognition. This diagnostic has not been run or tuned here.

The queued [recipient decision test](../L20-extension-1/recipient-founders/DECISION_TEST_PLAN.md)
still requires a frozen record-to-recipient rule on fresh cases against the
best common and simple family/writer choices. Another interaction alone does
not establish useful matching. No new matching result is claimed.

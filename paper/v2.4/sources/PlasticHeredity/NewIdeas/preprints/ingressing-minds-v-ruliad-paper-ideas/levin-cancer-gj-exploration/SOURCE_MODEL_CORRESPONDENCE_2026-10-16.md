The paper motivates miRNA gate-size selectivity through analogies to other
transported molecules; it does not supply direct validation of that miRNA gating
rule. Likewise, copying a model concentration block does not establish an
experimental means of manipulating that block independently.

## What P34G can add

The independent challenge denominator is **two prospectively fixed incoming
sequences**, conditional on one ordinary history and one historical source
bundle. Four component arms are paired interventions within each sequence;
q128/q256 are numerical checks. The two reused prefixes, eight preparation
cases, software fixtures, ordinary-history controls and P34T's known sequence
do not enlarge that denominator. This is not a population success-rate estimate.

The final comparison must show every parent and its transmission result,
including retention failures; success counts out of two; and improvement,
worsening or no change against mask 0 for both retention and transmission.
A successful treated arm whose control already succeeds is not a demonstrated
rescue. Mask 7 versus 6 and protein-open versus protein-cut contrasts must also
remain sequence-specific. Two alternative routes succeeding in P34T does not
establish that either works on every new sequence.

P34G was still running when this correspondence review was written. Its
registered final audit is the prerequisite for interpreting any interim
outcome. P34A's failed numerical qualification remains preserved.

The Hazan–Levin ion-channel compensation paper supplies a **conceptual lead**,
not this simulator's implementation. Its PDF cited in the supplied review was
not available at the named Downloads path in this workspace; this review does
not independently verify its numerical claims. P34 does not reproduce either
neuronal channelopathy case. Earlier E020, TT014 and manuscript claims from
other workspaces are likewise not newly audited here. The defensible bridge is
that supplied changes elsewhere in a model can recover an assay outcome;
autonomous repair, historical specificity, renewable inheritance and biological
generality each require additional evidence.

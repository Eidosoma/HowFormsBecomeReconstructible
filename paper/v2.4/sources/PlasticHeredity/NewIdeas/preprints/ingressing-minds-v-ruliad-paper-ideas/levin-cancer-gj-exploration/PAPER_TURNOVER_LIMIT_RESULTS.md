## Interpretation and limits

Renewal has a timing and survivor constraint in this tested three-cell model.
Two surviving E carriers during slow single-cell replacement support complete
carrier renewal and another handover. The tested single-survivor pair schedules
do not. Pair size, survivor position, state and exposure pattern are coupled,
so this is not a universal minimum-carrier theorem.

The temporal boundary remains unresolved: 100 fails and 20,000 succeeds, with
no intermediate intervals tested. Do not infer a precise threshold or assume
monotonicity. The earlier P15 observation of E labels by the first 100-unit
sample does not establish that cells can already support rapid repeated
replacement. P16 demonstrates failure of the tested rapid renewal schedule;
it does not by itself locate or explain the commitment process.

Ordinary E performs equally well, so no acquired-history advantage is shown.
There is one archived source per historical origin, shared earlier ancestry,
and a stock benchmark that is not matched for age or full state. This remains
finite model renewal under an external schedule, not autonomous reproduction,
cell division or a physical-heredity demonstration.

## Question and actual starting material

Can an established E relay be changed by temporary contact with an opposing
M population, and can the changed carriers then write M into fresh E cells
through six complete replacements? Distinguish durable change in the original
carriers from onward transmission; either may fail independently.

P5 completed 28 cases/804 integrations, passing 42,065 independent checks.
Both ordinary-E and restored-E ancestries retained E through round 12, while
blocked routes failed E writing at round 2. Neither a unique acquired-history
advantage nor autonomous heredity was established. P6 branches at the ACTUAL
live `r06_isolated_2` endpoint of `relay__ordinary_E__open` and
`relay__restored_E__open` in `paper-relay-20260911T150113Z-b8woml1d`.
Keep all three cells, their identities `relay:06:00..02`, and all 17 native
components. Do not substitute initialized E cells, averages or winners.
Six historical rounds are reused, not newly simulated or independent replicates.
The ancestry is selected from known positive discovery results, not held out.

## Fixed temporary rewriting contact

Put the three E carriers in the upper row of a 2 x 3 nonperiodic orthogonal
grid and three cue cells in the lower row. All fresh E and M stocks are the
unchanged P3 preparation's last-returned snapshot (columns 168 and 1,
respectively), archived in P5. No primary-variable or regulator edits are
made to the incoming carriers. Cue cells evolve freely, not under a clamp.

Contact lasts 20,000 native units at uniform expression power 60, Kd 1.25e-14,
OiET .5 and tness 1. Compare two preassigned native modes, depol and hypol.
The latter removes the extra state-dependent potassium gate; it is not a
calibrated hyperpolarizing voltage clamp. These are fixed paper-linked
settings, not a claim that a three-cell cue will suffice or an outcome-tuned
search. No new native pilot is used to choose conditions.

Remove ALL cue identities and take only the three original carriers into a
1 x 3 array, fully isolated, for two 10,000-unit holds under depol/power 1/Kd 0.
Both junction transport and neighbour-voltage reading are disabled. All actual
endpoints, including failures or mixed outcomes, enter the next stage.

Seven conditions for each ancestry and writing mode:

- M_open: three M cue cells, open writing and open onward transfer.
- E_sham: three E cue cells, otherwise identical contact and transfer.
- M_write_rna: M cue; Kd 0 only during writing, small-ion routes retained.
- M_write_cut: M cue; all donor/cue-boundary transport cut only during writing.
- no_cue: age-matched original carriers alone; all junction transport and
  neighbour reading off during the 20,000-unit writing window, same mode/power.
- M_relay_rna: open M writing; Kd 0 during every subsequent handover only.
- M_relay_cut: open M writing; all carrier/recipient-boundary transport cut
  during every subsequent handover only.

Cuts leave within-group connections available. Regions are defined by fixed
identities, not by changing E/M labels. The unchanged P4 external per-edge
wrapper multiplies both ionic and separately added miRNA flux. This is an
explicit experimental extension of the native model, not a new source equation.

## Six onward handovers, numbered 7 through 12

Each round uses all three actual held carriers in the top row and THREE FRESH
E recipients in the bottom row of the same 2 x 3 grid. Contact for 20,000 units
under common depol/power 60/Kd 1.25e-14, modified only by assigned relay blocks.
Remove every incoming carrier, isolate all three actual new recipients for
10,000 + 10,000 units under common depol/power 1, and carry their full live
states into the next round. Fresh IDs are `rewrite:07:00..02` through
`rewrite:12:00..02`. No cue or old carrier returns; no failed cell is replaced.

IMPORTANT: this is a new opposite-state readout, NOT an unchanged continuation
of P5's hypol/power-1 handovers into fresh M. M recipients merely staying M
would not establish acquisition. A common high-power M-writing readout is
used for all rewrite and sham branches, independently of the writing mode.
Fresh ordinary E and M three-cell donors also undergo one such handover, in
coarse and finer versions. These four capacity diagnostics test whether this
readout transmits M and whether the readout alone produces M from E. Their
scientific success is NOT a technical gate, nor a reason to retune or stop.

Even an apparent M-writing positive must be interpreted against the E-sham,
no-cue, ordinary-E and communication-block controls. If the same change occurs
without an M cue/carrier, the result does not show specific cue transmission.
External baths, fresh cells, power/voltage schedules and rehoming are supplied
by the experimenter. Removal of the rewriting cue does not mean autonomous
renewal or withdrawal of every later intervention.

## P. Remaining prospective tests {#proposed-tests}

### P.1. Multiscale error detection and corrective action {#p1}

The [multiscale track](sources/HowFormsBecomeReconstructible/experiments/multiscale-error-lane/README.md) now has fourteen completed development reports. C.68–C.70 and E114–E116 describe their selected results, including harmful reference updates and failed gating rules. The original v2.3 proposal remains in the preserved version. Neither implementation qualification nor a development statement labeled “holds” constitutes fresh-cohort scientific confirmation.

The next study should freeze one controller and its information interface before new histories: sites may receive local mismatch and recent activity; regional sensors may aggregate declared neighbors; an experimenter's target, true injury mask and future outcome remain unavailable. Distinguish sensing from action, and match cumulative action or explicitly report its cost. Include body damage, record damage, both and neither, including correlated disturbances that can make multiple sources agree on an error. Compare fixed body-directed, record-directed, reciprocal and inactive policies before asking an adaptive selector to improve on them. The pending LR004 third-source design contributes no result at this selection.

Confirmation should separate stable maintenance within a coupled body–record system from information crossing into a fresh recipient. Only the declared record may cross replacement; the original body, protected copy, supplied relational scaffold and any external detector must be accounted for. Preserve own-versus-foreign history contrasts, sign agreement and amplitude qualification, body and recipient endpoints, and failed formation or renewal. New pattern families and independently drawn environmental fields should test transfer rather than extend the same successful stripe panel. Repeated continuations from restored records can then estimate conditional future capacity and whether correction changes it.

The developmental-order result additionally calls for matched computational and physical-time budgets, varied cue layouts, and controls distinguishing boundary instruction from endogenous growth. Dimensionless resource variables should not be relabeled ATP expenditure or metabolic validation. A later biological correspondence study needs explicit measurable variables, intervention routes and source-model reproduction; it is separate from controller confirmation. Execution remains CPU first on twelve allocated CPUs; any shared-GPU implementation requires separate numerical qualification. No job is launched by this manuscript revision.

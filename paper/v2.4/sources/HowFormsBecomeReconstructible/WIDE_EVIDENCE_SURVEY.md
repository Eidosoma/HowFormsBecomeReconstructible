# Wider experimental evidence survey

**Initial survey for v0.2, 13 September 2026; cellular update for v0.7, 14 September.** This survey expands the source base beyond the initial strict-8 and laws selection. It records what changes the paper, what supplies useful limits, and what remains a lead. The claim-level details are in the [evidence ledger](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md); the resulting edits are in the [draft review](DRAFT_REVIEW.md).

## Scope and selection

The search covered the nine non-AI program folders directly under `NewIdeas/preprints`, plus the historical `replication` alias. Directory and document searches located reports, protocols, discovery logs, reviews, and selected structured result files. The survey followed promising findings into later qualification or failure reports. It is a broad, targeted repository synthesis, not an exhaustive raw-run audit or an independent replication.

AI application branches are excluded. Language-model, reinforcement-learning, neural-memory, and learned-agent performance results do not enter the manuscript. A statistical observer of catalytic assemblies, or a fixed codec in a CA experiment, does not turn the modeled phenomenon into an AI application. Sources containing mixed topics are used only for the specified non-AI results.

Completed result records take precedence over a folder's older README or narrative ambitions. A completed computation, passing software check, positive component, and passing scientific claim are different statuses. Fresh futures on a previously used panel are not an untouched-panel confirmation. Related cohorts, numerical refinements, and repeated readouts are not counted as independent replications.

## Results that materially change the argument

### Finite engineered renewal belongs among the positive findings

The most consequential omission in v0.1 was the CA and Wagner carrier work. L17's declining replacement-chain recognition is informative about its tissue architecture, but cannot represent the program's entire renewal result.

**CA Stage 4** reports `ROBUST_COMPACT_RENEWED_CA_PLASTIC_HEREDITY`. The selected carrier transmits sixteen four-bit Walsh coefficients. On 128 fresh confirmation founder pairs, both ordinary and moderate joint-stress environments pass the registered renewal gate through generation sixteen. The structured result record agrees with the report: the ordinary generation-sixteen fixed-form crossover is approximately 0.948, and the moderate-stress value approximately 0.758. These are the native crossover scores, not classification accuracies. [E21](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e21)

The experiment supplies an unusually clear inheritance boundary: every visible daughter starts from the same board, and only the latent carrier crosses. The daughter writes its next carrier from its own late activity. Erasure, rescue, content reversal, read-off, and rewriting controls identify causal roles. Two accounting details matter. The 64-bit payload excludes 656 reported shared codebook bits and the implementation machinery. The no-rewrite comparator applies 50% retention at each boundary; the resulting advantage establishes renewal against that decay, not an advantage over perfect indefinite storage. [Stage 4 report](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/codex.reconstructionsAndStressTesting/results/ca-motif-lineage-stage-4/REPORT.md)

Later CA experiments pursue locality, motion, or closure of additional mechanisms. The discovery log contains positive smoke checks, bounded control effects, and failed stronger scientific gates. In particular, Stage 5R's apparent locality still used the whole tested torus; Stage 6J/K smoke statuses do not constitute confirmations; later dynamic-carrier results do not establish the desired stronger closure. These do not overturn Stage 4, and Stage 4 does not answer them. [CA discovery log](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/codex.reconstructionsAndStressTesting/DISCOVERY_LOG_EIDOSOMA_SCIENTIST.md)

**Wagner** supplies a complementary distinction. Its retained records report strong inheritance through a full ten-entry latch on 128 fresh rulebooks. A later frontier experiment makes continued rewriting useful under mild dilution, with an approximately +0.130 generation-eight intact-minus-renewal-off contrast and positive intact effects at generations twelve and sixteen. That supports the existence of an engineered renewal mechanism. The same branch did not pass its stronger evolvability criteria: inheritance of a working carrier does not explain how a robust carrier and its maintenance machinery originate. [E22](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e22)

The bounded audit also clarifies a misleading failed comparison: a targeted full ten-locus carrier cannot outperform an otherwise identical random full ten-locus carrier simply by site selection. The actual `natural-full` arm had already passed; the correction concerns a structurally inapplicable duplicate gate. Nevertheless, the raw `gre-ideas/runs` tree referenced by these records is absent from this checkout. The manuscript now attributes these results to the retained discovery log and audit. Recovery and numerical reconciliation of the original artifacts are required for the public evidence package. [Wagner audit](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/gre-ideas/diagnostics/wagner-lineage-carrier-initial-replication-v1-gate-correction/report.md)

### The collective example now reaches twelve replacements

The earlier draft stopped at the three-round relay. Completed P5 continues actual P4 recipients through eleven additional handovers, giving twelve rounds in total. At every round, all three old carriers are removed, and all three new recipients undergo isolated holds before becoming carriers. Ordinary-E and restored-E ancestries maintain E transmission through round twelve. RNA-route blockade or a cut carrier–recipient boundary stops it at the first new handover. [P5 results](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_RELAY_RESULTS.md)

This is a stronger finite state-relay result than v0.1 described, but not autonomous reproduction. Cells, contact, bath conditions, and schedules remain supplied. Ordinary-E success prevents attributing a special transmission advantage to the restored ancestry. The historical first handover and the finer numerical continuations are not independent new biological replicates. [E14](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e14)

The follow-ons improve the interpretation of collective goals. P6's attempted M rewriting failed, but its ordinary-M transmission control also failed; the result does not identify special defense of an acquired goal. P7 obtained M transmission in 3-to-1 and 6-to-3 arrangements while tested 3-to-3 contacts failed. Cell numbers and topology changed together, so this is a qualified change of transmission conditions, not a general majority-vote law or a renewable M relay. [P6 results](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_REWRITE_RESULTS.md), [P7 results](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_M_CALIBRATION_RESULTS.md)

The 14 September follow-up extends that account. P8 used a qualified six-to-three cue to rewrite three targets to retained M in all four backgrounds, then demonstrated retained M in one fresh recipient. Stage-specific blocks separate acquisition from transfer. P9 used the actual rewritten groups before that probe and sustained six complete three-cell replacements through sequential contacts; ordinary M also succeeded. Simultaneous contact failed even when extended, but topology and pupil histories changed with scheduling. These are distinct additions to E14, not a revision of P5's twelve-round E count or proof of an isolated timing effect. [P8 results](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_CALIBRATED_REWRITE_RESULTS.md), [P9 results](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_STAGED_RELAY_RESULTS.md)

The same review adds E25's commitment and component interventions: the same M label can precede either loss or retained transmission, and selected protein/RNA substitutions change that future. E26 records H4b's conditional value of inherited, updateable state in four new environmental streams, together with complete agreement of its coarse checkpoints with a prespecified binary copy/update rule. Their source histories, inference units, and assays differ from P8/P9. The referenced original run arrays are absent from this checkout; the additions rely on retained reports, protocols, and selected code. P10 has a protocol and historical launch note but supplies no completed result. [Inclusion review](reviews/LEVIN_CANCER_INCLUSION_REVIEW_2026-09-14.md)

### Observer evidence concerns hidden distinctions as well as detected patterns

The continuous-GRN observer atlas adds a useful counterpart to the CA observer result. Among 24 candidate memories, response-only reset classifications cover 14; richer state, counterfactual, relearning, and information observers retain only two. There are 21 complete combined-reset profiles across 13 BioModels. These are exploratory, descriptive classifications, not confirmed erasure rates. A missing response can coexist with a history-dependent state. [E19](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e19)

Follow-up qualification matters. The contingency study's verdict is durable exposure state only. A stricter eight-candidate study found no robust history fingerprint; later gates dependent on that fingerprint were therefore not run. Another branch obtained persistent states under dilution but zero of eight qualified causal generation-four recurrences and no active-renewal candidate. The proposed CA-to-continuous-GRN bridge also failed its initial robust-acquired-form gate. We should not describe native GRN active renewal as already demonstrated. [E20](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e20)

Additional GRN autopsies prevent premature attractor language. Stable-looking exposure states were not causally localized, and a constrained-basin recovery study qualified zero of five robust acquired basins. These results do not prove an absence of attractors in the models; they fail the particular proposed acquired-form tests. A historical-washout report inspected during the search still marked itself `running`, so it supplies no completed verdict here. [Basin recovery report](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/gre-2-ideas/runs/grn-constrained-basin-recovery-v1/report.md), [exposure-state autopsy](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/gre-2-ideas/runs/grn-exposure-state-autopsy-v1/report.md)

**FEP Experiment 1.5** supplies both positive event evidence and a measurement correction. It observed 3,519 strict-eight events in 204,800 natural-GARD futures and zero in 102,400 null futures. Its overall recurrence criterion nevertheless failed. The legacy anomaly reversal came from smoothing counts before the compositional transform, which introduced dependence on total mass. Correcting that artifact restores the expected fission/growth directions. Event occurrence, calibrated predictability, and the full recurrence hypothesis have different evidential statuses. [E17](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e17)

The strict-8 audit material gives smaller examples of the same discipline. R11's doorway correlations were modestly inflated by zero-padded categories; removing them preserves positive bounds and the passing doorway gate. A separate R85 relabeling audit traces selected threshold flips to floating-point accumulation and center assignments, not biological effects of renaming species. Neither correction licenses ignoring threshold sensitivity in a future atlas. [R11 audit](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/strict-8-paper-preparations/codex.reconstructionsAndStressTesting/audits/r11_door_zero_padding/RESULT.md), [R85 audit](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/strict-8-paper-preparations/codex.reconstructionsAndStressTesting/cleanRoomR85CppTriangulation/audits/relabelAudit/results/REPORT.md)

### The latest body-context qualification now has a fresh repeat

The final source comparison found a newer master-law-ledger update. D112 repeated D111's matched-context test on eight fresh tissues: rearranging the surviving body, with its voltage multiset fixed at birth and reference feedback off, changed later preference by +0.68 points [0.24, 1.22]. Both readout gates pass, while one direction's interval remains unresolved. D113 preserves this weak steering result and rejects interpreting small local theta perturbation effects as evidence of zero pattern-storage capacity. ## Useful leads and limits

### Capacity in a landscape is different from a mechanism that writes it

Compositional-memory Experiment 5C.3 shows target-aware engineered landscapes meeting bidirectional recovery criteria through generation 48 after a common compositional reset. Its source panel was reused, despite fresh futures. The protocol explicitly calls for a later untouched-panel replication before a confirmatory preprint claim. The draft therefore uses it only as a labelled development example. The catalytic matrix plays a different experimental role here from a fixed shared matrix in strict-8: the manipulated matrix carries the proposed historical distinction. [E18](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e18)

The follow-up record does not supply a general autonomous writer. Experiment 5B.7.5 left fresh-panel writer capacity unresolved; 5D.19 separated locating useful changes from predicting their magnitudes and signs; 5D.110 failed both the target-blind writer families and its registered oracle-sign ceiling. When the positive control also fails, the outcome cannot isolate the learned writer as the sole bottleneck. These branches remain out of the paper's confirmatory core. [5B.7.5 report](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/compositional-memory-landscape-ideas/results_5b75/report/REPORT.md), [5D.19 report](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/compositional-memory-landscape-ideas/results_5d19/report/REPORT.md), [5D.110 report](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/compositional-memory-landscape-ideas/results_5d110/report/REPORT.md)

### Renewed ingredients do not guarantee renewed organization

The challenged dust-aggregate experiment failed to restore parent statistical form, despite successful material replacement in some conditions. Every resolved active parent mean was negative under both registered form observers. Later studies show selective retention of same-type incoming grains, but do not establish the proposed emergent compositional form. An externally imposed composition that remains visible immediately after fragmentation also does not supply a break-and-renew event. This is a useful separation of material turnover, local selectivity, and organized reconstruction. [E23](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e23)

### Collective observational evidence is not yet a confirmation

EVE's case register contains two completed historical reconstructions, neither qualifying as PH. Sparse behavior records limit what verified organizational transfers can establish. Calibrated detector work controls false positives but lacks adequate power for several relevant effect sizes and block quotas. The current-cache verdict is no-go, not an empirical demonstration of no collective heredity. Synthetic labels used for power calculations are not observed doctrines or memory transmission. [E24](EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md#e24)

The virtual-cell folder supplies useful experimental ideas, but no additional completed whole-cell result was identified for the argument. The historical replication alias supplies operational predecessors, including earlier F12-style PH; those contracts should not be silently relabelled strict-eight. Older notes about source-code availability should be read alongside the replication report's later clarification. [Virtual-cell ideas](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/virtual-cell-ideas/IDEAS.md), [historical replication report](../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/replication/REPORT.md)

## Implication for the paper

The following is the synthesis drawn from these studies, rather than a newly measured universal law: a form's availability, the acquisition of information about it, its effective readout, and the renewal of that information are separable achievements. Some architectures achieve several of them under explicit controls. Other architectures fail at identifiable steps. This supports a more concrete discussion of embodiment and active form while leaving spontaneous origin, indefinite robustness, cross-embodiment identity, and flexible collective competence open.

The revised manuscript keeps strict-8 and the current laws reviews central. It adds the strongest missing positive renewal evidence, uses observer and material-model work to sharpen the causal distinctions, and keeps observational feasibility and unqualified developmental leads visible without counting them as confirmations.

## Source-audit finding

The law is not confirmed as written. Six simulated model families can be
expressed in one six-coordinate behavioural representation, and ECA,
Life-like, and voltage-tissue populations place closer to the frozen reference
centroids than the registered random and shuffled controls. Translation-map
composition also has substantial retained support at the ensemble
early-behaviour level.

`One catalogue` is nevertheless too strong. The E13 reference contains four
GARD variants plus one XENO and one GRN world, with GARD contributing 96.95%
of all entities. The source `cross-world` statistic requires any three of
those six worlds and is therefore nearly one even under a conditional label
permutation. Requiring all three baseline formalisms reduces entity mass from
0.9982 to 0.3627. Per-world rank normalization also aligns all marginal
coordinates by construction.

The exact `3–7×` and `0.6–0.7` ranges have direct retained counterexamples.
Every complete atlas is at least 2× smoother than its registered random null
and has top-decile share at least 0.35, but this is a broader and weaker
statement. Top-decile share establishes concentration, not statistical
heavy-tailedness.

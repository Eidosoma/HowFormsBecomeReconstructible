# E098 — writer pairing changes the later pruning penalty

23 October 2026. **The prespecified primary conjunction passed on 32 new source
blocks.** Independent noise across the writer's cue episodes improved the
outgoing pruned records and reduced the pruning penalty. The paired-noise
baseline penalty was also established. This is a causal dependency on the
writer's cue pairing in the declared apparatus, not an exclusive explanation
through the fallback rule or a guarantee for every record.

The scientific run completed at **14:27:42 UTC**, in **2 h 27 min 41.56 s** on
four workers. Closeout passed at **15:49:03 UTC**: **1,997 outputs / 64 frozen
inputs** verified, job input snapshots checked, final figure inspected. Retained
scientific run size **1,758,207,300 bytes**. No extension or successor launched.

## What was changed

All source acquisition, ancestry, pulse/sham and continuation used the unchanged
paired apparatus. From each source, preserve an intact record and prune the
originally selected restored fallback connections once. Cross those inputs with
paired versus independent cue noise only during the read phase supplying one
subsequent rewrite. Initial activity, injury, noise amplitude, capture rule,
normalization, precision and holding remain fixed. Terminal readers still use
paired cue noises and fresh, matched nuisances.

There are **2,048 source contexts**, one supplying writer realization per
context, and **4,096 terminal context/draw rows** from two draws. Inference
resamples the **32 independent source blocks**, not these dependent rows.
Both source types, both histories, all initial successes/failures and individual
harms remain. The source recipe is E077 throughout.

## Primary result and absolute performance

For pooled mixtures, independent writer noise improves the pruned record's
terminal success by **5.273 percentage points**, adjusted whole-block **97.5%
interval [2.734, 7.812]**. The pruning interaction is **+5.469 points
[2.539, 8.398]** on the same adjusted scale. Both lower bounds are positive.
Both intervals overlap the declared three-point practical reference: evidence
for a positive effect is stronger than evidence that it exceeds three points.

The paired-noise pruning contrast is **−3.662 points**, descriptive **95%
interval [−5.714, −1.562]**. Under independent writer noise it is **+1.807 points
[−0.684, 4.199]**. Thus the penalty is significantly attenuated; a positive
pruning benefit under independent noise is not itself established. The intact
record's noise effect is **−0.195 points [−2.295, 1.855]**. The result satisfies
the actual-pruned-improvement safeguard, rather than relying on intact damage.

| Terminal success | Native (%) | Mixture (%) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Immediate intact I0 | 84.62 | 51.03 |
| Immediate pruned P0 | 85.06 | 59.23 |
| Rebuilt intact, paired writer noise | 85.84 | 59.38 |
| Rebuilt pruned, paired writer noise | 76.61 | 55.71 |
| Rebuilt intact, independent writer noise | 86.72 | 59.18 |
| Rebuilt pruned, independent writer noise | 84.42 | 60.99 |

Native outcomes were declared secondary. Their pruned-record noise gain is
**+7.812 points**, pointwise **95% interval [5.811, 9.912]**. The native pruning
cost falls from **−9.229 [−11.572, −7.031]** to **−2.295 [−3.516, −1.221]**;
a residual cost remains. The native interaction is **+6.934 [5.176, 8.789]**.

Mixture pruned gains are positive in both history means: **+5.762 points sham**,
**+4.785 pulse**. They are also positive in each terminal draw mean: **+5.762**
and **+4.785** points. These are subdivisions of the same experiment, not new
independent confirmations.

The 53 mixture noise-change harms occur in **20 source blocks**; **21 exact

blocks, with three exact contexts harmed on both draws. Average improvement
therefore does not establish a universally beneficial rule or individual safety.

The complete 4×4 immediate/later tables retain the E097 distinction between
formation and reconstruction. Among native comparisons where I0 and P0 both
succeed, later pruning harms decrease from **162/1,733 (9.35%)** to
**38/1,733 (2.19%)**. For mixtures they decrease from **97/1,031 (9.41%)** to
**31/1,031 (3.01%)**. These are descriptive conditional proportions on matched
observations. Every other starting category and its denominator remains in the
original summary; this subgroup was not used to choose the sample.

## Mechanism evidence and limits

In the pruned branches, the available originally selected connections use
ordinary fallback under paired noise and cue contrast under independent noise:
**100% versus 0% exact-fallback use**. All available selected connections receive
nonzero outgoing weights under both conditions. The improvement therefore does
not come from increasing the count of restored connections in this selected
set. This statement does not establish identical full-network support.

The contents and normalized contribution of those connections change. Mean
selected absolute weight mass in pruned records changes from **0.147 to 3.103**
for natives and **0.374 to 3.006** for mixtures. Mean selected share of absolute
normalized coupling after terminal injury changes from **7.10% to 9.33%** and
**8.15% to 9.65%**, respectively. These structural shares include all receivers,
including clamps, and are not fractions of functional mediation.

The supplying body changes too. Native pruned writer-end success changes from
85.84% to 86.23%; mixture pruned writer-end success changes from 57.42% to 59.28%.
The next-reader gains differ from these supplying-body changes. This is
consistent with a capture/content contribution, but does not isolate it: noise
affects the entire supplying trajectory and capture outside the selected set.
The stored ordinary, cue-independent and cue-dependent products permit a more
specific intervention without repeating source ancestry.

E098 establishes this one-rewrite interaction under shared cue birth and the
unchanged paired source and terminal apparatus. It does not establish robustness
to independent birth, reset errors, fallback tolerances, finite precision or a
normalization floor, nor rerun the entire E041 comparison under those changes.

## Next candidate

Use the saved E098 supplying bodies for a targeted capture intervention, keeping
the body fixed while changing how originally selected connections are written.
That can test whether the change from ordinary fallback content to contrast
content contributes to the benefit. Keep intact/pruned backgrounds, both
endpoints and every individual harm, and distinguish changed magnitudes from
changed signs or availability. A concrete contrast and runtime benchmark should
be frozen before new terminal outcomes. This is a proposed follow-up, not an
implemented or launched experiment. Another long ancestry run is unnecessary
for that first mechanistic check.

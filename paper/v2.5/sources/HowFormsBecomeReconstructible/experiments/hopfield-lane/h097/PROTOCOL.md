control. Fresh development pilot: seed **97097**, **16 independent networks**,

## One structural challenge

Keep the H095 task, original eight teaching observations, completion family,
128 coordinates and protected-context reader. Compare intact architecture
with loss at G1 and G2. At each replacement independently choose exactly
**4,064 of 8,128 undirected pairwise couplings** to be unavailable, and exactly
**24 of 48 higher-order response interaction channels** to be unavailable.
Use the same generation-specific architecture for all methods, sources,
policies and related births in a network. G0 is intact in both conditions.

Pairwise links and higher-order channels are separate resources in this model.
The pairwise mask does not secretly remove or restore a nonlinear channel;
its availability has its own declared mask. All inherited *physical* neural
records must have zero unavailable coefficients. There is no separate protected
blueprint containing absent coefficients. Availability can return in the next
body, but copying a zero cannot recreate its lost coefficient. This boundary
is part of the challenge, not a claim that all forms of copying lose information.
Native direct predictors are retained as stronger representations with a
different physical substrate; they are not subject to the neural coupling mask.

Copying keeps every available parent coefficient and removes the unavailable
ones. Reconstruction and repair receive exactly the same eight actual-parent
responses and the same architecture. Neither may restore a forbidden physical
coupling. All response-mask values remain part of the inherited record even
when their interaction coefficient is zero. No polarity change is added;
exact coordinate transport is therefore the identity, as covered by H096.

## Fixed constrained writer and repair control

Fit the unchanged scalar parity family to the eight actual observations and
generate sixteen predicted response rows. These predictions are not new
observations. Let P contain the public inputs and these predicted responses.
Build H093's unconstrained ideal record from P. Preserve the unit-strength
learned interaction terms only where a channel is available.

Choose the symmetric, zero-diagonal, permitted matrix J minimizing

`||P J - T||_F^2 / 16 + 0.1 ||J - J_prior||_F^2`,

where T is the ideal record's total field on P minus the available interaction
field on P. Reconstruction's prior is the ideal matrix restricted to permitted
edges. Repair's prior is its actual incoming matrix restricted to those same
edges. Both fit the same fields with the same regularization and solver; repair
also has the incoming record. Its nonlinear masks/signs are refitted from the
same observations. This is one declared repair procedure, not all possible
record decoders. Log residuals, field error and distance from the prior.

Use deterministic conjugate gradients on the symmetric masked correction,
relative residual 1e-12 (absolute floor 1e-12), at most 512 iterations, with
an independently checked normal-equation norm at most 5e-10. Fail execution
on nonconvergence; do not exclude a network or adjust regularization. When
there is no loss and the prior is the ideal record, the compiler preserves
that record exactly. Solver engineering includes a dense independent solve
on small symmetric problems and a finite-difference gradient check.

## Three frozen primary comparisons

Average sixteen draws per original record, then sixteen records per tissue;
weight all sixteen tissues equally. Donor accuracy is probability assigned
to the designated `(own + 1) mod 4` message, not merely reduced own accuracy.

1. **Useful repair:** kept live own-message accuracy minus damaged accuracy.
2. **Body organization:** kept live own-message accuracy minus scrambled live
   own-message accuracy.
3. **Content-specific redirection:** donor-ranked live donor-message accuracy
   minus scrambled live donor-message accuracy.

Use 10,000 paired whole-tissue bootstrap draws, seeds **20260908097401–303**,
quantiles **.05/6 and 1-.05/6**, strictly positive lower interval endpoint and
at least **12/16 positive tissue differences** for each comparison. No pooling
old cohorts or treating repeated readouts as independent tissue replicates.
Positive interpretation also requires parent readout >.25 in every tissue
and mean >=.75; weak legacy parent scores are diagnostic, not extra gates.

Let U/O/D denote the three qualified comparisons in order. Fixed labels:

- O and D: `BODY_CONTENT_SPECIFIC_REPAIR_SUPPORTED` if U, otherwise
  `BODY_CONTENT_REDIRECTION_WITHOUT_QUALIFIED_REPAIR`.
- O but not D: `BODY_ORGANIZATION_BENEFIT_ONLY`.
- D but not O: `DONOR_BODY_REDIRECTION_ONLY`.
- Neither O nor D: `REPAIR_WITHOUT_QUALIFIED_BODY_SOURCE` if U, otherwise
  `NO_QUALIFIED_BODY_SOURCE_EFFECT`.
- Unreadable parent or unqualified instrument retains its explicit label.

Report all three effect sizes, intervals and directions regardless of label.

and four negative, counting numerical variants separately, not as independent
biological replication. All successful held states pass M through both
fresh-pair handovers.

Thus protein and the RNA pair offer alternative sufficient interventions in
this finite contrast. Neither is individually necessary when the other block
has its eight-contact value. The ionic level does not alter the binary outcome
in this small factorial, but that does NOT show that ions or bioelectricity are
unimportant in general. The RNA pair was changed together: the experiment does
not localize the effect to just one RNA species. Sufficiency is restricted to
these backgrounds, endpoints and numerical model.

## What this adds and what it does not

This moves beyond a correlation between contact count and commitment: direct
state interventions can change the outcome while other primary variables are
preserved. It does not establish a unique memory molecule, new attractor,
autonomous reproduction, adaptive advantage or a wet-lab mechanism.
Ordinary M-state founders can transmit too; acquisition need not confer a
separate advantage to count as acquired-state inheritance.

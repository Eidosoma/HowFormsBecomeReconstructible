## Draws, observations and inference

Historical incoming cells use P16 seed 501 words 1–11. Held-out sequences use
seeds 17021 and 17022, namespace `P22_incoming_v1`, words 1–11. All words are
frozen in draws.json before execution. For event j, take the first eight bytes
of SHA256(`namespace|seed|turnover|j`) as an unsigned big-endian integer w.
Multiply stock-M ZEB protein by 1 + (floor(51*w/2**64) - 25)/100. Preserve every
other incoming variable. Thus robustness concerns two new protein sequences
within the previous ±25% range, not arbitrary cellular variation. The same
draws are paired across routes/histories/resolutions; short/long schedules
share their first five draws. There is one archived source per history/resolution.

Score final E retention using endpoint E and at least 95% late E in both
isolated holds, first newcomer stable E after the challenge, and E stability
in every full replacement window. Timing segments are joined without duplicate
boundary samples, then sampled on the common 0,15,...,300 observation grid for
the first full-window observation and resistance/recovery diagnostics. Each
raw segment is separately audited for native clock, state continuity and its
coupling settings. Retain all finer segment samples in raw output.

The first-cell categories are sampled E resistance, sampled recovery to E and
no E recovery by 300. Record observed M and first-loss/return brackets as
diagnostics, not exact crossing times. Compare complete phase-end labels and
registered categorical outcomes between coarse/fine settings; exact diagnostic
crossing brackets and identical raw values across resolutions are not required.

RNA sufficiency/reciprocal erasure is relative to the matched failing all-before
and successful paired-RNA controls. If neither single change works, report a
joint requirement in this tested RNA background. Timing effects are compared
to each route's segmented-open control; a first-cell outcome is distinct from
the whole group's outcome. Global blockade does not identify a direct sender.

Report all robustness route/draw/length outcomes and paired contrasts. If an
all-before group succeeds under a challenge, that condition does not establish
a preparation-specific rescue. A qualified onward chain requires retained E,
open transmission, negative RNA/cut readouts and verified inherited capacity/
wipe evidence. Neither native transport identity nor a unique memory molecule
is discovered by this programme: the pinned native model already transports
free miR200 and not Zeb mRNA or ZEB protein through junctions.

This is externally managed finite renewal. No division, autonomous reproduction,
independent biological replication, population success probability or acquired-
history advantage is assumed. Ordinary and written outcomes are both reported.

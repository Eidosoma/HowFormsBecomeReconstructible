## Primary prediction and interpretation

Two primary pooled-mixture contrasts require strictly positive adjusted **97.5%
whole-block bootstrap lower bounds**, using 10,000 shared resamples of the 32
source blocks, equal within-block histories/founders/recipients/draw weighting:

1. P-independent H minus O: selected cue-sensitive capture benefits the record
   from the same independently noisy pruned body.
2. P-independent H minus P-paired H: the E098 pruned noise benefit repeats under
   the new terminal readers on these reused source histories.

Both positive supports `FILTERING_CONTRIBUTES_WITH_REPEATED_NOISE_GAIN`.
Report each negative or unresolved direction explicitly. The second criterion
prevents interpreting an isolated same-body capture contrast as an explanation
of a noise benefit absent under the new readers. A positive first contrast alone
still establishes its conditional capture effect under its original interval.
No change to the sample or threshold after results. Three points remains a
practical reporting reference, not an alternate significance threshold.

Because P-paired H=O exactly, the following difference identity holds per case:
noise_gain_H = (P-independent H−O) + (P-independent O−P-paired O).
The second term changes the supplying body and the outside captured background.
This is one intervention path, not a unique causal mediation fraction. Negative
or unresolved terms remain; do not infer equivalence, exclusive mediation or
complete explanation from a small residual.

The four conditional sign/magnitude effects, their interaction, intact H/O
effects, native controls, separate histories/draws, natural/ordinary pruning

## Saved-state diagnosis: query transition and corrupted teaching

This section is explicitly post hoc, calculated from sealed states, traces
and packets. It adds no dynamics, fitted rules or intervention outcomes.
[Diagnosis receipt](../.cache/h093_handoff_diagnosis_v1.json).

Every one of the **492 archived failed unseen equal-energy endpoints** has a
strictly higher energy under H093's founder record than the intended answer.
The independently computed energy penalties range from **10 to 58**. This
confirms the specific repair H093 was designed to make.

New failures have a different structure. Own G0 retains the correct context
at the end of context preparation and throughout the gap in **512/512 unseen
queries**. After the first query sweep, 423 retain it; after the second, 494
retain it, with no later recovery. Every one of the remaining **18 failed unseen
queries** loses context in the first query sweep. The 14 taught failures behave
the same way. All 32 are on network **5** across its four births: context sites
16–23 flip, while the other context features do not update consistently.
These endpoints fail the declared input-encoding consistency check and each
has 23 wrong response bits. Every own G0 query with its input intact is exact.

These states can still have minimum energy because the intervention binds
cross-input response rules to latent context/query coordinates without enforcing
all relationships among the redundant within-block input features. An invalid
context encoding is consequently a distinct remaining failure mode. The
frozen energy-repair gate concerned archived states with matching inputs; it
does not claim removal of every possible wrong ground state.

Later handoffs introduce errors in the learned record as well as the read.
The actual G1 learned completions would answer **60/64 unseen tasks** directly;
G2 learned completions would answer **56/64**. The neural recipients achieve
57/64 and 40/64 respectively. At G2, the 98 failed unseen queries split into
**49 with final context lost**, and **49 with context intact but a wrong learned
answer retrieved**. Across a different, overlapping split, 50 have an incorrect
learned answer and 48 fail despite a correct learned answer; one failure has
both an incorrect learned answer and context loss.

For the 49 G2 final context losses, the last transition into a lasting mismatch
is the first query sweep in 47 cases and the gap in two. Independent copying
keeps all 512 unseen learned answers correct, but loses context in its 47
failed unseen queries. This separates degradation of teaching/reconstruction
from a reader problem that copying also experiences.

The next bounded test should examine preservation of internal context during
the query transition and consistency of its redundant features, with identical
apparatus supplied to copying. It must preserve the observation boundary and
avoid re-supplying the true context. Learned-rule errors and retrieval errors
need separate outcomes. Longer settling under the current dynamics is not
supported by these traces: endpoints are fixed, continuation makes no changes,
and the response-pair step never moves. Richer inherited tasks remain a
separate priority. No H094 has been launched.

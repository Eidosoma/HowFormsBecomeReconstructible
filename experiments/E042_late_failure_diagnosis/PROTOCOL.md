For the four core policies report selected packet and matched injury-graph

Success with the same record in another recorded challenge witnesses retained
functional capacity despite one failed challenge. Failure in every observed
context does **not** prove that no usable information remains. Observing body
success followed by receiver failure does not alone distinguish faulty capture
from a receiver limitation. The diagnosis explicitly preserves those uncertainties.

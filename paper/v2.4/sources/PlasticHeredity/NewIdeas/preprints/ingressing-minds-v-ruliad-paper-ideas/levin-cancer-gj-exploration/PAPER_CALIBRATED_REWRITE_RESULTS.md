## Plain-language result

Six M-state cue cells rewrote all three E-state target cells. After all six
cues were removed, the targets retained M through both isolated holds. The
three actual targets then caused one fresh E cell to acquire M. That new
cell also retained M through both holds after every target was removed.

This worked for all four target backgrounds: ordinary fresh E, isolated
time-matched E, and the actual round-6 E carriers of both ordinary-E-founded
and restored-E-founded lineages. There is no endpoint advantage for a special
history in this strong-cue panel; it does not prove history could never matter
at weaker cues or in other contexts.

| Target background | Three targets after cue removal and isolation | Fresh probe after target removal and isolation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| fresh_E | MMM | M |
| aged_E | MMM | M |
| ordinary_E lineage | MMM | M |
| restored_E lineage | MMM | M |

The tested contact setting is depol, power 60, Kd 1.25e-14. Writing uses a
3x3 sheet with six cues and three targets; the probe uses a 2x2 sheet with
three held targets and one fresh E recipient. These are regulatory states,
not complete health/cancer diagnoses.

## Controls distinguish writing from transfer

In every background, E shams and no-cue controls leave targets E and the
probe E. Blocking miRNA transfer or cutting the group communication boundary
during writing prevents rewriting: targets remain E, and the probe remains E.

Blocking those routes only during the probe has a different effect: the
targets successfully acquire and retain M, but the fresh probe remains E.
Thus the blocked onward result is not merely a failure to write the targets.
All four backgrounds satisfy the registered control-qualified chain rule.

## What changed in our interpretation

P6's failed equal-group cue did not establish that an established lineage was
intrinsically unrewritable. P7 supplied a working unequal-group arrangement;
P8 now shows retained rewriting and onward transfer using it. Cue number and
geometry changed together, so this does not isolate a pure number effect.

For plastic heredity, this joins rewriting, persistence and one onward
transfer in the same model trajectory. Three teachers produce only one new
carrier in the probe: P8 does NOT demonstrate renewable same-sized M
inheritance, autonomous reproduction, a unique PH mechanism, wet-lab effects,
therapies or a new physical law.

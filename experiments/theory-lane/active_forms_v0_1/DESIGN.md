# AF01 — Does the body supply a missing distinction?

**Status: DRAFT_NOT_REGISTERED; no simulation or new source-array audit.**

reports and later donor studies. None of those panels is a hidden test of this

## Question and native starting point

The tissue-sheet reciprocal-repair apparatus already establishes that a
surviving body's arrangement changes the usefulness of its repaired record.
Its donor-redirection criterion failed. The unresolved issue is whether
organization supplies corrective message content, or helps use content still
available in the damaged record.

Use that apparatus first: its four declared spatial messages, activity V,
reference R, threshold record theta and fresh-body message-recognition assay.
The only output transferred to a recipient is the written theta field. Keep
the source's native equations, boundary and endpoints explicit. A construction
needed for source identification is an extension, not an exact replication.

## A. Establish the replication boundary

First reproduce the source information routes on disposable development cases:
damaged record, fixed-reader writing, live reciprocal writing, spatially
scrambled body and writing disabled. Verify the selective interventions before
interpreting performance. Native recovery starts with R at zero and the
activity average initialized to that condition's V; preserve this in the
replication. If old raw records are unavailable, report a new
implementation comparison; do not label it an exact historical replay.

A later fresh-cohort replication should retain the original D074 primary
contrast and qualification rule as its own test. Keep it separate from the
new content-source endpoint. Do not pool old and new tissues or add repeated
readouts as if they were independent founders. Determine the final sample and
runtime from development-only variance and a disposable benchmark, then freeze
them before drawing scientific cases.

## B. Make the source of the missing distinction identifiable

Construct paired conditions in which **all non-body inputs at the start of
repair are identical**, while surviving body organization differs:

1. Choose two source messages using a fixed rule and balance their roles.
2. Give both conditions the same incoming theta, byte for byte. A development
   candidate is a symmetric common record derived from the two acquired
   records. It may retain information about the pair, but cannot identify
   which member is intended within that pair. This is a new controlled
   boundary, not the original random-damage protocol.
3. Reset R and the activity average identically. A common zero average is
   a proposed extension of the source's body-dependent initialization, not
   part of the native replication. Check that it leaves a demonstrable repair
   opportunity and freeze its use before confirmation. Fix the tissue parameters,
   fresh-cell fill, injury mask, writer, reader, schedule and external inputs
   across the pair. No donor record, private guide or target label is available
   to the dynamics.
4. Contrast the corresponding body organization, another message's
   organization, a value-matched spatial scramble and a body-absent reference.
   Random streams are shared across these intervention arms but generated
   independently of target labels and founder acquisition.
5. Let the declared native writing operation run. Transfer only its outgoing
   theta to identically distributed fresh recipients, resetting activity,
   reference, averages and random state at the boundary.

Matching genuine acquired bodies for every voltage value may be impossible.
If so, retain actual-body substitutions with donor-matched scrambles as one
comparison, and construct exact-value-multiset permutations as a separate
mechanistic panel. Label the latter as supplied body arrangements. Do not
claim that a constructed arrangement demonstrates spontaneous acquisition.
Feasibility must establish both body conditions and an informative reader
before a failed redirection is interpreted as absence of body content.

## Outcomes and comparators

Use the original fresh-body message-recognition endpoint. A useful
body-source result needs both a specific direction and a benefit:

| Comparison | Question |
|---|---|
| Own-body written record versus foreign-body written record, scored against the same intended message | Does the transmitted distinction follow body organization? |
| Own-body written record versus the identical incoming common record | Does writing improve recovery of the declared pre-damage distinction? |
| Own body versus its value-matched spatial scramble | Does arrangement contribute beyond the amount/distribution of activity? |
| Live loop versus fixed-reader writing | Does returning newly written information to the body add a benefit? |
| Writing disabled, reading disabled and positive supplied-record control | Are the route and assay operative and selective? |

Counterbalance both message directions. Loss of the old message alone does
not demonstrate transfer of the alternative; alternative recognition alone
does not demonstrate correction of the intended original. Report both.

Compare the native feedback writer with a conventional body-informed
averaging/denoising writer receiving the same activity samples and record,
under matched time and storage budgets. A record-only denoiser is useful for
source attribution but lacks the body information; beating it does not show
RDT-specific predictive value. Protected copying of the common record is a
preservation baseline; copying under matched loss is a different comparator.
An intact original target record is an explicitly informed positive control.

Estimate outgoing-record usefulness with multiple independent fresh readers,
paired across conditions. The independent inference unit remains the tissue
block. Immediate fresh reading is the primary boundary. A prespecified short
renewal continuation can be secondary only after one-read feasibility is
established; do not turn it into a claim about indefinite renewal.

## What the two lanes would contribute

**Theory lane:** make a quantitative prediction about which declared body–record
conditions permit content transfer and useful repair, using development data
only. Name any reduced diagnostic, its cost, conventional comparators, native
effect ranges and failure conditions. Without these commitments, this is a
mechanism experiment motivated by RDT, not confirmation of a predictive law.

**Theory-tests lane:** independently implement or check the native writer,
state interventions, fresh-reader boundary and analysis. Verify paired packet
identity, reset state and the absence of target-label access. Register exposed
records and inspect frozen quantitative commitments before generating a fresh
cohort. Do not copy a favorable source interpretation into the verdict.

The critical negative cases are informative: no writing benefit despite a
qualified reader; a generic gain without body-specific content; donor
redirection without correct-message recovery; or a conventional comparator
matching the proposed predictor. These narrow distinct claims. Failure of a
feasibility or positive control instead limits interpretability.

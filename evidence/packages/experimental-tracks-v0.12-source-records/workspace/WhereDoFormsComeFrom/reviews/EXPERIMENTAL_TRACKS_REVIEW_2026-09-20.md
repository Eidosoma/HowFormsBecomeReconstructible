Review of completed results available during this session, compared with the current v0.11 manuscript selection. The strongest additions are a retained elementary conditional function across three replacements, founder-specific information across sixteen rewrites, a fresh confirmation that correct wiring information improves repair selection, and isolated cellular state transmission with a turnover-rate limit.

This is a review, not a manuscript revision. The manuscript, experiment settings, existing outputs, and PDF remain unchanged. E023 and cellular P17 were listed as running in the inspected status records; their outcomes are excluded. AI experiments remain excluded.

## 1. The most important addition: functional renewal

[E020 report](../experiments/E020_serial_capture/runs/renewal_v1/REPORT.md) and [protocol](../experiments/E020_serial_capture/PROTOCOL.md).

The task is elementary but functional: one output follows the input cue and another opposes it, with the alternative taught rule reversing those assignments. Both positive and negative cues are tested. The transmitted object is a 4,096-byte record of 512 local edge weights; the original developing body does not cross the boundary.

During renewal, current activity under the two input cues writes the next record. The output targets are not taught again. The incoming weights are erased from the effective writing apparatus, local activity correlations are captured immediately, and the new record is protected during the following scaffold evolution. The supplied timing and protection are part of the successful mechanism.

After three successive replacements, all **512 tested lineages** pass the intact both-cue test. These comprise **128 founders in 32 independent blocks**, not 512 independent experimental units. The full registered renewal qualification passes. Reading-disabled and writing-disabled controls fail. The reported bootstrap interval is degenerate at 100% because every observed block succeeds; it is not evidence of a universally perfect success probability.

Fresh terminal readers also retain both-cue performance under scattered injury in **85.9% [82.2, 89.5]** of cases. The advantage over protected copying under that injury is **+5.9 [2.1, 10.0] percentage points**, a secondary comparison. Intact protected copying also achieves 100%, so intact renewal does not establish superiority to copying.

Two predecessors explain why this is more informative than another high score:

- [E017](../experiments/E017_reader_timing/runs/timing_v1/REPORT.md) changes when positive amplification is enabled. Its staged reader improves intact both-cue success by **+65.0 [60.5, 69.5] points** over an equally long unstaged reader. Timing, rather than extra duration alone, matters in this comparison.
- [E019](../experiments/E019_capture_timing/runs/timing_v1/REPORT.md) captures local correlations before subsequent scaffold updates can overwrite their usefulness. Immediate capture gives **99.8%** success after one replacement, versus **29.1%** for delayed capture: **+70.7 [60.9, 79.7] points**. This is a supplied capture mechanism, not a discovered autonomous clock.

**Interpretation:** the program now demonstrates finite inheritance of an instructed conditional response through activity-written records. It does not yet demonstrate spontaneous acquisition of a new competence, open-ended learning, autonomous goals, or flexible reassignment of roles after injury. The functional experiment must remain distinct from the unprescribed-form experiments: the two successes do not establish their combination.

## 2. The damage results locate the next obstacle

[E021](../experiments/E021_damaged_renewal/runs/damage_v1/REPORT.md) and [E022](../experiments/E022_reader_compensation/runs/compensation_v1/REPORT.md).

E020 damages terminal assay bodies after intact renewal. E021 instead removes 24 non-port cells from **each body that must write the next record**. After three such replacements, intact terminal both-cue success falls to **1.6% [0.4, 2.9]**, while intact renewal and protected copying retain 100%. Copying under matched edge erasure retains 6.6%; damaged renewal performs worse than that control. E021 therefore fails its primary and full qualification.

E022 supplies a local adjustment to incoming drive, normalizing by surviving connection strength rather than the original fixed degree. After **one** damaged replacement, intact terminal success improves from **67.4% to 86.7%**, a registered primary difference of **+19.3 [15.4, 23.4] points**. This positive primary meets its declared effect threshold. The full qualification nevertheless fails: under additional scattered injury in the terminal reader, both-cue success is only **25.4%**, and two rule-specific reliability gates fail.

A particularly useful observation is that E022's strength-compensated renewal bodies perform correctly on both cues in all tested cases before capture, yet their records produce only 86.7% success in intact fresh recipients. Successful current behavior therefore does not guarantee a record that enables successful behavior elsewhere.

**Interpretation:** using a function under damage, reconstructing a function after replacement, and writing a usable record while damaged are experimentally different capacities. Reader compensation is a supported partial improvement; robust serial renewal under damage remains unestablished. E023 is addressing the receiving side, but no result from it enters this review.

## 3. Unprescribed form: a longer horizon and a useful embodiment map

[E014](../experiments/E014_long_renewal/runs/confirmation_v2/REPORT.md) extends the earlier unprescribed-formation apparatus to sixteen rewrites without changing its reader/writer machinery. The fresh cohort contains 64 blocks and 256 founders; all founders are retained. Formation qualifies in 193/256 cases, narrowly above the 75% criterion.

| Record budget | Own minus foreign initial-record contrast after 16 rewrites | Own agreement, rewrite 3 → 16 | Rewrite-16 deficit against protected copying |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 128 bytes, primary | **+1.387 [0.848, 1.954] points** | 66.91% → 64.38% | −3.883 points |
| 2,048 bytes | **+1.402 [0.925, 1.909] points** | 71.24% → 65.60% | −10.950 points |

All declared gates pass. This supports a persistent, modest signature of particular history while absolute fidelity deteriorates. The unresolved change in compact-record specificity between rewrites 3 and 16 is not equivalence or indefinite stability. Budget changes also change spatial resolution, so this is not an isolated information-capacity law. The `confirmation_v2` label follows a failed detach before any scientific blocks were generated; the report records no change in scientific settings or cohort.

[E013](../experiments/E013_wiring_replication/runs/evaluation_v1/REPORT.md) is the fresh confirmation that was pending in v0.11. Models and feature transformations from E012 remain frozen. On 128 fresh blocks, the graph-informed selector produces **+1.287 [1.180, 1.399] points** of specificity recovery over unrepaired records. Its incremental gain over native measurements is **+0.068 [0.041, 0.098] points**. Giving the same predictor an independently generated, structurally matched wrong graph removes **0.102 [0.067, 0.139] points** of performance relative to the correct graph. All seven gates pass.

The small wrong-graph contrast matters mechanistically: correct wiring information helps select a useful intervention under this fixed repair menu. The reader still receives supplied resources, including a clean external backup; this is not autonomous body repair. It is a bounded example of the paper's proposed map becoming useful for prediction and intervention, without demonstrating a universal atlas or equivalence between unrelated embodiments.

## 4. Cellular results: editable state, local transmission, and time

Source reports: [P13](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_LINEAGE_RETURN_RESULTS.md), [P14](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_TISSUE_PERSISTENCE_RESULTS.md), [P15](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_TISSUE_DEPENDENCE_RESULTS.md), [P16](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/levin-cancer-gj-exploration/PAPER_TURNOVER_LIMIT_RESULTS.md).

**P13 extends the actual ancestry.** Previously renewed E carriers are instructed back to M, retain M after cues disappear, and complete three further replacements. The actual histories now span M→E→M across nine externally managed handovers. Both written-origin and ordinary-origin histories succeed. The M return is newly instructed, not evidence of spontaneous ancestral recall.

**P14 establishes a useful negative comparison.** In an E-dominated tissue, sham already corrects the M patch and supports the later retention/turnover endpoint. The proposed temporary expression interventions add no registered benefit. Blocking RNA transport or cutting the patch boundary delays correction until communication returns. This should not be described as an effective treatment contrast.

**P15 removes ongoing surrounding-tissue instruction.** With the patch boundary cut but internal contacts maintained, the patch corrects the incoming replacement cells, retains E after isolation, and the actual replacement cells transmit E to fresh recipients. Blocking RNA transport abolishes the successful sequence. At the tested long interval, the small group's internal communication is sufficient for continuation after separation from surrounding tissue. This does not retrospectively identify the cause of the patch's initial correction.

**P16 tests how quickly members can be replaced.** A detached three-cell chain undergoes six replacement events, replacing each position twice. Tested single-cell intervals are 1, 10, 100 and 20,000 model time units. Only single-cell replacement at **20,000** supports the complete E-retention result and onward transmission. Faster single replacements fail; paired and whole-trio replacements fail at all tested schedules. The slow successful condition requires RNA communication. Both historical origins and ordinary-E stock show the same categorical pattern.

The spacing between 100 and 20,000 leaves the transition interval unresolved. The experiment does not establish a universal minimum number of survivors, because replacing one versus two cells also changes geometry and exposure. Nor does early E classification establish that a member is already competent to sustain repeated transfer.

**Interpretation:** state renewal depends on a temporally extended relationship among cells. The group can maintain and pass the state without continuing surrounding-tissue instruction, under supplied contact and replacement schedules. That is a substantive collective result. It remains finite regulatory-state transmission, with no demonstrated acquired-history advantage, autonomous cell reproduction, cancer efficacy, or inherited flexible collective problem-solving. Numerical-resolution pairs and counterfactual forks are not independent biological replicates.

## 5. L20: another causal handle; forecasting remains development work

[D147](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/codex.lawsConfirmation/L20-extension-1/stopping-time/RESULTS.md) finds no resolved primary benefit of stopping at write1000 versus continuing to write1300. [D148](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/codex.lawsConfirmation/L20-extension-1/packet-placement/RESULTS.md) finds no resolved average rescue from restoring the earlier packet placement. Particular cases nevertheless show that increasing own-shape correlation can coincide with a worsening qualified readout. Appearance and usable reconstruction remain separate endpoints.

[D149](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/codex.lawsConfirmation/L20-extension-1/competing-component/RESULTS.md) introduces a more specific intervention. It attenuates the analyst-defined competing row/column component of the record while compensating in the residual, preserving the own component, total norm, mean, placement, and matched noise. Equally sized residual edits supply the two sham controls.

The primary B/G2 improvement over their average is **+0.482 [0.273, 0.706] qualified-preference points**; the secondary improvement over untouched records is +0.467 [0.287, 0.653]. Fourteen of sixteen block differences are positive. The edit is not pure removal: attenuation and residual compensation are coupled. It is not uniform rescue or an autonomous correction mechanism. These preference points retain the amplitude-qualified observer and the −50 to +50 target-pair range; they are not ordinary accuracy percentage points.

D150–D159 progressively investigate forecast errors on known development material. Some two-reading comparisons now favor a control-derived observer, but cheaper one-copy or starting-only baselines remain competitive or better, and rankings depend on regularization and which saved future is scored. [D159](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/ingressing-minds-v-ruliad-paper-ideas/codex.lawsConfirmation/L20-extension-1/reading-components/RESULTS.md) locates a favorable contribution from the difference between readings relative to another two-reading observer; that contribution becomes negative against the one-copy reference. This is an accounting decomposition, not a causal channel-removal experiment.

**Interpretation:** D149 strengthens the mechanistic discussion. The later work gives leads for a future frozen prediction test, but it does not convert D141 into a prospective forecasting success. L20 remains STRESS_TESTED / MIXED.

## 6. FEP: useful information now has more explicit conditions

The new FEP work is a mixture of exact finite-model calculations and bounded numerical inference comparisons. Its fixture counts and arithmetic checks are not sampled biological evidence.

[Record-dependent reading](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/FEP_RECORD_DEPENDENT_READER_V1_REPORT.md) separates an intact record with an unusable reader from a readable record whose content has lost its relationship to the target. Either can leave performance at chance. A controller rewarded for learning the stored bit can pay to read random replacement content more accurately without improving target recovery.

[Reader evidence](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/FEP_READER_EVIDENCE_V1_REPORT.md) shows why repeated reads of the same record cannot by themselves validate its connection to the target in the declared model. An independently grounded route, with its reliability and independence specified, is needed. [Transfer continuation](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/FEP_TRANSFER_CONTINUATION_V1_REPORT.md) supplies a constructive example: calibration improves net task utility from **0.625 to 0.638** when it arrives in time to change a later resource choice. The subsequent review preserves the result while exposing dependence on the assumed transfer law.

[Hidden preferences](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/FEP_HIDDEN_PREFERENCES_V1_REPORT.md) and [viability preferences](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/FEP_VIABILITY_PREFERENCES_V1_REPORT.md) sharpen the agency discussion: specifying the physical task does not uniquely determine hidden-state preferences, tolerated failure risk, or reference policy. A useful decoder may invert a signal; a separate preference for literal agreement can choose costly maintenance instead. Physical resources can ground a continuation constraint without supplying a unique valuation of it.

[Structured inference](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/FEP_STRUCTURED_INFERENCE_V1_REPORT.md) reduces mean action error by **47.49%** when independent beliefs are replaced with small joint blocks. However, removing the resulting correlations slightly improves the prespecified risk-prediction metric. The decision gate passes, the proposed correlation mechanism gate fails, and the overall experiment fails its qualification. Larger-scale fixtures were not run. Exact inference is faster in every completed matched case. This representation result concerns beliefs inside the inference model, not a demonstrated increase in collective cellular intelligence.

The latest [safe-cap result](../../PlasticHeredity/NewIdeas/preprints/fep-ideas/FEP_SAFE_CAP_RELEASE_V1_REPORT.md) is constructive. Using the known model and available risk bounds, it relaxes an unnecessarily restrictive action cap while preserving the declared 10% model-risk limit and guaranteeing no worse objective. It improves **3,865 of 13,056 saved decisions**, leaving the others unchanged. At the primary checkpoint, mean regret falls **25.95% for independent beliefs** and **13.26% for grouped beliefs**. These are relative changes in a specified objective loss, not clinical risk reductions or independent-trial success rates. The analysis uses existing beliefs and known outcomes, not a new prospective inference cohort; all policy inputs exclude the exact-reference answer.

**Interpretation:** this track can strengthen a focused discussion of information that remains interpretable and useful for future action. It does not establish an advantage unique to FEP: appropriately matched conventional Bayesian planning shares the relevant policy results. The new positive follow-ups do not reverse the original mechanism failure or establish autonomous goal formation.

## 7. What should change in the next manuscript selection

1. Add E020 as an empirical case of **elementary instructed functional renewal**, with E017/E019 explaining the mechanism and E021/E022 showing its damage boundary. Replace blanket statements that the program has tested only static pattern fidelity; retain the open question of flexible collective competence.
2. Extend the unprescribed-formation continuation account to E014's sixteen replacements, showing absolute fidelity and protected-copy deficits beside historical specificity.
3. Replace the pending E013 discussion with its fresh confirmation and wrong-wiring comparison. Describe bounded predictive usefulness without claiming a universal atlas.
4. Update the cellular sequence through P16. Remove the prospective-only treatment of P13; distinguish tissue correction, isolated renewal, and turnover-rate limits. Do not infer therapeutic benefit from P14's successful sham.
5. Add D149 as a modest mechanistic result. Keep the detailed D150–D159 forecasting history in supporting material, preserving the unresolved prospective claim.
6. Consider a short, clearly labeled theoretical FEP contribution on retained content, interpretation, and useful action. The growing FEP program need not become a second main narrative in this paper.

The cross-track synthesis is that an organization's present appearance, current performance, and capacity to produce a useful successor can diverge. Reading, capture, protection, communication, and maturation provide specific intervention points. This is a synthesis of distinct models, not a demonstrated common quantitative law.

The evidence supports stronger discussion of active embodied maintenance and historically acquired access to form. It leaves independently active patterns in another realm underdetermined. It also leaves open the crucial combined test: a competence that first appears without a supplied task and then enters a durable reconstruction cycle.

## Fixed writer and nine policies

Let x=tanh(3*v), d=(x_plus-x_minus)/2. Standard capture is the mean neighbor
coactivity across cues; contrast capture is d_i*d_j. Bridge capture takes the
contrast value when it is exactly nonzero, and otherwise takes the standard
coactivity value from the SAME body. It uses no output labels, donor records,
damage history or endpoint scores. The exact-zero comparison is deliberate:
there is no epsilon, interpolation weight, magnitude floor or threshold search.
Fallback is permitted at any zero cue product, not only a returning connection.
Tiny nonzero cue products remain unchanged; the hypothesis can fail.

Each policy reads its own previous outgoing packet through sixteen successive
bodies, each with a fresh injury removing 24 non-port cells. The nine conditions,
in fixed order, are:

0. standard_preserve: standard capture; preserve available incoming |w|>0.5.
1. standard_rewrite: standard capture; rewrite all available weights.
2. bridge_preserve: bridge capture; preserve available incoming |w|>0.5.
3. bridge_rewrite: bridge capture; rewrite all available weights.
4. contrast_preserve: pure contrast capture; preserve available |w|>0.5.
5. bridge_read_blocked: condition 2, but erase the record used for reading.
   Its physically surviving strong values remain eligible for copying.
6. bridge_write_blocked: condition 2, but erase every outgoing record.
7. protected_copy: unchanged acquired record, no rebuilding body.
8. masked_copy: cumulative matched physical edge erasures, no rebuilding body.

Preservation applies to policies 0,2,4,5,6; the others either rewrite fully or
are copying references. Available weak values continue to be refreshed in each
new body; unavailable connections are zero. Capture occurs before the unchanged
128-step holding phase, which cannot change the outgoing packet.

All seven active bodies settle for 1024 of 2048 reading steps with local
absolute-strength normalization (zero denominator means zero drive). All final
readers use the same 1024/2048 timing. Births, injury and dynamical noise remain
paired across policies/cues following E022; only source ports are cue-clamped
during renewal. Teaching512/capture128/observe32, dt0.2, noise0.02, birthSD0.05,
gain2, 16x16 tissues, 4096-byte records and strict output margin>0.25 are unchanged.

The first four policies form a bridge-versus-standard by preserve-versus-rewrite
comparison. Condition 4 tests the fallback against pure contrast with copying
held fixed. Different policies subsequently develop different histories; this
does not isolate cue signs from magnitudes within the same body. Blocking the
reader leaves the stated copying route available and does not erase all history.

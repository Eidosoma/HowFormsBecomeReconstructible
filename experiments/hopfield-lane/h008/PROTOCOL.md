one-replacement feasibility screen at every dose and nominated eight hidden
pair swaps by its frozen largest-passing-dose rule. H008 asks whether that
intervention changes functional durability over sixteen replacements. H007
did not test this question. H005/H006's failed durability claims remain intact.

Retain the receipts back to H003. Do not reconstruct or change the selected

and a feasibility-selected intervention, not independent-base confirmation.

## Sixteen replacements and complete controls

For each original candidate, generate eight independent new branches of
sixteen replacements, starting from the pre-replacement H007 input. Use the
same supplied reader (gain 4 at cue sites 0–3, eight asynchronous sweeps per
cue), complete body-only writer and independent 50% undirected edge erasure
without rescaling at every replacement. Use paired damage, fresh births and
update orders across variants and controls. H008 tapes are independent of
H007 tapes and of H008 current probes. The teacher is absent. Continue all
branches after failure to observe possible recovery; no conditional stopping.

Seven arms, unchanged from the selected H007 comparison:

1. renewal: start from each variant's own packet, then write each new body;
2. restored: give both variants the original unchanged packet, then renew;
3. foreign: transplant the same variant from opposite-rule founder [2,3,0,1];
4. protected_copy: keep each source packet without injury or writing;
5. damaged_copy: retain only surviving edges cumulatively, without writing;
6. read_off: develop with gained base weights alone, then write the body;
7. write_off: read normally, but make each outgoing packet zero.

Restored trajectories must equal unchanged renewal exactly in packets,
bodies and fresh readers at every observed boundary. Every arm receives the
same terminal reader. Protected and damaged copying use less computation.

Primary outcome: success of a fresh, undamaged reader after sixteen new
replacements, requiring all four original task-aligned output means >.5.
The prespecified contrast is **swapped minus unchanged renewal**, evaluated
in either direction. A coherent hidden swap has no established long-term
direction, so this is explicitly a two-sided question. The sign is reported;
it is not chosen after outcomes to convert a directional failure into success.

Record fresh-reader success at generations 1, 4, 8 and 16, every damaged-body
success, and uninterrupted success through every body plus the terminal
reader. Also retain exact self-retention relative to each original input,
pairwise record distinctness at all seventeen boundaries, and H007's joint
functional exact-retention event at the four read checkpoints. These are
diagnostics. Continued distinctness or faithful record transmission alone
cannot establish different functional durability or rescue the primary.

## Frozen inference and qualification

Average enrolled pairs and eight branches within each nonempty network,
then weight networks equally. Bootstrap those whole-network rows jointly,
using 20,000 draws, seed 980008 and namespace `H008 whole-block bootstrap`.
Use the same block resample for every current and future statistic. Related
packets, forks and generations are not independent population replicates.
Retain all block rows, empty blocks and per-pair outcomes.

Qualification requires all current and integrity gates, plus:

- absolute terminal swapped-minus-unchanged renewal difference >=.10;
- its adjusted two-sided interval excludes zero;
- in **each** variant, renewal exceeds foreign, read_off, write_off and
  damaged_copy by >=.15, with positive adjusted one-sided lower bounds.

There are nine inferential tests: one two-sided primary and eight directional
route controls. Use Bonferroni level .05/9 for each: primary quantiles
.05/(2*9) and 1-.05/(2*9), control lower quantile .05/9. Display marginal
two-sided 95% intervals separately. This retains the earlier ten-point
minimum effect while explicitly allowing either primary direction; it does
not retroactively change H005/H006. No minimum difference is claimed against
protected copying. At least two networks are needed to form intervals, with
the sixteen-network current-coverage gate still required.

No horizon, new cohort, alternate observer, selected direction, subgroup,
smaller effect, retention metric or uninterrupted endpoint can replace a
failed primary. A positive result is a development candidate conditional on
this cohort and intervention. A null result is bounded to this finite assay;
it does not establish that all hidden-pattern changes are functionally
equivalent. No further study or manuscript edit is automatically launched.

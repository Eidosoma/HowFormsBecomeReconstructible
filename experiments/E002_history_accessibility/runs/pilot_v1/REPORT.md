## Interpretation boundary

Records can change the effective fast transition map while the declared body-plus-record update rule remains fixed. The graph census distinguishes basin redistribution from new/lost recurrent orbits exactly for these finite systems. The activity writer, address mask, and additive reader are supplied mechanisms. This study does not test serial renewal, prospective out-of-sample map prediction, arbitrary biological networks, or a universal inventory of forms.

Bootstrap intervals resample 16 complete rulebooks and are exploratory pointwise summaries. Related records, gains, and all 1,024 states within a rulebook are not independent replicates.

# E119 — the cost weakens after two rewrites; catch-up is not established

Reviewed 2 November 2026. All 32 archived E077 source blocks completed four
actual rewrites in both renewal conditions. Failures transmitted their own
outgoing records. Assessments were independent of renewal and matched across
branches and horizons; they never fed back. This is a continuation of reused
sources, not a fresh-source replication.

## Primary result

In pooled mixture records with repeated renewal injury, pruning helps the
immediate read, harms the first two successors, and has a much smaller average
cost at generations 3 and 4. Differences below are pruned minus intact in
percentage points; intervals are the frozen simultaneous 95% bands over the
five horizons within this injury condition.

| Rewrites | Intact success | Pruned success | Difference [simultaneous 95%] |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 55.908% | 62.695% | +6.787 [4.150, 9.424] |
| 1 | 64.502% | 59.766% | −4.736 [−7.373, −2.100] |
| 2 | 65.332% | 60.840% | −4.492 [−7.129, −1.855] |
| 3 | 65.918% | 64.990% | −0.928 [−3.564, 1.709] |
| 4 | 65.576% | 64.990% | −0.586 [−3.223, 2.051] |

The prespecified one-generation-delay conjunction **fails**. There is still a
resolved penalty at generation 2, P2−P1 is unresolved, and none of generations
2–4 meets the simultaneous ±3-point equivalence criterion. At generation 4,
the lower band remains just outside the margin; do not substitute its narrower
pointwise interval or widen the margin after seeing this result. All late
absolute-performance gates pass. No sustained equivalence boundary, practically
negative late generation under the stricter −3-point criterion, or resolved
alternation was established. This does not demonstrate permanent harm either.

Without further injury during renewal, the pruned-minus-intact differences at
generations 1–4 are +1.367, +1.514, +1.367 and +1.367 points, all unresolved.
Both branches reach roughly 73–74% success. There is no resolved successor
penalty in this condition, but its ±3-point equivalence gates also fail.
The independent terminal assessment still injures the fresh recipient; only
injury during the supplying rewrite was removed.

## Costs and individual outcomes

Across generations 1–4 under repeated injury, pruning adds **0.107 unsuccessful
assessments per context [0.045, 0.169]**, a pointwise whole-block 95% interval.
The 2,048 matched assessment contexts contain 32 independent source blocks;
the repeated draws are not extra independent samples. At generation 4 there
are **121 gains and 133 harms**, despite the small net difference. Without
renewal injury, excess failures are −0.056 [−0.123, 0.006], unresolved.
First and sustained recovery counts, censoring and the full initial-versus-later
effect tables are retained in the frozen summary and recovery table.

Pruning initially re-isolates an average 21.91 nonport cells. The prespecified
associations with immediate gain and first-successor loss are unresolved.
The generation-2 association is negative (−1.494 percentage points per extra
re-isolated cell [−2.760, −0.432], pointwise 95%); it is an association, not
proof of the proposed causal chain. Saved masks, capture routes and strengths
remain available for a separately declared mechanism analysis.

## Interpretation and scope

The data support a cost that extends beyond one rewrite under recurrent injury
and then becomes smaller within this window. They do not license either
“exactly one generation of delay” or “lasting impairment.” A temporary cost can
still accumulate unsuccessful reconstructions. All renewal and assessment cue
resets and ongoing noises are shared; the writer uses exact-zero fallback,
float64 records and zero normalization floor. E120 and E121 test different
regimes separately. The second-substrate question remains open.

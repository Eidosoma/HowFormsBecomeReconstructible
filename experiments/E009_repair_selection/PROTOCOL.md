## Cases and supplied repair resources

64 fresh blocks 7000–7063, four founders per block; one new E008-style rewired
graph per block and eight recipient futures per body. This increases the number
of independent decision blocks relative to E008's forecast test; it is fixed
before results. Technical block 900109 and unit cases 900110–900111 are excluded.
All founders remain included. Native A forms and captures each founder pattern;
B replaces exactly 25% of edges with long-range links, retaining degree four,
total coupling, connectivity, coordinates and the 2048-byte reader interface.

For each founder, erase exactly 192/256 entries (75%) from its clean activity-
written record using an independent uniform random mask. Retain a declared
external backup. Every candidate restores exactly 64 erased float64 entries
(512 value bytes) from that backup; 128 entries remain erased afterward.
There is no amplitude normalization. All policies use the same eight candidates:
seven uniform random 64-subsets of erased entries and the subset with largest
absolute backup values. Distinct subsets only; ties in the heuristic use lower
site index. Randomize the menu order before probing. A fixed candidate-generation
attempt limit fails technically rather than selecting a new seed.

One injury/menu per founder, shared across its recipient futures and A/B bodies.
References: intact, unrepaired, complete foreign record (next founder cyclically),
and complete erasure. Source and target each develop for 300 steps from fresh,
independent births/noise, with noise paired across options within a body.
There is no body sign-flip challenge in this study. Restoration happens before
the fresh body develops, not as autonomous repair of an injured living body.
The backup, intervention addresses and source probes are supplied resources;
equal restoration budgets do not imply equal sensing/computation/information cost.

## Frozen selectors and chronology

The map receives the unchanged E008 features and source A observations. All
repaired candidates have the same descriptors: damage 0, mixture 0, deletion
.5. This is an intermediate deletion fraction and a specific injury/menu task
not used to fit E008. The intact reference, unrepaired (.75), foreign (mixture 1)
and erased (deletion 1) are also passed through the frozen map for transparency.

Score qualified agreement exactly as before (matching sign and |V|>.2), averaged
over eight recipients. Let specificity be Qown minus Qcyclic-foreign. Policies:

- Map: choose largest predicted target specificity, equivalently largest
  predicted change in specificity relative to the unknown but common intact B.
- Source: choose the candidate with largest observed A specificity.
- Heuristic: choose the largest-absolute-value restoration candidate.
- Random menu choice: uniform probability over the same eight candidates.

Break exact selector ties by first menu index. Freeze one-hot choices for the
three deterministic policies and uniform weights for the randomized policy.
For random, the primary comparator is its exact mean over the evaluated menu,
which estimates the frozen randomized policy without extra action-draw noise.
This is uniform choice over the menu, not uniform sampling over all possible
repair subsets (the menu deliberately includes a strong heuristic candidate).

Generate A only, calculate and hash every predicted response and selection,
and retain source graph/menu/packet hashes. Only after that completed run and
audit may B test outcomes be generated. Evaluate every menu option afterward
to estimate each frozen policy on paired futures; no B outcome selects an action.
The technical benchmark can exercise both bodies, with no blind-result claim.

## Endpoint and qualification

Primary utility: B specificity after selected repair minus B specificity with
the unrepaired record. Average recipients, then four founders within each block.
Use 10000 whole-block percentile 95% intervals (E009:utility). The full claim
requires positive lower bounds for map utility minus each of source, heuristic
and random utility, plus positive map specificity recovery and positive map
own-agreement recovery versus unrepaired. This is one conjunction. Report every
failed or unresolved contrast, with no revised criterion or follow-up sample.

Secondary: absolute specificity and own agreement, own-agreement recovery for
each policy, choice agreement, candidate-frequency counts, and the gap to the
hindsight best B option in each menu. That hindsight comparison uses noisy
finite-recipient outcomes and is an optimistic diagnostic, never an available
selector. Report per-founder values, all candidate predictions and outcomes,
and reference contrasts. The claim concerns supplied-budget decision utility
in this tissue family; forecast accuracy alone cannot pass it.

# H105 — fixed projection writing through four actual handoffs

2 November 2026. Authorized after H104's fresh one-handoff writing gain and
passing independent closeout. This is a finite test of continued transmission
before another pruning screen. It does not change H101/H103's failed reversal
verdicts, consume their reserved confirmation cohorts or resume learned-reader
work. No pruning or new writer selection occurs here.

## Fixed task, reader and lineage

Reuse the qualified H104 apparatus unchanged: 64 binary neurons (eight cues,
40 hidden, sixteen outputs), eight orthogonal Walsh cues, two independent
balanced-output founders, two lineages per founder, and two assessment draws.
The symmetric Gaussian base has scale .12/sqrt(64), with zero diagonal.
Acquisition uses cue gain four and teacher-clamped outputs for eight sweeps.
Subsequent bodies receive only cues, an inherited record, fresh random initial
activity and update orders. Cue gain is two, record-edge deletion is 20%,
the base is intact, and eight asynchronous sweeps use threshold +/-1e-12.
Task success remains at least fourteen of sixteen correct output bits.
Targets are restricted to acquisition and external scoring.

Start four writer policies from the identical acquired record: ordinary
Hebbian, centered, projection, and projection-norm-matched ordinary writing,
using H104's unchanged strict activity-only writer. Each policy has its own
actual supplying bodies at each boundary. A fresh body receives its preceding
record, reads all eight cues, and supplies the resulting activity to its fixed
writer. The newly written record is the only inherited input to the next
body. All four policies share supplying births, injury masks and orders at
each generation. These tapes are independently drawn between generations.

At the first boundary all four writers receive exactly the same activity.
Later their activity differs because of their earlier writes. Later policy
comparisons therefore measure cumulative effects of the fixed writing rules,
not equal-valued input patterns at every generation. All policies have the
same input types and no ancestral activity, teacher targets, incoming record,
recipient outcomes or previous states available to the writer.

Retain three protected record benchmarks: copying the own initial record,
copying the other founder's initial record while scoring the own task, and an
erased record. These records remain unchanged across handoffs. The protected
copy has access to the incoming record and is not an equally informed writer.
The seven arms are ordered Hebbian, centered, projection, norm-Hebbian, copy,
donor, erased. No captured-pattern or ancestral-activity rescue branch is used.

Measure G0 and descendants after G1, G2, G3 and G4 actual handoffs. Assessment
births, injury and update orders are identical across arms AND horizons; they
are separate from all supplying-body tapes. Thus protected-copy/donor/erased
assessments must be exactly invariant across G0--G4. Assessment reads do not
write the next record; each boundary's separately matched supplying bodies do.
Every supplying body and actual outgoing record is retained, along with all
reader traces, fields, energies, static fields and random tapes.

## Distinct scientific verdicts

Root seed **105105**. Use four excluded engineering networks and **32 fresh
main networks**, with all 36 input tapes and all sources frozen before
acquisition. A separate fixture network is excluded. There is no selection
stage, adaptive expansion or automatic subsequent campaign. Networks are the
independent units; founders, lineages, draws and queries remain nested.

The first verdict is **FOUR_HANDOFF_TASK_ADVANTAGE**. At every G1--G4, require
projection task success >=50%, protected-copy task success >=50%, and projection
gaps >=25 percentage points from each source control. At each horizon compare
projection with each of the three other writers: every mean gain must be >=5
points, its adjusted two-sided lower interval bound >0, and differences must
be positive on at least 16/32 networks. These are twelve primary task contrasts.
Failure of any requirement yields **NO_QUALIFIED_FOUR_HANDOFF_TASK_ADVANTAGE**.
Report terminal G4 effects and every earlier horizon, including failures.

The second, explicitly noncomparative verdict is
**FOUR_HANDOFF_LOCAL_CAPTURE_FIDELITY**. At each G1--G4, projection's fresh
recipient must reproduce the exact output of its own immediately preceding
supplier on a mean >=50% of queries, with adjusted lower interval bound >50%.
These are four primary absolute-fidelity intervals. Failure at any boundary
yields **NO_QUALIFIED_FOUR_HANDOFF_LOCAL_CAPTURE_FIDELITY**. This is not recovery
of the original task, ancestral information, or an advantage over the other
writers. H103 already showed that a failed task can become highly stable, so
superiority over later, potentially incorrect captured states is not required
for this local-fidelity verdict.

Use 20,000 paired whole-network bootstrap resamples from
`105105/main/inference`. All sixteen primary intervals have level
**1 - .05/16 = .996875**, a nominal 95% family target. Both verdicts and their
individual gates are always reported. The joint verdict is
**FOUR_HANDOFF_TASK_AND_CAPTURE_QUALIFIED** only if both pass; otherwise
**NO_JOINT_FOUR_HANDOFF_QUALIFICATION**. Neither verdict implies equivalence or
superiority to copying, a present-benefit/successor-cost reversal, or permanent
retention beyond the four-boundary observation window.

## Secondary scores and diagnosis

Report all seven task curves: query success, exact queries, complete eight-query
tasks, output-bit accuracy, and matched G0-to-later retained/lost/gained query
fractions. For the four actual writers at G1--G4, separately report exact
captured outputs, exact complete free patterns, and output/free-bit fidelity,
always relative to the immediately preceding actual supplier. These capture
scores are undefined for G0 and protected record benchmarks and are omitted
there, not assigned zero. Report supplier task accuracy, hidden-pattern
diversity/overlap and static stability of captured patterns under each matched
recipient matrix. Report records' norms and isolated cells separately from
effective supplying-body isolation. Static stability does not measure basin
size or semantic correctness. All secondary curves and differences use
descriptive paired 95% intervals and cannot replace a failed primary.

If task competence fails while capture remains high, locate when errors enter
the actual supplier rather than promoting accurate propagation of errors to
task retention. If both fail, inspect stored-pattern stability and fresh access
before another writer/predictor search. No new diagnostic dynamics are launched
automatically; the retained evidence supports these finite observations.

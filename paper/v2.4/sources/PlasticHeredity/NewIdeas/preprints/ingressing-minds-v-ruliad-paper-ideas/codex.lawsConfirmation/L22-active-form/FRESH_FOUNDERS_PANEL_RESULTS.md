## Answer in plain language

The direction substitution's modest benefit for residual records and larger
harm for common records both recur on the four newly acquired histories.
The residual benefit is not universal: three block means improve and one
worsens. Common records worsen in three blocks and tie in one. This is
descriptive recurrence, not a powered confirmation or a universal law.

Protected captured information remains a strong comparator. Its common-record
recognition is slightly higher than captured live repair, while its shape
correlation is lower. These observations do not establish that ongoing
embodiment is necessary, nor that a recognizable label preserves full shape.

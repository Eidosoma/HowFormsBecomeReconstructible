## Bottom line in plain language

Three findings survive. The arrangement of surviving tissue weakly steers
repair. Active reference feedback helps the tissue recover. A deliberately
arranged theta field can tell a fresh body which of two patterns to grow,
at a fixed strength within the source's nominal native-scale range.

That is a useful division of roles, not an exclusive division of memory.
In particular, the unrestricted claim that theta cannot store an instance
at native-scale strength has a reproducible counterexample. Conversely,
supplying a pattern to theta is not showing how experience writes it, how
an ordinary unaligned field uses it, or how descendants renew it.

“Closed” means this round has a clause-level verdict and replacement wording.
It does not mean every clause was confirmed, or that L16 can never be reopened.

## Proposed replacement wording

In this sheet model, state roles depend on intervention, observer and timescale.
Active reference feedback helps short-horizon repair, and matched fresh-context
experiments show a small repeatable influence of surviving spatial organization
on pattern preference. Ordinary theta has small aggregate effects under the
tested local repair knives, while an aggregate transplant assay reports strong
long-run donor bias. Deliberately target-aligned theta at fixed native-scale
norm can specify either of two patterns in fresh bodies, with a large advantage
over same-value shuffled fields. These findings establish conditional repair
roles and supplied static capacity, not exclusive carriers, zero instance
capacity, class equivalence, horizon causality or autonomous renewable heredity.
E66's writable-main positive and separate imprint failure remain distinct,
aggregate-only evidence requiring their own causal controls.

This wording is a review recommendation, not an edit to LAWS.md or the preprint.

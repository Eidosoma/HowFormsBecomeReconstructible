## Baseline and unconditional onward panel

both resolutions), six exact maximum-age no-turnover replays, and eighteen
whole-trio wipe controls. Each wipe pair matches one ordered schedule: replace

## Contrasts and interpretation

Show the full 3x3 open/RNA grid per founder, with both numerical resolutions.
At fixed later=100, compare first=300 and 1,000 against first=100. These test
whether longer initial preparation rescues the complete fast schedule. At
fixed first=100, compare later=300 and 1,000 against later=100. These test the
effect of lengthening all later windows with unchanged initial preparation.
Report first-newcomer survival separately from complete renewal. A positive
open renewal with negative matched RNA renewal supports RNA dependence for
that schedule. Missing controls cannot qualify a contrast or chain.

If longer initial preparation fails to rescue later=100, conclude only that
the tested initial waits were insufficient under that later schedule. If it
rescues, conclude that extra initial preparation suffices in that tested
schedule, not that every later interval can be arbitrarily short. Later-window
changes alter all five exposures, so their individual contributions remain
unresolved. No exact threshold or monotonicity is assumed. Preparation duration
and connected exposure are coupled; an extra connected-versus-isolated waiting
fork is outside this panel. Molecular maturation, acquired-history advantage
and autonomous reproduction require separate evidence. Numerical pairs and
counterfactual forks are not independent biological replicates.

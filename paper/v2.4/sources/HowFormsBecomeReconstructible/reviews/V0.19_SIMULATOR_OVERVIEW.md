## Model correspondence

- Tissue sheets: a 16 by 16 periodic activity field, reference feedback, writable threshold/bias and noisy local coupling. The diagram separates a body from the spatial record sent into a fresh body. Which field serves as the record varies by assay; it does not imply that all three state layers are always transmitted. Sources: Appendix C.1 and C.16 in [the methods](../METHODS_AND_PROVENANCE.md).
- Local functional networks: a 16 by 16 periodic activity model with 512 reciprocal nearest-neighbour weights. Two supplied cue trials feed a local activity-based writer; weights pass into fresh activity states with declared damage. The network is initially taught one of two conditional mappings; the renewal writer receives activities from input-cued trials, not desired output labels. Sources: C.17 and C.23.
- Cellular regulation: the published BITSEY implementation supplies regulatory/ionic dynamics and junction transport; program extensions supply contacts, donor removal and replacement assays. Whole simulated cell states continue in live cells. No separate digital packet or simulated division is implied. Source: C.7; its unresolved source-paper table/geometry correspondence remains unchanged.

The lower workflow summarizes numerical experiments: initialize, step equations, intervene, continue and measure. Controls, fresh assays and repeated declared cases are shown without implying identical cohorts, stochasticity, update algorithms or inference units across the three families. Grid patches, output ports and cell counts in the drawing are schematic.

# E032 — saved-record diagnosis of serial renewal failure

Authorized by “lets go” after E031's failed sixteen-renewal criterion on
27 September 2026. This is an exploratory, post hoc diagnosis of the **same
32 E031 blocks / 512 lineages**, not a fresh replication or causal intervention.
All declared cases, ten record policies, eight body branches, all sixteen
boundaries and all five terminal horizons remain included. No trajectories
are simulated and no thresholds or scientific outcomes are retuned.

Freeze this analysis before examining new per-record measurements. Input
provenance binds E031's completed run, independent audit and closeout. Read
the acquired source packets and compact boundary/terminal arrays; verify
original file hashes, retained-array shape/dtype/byte digests and all inputs.
The reference weights are the acquired consistent-teaching record. A change
in their sign is a descriptive difference, not proof of an incorrect function.

## Fixed descriptive measurements

For every outgoing packet at g0–g16 report nonzero, strong (|w|>0.5), weak
nonzero and sign-changed edge counts, absolute weight mass, mean absolute
deviation from the acquired record, and original-strong edges still strong
with the same or opposite sign. At g1–g16 also count incoming strong edges
removed physically, newly strong edges among available weak inputs, strong
inputs weakened by writing, strong sign reversals, and how many newly strong
or retained-strong edges disagree with nonzero acquired signs. Track copied
and refreshed coordinate counts for the maturing selector. The >0.5 cutoff
is inherited from the tested policy, not selected here.

For the maturing and full-rewriting packets at every generation, measure
source-to-output reachability in the undirected subgraph of strong edges:
fraction of the eight output-port cells reachable from any source-port cell,
and whether every output cell is reachable. This diagnostic ignores weak
edges and edge signs; it is not the actual weighted reader or a sufficiency
test. Other policies receive the same packet measurements without graph
analysis; missing graph measurements are explicitly absent.

Reproduce body and terminal scores from retained mean ports. Each two-cue
case belongs to exactly one class: both correct; both fully reversed; at least
one output locked strongly to the same sign under both opposing cues; or
remaining failures. Strong means absolute output>0.25, the original scoring
margin. Also retain cue-response amplitude and cue-independent bias for each
output. Classes are operational descriptions, not inferred dynamical attractors.

For each body policy report first observed failure, continued success, recovery
after failure and new failure between consecutive challenges. These successive
bodies have independently drawn injuries/births/noise, so failure need not be
absorbing. Compare packet measurements in successful versus unsuccessful
current bodies and, separately, outgoing packets with successful versus
unsuccessful intact terminal assays at h1/h3/h8/h16. Conditional associations
do not establish that the selected feature causes failure. No future outcome
is used as an intervention or eligibility filter.

Every numerical summary retains its per-block means. Whole-block bootstrap
intervals use 10000 resamples with namespace ["E032","bootstrap","diagnosis"].
Conditional ratios resample complete block numerators and denominators rather
than averaging unequal group rates; zero-denominator summaries remain null.
All intervals are descriptive and pointwise, with no new pass/fail gate.
